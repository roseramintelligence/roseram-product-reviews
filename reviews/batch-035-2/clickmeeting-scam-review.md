**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Webinar and online event platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

ClickMeeting is a browser-based platform for live and automated webinars, online meetings, training and paid events. ## Editorial verdict

ClickMeeting can fit teams that need to host recurring webinars, run training or sell access to live events. Its differentiators are event registration and follow-up flows, interactive presenter tools and integrations such as REST API and webhooks. ## Webinars, meetings and training in one browser workflow

ClickMeeting is a browser-based event platform for webinars, online meetings and training. Hosts can create registration pages, schedule events, invite attendees, present media and use interactive tools such as polls, Q&A and whiteboards. The platform also supports recordings and event reports, so teams can review attendance and follow up after a session.

The official feature comparison includes paid webinars, integrations, breakout rooms and on-demand event options, with availability varying by plan. A team that delivers the same course or product demonstration repeatedly may value reusable materials and registration automation. A small group that only needs occasional video calls should compare the additional webinar workflow against a lighter meeting product.


## Choose a plan based on audience size and event frequency

ClickMeeting offers Free trial, Essential, Standard, Pro and Enterprise packages. Its page ties plan pricing to the attendee cap and lets buyers compare monthly and annual payment. The free trial is listed as 14 days with no card required, while Enterprise is intended for larger events and custom requirements. Exact price values may be rendered dynamically according to location and configuration.

Start the comparison with expected peak attendance, number of events at once, presenters, recording hours and file storage. Then add automation, branded pages, integrations, reporting and security requirements. The upper limit of a package matters more than a generic starting price if a webinar is expected to attract a large audience.


## Review registration, reminders and paid-event tools

The platform’s public comparison describes registration pages, confirmation and reminder messages, waiting rooms, event pages and attendee information collection. Automated and on-demand webinars, follow-up messages and certificates appear in higher-level workflow descriptions. Confirm which messages are included, whether contacts can be exported and how consent data is retained.

ClickMeeting also describes accepting payment for events through supported payment services. If ticket sales are central to the business model, test checkout, refunds, currency, taxes and attendee access before promoting an event. Confirm whether payment processing fees come from the external payment provider and whether the webinar plan adds its own fees.


## Check recording, integrations and operating limits

Recordings and file storage have tier-specific limits. Check how many hours can be retained, where event files are stored, who can access exports and what happens when an account reaches its quota. The official plan comparison describes REST API and webhooks for account and event activity, plus Zapier and connections to tools such as Dropbox, Slack and Moodle. Validate the specific integration required rather than assuming every workflow is included.

For multi-host programs, test presenter permissions, subaccounts, parallel events and administrative access. Enterprise features such as SSO, white-label, support commitments and onboarding are presented as custom requirements. Ask for the exact service level and contractual scope in a quote.


## Who should evaluate ClickMeeting?

ClickMeeting is worth testing for organizations that run regular webinars, online courses, lead-generation events or paid training. Its combination of event pages, interactive tools and follow-up can keep several tasks in one platform. The tradeoff is that features, audience ceilings and storage vary by package, so the team should model its actual event calendar before subscribing.

This editorial is based on ClickMeeting’s public pricing and feature pages, not an independent event trial. Confirm current capacity, localized price, recording limits and feature entitlements directly in the account or a written vendor quote.
