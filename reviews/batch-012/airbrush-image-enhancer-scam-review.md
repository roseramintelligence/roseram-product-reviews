## AirBrush Image Enhancer: distinguish clarity from evidence

Enhancement can make edges and textures appear clearer, but that does not mean new-looking detail was present in the original photograph. Decide which aspects need to remain faithful before evaluating the output. A decorative image and a photograph used to document a product or incident require different standards. Visual sharpness should not become a substitute for source accuracy.

The publisher describes AI enhancement and upscaling. Confirm the exact mode and current output conditions for your intended use. A selected example does not establish the behavior of a low-light portrait, small product label or another difficult input. Evaluate representative images and keep the originals available throughout review.


## A source-to-output fidelity check

Use an authorized image containing details your reviewer can recognize. Inspect the enhanced result at the intended display or print size and compare faces, text, object boundaries and fine patterns. Reject a change that invents a meaningful product detail even if the overall photograph appears cleaner.

Ask how the current workflow handles revisions, downloads and stored uploads. This review does not audit privacy practices or establish rights for a particular photograph. Confirm those conditions directly and use a low-risk input during evaluation. An acceptable visual result does not resolve the obligations attached to the original material.

A useful exception is a small label that becomes more legible-looking but changes its characters. Ask a reviewer to compare it with a verified source rather than infer that apparent clarity means correctness. This illustrates why enhancement should be assessed by the intended use: a pleasing reconstruction may be unsuitable when a viewer relies on the image for factual information.

For a batch of photographs, include more than the cleanest input in the pilot. A difficult face, a small label and a patterned surface can expose different reconstruction problems. Record which images remain unsuitable instead of assuming that a successful example generalizes. This makes the evaluation useful for the actual collection and prevents selective samples from defining an unrealistic acceptance standard.
