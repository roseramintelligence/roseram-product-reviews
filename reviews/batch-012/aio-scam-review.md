# AIO review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-25  
**Category:** Fashion product development and AI-assisted apparel design platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

AIO positions itself as a fashion product development platform connecting design, technical packs, bills of materials, collaboration, sampling, production stages and Digital Product Passport information. Its account page lists Studio, Business and Enterprise subscriptions with different AI credit allowances, support and enterprise features; displayed prices use annual billing and the page also notes annual savings. A separate image license permits commercial and personal use of generated images while restricting resale or redistribution of raw images. Fashion teams should confirm the applicable plan, credit terms, workflow fit, data residency and licensing before using it for a live collection.

## Editorial verdict

AIO may be relevant to fashion teams trying to connect concept development with product specifications, approvals, supplier handoffs and compliance data. The breadth is useful only if the team’s product records, manufacturing partners and compliance process can be represented accurately. Validate one style from sketch through bill of materials and supplier review, check data export and audit trails, and verify which claims and integrations apply to the actual contract.

## Follow a garment record across the product lifecycle

AIO’s product materials present a fashion workflow organized around one evolving product record. A style begins with concept and sketch, then adds development information such as a technical pack and bill of materials, sample approvals, production details and Digital Product Passport fields. The company also describes AI design, virtual sampling, 3D prototyping, collaboration and an agent that can handle product updates or reports through email. Ask to see these capabilities in the account and plan being considered.

Choose one non-sensitive style and trace it through the proposed process. Check whether sketches, measurements, fabrics, colorways, supplier records, approvals, cost fields and version history map cleanly. Ask where suppliers interact, what data they see, and how an edit is attributed or rolled back. A coherent demo should show the same product identifiers and current specification at every stage rather than disconnected screens.


## Compare AI credits and annual billing carefully

The account page showed Studio at $120 monthly with annual billing and 1,000 credits, Business at $240 with 5,000 credits, and Enterprise at $640 with unlimited credits; it also presented a custom Enterprise option. Tiers differ in AI engine, support, onboarding and collaboration or enterprise options. These monthly amounts are attached to annual billing, and the page notes 20% savings. Confirm upfront amount, renewal, credit refresh and usage charges before relying on the monthly equivalent.

Estimate design work from the number of styles, iterations, teams and outputs expected in a season. Ask which actions consume credits, whether failed or revised jobs use credits, if credits roll over and what happens after a limit is reached. Verify whether private models, data residency, ERP/PLM integration, private instances or custom workflows are included or separately quoted.


## Review image rights, records and supplier data

AIO’s image license says an active subscription is required for generation and use, permits specified commercial and personal uses, and restricts redistribution, resale or sublicensing of raw images. It says download access is limited to an active subscription, while previously downloaded images may continue to be used if the license was valid when generated. Confirm how these terms affect source files, client work, marketplace use and supplier sharing.

The platform can involve designs, bills of materials, suppliers, costs, comments and product compliance data. Ask about data ownership, retention, export formats, region, backups, permissions, audit history and use of customer data in model training. Confirm how factories or reviewers get access and whether supplier details can be restricted. Keep exports and the governing plan and license for each collection.


## Pilot one style before onboarding a collection

Use a pilot style with known measurements, materials, supplier details and approval stages. Ask design, development, sourcing and compliance users to perform normal tasks and record where data must be re-entered. Verify whether the bill of materials exports, supplier handoff carries the correct version, and passport fields are complete and traceable. Treat generated concepts or illustrative passport data as drafts until responsible staff validate them.

Define acceptance criteria before the demo: version accuracy, permission boundaries, integrations, export quality, support and late-change handling. Compare the process with existing tools and include migration, training, annual prepayment and implementation in cost. This guide uses public information and the listed license; it is not a production trial, compliance assessment or claim that generated designs are manufacturing-ready without review.


## Features and decision criteria

- One product record is presented for design, technical pack, bill of materials, sampling, production and passport data
- AI-assisted fashion design, virtual sampling, 3D prototyping and fashion-agent workflows are described by the vendor
- Digital Product Passport information is presented as derived from product and material records
- Plan tiers vary in credits, model access, support, onboarding and enterprise integrations or governance options

## Pricing considerations

The account page displays monthly-equivalent prices with annual billing and indicates 20% savings. Confirm whether amounts are billed upfront or monthly, actual total, taxes, credit reset and overage behavior, and which regional or organization terms apply. Distinguish the displayed $640 plan from the custom enterprise offering.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is AIO for fashion teams?

AIO describes a platform for fashion product design and development connecting design records, technical packs, bills of materials, collaboration, production tracking and Digital Product Passport data.

### How much does AIO cost?

The account page displayed Studio at $120, Business at $240 and Enterprise at $640 monthly with annual billing, plus a custom enterprise option. Verify the total and terms before purchase.

### Can AIO-generated images be resold or redistributed?

Its image license permits certain commercial and personal uses but says raw images cannot be redistributed or resold, and generated images require an active subscription. Review the current license for your intended use.
