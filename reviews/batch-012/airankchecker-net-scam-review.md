# AIRankChecker.net review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-28  
**Category:** AI search visibility and managed content operations  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

AIRankChecker.net retains legacy wallet language while its pricing page says Cognizo acquired the service and retired that model. This review examines current-offer confirmation, old balances, subscription scope, prompt sampling, content approvals, exports and attribution limits.

## Editorial verdict

This desk review does not establish that AIRankChecker.net is a scam or certify the business as safe. The cited publisher material describes an offer that can be evaluated against your requirements. Before paying, verify the seller, the current terms, any cancellation rules and the capabilities that matter to your use case. The discussion below separates publisher claims from suggested buyer checks; it does not report hands-on testing or an independent security audit.

The presence of legacy language is a concrete reason to clarify the offer, not sufficient evidence that an individual was charged incorrectly or that a refund is due.

If the old pay-as-you-go structure was the reason for considering the product, reassess the decision using the current subscription scope. The newer page bundles monitoring, content-related work and analytics rather than describing only individual prompt checks. That changes both the price comparison and the work being purchased. This review has not tested checkout or established which legacy accounts retain access. Avoid making a new account or repeating a payment merely to discover the answer. Use the official account and support route to confirm the actual offer associated with the intended purchase.

## Platform and managed service assign different responsibilities

The captured pricing page presents a Platform offer and a Done-for-You offer, with different starting subscription prices. The descriptions distinguish a team using the system directly from a provider executing work on the customer's behalf. That difference affects the required internal capacity. Access to insights and content tools is not the same deliverable as a managed publishing program. A buyer should specify who researches prompts, approves content, changes the website and reviews results before comparing the two tiers or assuming the managed offer eliminates all customer work.

Write down the expected deliverables and retained responsibilities for the selected service. A self-directed marketing team may need raw evidence and flexible reporting; a managed engagement may need an explicit approval schedule and publication scope. These are suggested procurement questions, not contractual details independently verified here. Confirm whether access, setup, production volume and support are included in the current quote. The captured starting price is not necessarily the total for every brand count or requirement. A meaningful comparison considers the accepted work and operational burden, rather than the idea that both plans simply provide more or fewer identical checks.


## Prompt counts need a defined tracking unit

The Platform card lists a prompt allowance and a choice of platforms, while the FAQ describes prompt editing and suggestions. A buyer should ask what the tracked unit means: a topic, an exact prompt, a prompt-platform combination or another billing definition. The public summary does not establish every detail of scheduling, replacement and rerun behavior. A count is useful only when it can be mapped to the team's actual monitoring questions. More prompts do not automatically create a more representative sample if they all ask nearly the same thing or omit the buyer intents that matter.

Build a small prompt set around distinct audience needs and preserve the exact wording used for comparison. Include branded and non-branded queries where appropriate to the research objective, without treating either as a complete market picture. These are proposed evaluation practices, not a prompt study conducted with the service. Ask how prompt changes affect historical comparisons and whether the report distinguishes old from new inputs. The goal is interpretable evidence about a defined set of questions, rather than a large number whose composition changes in ways that make a visibility trend difficult to explain.


## AI visibility is an observed sample rather than universal rank

The captured pages describe tracking mentions, citations, sentiment and position across AI systems. Those measures can be useful, but they concern the responses collected under particular conditions. A result from one prompt, account context or execution time does not establish the answer every user receives. This review has not examined the sampling method, platform access or response reproducibility. A buyer should ask how the service represents variability and whether raw examples are available to support an aggregate score. A metric should not imply greater certainty than the collection method can support.

For a pilot, inspect a few underlying responses and check the distinction between a brand mention, a recommendation and a cited source. Record the platform, prompt and collection time where available. These are suggested checks, not verified Cognizo results. Compare changes using reasonably consistent inputs and note product or platform changes that may affect interpretation. An upward score can prompt investigation without proving a causal improvement attributable to one campaign. The practical value lies in evidence the team can understand and use, including absences and ambiguous matches, rather than a number presented without its scope.

## Managed publishing requires an explicit approval boundary

The newer pricing descriptions use language about executing work and publishing content for customers. That raises an access and governance question separate from monitoring. The saved page does not establish the complete permissions or review configuration for every engagement. ## Lead attribution is a separate measurement problem

The pricing page describes traffic analytics, lead tracking and attribution. Those features can help connect visibility work to business activity, but a mention in an AI answer is not the same event as a visit, inquiry or accepted sale. A buyer should ask how the service identifies each stage and handles cases with incomplete referral information. The captured material does not establish a universal way to attribute every customer journey or prove that all reported leads were caused by the content program. This review has not inspected analytics events or compared attribution with transaction records.

Define the business's conversion event and compare it with the provider's report terminology. Separate anonymous visits, form submissions, qualified inquiries and paying customers rather than combining them into a single success total. These are suggested measurement controls, not observed reporting defects. Record other campaigns and website changes that could affect outcomes during the evaluation. A useful analytics product helps the team make qualified decisions from incomplete evidence; it should not require treating every correlation as a causal result. The commercial assessment should reflect accepted leads and appropriate costs, with limitations visible instead of hidden by an impressive aggregate.
