# HelpScout review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Shared inbox and customer support software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Help Scout combines a shared customer-support inbox with knowledge-base publishing and multichannel support on paid plans. Its Free plan supports up to five users but limits monthly contacts, inboxes and documentation capacity.

## Editorial verdict

Help Scout’s public plan page clearly describes user, inbox, contact and AI resolution limits. A small team can trial the free tier, while growing teams should calculate paid seats, extra inboxes, Docs sites and AI charges using their expected conversation volume.

## Help Scout review: shared inbox and knowledge base

Help Scout combines a shared inbox with contact history, saved replies and a documentation site. Paid tiers add additional inboxes, customer channels, workflows, SLAs and collaboration controls. The product is designed to make a support exchange feel like a conversation while giving a team common ownership of customer questions.

A practical pilot should test who owns a new conversation, when it becomes assigned, how duplicate messages are joined and whether a customer’s prior history appears to the right agents. Put the most common answers into the Docs site, then verify that the article is easy to find and remains accurate. The inbox should help staff resolve a request without exposing private internal notes to a customer.


## Help Scout Free plan limits and paid pricing

Help Scout lists a Free plan with up to five users, one shared inbox and one Docs site. Its Free-plan guide says that teams can reply to up to 100 contacts per month and has additional limits on articles and reporting. Paid Standard, Plus and Pro plans are listed at $25, $45 and $75 per user per month respectively, with paid features and seat limits varying by tier.

The free contact limit is about unique people helped, not simply how many messages the team sends. A single person with several conversations may count once, while a busy month can reach the ceiling quickly. Estimate the active team seats and monthly unique contacts; then check if the account needs extra inboxes or documentation sites, which Help Scout lists as separately priced options.


## Help Scout channels, automation and Envoy billing

The plan comparison describes live chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger support on paid plans, with service-level policies, workflows and routing added at different levels. Envoy is listed as an AI assistant priced per resolution after a three-month free trial. Help Scout defines a resolution as a conversation answered by AI without human assistance and says a monthly spending cap can disable AI Answers after the cap is reached.

Before enabling AI, decide which help-center sources it may use and what topics always require a person. Create a small set of questions from actual customer history and inspect the answer and escalation behavior. Configure a spending cap and alert. AI answers should not be allowed to make promises about refunds or policies that do not match the company’s approved support guidelines.


## Help Scout data ownership and migration planning

Changing an inbox provider affects contact records, conversation history, help articles and support-widget links. Export a sample and verify timestamps, attachments, customer identity and private notes before migrating the full support history. Keep suppression and privacy requests linked to the originating customer record.

If the Docs site uses a custom domain, update DNS and verify links as part of the transition checklist. Retain a route for customers to reach a real person while agents learn the new system. Set permissions by team and role, and define who approves changes to saved replies, automation and public articles.


## Help Scout alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Help Scout with Zendesk, Freshdesk and Front using the same support team size, unique contacts, inbox count, channels and AI usage. Include a per-seat plan cost, additional inbox and Docs-site charges, agent training and time spent maintaining help articles. A small company may appreciate a straightforward shared inbox; a contact center may need more advanced phone, workforce or analytics controls.

Help Scout’s official plan and billing materials describe a commercial customer-support product; they do not show that Help Scout is a scam. This desk review did not run an account or measure response quality. Confirm the current seat minimums, Free-plan limits, add-ons and AI caps at checkout before moving a production support address.


## Features and decision criteria

- Shared inboxes, contact history and saved replies
- Knowledge-base sites and website support widgets
- Paid tiers add channels, workflows, routing and AI options

## Pricing considerations

Prices and limits checked on the official page on 2026-11-16; billing cadence and contact counts should be confirmed in the current quote. Envoy is a separate AI resolution add-on listed at $0.75 per resolution after a three-month trial. Additional inboxes and Docs sites may also be charged separately.
