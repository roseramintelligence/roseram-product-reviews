# Help Desk Migration review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Help desk and ITSM data migration  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Help Desk Migration offers a no-code migration wizard and expert migration services for transferring tickets, contacts, companies, attachments and knowledge-base content between supported help desk, ITSM and related platforms. Its site says it supports more than 100 platforms, offers a free demo migration and produces data-integrity reports. Exact migration pricing is personalized by record volume, mapping, support and customization; the vendor says a free demo can produce a more precise quote.

## Editorial verdict

This service may help a support or IT team move historical records between help desk systems without building every import and mapping workflow itself. The core decision is migration confidence: confirm the exact source and destination pair, test representative records and attachments, inspect field mapping and failed-record reports, and verify the final destination with the receiving platform. Do not schedule a cutover until ownership, retention, privacy, delta migration and rollback responsibilities are written down.

## Map the move from one support platform to another

Help Desk Migration describes tools for transferring tickets, contacts, companies, attachments and knowledge-base data between help desk, ITSM and related platforms. The company lists more than 100 platform options and both self-service and expert-led migration paths. That breadth is a vendor statement; before planning a project, verify that your exact source and destination products, editions, regions and data entities are supported.

Build an inventory of the records your team relies on: open and closed tickets, internal notes, public replies, attachments, users, organizations, tags, custom fields, macros, knowledge articles and relationships. Note which records are legally or operationally required, which can be archived separately and which fields must map to a different schema. This turns a migration demo into a practical acceptance test.


## Use a demo migration to inspect record quality

The vendor offers a free demo migration and says a sample can be moved without a credit card or commitment. Use it to test common records and difficult exceptions: long threads, large attachments, private notes, merged tickets, deleted users, unusual encodings and fields with different target types. Compare counts and inspect representative records in the receiving system rather than relying on a progress indicator.

Help Desk Migration describes a data-integrity report with source and target identifiers, field mappings, transformations and reasons for failed or skipped records. Request the report format and determine who reviews each exception. Set acceptance thresholds for missing records, malformed fields and attachment integrity before scheduling the complete transfer. The site’s compliance and migration claims are vendor statements, not an independent audit by Roseram.


## Plan for live changes, cutover and rollback

A support queue may continue receiving tickets while historical data is being moved. The vendor markets a zero-downtime process, but the practical workflow depends on your source and destination, migration options and timing. Ask how new or updated records are synchronized, whether a final delta migration is available, how duplicate events are prevented and how inbound channels will be switched at cutover.

Write a runbook with the migration owner, destination administrator, support lead and communications contact. Include a freeze or change window if needed, reconciliation checks, a fallback route for new tickets, escalation contacts and a rollback decision point. Keep the source accessible until the destination has passed operational checks and retention obligations are satisfied.


## Review data protection and pricing assumptions

The vendor states that its service is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant and links to a trust center. Treat these as vendor-published statements until your security team reviews the current reports, scope, subprocessors, data locations, retention and deletion terms. Determine what credentials or API tokens the migration requires, how access is limited, how long copies and logs are retained, and how they are securely removed after the project.

The pricing page says costs are personalized, based on factors such as record volume, support and customization. Request a written quote after the demo that identifies entities counted as chargeable records, any caps on demo records, custom mapping fees, support level, expedited timing, retention, reruns and taxes. Confirm whether a delta or failed-record retry is included and how cost changes if the actual data count differs from the estimate.


## Who should evaluate Help Desk Migration?

Support and IT teams moving to a new ticketing platform may consider this service when historical context, attachments or knowledge content are costly to recreate manually. A small system with a clean export and a simple target schema may be manageable with built-in import tools. Compare the service with native migration tools and an internal scripted migration using the same representative test set.

This buyer guide summarizes Help Desk Migration’s public product and pricing pages. It does not claim that Roseram ran a migration or independently verified the provider’s security or success rates. Validate exact platform compatibility, data handling, service scope and acceptance criteria with the vendor and receiving-system administrator before a production cutover.


## Features and decision criteria

- No-code migration wizard and expert-led migration options
- Vendor lists support for more than 100 source and target platforms; verify the exact migration pair
- Demo migration lets a buyer inspect a sample transfer before scheduling full migration
- Vendor describes a data-integrity report with record matching, field mapping and reasons for failed or skipped records

## Pricing considerations

The vendor does not publish one universal price for a migration; the pricing page directs buyers to a free demo and personalized quote. Ask for an itemized proposal covering record counts, entities, attachments, support level, customizations, demo limits, delta migration, retention period, taxes and any follow-up work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I test a migration before moving all records?

The vendor offers a free demo migration using a sample of records. Review ticket history, attachments, contacts, custom fields, timestamps, comments and relationships in the destination before approving a full transfer.

### How much does Help Desk Migration cost?

The official pricing page says migration costs are personalized. It identifies record counts, support level and optional customizations as factors and offers a free demo to produce a more exact quote.

### Does the vendor guarantee no downtime?

The product site markets a zero-downtime approach. Validate how the source and destination remain usable for your specific migration, how changes made during transfer are synchronized and what cutover process applies.
