# Gorgias review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Ecommerce customer support and service automation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Gorgias is a customer-support platform focused on ecommerce conversations. Helpdesk usage is priced around billable tickets rather than agent seats, while optional AI Agent interactions and voice or SMS can add usage-based charges.

## Editorial verdict

Gorgias documents when support tickets and successful AI resolutions are billed, including overage rules. Ecommerce teams should estimate reply-generating ticket volume, AI resolution usage, channel add-ons and Shopify data access before enabling the system across a live store.

## Gorgias review: ecommerce support from one workspace

Gorgias positions its helpdesk for ecommerce customer conversations. Its current product information describes support through common commerce channels and integration with store context, while its billing documentation explains how messages, tickets and AI Agent outcomes are measured. This can help a retail support team see a customer’s question alongside relevant order information, but the integration scope should be tested against the store’s actual setup.

Create a pilot with a small set of agents and a staging or low-risk store workflow. Test a shipping question, a refund request and a product compatibility question. Confirm which order details appear, what an agent can change, how a ticket is created from each channel and whether the conversation can be safely handed to a human. Do not assume that a customer-service connection is automatically permitted to issue refunds or edit orders.


## Gorgias ticket pricing and AI Agent charges

Gorgias says helpdesk billing is based on usage rather than the number of agents. Its documentation defines a billable helpdesk ticket as one where at least one message is sent from the helpdesk, whether by a human, AI Agent or automatic rule. The applicable ticket price depends on the selected plan. Exceeding a tier’s baseline can trigger overage fees, with the exact rate shown in account billing.

AI Agent is an additional product with an outcome-based fee when it resolves a ticket without handing the conversation to a human. The company’s official product information lists monthly and annual per-resolution rates and plan-included interaction allowances, but the price and quota depend on subscription terms. Model ticket volume and AI-resolved conversations separately, and test how merged, split, ignored and human-escalated threads count before forecasting spend.


## Gorgias channels, rules and ecommerce integrations

The billing guide identifies email, chat, contact forms and social integrations as sources of helpdesk tickets. Voice and SMS are separate add-ons, and ticket charges can apply when a rule or automated message sends a reply. This makes channel design a cost-control decision as well as a customer-experience decision.

Review every automatic rule before launch. A repeated out-of-office response, a reply loop or a confirmation message can generate billable activity even without an agent typing. Set alerts for volume changes and keep a clear record of which integrations can read or write order data. Use test orders to confirm permissions, currency handling and account separation.


## Gorgias customer data, permissions and failure planning

A support platform may hold email addresses, order details, social messages and customer histories. Limit access to the information each agent needs, use individual logins and review connected applications. Decide how deletion and export requests are handled across both the helpdesk and the commerce platform so the same customer is not retained in a forgotten data store.

Plan for outages and provider changes. Keep a way for staff to answer urgent customer messages if an integration is unavailable, and export unresolved conversations before switching vendors. Verify which ticket and AI usage reports are available to the account administrator. Document how an agent should proceed when order data appears incomplete or when the platform’s suggested answer conflicts with store policy.


## Gorgias alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Gorgias with Zendesk, Freshdesk and a shared inbox using expected billable tickets, successful AI outcomes, channel count, commerce integrations and support staffing. A per-ticket plan can be attractive for a small team, while reply-based billing may change the economics when the team sends many short messages. Check included volumes, overage fees and AI outcome rules against a real month of historical tickets.

The official materials reviewed here explain a commerce helpdesk and its usage billing; they do not establish that Gorgias is a scam. This review did not operate a store account, issue refunds or independently test AI resolution quality. Ask for the current plan terms and test billing scenarios before connecting high-volume channels or enabling automated customer actions.


## Features and decision criteria

- Shared ecommerce helpdesk across customer channels
- Store context and commerce-oriented support workflows
- Ticket and AI outcome billing with tier-based allowances
