## Core Architecture: Typography Manipulation and Visual Anchoring

At its foundational technical layer, ADHD Reading is a browser enhancement tool engineered specifically for the desktop Google Chrome environment. Its primary objective is altering digital typography across arbitrary web pages to diminish cognitive fatigue and visual crowding. The tool accomplishes this by implementing bionic-style word-start emphasis, applying subtle bolding to the opening letters of vocabulary terms. This visual anchoring mechanism aims to guide saccadic eye movements, providing physical reference points when attention wavers across dense text blocks. Rather than rewriting content, the tool merely restructures paragraph presentation on the fly.

The software structures its typographic modifications through three standardized presets labeled Light, Balanced, and Deep. Users can toggle these presets depending on the visual density of the target material, progressively altering character weight and spacing parameters. Beyond preset defaults, the software exposes granular typography controls, allowing individuals to calibrate font scaling, vertical line height, letter spacing, paragraph margins, and visual contrast. These parameters enable readers to carve out breathable layouts on cluttered sites without permanently destroying the underlying structure of the host page.

A notable technical constraint disclosed by the developer is that selecting custom font families is not currently supported directly within the extension controls, despite educational comparisons of popular accessible fonts provided on the company website. The tool concentrates strictly on geometric spacing, letter-weight distribution, and contrast adjustments. Users who rely on specific, specialized typefaces must recognize that this utility adapts existing page typography rather than injecting replacement font families into the host stylesheet.


## Local Processing Architecture and Data Privacy Protections

## Navigational Aids: Focus Overlays, Speech, and Reversibility

Beyond typographic recalibration, ADHD Reading provides dynamic contextual overlays designed to minimize peripheral sensory distraction. The focus overlay implements a soft background mask that visually tracks the specific paragraph hovered by the user's cursor. Simultaneously, an optional quiet page mode softens secondary layout elements such as sidebars, sticky navigation headers, advertising blocks, and extraneous media containers. By subduing peripheral layout components, the software seeks to prevent visual wandering without requiring destructive DOM manipulation.

The extension also incorporates basic auditory support via native browser speech synthesis. Buyers should recognize that the developer explicitly disclaims the inclusion of cloud-hosted, premium artificial intelligence voice models or third-party neural audio platforms within this beta package. Instead, auditory reinforcement depends on standard browser-level accessibility hooks. The software pairs this playback with a focus timer utility, assisting readers who track study intervals or require temporal boundaries when parsing dense professional documentation.

Crucially, all visual adjustments introduced by the tool are entirely reversible. Users can instantly revert any manipulated document to its standard layout by selecting the clear restoration command within the extension popup or by issuing the keyboard shortcut Alt+Shift+X. Triggering this command immediately strips custom injected styling, stops active text-to-speech audio, and halts internal reading timers. This ensures that dynamic web applications, interactive dashboards, or complex enterprise pages can be immediately returned to their default state whenever layout formatting errors occur.

### Does the extension transmit article content or browsing history to remote servers?

Page formatting and keyword matching are performed locally within the browser without uploading article text to remote servers. ### How does the 72-hour trial operate across multiple computers?

The trial runs for exactly 72 hours from the moment of initial activation. It requires Chrome account sign-in and email verification, allowing authorization on up to two desktop browsers. Because trial duration is recorded server-side, reinstalling the software or switching between devices will not reset the clock.

### Does ADHD Reading include AI text summarization or cloud-hosted voice synthesis?

No. The publisher states that third-party AI services and paid voice synthesis platforms are neither included nor connected within this beta release. Any text-to-speech functionality relies strictly on native browser speech utilities.
