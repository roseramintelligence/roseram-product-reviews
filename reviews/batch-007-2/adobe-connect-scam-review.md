# Adobe Connect review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Virtual training and webinar platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Adobe Connect is a virtual training and webinar platform built around reusable rooms, configurable layouts and interactive audience tools. Its public individual plans list Standard at US$190, Premium at US$290 and Enterprise at US$390 per host annually, with capacity, storage and add-on differences; team plans are quote-led.

## Editorial verdict

Adobe Connect is aimed at organizations that run repeatable training, events or webinars and want room layouts, presenter controls and engagement reporting. The reusable-room model can reduce setup repetition, while the number of plan options means buyers should model host count, room capacity, storage and add-ons before comparing costs. The listed prices are per host each year and do not represent a complete quote for larger capacity or team licensing.

## Virtual rooms designed for training and webinars

Adobe Connect is positioned for training, webinars and meetings where an event needs more structure than a basic video call. The product uses configurable rooms with layouts and pods for content, polls, chat, Q&A and other interactions. Hosts can prepare a room in advance and reuse persistent rooms and templates across sessions. That model may help teams who run a recurring curriculum or a consistent series of events.

Adobe’s product overview also describes presenter controls, automated invitations and reminders, post-session surveys, engagement analytics and participant profiles. These capabilities support a more managed event workflow, but they require careful room design and operator preparation. A buyer should map the work involved in creating templates, training hosts and maintaining event content alongside the license cost.


## Evaluate the attendee experience, not only the feature list

The pricing guide lists video, screen sharing, polls, chat, moderated Q&A, whiteboards, annotations, recordings, captions and role-based participant controls across its plan comparison. For a real evaluation, run a test session with the audience’s typical devices and network conditions. Check joining steps, accessibility, caption behavior, mobile participation and how recordings expose resources after an event.

Persistent virtual spaces and multiple resizable pods offer control over the presentation, but a room can become crowded if every feature is enabled. Design a simple default layout and give presenters a clear sequence for moving between content, discussion and activities. Ask learners to complete the same task you expect in a live session, such as answering a poll or opening a handout.


## Compare host count, room capacity and storage

Adobe’s published individual Standard, Premium and Enterprise plans list US$190, US$290 and US$390 per host per year. Standard and Premium list a 100-person room capacity, while Enterprise lists 300. The page associates host-count bands with the plans and lists 5 GB, 10 GB and unlimited cloud storage respectively, with the unlimited tier subject to a fair-use policy.

Capacity upgrades are separate annual charges: the pricing page lists US$2,200 for 500, US$3,900 for 1,000 and US$4,900 for 1,500 room capacity per host. It also lists US$600 per host each year for Training or Webinar Pro Packs when they are not already included. These are meaningful add-ons, so model the actual audience size and host roles before treating the base price as total cost.


## Check team licensing and integrations

For larger groups, Adobe lists shared-event, shared-room and shared-account licensing. Shared events allow a chosen number of events one at a time; shared rooms provide unlimited events one at a time; shared accounts provide individual rooms for concurrent use within the number of licenses. The guide notes constraints and directs some purchases to sales. Compare the model with the frequency of events and the number that must run at once.

The product overview describes a web-services API, custom pods and integrations with Adobe Learning Manager and Marketo Engage. Confirm the integration edition, implementation requirements and any data flow or retention questions with your administrator. Request a quote that includes host entitlements, concurrent event needs, additional storage and all selected add-ons.


## Who should shortlist Adobe Connect?

Adobe Connect merits evaluation when a team wants purpose-built online training or webinar rooms, reusable event design and audience interaction reporting. It may be more than a small group needs for occasional informal meetings. A short pilot with one recurring session, one webinar and one recording can reveal whether the room model reduces work enough to justify its annual host and capacity costs.

This is an editorial buyer guide based on Adobe’s public product and pricing pages, not a Roseram hands-on test. Pricing can change and may vary by region or agreement. Validate current feature entitlements, accessibility requirements and the final quote before purchasing.


## Features and decision criteria

- Persistent virtual rooms, reusable layouts, pods and breakout rooms
- Polls, moderated Q&A, chat, whiteboards, quizzes and engagement dashboard
- Room capacity and included storage vary by individual plan
- Team licensing supports shared events, shared rooms or concurrent-user accounts

## Pricing considerations

Adobe presents separate Training and Webinar packages, individual and team licensing, capacity upgrades and add-ons. Its page says team licensing and some combinations require contacting sales. Confirm region, taxes, billing terms and the exact account configuration before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Adobe Connect cost?

Adobe’s page lists individual host plans at US$190, US$290 and US$390 per year, with capacity upgrades and optional packs priced separately.

### What is the difference between Adobe Connect Standard and Premium?

The listed plans share a 100-person room capacity, while Premium covers a different host-count band, includes the relevant Pro Pack and lists 10 GB instead of 5 GB storage.

### Does Adobe Connect have team licensing?

Yes. Adobe describes shared-event, shared-room and shared-account team licenses, with configuration and pricing handled through its sales process.
