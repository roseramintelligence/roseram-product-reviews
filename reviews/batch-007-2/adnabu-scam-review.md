## AdNabu: feed quality starts with the underlying catalog

A product feed carries facts that shoppers and advertising systems rely on, including identity, availability and commercial details. Optimization should not invent a material, compatibility claim or product benefit that the store cannot support. Start with a small, accurate catalog and identify which fields need mapping or revision. Improving the wording of an incorrect record does not fix the record.

The publisher describes Shopify-focused feed workflows and channel management. Confirm the exact destination, account and catalog arrangement you intend to use. A general list of supported channels does not establish that every category, region or custom field is handled identically. Your evaluation should follow one product through the proposed mapping into the actual destination format.


## Test a variant, a stock change and an incorrect attribute

Prepare a representative product with variants and write the expected identifier relationships before the pilot. Change its availability and inspect how the proposed workflow handles the update. Include an intentionally incorrect noncritical attribute in a test record and evaluate how the issue is surfaced and corrected.

Keep destination policy decisions separate from feed transmission. A channel may reject an item for reasons beyond the feed tool’s operation. Ask who reviews those errors and which record becomes authoritative after a correction. This review does not claim that Roseram connected AdNabu to a merchant account or verified advertising approvals.

A useful variant test changes one attribute that distinguishes two otherwise similar items, such as size or pack contents. Inspect the proposed destination record and ask whether a customer could understand exactly which item is offered. Confusing identifiers can undermine otherwise polished copy. Keep the store’s factual record authoritative and investigate any transformation that makes separate variants appear to be interchangeable products.
