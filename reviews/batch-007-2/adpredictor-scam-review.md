## AdPredictor: decide what a useful conversion means

An advertising account can record many events that do not represent commercial value. A button click, an unqualified inquiry and a completed sale should not automatically receive equal weight. Before judging an account diagnosis, explain the conversion definitions and known tracking gaps. A tool can analyze the available records without knowing that the underlying event is misleading.

The publisher describes an account-analysis workflow and states that it does not change the account in the advertised setup. Confirm the current permissions requested during connection. Read access and permission to modify campaigns are different capabilities. This review has not inspected a connected account or verified how a particular permission screen is configured.


## Trace one keyword finding back to business evidence

Choose a small historical period and one finding from a proposed analysis. Compare it with the corresponding search context, conversion records and the business’s own outcome data. Ask whether delayed sales or a low-volume but valuable customer could change the interpretation. A useful recommendation should make its assumptions understandable.

Keep a change log if you later test a recommendation. Change a bounded part of the account and agree how long the comparison needs before deciding whether it helped. Do not present a before-and-after movement as a guaranteed causal result when other campaign conditions also changed. The exercise described here is a suggested evaluation, not an optimization Roseram performed.

An additional check is to examine a keyword with few recorded conversions but a long sales cycle. Ask what evidence would be needed before classifying its spend as waste. The answer may involve an unresolved measurement problem rather than an immediate campaign change. A useful account-analysis workflow should make that distinction understandable so the owner can prioritize investigation instead of treating every recommendation as an instruction.


## An account score is a starting question

Compare the usefulness of the explanation with the effort needed to verify it. A simple score is convenient, but it should not obscure uncertainty in attribution or the value of different customers. Confirm which account scope, reporting period and limits apply to the current offer rather than assuming that one analysis covers every advertising property.
