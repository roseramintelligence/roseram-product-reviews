# AI Social Media Post Writer review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** AI social post drafting tool  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

SocialBu’s AI Social Media Post Writer is a browser-based generator that turns a short brief into an editable platform-oriented draft. The public tool can be tried without signing in, subject to usage limits; users can choose a destination platform and tone, review or regenerate the result, then move approved copy into SocialBu’s publishing workflow. It is a drafting aid, not an autonomous publishing or fact-checking system.

## Editorial verdict

This generator can help a solo creator or small team get from a blank page to a first social caption, especially when the brief names the audience, platform and desired action. Its main value is speed and a focused interface; the result still needs editing for accuracy, voice, disclosure and platform rules. If you need ongoing calendars, approvals, analytics or listening, compare the separate SocialBu platform plans rather than assuming the free generator includes them.

## A lightweight path from idea to editable draft

The SocialBu AI post generator is a focused web tool: describe an idea or product, optionally choose the destination platform and tone, generate copy, then edit, copy or regenerate it. SocialBu says the public generator can be tried without signing in, though usage limits apply. This setup lowers the effort of starting a draft when a creator has a topic but no first sentence. Platform selection can also remind a writer to adapt length and style rather than paste identical wording everywhere.

A useful brief includes the audience, key fact, purpose, desired response and any claim that must not be made. “Write a post about our new service” gives little context; “Explain who the appointment tool helps, name its actual available feature, and invite salon owners to see a demo without promising more bookings” provides stronger guardrails. Treat the generated response as a working draft, verify every factual detail and rewrite anything that does not sound like the organization.


## Separate the free writing tool from the publishing subscription

SocialBu’s public generator is not the same as its connected social management account. The generator helps make copy; the broader platform provides account connections, queues, scheduling, collaboration, analytics and automation according to the selected plan. SocialBu’s current pricing page lists a Free tier with three social accounts and ten lifetime posts, while its paid plans raise account, post and AI-credit limits. A free generator session should not be interpreted as unlimited use of every platform feature.

The pricing page showed Starter at $29 per month or $22 per month on annual billing, Growth at $79 or $59 annually, and Agency at $199 or $149 annually. It lists different social-account, post, team, listening and AI writer-credit allowances. Before upgrading, estimate monthly drafts and scheduled posts separately: generator usage, AI writer credits, calendar publishing and social-account connections may have distinct limits. Verify the actual checkout total, annual charge, trial conversion and account caps.


## Use prompt context and human review to improve quality

Generate several alternatives from a concise, specific brief, then choose the one that accurately reflects the offer and audience. Add constraints such as “no unsupported statistics,” “do not imply a guaranteed result,” “use one clear call to action,” or “avoid repeating the opening hook.” Refine the draft with concrete proof you can substantiate, such as an exact product capability or event date, rather than asking the model to invent testimonials or performance data.

Before publishing, check names, prices, links, dates, legal claims, accessibility, image-caption consistency and character limits. For financial, health, legal or safety topics, have a qualified person review the copy. Get consent before including customer details, avoid pasting confidential information into a public tool and read the provider’s current privacy policy to understand how submitted prompts are handled.


## Decide whether the wider workflow fits your team

A solo marketer can use the generator to draft one post and then publish it through a native network tool. A team with recurring content may value the connected SocialBu calendar, queues, approvals or analytics, but should test each account type and network. Verify whether a planned format auto-publishes or requires a mobile confirmation. Compare the time saved against the cost of the plan, the overhead of reviewing drafts and the operational risk of publishing a mistaken claim.

Run a small pilot: create several posts from different briefs, edit each to match the brand, schedule them to test accounts if available and compare the rendered result with the preview. Keep a record of draft quality, edit time, failed publishing steps and which ideas generated useful conversations. This guide summarizes public SocialBu tool, pricing and help pages; it is not a hands-on test or claim that AI content will improve reach, engagement or sales.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is SocialBu’s AI post generator free?

SocialBu says the public generator can be tried without signing in, with usage limits. The separate SocialBu Free platform plan has account, lifetime post and AI-credit limits.

### Does the generator automatically publish posts?

The generator returns editable draft text. SocialBu describes moving reviewed drafts into its scheduling and publishing workflow; review and approval are still required before publishing.

### Can AI-written posts be trusted without editing?

No. SocialBu warns that AI-generated content can contain mistakes and recommends review. Check factual claims, brand voice, rights, disclosures and network-specific rules before use.
