## A consistent-input comparison exercise

Use an authorized photograph and define a small set of style directions in plain language. Review the outputs together with the original. Ask another person to identify which differences belong to the hairstyle and which are unintended changes. This makes the acceptance decision more precise than choosing the most flattering image.
