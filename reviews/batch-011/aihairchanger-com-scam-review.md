## aihairchanger.com: a hairstyle image is an illustration

The appearance of hair depends on the photograph, lighting and the way the generated change interacts with the rest of the face. Before evaluating the tool, decide which parts of the portrait must remain unchanged. A flattering output can still be unsuitable if it changes facial features or gives the impression of a different person rather than a different hairstyle.

The publisher describes changing style and color through an AI image workflow. Confirm the exact input requirements and revision options in the current offer. A preview should not be treated as professional advice about treatment, texture or physical suitability. It is a visual concept whose relationship to the actual person needs review.


## A portrait-preservation evaluation

Use a photograph you are authorized to process and specify one bounded appearance change. Compare the output with the original at the size you intend to use. Look at the hairline, face, background and accessories as well as the requested style. Reject unintended identity changes rather than accepting them because the overall image is attractive.

Ask about the current storage, deletion and reuse conditions before uploading personal or customer photographs. This review does not audit the service’s data handling or establish permission for any particular image. The person responsible for publication should understand both the source photograph and the terms attached to its use.

A useful additional case uses an image with lighting that makes color judgment difficult. Ask a reviewer to distinguish the intended hair-color change from unrelated changes in skin tone or exposure. This helps prevent a synthetic result from being interpreted as a dependable prediction. Keep the original image available whenever the preview is used to support a real discussion about appearance.

For repeated previews, keep the original photograph and the requested change together with each output. Otherwise, later reviewers may compare images produced under different assumptions and mistake the difference for tool quality. A simple version record also helps prevent an unsuitable draft from being reused after a more accurate illustration has been approved for the intended conversation.
