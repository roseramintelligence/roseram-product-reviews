## Commercial Pricing Tiers and Credit Expiration Schedules

The pricing model for AI-Text-Humanizer divides usage across a free entry tier and two distinct commercial options managed via the Paddle payment processor. The zero-cost tier requires no credit card details and grants a lifetime pool of 500 total words, limited to 200 words per transformation cycle. This entry tier enables prospective users to test the rephrasing engine on short paragraphs without opening their wallets, although handling full-length articles requires moving to a commercial plan.

For ongoing content needs, the Pro Monthly subscription provides 50,000 words each month for $19.99 billed monthly. This tier increases the single-process limit to 2,000 words per run, grants programmatic API connectivity, and unlocks multi-language processing. A vital operational constraint applies to this recurring subscription: unused words do not roll over. Alternatively, the publisher offers a Pro Prepaid tier for a single payment of $69.99, avoiding an ongoing subscription. This package delivers 150,000 words with the same 2,000-word per-process limit and full API access. Words purchased under this prepaid tier remain valid for up to 24 months, providing an economical choice for intermittent writing workloads. Buyers can also order multiple seats or word packs at checkout, which provides volume discounts ranging from 20 percent for five seats up to 50 percent for orders of 40 seats or more.
