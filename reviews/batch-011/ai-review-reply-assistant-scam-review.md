## MARA review replies: empathy should not invent a resolution

A polite response can still be misleading if it claims the hotel investigated an issue or completed a correction that staff have not confirmed. Define which facts the draft may use and which require review. Separate acknowledging a guest’s experience from accepting an unsupported account of what happened. The reply needs to reflect the business’s actual knowledge and authority.

The publisher describes responding to reviews and extracting useful guest feedback. These are related but distinct tasks. A reply addresses the public conversation, while an operational insight may need a staff member to inspect the underlying issue. Do not treat a high response rate as proof that the hotel resolved the concerns raised.


## A hospitality review-and-approval exercise

Use fictional reviews covering a compliment, a specific complaint and an ambiguous incident. Write down which details the team can verify and which remain unknown. Inspect whether generated drafts preserve those boundaries and sound appropriate for the hotel’s actual tone. Keep a human responsible for approving a commitment or sensitive statement.

Follow one repeated issue into the internal process. Ask who reviews it, what evidence is needed and how the team records a genuine correction. This review does not claim that Roseram operated MARA on a hotel account or measured reputation improvements. It describes a practical way to connect public responses with accountable follow-through.

A useful exception includes a review mentioning another guest or employee by name. Decide what information should remain out of the public reply and ask how the proposed workflow supports that review. Fluency is not enough when a draft could disclose private details or speculate about an incident. The appropriate response may be a restrained acknowledgment and an agreed private contact route.

Compare the published response with the approved draft rather than assuming the handoff preserved it. A small change in wording can turn an acknowledgment into a promise of compensation or a completed investigation. Keep approval responsibility clear and ask how staff identify the final version. The relevant outcome is a truthful response that the hotel is prepared to stand behind.
