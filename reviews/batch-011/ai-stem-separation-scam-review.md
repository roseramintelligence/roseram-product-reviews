## AI Stem Separation: a separated track is an estimate

A mixed recording contains overlapping sounds. Separating it can produce useful material for practice, editing or analysis, but the output should not be assumed to equal the original multitrack recording. Decide which sound needs to be isolated and what level of interference would make the result unsuitable. The acceptance standard for a rough arrangement idea can differ from a final published production.

The publisher describes browser-based splitting and several stem arrangements. Confirm the current supported input and output in the exact workflow you intend to use. A format name or a selected sample does not establish the quality of your own material. Keep technical availability separate from your permission to process and reuse the recording.

Inspect how the resulting files align in your editing application and whether their duration and naming make them usable. A revealing test includes a quiet passage followed by a dense section containing the same instrument. The goal is to understand the limitation for your recording, not to assume a universal quality ranking from one selected example.
