# Campus 24x7 review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-18  
**Category:** School ERP and student administration software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Campus 24x7 presents a cloud school and college ERP for admissions, attendance, examinations, fee records, communication and related administration. Its current pricing page lists Foundation, Growth and Scale plans from ₹2,999 monthly, offers the first two months at ₹0, and maps tiers to student capacity. Two current publisher pages describe fee collection differently: one lists online fee collection, while a city page says parents cannot yet pay online. A school should resolve that feature difference in a written demo and agreement. This review makes no fraud finding.

## Editorial verdict

Campus 24x7 describes an integrated school-management system with tiered pricing and a two-month software-fee-free start. Public material covers admissions, attendance, timetables, examinations, fee records, WhatsApp messaging, mobile apps and audit logs, with student caps by tier. The most material buyer question found in the reviewed pages is not an allegation: the pricing page lists online fee collection, while a current city landing page says parents cannot pay fees online yet. Schools should ask the vendor to demonstrate the exact payment flow, reconcile the wording and put the included capability in the order form. The reviewed evidence supports neither a fraud claim nor a verified implementation outcome.

## The connected school-record workflow

Campus 24x7 positions its ERP around one student record flowing through admissions, attendance, fees, communication and reports. This can be useful to an institution whose data is currently divided between spreadsheets, registers and multiple apps. The published tiers also specify student and staff capacities, which gives a buyer more to compare than a general promise of scalability. The fit depends on how the school's actual divisions, academic calendars, fee rules and roles map to those modules; a demonstration using the vendor's generic sample school does not establish that local practices are configured correctly.

The pricing page names Android access across its plans, with iOS apps in the Growth and Scale descriptions, and lists additional capabilities such as transport, GPS tracking, library management and longer audit logs at higher tiers. Ask which user roles receive each mobile app, whether student counts are measured by enrolled records or active accounts, and what happens at a capacity boundary. A school should also identify who controls role permissions and whether records can be imported and exported in a format the institution can use independently.


## Clarify the online fee-collection wording before purchase

There is a concrete point to resolve in the first-party material. Campus 24x7's pricing page lists online fee collection, receipts and reminders as included in the Growth tier. A current city-specific ERP page says parent-facing online fee payment is not part of the product yet, while describing staff-recorded cash, cheque, UPI, card, bank-transfer or wallet payments. Those statements could describe different product editions, a feature rollout or separate parts of the collection workflow; the public pages do not reconcile them. They are not proof of a deceptive act, but the difference matters to a school choosing a fee process.

Ask the vendor to show a parent making a payment in the exact deployment being quoted, then show the receipt, ledger entry, failed-payment handling, reconciliation and gateway fees. If the current system only records a payment entered by office staff, state that clearly in the purchase requirement. If an online gateway is available only with an add-on or through a partner, record the vendor, price, settlement timing and support responsibility. A signed scope should specify the workflow and the plan tier instead of repeating a broad phrase such as online fee collection.


## Trial pricing, student capacity and add-on economics

The reviewed pricing page lists Foundation at ₹2,999 per month or ₹29,999 annually for up to 500 students and 30 staff; Growth at ₹4,999 monthly or ₹49,999 annually for up to 1,000 students and 50 staff; and Scale at ₹10,999 monthly or ₹1,09,999 annually for up to 2,500 students and 100 staff. It says the selected plan's software subscription fee is ₹0 for the first two months, with normal billing beginning in month three. The page also describes optional modules such as transport, extra storage, WhatsApp volume and added student capacity.

Before calculating first-year cost, ask when an annual payment is collected after the two-month offer, whether the offer is available to the institution's legal entity and campus, how add-ons are priced, and whether onboarding or data migration falls outside the included setup. The site says onboarding is included and custom migration may be separately scoped. Treat those as terms to obtain in writing. A price is only comparable when the capacity, billing cadence, communication use, support window and implementation scope match the school's needs.


## Student data, access roles and operational continuity

A school ERP holds information about students, families and staff, so the school should review the privacy notice, data-processing terms and security commitments before transferring records. Campus 24x7's public material refers to role-based access and encrypted data, and its pricing page describes audit-log periods that vary by tier. Confirm the precise scope: which fields are visible to parents, teachers, finance staff and administrators; whether a user's actions are recorded; who can export records; and how access is removed when a staff member leaves. Do not infer a regulatory certification or a particular data-residency guarantee from a general page description.

Run a small migration rehearsal with a copy of records approved for testing. Check that student identities, historical balances, attendance and guardian contacts map correctly, then test a correction and an export. Ask how the school can retrieve data at the end of the contract and whether an export includes attachments, audit logs and message history. During a live evaluation, use synthetic records when possible. A successful admissions demo is not enough to establish that a whole academic year can be moved safely.


## Features and decision criteria

- Admissions, student records, attendance, timetable and examinations
- Fee structures, invoices, receipts and parent notifications
- Role-based mobile access for staff, parents and students
- Transport and GPS tracking on higher plans
- Audit logs with longer stated retention on higher tiers

## Pricing considerations

The publisher says regular plan billing begins in month three and lists annual options. Ask whether the annual invoice is due at month three, how optional modules and communication volume are charged, and whether payment-gateway fees are separate. Confirm the exact free-period and cancellation terms in the institution's order.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Campus 24x7 a scam?

The reviewed first-party pages describe a school ERP, pricing, terms and product workflows. This review found no basis for a fraud finding and has not tested an implementation. It did find a difference in the publisher's public wording about online fee payments that a school should clarify before purchase.

### How much does Campus 24x7 cost?

The pricing page lists plans from ₹2,999 per month, with student and staff capacities, two months at ₹0 for the software subscription, and regular billing beginning in month three. Confirm the annual invoice timing, add-ons and gateway charges in the written quote.

### Can parents pay school fees online through Campus 24x7?

The reviewed first-party pages differ. The pricing page lists online fee collection in Growth, while a city page says parents cannot pay online yet and describes payments recorded by school staff. Ask the vendor to demonstrate the exact workflow and identify what is included in your plan.

### What should a school test during onboarding?

Test student import, role permissions, an attendance correction, a fee record and receipt, parent notifications, reporting, and a full data export. Use a test environment and reconcile the payment workflow before relying on it.
