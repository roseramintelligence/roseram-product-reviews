# Campaign Monitor review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Email marketing and campaign automation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Campaign Monitor offers campaign creation, signup forms, reporting and tier-dependent email automation. Pricing is selected using contact volume and billing cadence, while monthly send allowances vary by plan.

## Editorial verdict

Campaign Monitor’s official plan comparison makes contact count, send allowance and feature tier the key cost checks. Obtain the current account-specific quote and verify which workflows and transactional-email volume are included before switching a production list.

## Campaign Monitor review: campaigns and subscriber journeys

Campaign Monitor combines a visual email builder with audience management, signup forms, campaign reporting and automated journeys. The current feature comparison also describes AI-assisted campaign creation and tools for reviewing links, design and spam signals. Teams should identify which of these features are in the selected plan rather than assuming every item shown on the platform is included.

For a small list, the important question may be whether a staff member can create, approve and send an accurate newsletter without hand-coded templates. For a lifecycle program, the test is different: confirm that triggers, suppression rules, timing, preference changes and transactional messages behave correctly. Create a short test sequence with internal addresses before any campaign reaches customers.


## Campaign Monitor pricing, contacts and send allowances

Campaign Monitor’s pricing page asks the buyer to choose a contact count and monthly or annual billing. The visible comparison describes Lite as including five subscriber emails per month, alongside selected core features; other plan limits vary by subscriber tier and package. The public page uses a live selector and localized currency, so a single static starting price would not represent every buyer’s quote.

Estimate the bill with the number of billable contacts, not just the number of people you expect to email this month. Then compare the included send volume with newsletters, journey messages and any transactional traffic. The billing help notes that newly created accounts may use a changed billing system and that price can vary with subscriber count and send volume. Save the account quote and billing cadence before accepting a plan.


## Campaign Monitor automation, reporting and team access

The official feature catalog lists drip campaigns, automated journeys, signup forms, engagement segments, subscriber growth reports, role permissions and API access. It also describes plan-dependent features such as send-time optimization, advanced link tracking, transactional email and design or spam testing. This makes tier verification important for agencies and teams that divide campaign work among multiple staff members.

Set up a permissioned test audience and exercise the complete lifecycle: signup, confirmation, preference change, unsubscribe and re-subscription. Check whether each person can access only the work appropriate to their role. If the marketing plan also needs API-triggered transactional mail, verify volume, authentication, logging and support arrangements separately from ordinary campaign sends.


## Campaign Monitor deliverability and account transition

A campaign tool can report that a message was sent without proving that every mailbox accepted or displayed it. The platform’s own product pages describe engagement and testing features, but send results remain affected by list quality, sender authentication, content and recipient providers. Treat open and click statistics as signals with known measurement limitations rather than guaranteed evidence of business outcomes.

Before import, preserve the source of consent and suppressions and test a small sample for field mapping and encoding. Authenticate the sending domain, ensure replies reach a monitored inbox, and confirm how unsubscribes sync back to the source CRM. For a planned cancellation, document exported contacts, templates, automation logic and sender settings so the next provider can resume without re-contacting people who opted out.


## Campaign Monitor alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Campaign Monitor with MailerLite, Brevo and an API-focused email provider using the same list size, sending cadence, automation and user seats. Include annual-versus-monthly billing, overage rules, account permissions, support access and data portability. A newsletter-only team may want fewer operational features; a business with complex customer events may need a more data-centered product.

The official pricing and feature pages reviewed here describe a commercial email platform, not evidence of fraud. This is a desk review, not a first-hand test of deliverability or customer support. The decision is whether the current quote, included send volume and exact automation features match the organization’s use case.


## Features and decision criteria

- Email campaign editor, templates and signup forms
- Plan-dependent campaigns, journeys and transactional sends
- Engagement reporting, API access and team permissions
