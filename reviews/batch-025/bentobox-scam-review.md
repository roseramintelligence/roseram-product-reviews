# BentoBox review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Restaurant marketing, websites and online ordering  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

BentoBox is now part of Clover Hospitality. Its restaurant tools cover branded websites, menus, online ordering, catering, guest data and related commerce features, but the official website says new BentoBox services are currently available only to Clover POS customers. Existing products and terms can differ by account. Restaurants should confirm eligibility, integrations, transaction costs, migration options and current pricing directly with Clover before planning a new deployment.

## Editorial verdict

BentoBox may remain relevant to restaurants already using its website and ordering tools, particularly when direct online ordering and guest data are central to their digital presence. However, buyers should evaluate it as part of the current Clover Hospitality ecosystem and confirm whether a new account can be opened. A restaurant comparing alternatives should model the full POS, ordering, payment and add-on stack rather than relying on older standalone BentoBox pricing or feature descriptions.

## Start with the current Clover relationship and eligibility

BentoBox’s current website says its restaurant marketing and commerce solutions are now part of the Clover ecosystem. It also says new BentoBox products and services are currently available only to Clover POS customers. This changes how restaurants should evaluate old recommendations, legacy pricing pages and historical product comparisons. First confirm whether the restaurant is an existing BentoBox customer, a Clover POS customer or a new buyer. Ask which dashboard, contract and support team would apply today.

For an existing customer, request written confirmation of which modules remain supported, what changes with migration and whether payment, ordering, website hosting or customer data are affected. For a new buyer, do not assume that an old BentoBox page, price or sign-up link represents a currently purchasable standalone service. Clarify the Clover POS requirement, any onboarding steps and the current alternative package before spending time on migration planning.


## Evaluate direct ordering and restaurant operations

Clover Hospitality’s help material describes pickup, delivery and dine-in ordering, menu and item management, order status, location settings and payment integrations. A direct ordering page can keep a restaurant’s brand visible and reduce reliance on marketplace discovery, but staff still need a dependable process for menu updates, prep time, inventory, special instructions, cancellations, refunds and order handoff. Map these tasks across the website, POS and kitchen workflow before deciding whether the integration is complete enough.

Run a test order for each fulfillment type and a representative modifier. Verify tax, service fees, tips, discounts, gift-card behavior, scheduled orders, availability windows, receipts and payment settlement. Ask what happens when the POS disconnects, an item sells out or a location is temporarily closed. Confirm whether an online order is automatically entered into the restaurant’s Clover system or requires a manual step, and test how duplicate or incomplete orders are handled.


## Compare current fees rather than relying on legacy pricing

Official help pages include pricing examples such as online ordering at $49 per month with a BentoBox website or $79 per month without one, and a QR Order & Pay add-on listed at $19 per location monthly with a $0.99 diner convenience fee. Those pages may apply to specific product configurations or existing customers. The current site states that new BentoBox services are limited to Clover POS customers, so treat older pricing as a prompt for questions—not as a current offer for every restaurant.

Ask Clover for an itemized proposal covering POS hardware and software, website, online ordering, each location, processing rates, convenience fees, delivery options, setup, add-ons, minimum terms and renewals. Model the total monthly cost using the restaurant’s actual ticket volume and average order size. Check who pays each fee, whether a fee is waived for repeat orders and how the order appears on the diner’s receipt.


## Protect menus, guest information and the exit path

A restaurant ordering system can hold menu details, order history and diner contact information. Review the current privacy terms, payment processor, data export and account-access controls. Confirm which entity provides hosting and support after the Clover transition, where guest data is stored, which systems receive it and how a restaurant can export or delete records when a contract ends. Do not promise that a “commission-free” or direct order is cost-free; card processing, subscription, service and diner fees may still apply.

Before committing, test mobile checkout, accessibility, menu editing, a refund and a customer support escalation. Document how the restaurant updates hours, holiday availability, allergens and prices. This guide summarizes current BentoBox and Clover public pages; it does not independently verify your account’s offer, the full feature set, transaction rates or historical migration behavior.

- Menu, order timing, availability, promotions and POS workflows are managed through Clover Hospitality tools
- The vendor says new BentoBox services are currently available only to Clover POS customers; confirm current product eligibility

## Pricing considerations

BentoBox moved into the Clover ecosystem. Public support pages include plan details that may apply to existing customers or particular configurations, while the current website limits new BentoBox services to Clover POS customers. Request an itemized current quote and written eligibility confirmation, including POS, location, card processing, diner fees, setup, renewals, cancellation and data export.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a new restaurant sign up for BentoBox?

The current BentoBox website says its products and services are currently available only to Clover POS customers. Contact Clover to confirm eligibility and the product package available to your business.

### How much does BentoBox online ordering cost?

Official help material lists some configurations at $49 per month with a BentoBox website or $79 per month without one, plus a QR Order & Pay add-on price. Because these pages may describe specific or existing customer packages, confirm the current quote and fees directly with Clover.

### Is BentoBox independent from Clover?

No. BentoBox’s current site says its restaurant marketing and commerce solutions are now part of the Clover ecosystem. Confirm account ownership, support, login, payments and contract terms under the current provider.
