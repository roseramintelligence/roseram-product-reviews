## Platform Origin and Technical Fundamentals

BaZi Lab describes itself as an intelligent platform that merges traditional Chinese metaphysics with modern computational methods. In Chinese cultural history, BaZi, also called the Four Pillars of Destiny, evaluates an individual's life trajectory by analyzing the year, month, day, and hour of birth. The system structures these data points through Heavenly Stems, Earthly Branches, the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water), and Yin-Yang principles. Historically, calculating these relationships required cross-referencing complex sexagenary calendar charts by hand.

The software digitizes this workflow by calculating the Four Pillars programmatically. A key feature of its technical engine is the true solar time adjustment. Because standard clock time reflects broad geopolitical time zones rather than the sun's exact zenith at a person's birth coordinates, raw clock entries can assign an incorrect Hour Pillar. BaZi Lab incorporates a location search engine that calculates longitudinal offsets, adjusting the birth minute forward or backward before mapping the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches.

Beyond generating static pillar charts, the software includes a life trajectory visualization. This tool divides an individual's timeline into ten-year spans known as luck pillars. The platform explicitly notes that these pillars and individual year markers derive strictly from astronomical calendar calculations rather than generative language models. The software then renders a continuous mathematical curve illustrating relative shifts across decades, providing a visual baseline before any interpretive text is generated.


## The BaZi Agent Conversational Interface

Rather than forcing users to parse traditional almanac tables or archaic terminology, BaZi Lab deploys a conversational assistant named the BaZi Agent. Users interact with the agent through a standard chat box. To begin, the agent collects birth year, month, day, exact minute, gender, and geographic location through an interactive form. Once the calendar engine corrects for solar time and builds the Four Pillars, the assistant translates the structural relationships into conversational English or Chinese.

The assistant is programmed to field specific life inquiries. For example, users can ask whether an upcoming year favors a job change, inquire about relationship timing, identify their current luck pillar, or check favorable dates for moving house. In a documented sample calculation for an individual whose Day Master is Yin Metal born during a Fire month, the agent identified the governing elements, noted the presence of officer stars in a subsequent Fire Horse year, and explained the workplace implications in direct, modern prose.

This workflow bridges the gap between raw metaphysical tables and actionable self-reflection. However, buyers should remember that the conversational component relies on generative language models parsing symbolic relationships. The system does not possess sensory knowledge of your actual life circumstances. It applies traditional interpretive rubrics to your calculated elemental profile, which means the output serves as an introspective mirror rather than an empirical prediction.

## Subscription Tiers and Monthly Credit Allocations

Users who require recurring analysis or deep-dive topical readings can enroll in paid memberships billed in British pounds. The entry-level subscription, designated as Starter, costs £14.99 per month. In exchange, subscribers receive 150 monthly credits. This tier is designed for individuals seeking steady personal tracking, allowing one comprehensive monthly report alongside fifty supplementary chat turns beyond the daily free threshold.

For broader usage, the Plus membership costs £39.99 per month and grants 500 monthly credits. The highest tier, Pro, is priced at £54.99 per month and delivers 1,000 credits. The publisher highlights that Pro provides twice the monthly credits of Plus for an additional £15 each month, lowering the unit price per credit significantly. Subscribers on any tier can opt for annual billing, which discounts the total cost by approximately 16.6% across twelve months.

Buyers must pay close attention to the expiration mechanics of these subscription plans. Monthly credit grants do not accumulate indefinitely; each grant remains valid for 90 days after delivery. If an account goes unused for an extended period, older unused subscription credits will expire. However, canceling a membership does not immediately wipe out valid balances; unexpired credits remain active until their standard 90-day window concludes.


## One-Time Credit Packs and Financial Governance

Recognizing that recurring memberships do not fit every consumer's habits, BaZi Lab provides standalone, non-subscription credit packs. These one-time purchases are positioned for users who only want to audit a specific life event or purchase a single detailed report without incurring recurring billing obligations. The packs are tiered into 100 credits for £14.99, 300 credits for £39.99, and 650 credits for £79.99.

The primary operational difference between subscription credits and credit packs lies in expiration rules. Purchased credit packs never expire. While the unit cost of credits in a one-time pack is higher than in an active subscription—100 credits cost £14.99 in a one-off pack versus issuing 150 credits for the same price in a monthly Starter plan—the absence of an expiration date makes credit packs safer for infrequent users.

Financial transactions on the platform are managed externally by Stripe, meaning BaZi Lab does not collect or store full credit card numbers on its servers. The checkout system supports standard credit and debit cards (including UnionPay), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, Revolut Pay, and Amazon Pay. One-off credit packs also accept regional alternatives such as WeChat Pay and Clearpay, though recurring subscriptions cannot be initiated via WeChat Pay due to recurring billing limitations.


## Deep-Dive Report Catalog and Credit Consumption

The platform's primary paid deliverables are its specialized vertical reports. Generating a full report consumes exactly 100 credits. BaZi Lab offers eight distinct report types covering strategic life domains: career advancement, romantic relationships, personal wealth cycles, overall fortune, luck pillar transitions, wellness and health sensitivities, and monthly elemental energy shifts.

A critical consumer protection feature in the platform's billing architecture is the handling of saved reports. When a user spends 100 credits to generate a specific report, that document is saved permanently to their account profile. Reopening, reviewing, or rereading that report in subsequent months never incurs an additional charge. If a user wishes to generate an updated report reflecting the new temporal energy of a subsequent calendar month, that constitutes a new generation and costs another 100 credits.

Credit billing within conversational turns is similarly structured. Standard chat turns consume 1 credit once the account's daily free chat allotment is exhausted. The publisher specifies that background tool operations, form completions, and internal multi-step calculations occurring within a single prompt do not deduct extra credits. Furthermore, credits are temporarily reserved during report generation and are only settled once the output completes successfully; failed requests release the hold immediately.
