# Beautiful.AI review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** AI presentation maker  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Beautiful.AI is a presentation editor with Smart Slide layouts, AI writing and image generation, theme controls and team collaboration. Plans differ in seat count, branding, analytics, exports and administration; the 14-day paid trial requires a payment card according to the vendor’s pricing FAQ.

## Editorial verdict

Beautiful.AI is designed for people who want presentation layouts to adapt as content changes, with team branding and collaboration available on paid tiers. Its most important fit question is portability: animations and media may not survive export to other tools, while editable PowerPoint is restricted to paid plans. Review the card-required trial and cancellation timing before testing, and build a deck that exercises the exact export path your team needs.

## Use Smart Slides where layout speed matters

Beautiful.AI centers its editor on Smart Slides that automatically arrange content within prepared layouts. That can reduce manual alignment work for recurring sales, proposal and update decks. The tradeoff is that users may have less freedom than in a blank-canvas presentation tool, particularly when a brand or data story requires a custom composition.

Try the product on a deck with short text, dense data and a branded opening slide. Check how the layout responds when the content changes and whether the design controls preserve the intended hierarchy. A polished first draft still needs human review for accuracy and audience fit.


## Check how exports change the final presentation

Beautiful.AI’s help material lists editable PowerPoint and Google Slides exports on Pro and higher tiers. It also warns that transitions and animations do not transfer to external platforms, and other embedded media may be lost. Teams that present outside the native player should treat export behavior as a core plan requirement.

Export one deck with animation, notes, charts, audio and fonts. Compare it in the target presentation software and check the saved file on another computer. Decide whether the native web player is acceptable to clients before depending on a feature that cannot move with the deck.


## Compare individual and team seat costs

The pricing page lists a Pro plan for an individual, a Team plan for two to twenty seats and custom Enterprise terms. Team adds shared libraries, collaborative editing, version controls, branding and viewer analytics. A solo user should not pay for governance features they do not need; a team should include all required seats in its estimate.

Ask whether commenters, contractors and viewers require paid seats, and verify how workspaces handle ownership when an employee leaves. Include the annual commitment, one-time presentation option and any applicable SMB plan before finalizing a budget.


## Treat the free trial like a scheduled purchase

Beautiful.AI’s pricing FAQ says the 14-day trial requires a payment card and converts to a charge unless canceled before the period ends. This is a real billing condition, not a hidden product capability. Record the start date and cancellation deadline if the organization is only evaluating the software.

Use the trial to test imports, editable exports, team collaboration and data handling rather than creating a throwaway sample. Confirm the cancellation route and who controls the payment method. This assessment is based on public vendor materials and does not verify trial cancellation behavior in a live account.


## Features and decision criteria

- Smart Slides that adapt layouts to content
- AI writing, presentation creation and image generation
- Pro individual, Team and Enterprise governance plans
- PowerPoint and Google Slides exports can lose native animation or media

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page is subject to promotions and the Pro plan may have a monthly billing option with a different rate. Confirm the current trial, automatic billing, cancellation, seat minimums and one-time project terms before entering payment details.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the 14-day trial require a credit card?

The official pricing FAQ says a credit card is required and the account is charged unless the user cancels before the trial ends.

### Do slide animations survive PowerPoint export?

Beautiful.AI’s help center warns that external platforms do not support its transitions and animations, so they may be lost on export.

### Is editable PowerPoint export available on every plan?

The help center lists editable PowerPoint export for Pro, Team and Enterprise subscriptions.
