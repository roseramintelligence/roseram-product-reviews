# BeeFree.io review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Visual email and landing-page design  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Beefree is a visual email and landing-page design platform; its current studio is branded RGE Studio. The official pricing page lists a Starter plan with up to 10 designs and six exports monthly, Professional at $30 monthly or $25 monthly billed yearly, and Business at $160 monthly or $134 monthly billed yearly. Beefree designs and exports content but does not send email, so a separate delivery platform is needed.

## Editorial verdict

Beefree can suit marketers and teams that need reusable, responsive email designs without hand-coding every layout. Professional adds project organization, reusable content and an AI writing assistant; Business adds workspaces, approval workflows and stronger brand controls. The most important check is the full production path: editing and export quality, mobile and dark-mode rendering, accessibility, team permissions and compatibility with the email service that actually sends the campaign.

## Design reusable email content without treating it as a sender

Beefree provides a visual editor for creating email and landing-page layouts. Its current studio pricing route identifies the workspace as RGE Studio, and the product materials describe a drag-and-drop builder, templates, reusable rows, brand styles, design comments and export connectors. This can help a marketing team build consistent campaigns without manually assembling every HTML block.

The distinction between design and delivery is important: Beefree’s own abuse-report page says the service does not send emails. A campaign still needs an email service provider to manage the audience, consent, suppression, delivery, bounce processing and analytics. Confirm how the exported HTML and tracking links behave in that provider, and keep subscriber data and sending permission in the appropriate delivery system.


## Choose a tier based on production volume and collaboration

The official RGE Studio pricing page lists Starter at no cost, with up to 10 designs and six exports per month. Professional is $30 monthly or $25 per month billed yearly; Business is $160 monthly or $134 per month billed yearly; Enterprise is custom. Professional lists additional project capacity and reusable content, while Business lists multiple workspaces, brand controls, approval and QA features. Do not assume the SDK uses these same prices: the embedded builder is a separate product.

Estimate how many campaigns, landing pages, brands and export events the team handles each month. Count editors, reviewers, view-only users, reusable design blocks and approval steps. Check whether the listed export allowance fits routine testing, whether the email provider has a native connector or whether HTML copy-paste is required, and whether extra seats or workspaces change the bill. Compare the yearly discount with the contract commitment and your expected design volume.


## Check rendering, accessibility and handoff into the email platform

A design that looks polished in an editor can render differently across email clients, dark mode, mobile screens and assistive technology. Test representative templates in the actual sending platform and major inbox environments used by the audience. Check image dimensions, button targets, fallback fonts, line breaks, alt text, semantic headings, contrast and a plain-text alternative. If the design includes dynamic content or merge tags, verify that the receiving platform recognizes them exactly as exported.

Run the handoff with a non-campaign test audience. Confirm that links have the intended tracking parameters, unsubscribe and preference links remain present, and the email provider does not strip CSS or replace content. Establish a naming and approval convention so the final approved version—not a stale draft—is exported. Keep a source copy and document who is responsible for last-minute edits after the design leaves Beefree.


## Evaluate governance and the right Beefree product

For a solo user, the free Starter plan may be enough to test the editor and export workflow. A small team may value shared blocks and folders; a larger brand operation may need separate workspaces, approval stages, user roles or SSO. Verify the exact permissions in the current plan matrix and test what happens when an editor leaves or a brand template changes. Avoid giving every collaborator full publishing authority simply because the design tool supports collaboration.

If your company wants to embed an email builder inside its own SaaS product, evaluate Beefree SDK separately from the self-service RGE Studio. SDK pricing, API calls, user limits, data traffic and white-label options follow a distinct model. This guide summarizes official Beefree and RGE Studio pages and does not represent independent rendering tests or an endorsement of email deliverability.


## Features and decision criteria

- Drag-and-drop email and landing-page design with a template library and reusable content blocks
- Responsive preview, brand styles, team review and approval features that vary by plan
- Exports to email service providers or HTML; the product is a design tool and does not send emails itself
- SDK offerings are a separate embedded-builder product with substantially different pricing from the self-service studio

## Pricing considerations

Beefree’s current studio is presented as RGE Studio on its pricing route. The prices above are from the official page checked on 2026-11-24; the SDK has separate, higher-priced developer plans. Confirm the product, billing period, export limits, additional seats, storage, connector compatibility and tax before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Beefree send marketing emails?

No. Beefree’s official product-scope statement says it provides email and landing-page design and does not send the emails. Export the design to a separate email service provider.

### How much does Beefree cost?

The current RGE Studio pricing page lists Starter at $0, Professional at $30 monthly or $25 monthly billed yearly, Business at $160 monthly or $134 monthly billed yearly, and a custom Enterprise plan.

### Is Beefree SDK the same plan as the email builder?

No. Beefree SDK is an embeddable builder for software products and has its own developer pricing. Compare the correct product before evaluating a quote.
