# AllClinics.io review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-27

**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

AllClinics.io is an American healthcare market intelligence platform built around public records from CMS, SAM.gov, USAspending.gov, and NPPES. It delivers three tailored commercial workflows: tracking federal medical procurement solicitations, reverse-searching Affordable Care Act insurance coverage, and targeting hospital accounts for medical technology sales. The vendor offers annual per-seat licensing, CSV data downloads, and custom REST API connections. ## Platform Architecture and Public Data Provenance

AllClinics.io positions itself as a centralized healthcare market intelligence platform designed around a single shared repository that powers three separate commercial front doors. The architecture does not attempt to serve as an electronic health record system, clinical decision support software, or patient diagnostic tool. Instead, it aggregates and indexes public administrative records to answer commercial questions. The core foundation of the platform rests upon established federal government repositories, specifically the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the System for Award Management (SAM.gov), USAspending.gov, and the National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (NPPES).

A critical consideration for any healthcare software buyer is regulatory compliance, specifically surrounding the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Because AllClinics.io compiles exclusively open, public-source records without handling individual patient protected health information, the vendor explicitly states that organizations do not need to sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) to license the database. Each record inside the platform carries source attribution back to its origin repository. This clear lineage simplifies internal compliance reviews, allowing corporate risk teams to license the tool without worrying about patient privacy liabilities or clinical compliance protocols.


## Federal Medical Procurement Tracking

The first dedicated workflow within AllClinics.io targets government contractors, medical equipment suppliers, and healthcare staffing agencies that bid on federal solicitations. Traditional government procurement portals can be frustrating to navigate, often requiring complex search queries across fragmented federal websites. The platform addresses this pain point by synchronizing daily with SAM.gov, allowing capture teams to track active contract notices, monitor agency solicitations, and review vendor supplier histories through a streamlined dashboard. Contractors can quickly filter opportunities by North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) codes, issuing government agencies, and specific set-aside classifications like veteran-owned, woman-owned, or small-business designations.

Beyond initial discovery, the system provides automated deadline notifications and supplier intelligence to help capture managers prioritize active proposal pipelines. When bidding on federal healthcare work, missing an amendment deadline or filing late can disqualify an otherwise competitive proposal. To evaluate this workflow properly, a contractor should establish a straightforward side-by-side verification exercise. During an initial trial, a capture analyst should identify five active agency solicitations inside AllClinics.io and simultaneously cross-check those records against direct search queries on SAM.gov. This comparative test will confirm whether notification alerts, solicitation amendments, and procurement deadlines appear accurately and without latency.


## Reverse-Lookup Workflows for ACA Insurance Brokers

The second major workflow on the platform is configured for health insurance brokers, employee benefits consultants, and healthcare navigators working within the Affordable Care Act (ACA) marketplace. When an individual client or small business employee requires insurance that covers a specific surgical procedure, diagnostic screening, or specialized therapy, finding the exact policy details can be grueling. Traditionally, an agent must open numerous separate carrier benefit summaries, search for individual medical codes, and manually locate detailed exclusions hidden in plan fine print. AllClinics.io seeks to streamline this process through a reverse-search search interface.

Instead of forcing brokers to examine plans individually, the software allows them to type in a specific clinical service or medical procedure to instantly surface all regional ACA plans that cover it. The search interface displays essential benefit terms side-by-side, detailing patient copayments, coinsurance percentages, and buried coverage limitations. The system also includes an automated export feature that converts comparative plan tables into branded summary documents for client presentations. A broker evaluating the platform should take two complex outpatient procedures, run searches across target county exchanges, and audit the generated copay figures against primary carrier policy booklets to verify that plan details remain up to date.


## Medtech Hospital Prospecting and Directory Scope

The third workflow is designed for medical device manufacturers, clinical hardware vendors, and medtech sales representatives who need to identify and prioritize hospital sales targets. Successfully selling high-value clinical capital equipment requires knowing which facilities perform enough surgical volume to warrant an outreach campaign. The vendor claims its hospital directory includes 4,700 medical facilities across all fifty states. Through the dashboard, sales representatives can segment facilities by procedure volumes, Merit-based Incentive Payment System (MIPS) quality scores, and regional health network affiliations.

The platform also pairs these operational facility metrics with verified hospital leadership and C-suite contact records. In corporate outbound sales, executive turnover creates severe data degradation, rendering stale contact lists useless within months. A sales operations director evaluating AllClinics.io should pull twenty prospective hospital accounts across three distinct geographic territories. The evaluation team should verify whether listed procurement heads and chief executives currently hold their positions, while confirming that listed procedural volumes align reasonably with recent facility utilization numbers before deploying the list to field sales reps.


## Commercial Pricing and Subscription Mechanics

Pricing transparency is fundamental when assessing whether a B2B data vendor fits within an organization’s software budget. AllClinics.io utilizes an annual per-seat licensing model rather than selling separate modular subscriptions for its individual workflows. According to published company pricing, a single user license covers all three workflows—federal contracting, insurance search, and medtech targeting—along with access to the full underlying healthcare dataset. The vendor lists an introductory founding rate of $1,500 per seat per year, which is presented as a permanent lifetime locked discount compared to an advertised standard price of $2,500 per seat per year.

The publisher supports this subscription with a 14-day evaluation trial and self-service cancellation terms. However, buyers should approach promotional founding offers with careful commercial scrutiny. The vendor’s documentation notes that the discounted rate is restricted to fifty founding seats. Furthermore, corporate organizations requiring multi-user agency licensing, team-wide activity tracking, programmatic REST API feeds, or bespoke offline data licensing must contact sales for custom enterprise quotes. Procurement managers should always secure written verification detailing multi-seat expansion costs, renewal price guarantees, and API data limits before completing the trial period.


## Enterprise Integration and Data Export Architecture

A market intelligence platform cannot deliver full operational value if its proprietary records remain confined within a third-party web browser interface. Business development teams, bidding groups, and insurance agencies rely heavily on existing customer relationship management software, applicant management platforms, and internal business intelligence dashboards. Recognizing this organizational requirement, AllClinics.io advertises standard tabular CSV data downloads alongside custom REST API capabilities to allow external records to flow into an organization’s everyday software stack.

According to the publisher, these export features enable users to push federal contract watchlists, ACA insurance tables, and prioritized hospital directory files directly into CRM systems like Salesforce or internal data lakes. Enterprise technology leaders considering this system should evaluate integration mechanics early in their discovery process. A thorough technical audit should confirm endpoint rate limits, JSON response schema stability, and whether pre-cleaned unique identifiers can merge cleanly with internal account databases. Asking whether enterprise data licensing fees apply to API consumption is essential before incorporating the vendor’s endpoints into internal reporting pipelines.


## Content Strategy and Public Healthcare Price Analytics

Alongside its operational software tools, AllClinics.io maintains a regular publishing program examining healthcare price variations and market trends across the United States. Their editorial articles discuss the underlying economic factors that drive massive cost discrepancies between medical facilities in different states. Citing independent studies, such as the Rand Corporation’s report on hospital prices paid by private health plans, the vendor explains how state-level regulatory systems, hospital market consolidation, regional cost of living, and Medicaid expansion programs directly shape clinical pricing across regions.

The company’s research library also includes pricing reports detailing average out-of-pocket and insured expenses for routine clinical procedures. For example, their publications explore cost ranges for diagnostic colonoscopies, outpatient CT scans, soft-tissue biopsies, screening mammograms, and knee replacements, noting how surgical costs can range from roughly $19,934 in low-cost states like Iowa to over $61,750 in expensive metropolitan areas like New York. This public content library serves a dual role: it demonstrates how the company analyzes raw CMS price transparency datasets, while establishing an educational resource for commercial teams evaluating American healthcare market dynamics.


## Structural Boundaries and Operating Constraints

While consolidating multiple federal datasets into a clean user interface provides clear productivity benefits, prospective subscribers must understand the platform’s structural limitations. Because AllClinics.io relies on public-sector repositories like CMS, SAM.gov, USAspending.gov, and NPPES, its data accuracy is inherently dependent on external government update schedules. If an agency delays posting a procurement modification on USAspending.gov, or if a hospital system delays filing its annual CMS transparency files, the platform cannot artificially accelerate those government updates.

Similarly, while the reverse-lookup engine is valuable for exploring ACA marketplace policies, many health insurance brokers manage commercial group plans, self-funded employer trusts, and association policies that fall entirely outside federal exchange datasets. Medtech sales representatives must also recognize that hospital administrative staffing changes rapidly due to continuous healthcare mergers, acquisitions, and executive turnover. Buyers should not expect a turnkey tool that eliminates internal verification. Recognizing these operational boundaries ensures that teams adopt the platform for its real aggregation strengths rather than expecting unrealistic automated prospecting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does AllClinics.io require a Business Associate Agreement or handle protected health information?

According to the publisher, AllClinics.io relies entirely on public-source administrative and procurement data and contains no patient protected health information (PHI). Because no patient identifiers or private clinical medical records are processed, a formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is not required.

### What primary public data sources does AllClinics.io aggregate?

The platform compiles records from four core federal government repositories: the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the System for Award Management (SAM.gov), USAspending.gov, and the National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (NPPES).

### How can corporate teams export or integrate data from AllClinics.io?

The vendor provides standard tabular CSV file exports from its interfaces, along with custom REST API endpoints to feed contract opportunities, insurance tables, and hospital directory lists into internal CRM, agency management, or business intelligence platforms.

### What terms govern the AllClinics.io evaluation trial?

The platform offers a 14-day trial period that permits prospective buyers to test the three workflows against their own accounts and workflows, with cancel-anytime terms before annual billing locks in.
