# Prezi review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Presentation and visual storytelling software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Prezi offers presentations, video and infographic tools with a zooming visual canvas, AI presentation features and audience interaction options. Individual plans differ in privacy, export and desktop access, while team tiers add collaboration, branding and administrative capabilities.

## Editorial verdict

Prezi is a distinct choice for speakers who want a visual narrative that moves through a canvas instead of following a conventional slide sequence. The entry plan is free but has fewer privacy and export features, while offline use and full presenter tools require an appropriate paid plan. Try a real presentation and confirm that motion, accessibility and export requirements fit the audience before committing.

## Match the visual format to your audience

Prezi’s zooming canvas can help a presenter connect ideas visually and move between an overview and details. That approach can feel different from a deck of linear slides, which may suit a live talk but requires a clear route so viewers do not lose the narrative.

Build a short version of an actual presentation and share it with someone unfamiliar with the topic. Check whether they can follow the order, understand where they are on the canvas and find the takeaway. For formal reports or simple status updates, a linear slide deck may be easier to scan.


## Compare privacy and export needs before selecting a plan

Prezi plan features vary in privacy controls, PowerPoint import, PDF export, desktop access and analytics. A free account can be enough to try the editor, but an organization should verify the sharing visibility and export rights required for private client or internal presentations.

Test the exact delivery path: web link, downloaded PDF, exported PowerPoint or offline presentation. Motion, media and formatting may differ between the original Prezi player and a static export. Make sure the recipient can open the chosen format without being forced to create an account unless that is acceptable.


## Budget AI credits as a plan feature

The official plan guide lists a bounded AI credit allowance on Basic and Standard, while Plus and higher plans describe expanded AI use. Check how credit consumption is counted for presentation generation, rewriting and image creation before estimating how many projects fit into the included amount.

Treat AI output as a first draft and review factual claims, sources, names and numbers before presenting. Do not place confidential company material into an AI tool until the organization understands the applicable data settings and terms.


## Test presenter and team functions in a short trial

Prezi’s individual and business tiers include different presenter tools, analytics, training, collaboration and administration features. If multiple people maintain a shared library, review who can edit, publish or revoke links. For education, verify which plan is available to the institution and what evidence of eligibility is required.

Create a sample with video, voice-over or a remote presentation and test it on the same devices the audience will use. Confirm accessibility, browser compatibility and offline readiness. This review reflects the vendor’s published plan materials and is not a live usability or compatibility test.


## Features and decision criteria

- Presentation, video and infographic creation
- AI tools for generation and transformation with plan-specific credits
- Privacy, desktop app, offline use and export vary by tier
- Business and education plans provide additional team or institution features

## Pricing considerations

Prezi has separate business, education and team plans. Free trial availability and terms vary by plan; confirm the current billing interval, trial auto-renewal, privacy settings and AI credit policy before starting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a free Prezi presentation be private?

The plan comparison places advanced privacy and sharing controls on paid plans; verify the exact visibility of the Basic plan before creating sensitive work.

### Does Prezi Basic include unlimited AI generation?

No. The official materials list limited AI credits for Basic; the Plus plan is described as including unlimited AI features.

### Can I work offline?

The plan guide lists desktop app and offline access with Plus and higher eligible plans, not the basic web plan.
