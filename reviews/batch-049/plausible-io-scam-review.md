# Plausible.io review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-17  
**Category:** Software & online services  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Plausible is a hosted web analytics product organized around pageview tiers, with plan differences in sites, teams, retention and advanced reporting. Custom events count toward usage, and repeated high-traffic months can require an upgrade.

## Editorial verdict

Plausible’s small analytics dashboard and hosted service suit teams that want a focused traffic view with limited setup. Check how pageviews and custom events are counted across the team’s sites, and understand what happens after recurring over-limit usage before using the free trial as a proxy for long-term cost.

## Plausible Analytics review: a focused traffic dashboard

Plausible provides a hosted analytics dashboard designed to make a site’s traffic, pages, referrers and goals legible without a large reporting suite. Its documentation distinguishes pageviews from unique visitors and explains how custom events contribute to engagement and usage. The product can be a good fit when a small team wants to answer basic questions about content discovery and conversion without maintaining a self-hosted analytics stack.

A concise dashboard still needs a careful measurement plan. Define the business event that represents a conversion, ensure it is tracked once, and compare it against an authoritative backend record where possible. A pageview measures a page load; it does not prove a sale, a qualified lead or a person’s intent. Review direct traffic, bot filtering, consent behavior and query parameters before using a report to make budget decisions.


## Plausible pricing tiers and what separates the plans

Plausible’s official plan documentation says all plans start with the same pageview tiers; the main differences are the number of sites, team features and advanced reporting. Starter is aimed at one site and solo use, Growth adds multiple sites, team access and shared dashboards, and Business includes features such as funnels, user journeys, revenue tracking, custom properties and the Stats API. Enterprise is custom and adds items such as SSO, Sites API, managed proxy and raw event exports. The service offers a 30-day trial without a credit card and advertises two free months for annual billing.

The pricing tier is calculated from pageviews and custom events across all sites within a team. Outbound clicks, downloads, form submissions, 404 tracking and manually tracked events can all count. Separate teams have independent subscriptions, which matters for agencies or organizations that divide ownership. Before enabling a large set of custom events, estimate their monthly volume alongside pageviews and compare the total with the selected tier.


## Plausible traffic spikes, upgrade notices and dashboard access

Plausible’s documentation says a single month above the pageview allowance does not cause an extra overage fee or immediate dashboard interruption. If traffic exceeds the tier for two consecutive months, the service notifies the customer to upgrade; if no upgrade is made after the documented period, dashboards may be temporarily locked, while statistics continue to be collected. A suitable upgrade is prorated, and the customer may downgrade after traffic returns within the tier. These are operational rules worth understanding before a launch or seasonal campaign.

Check usage in the account subscription settings rather than infer it from a single day’s chart. A viral post can consume the month’s allowance quickly but does not necessarily imply a permanent plan change. For an event-heavy implementation, review which events are considered billable and remove duplicate or diagnostic events that do not inform a decision. Keep an export or reporting process for stakeholders who cannot access the dashboard if a usage threshold is reached.


## Plausible data, events and reporting considerations

Plausible offers reports for traffic sources, pages and goals, with more advanced funnel, revenue and API features on higher plans. Its metric definitions specify that pageviews, visits and unique visitors are different measures; for example, a single visitor can generate several pageviews. This distinction should appear in campaign reports so that a team does not describe page loads as individual people or equate traffic with outcomes.

Design custom event names and properties before deploying the script. Use consistent names across the site and avoid sending personal details in URLs or event properties. When blending analytics with ads or CRM data, record the attribution method and any missing consented traffic. Test the script on a staging page, validate events in the dashboard and compare totals with server records. A privacy-oriented product label does not automatically configure a lawful consent experience for every business.


## Plausible alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Plausible with Matomo, Google Analytics and first-party server-side analytics using the same set of sites and events. Include traffic tier, event count, retention, team roles, export requirements, data control and whether funnels or revenue reporting are essential. A small publisher may prefer a compact dashboard, whereas an organization needing raw events, SSO or extensive integrations may require a higher plan or another system. Count the full team’s sites and events, not just one domain.

Plausible’s official documentation describes its pricing, analytics features and overage process; it does not establish that Plausible is a scam. This source-led review did not install the tracker or reconcile a customer account. Confirm current plan pricing, annual renewal, team/site limits, event counting, data retention, cancellation timing and what happens to access after the trial before relying on it for ongoing reporting.


## Features and decision criteria

- Privacy-focused website analytics dashboard
- Pageview and custom-event measurement
- Goals, segments, funnels and user journeys on eligible plans
- Stats API, exports and team sharing on higher tiers

## Pricing considerations

All self-serve plans use pageview tiers, and custom events are included in total usage across sites in a team. The trial lasts 30 days without a card; annual billing saves two months. Sustained overages can lead to an upgrade request and temporary dashboard lock if no upgrade is made.
