# Podia review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Creator storefront and course platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Podia combines a website, email, checkout, digital downloads, courses, coaching, events and community in a creator-focused platform. Its plans differ by subscriber, product and feature limits, and the entry paid tier charges an additional platform transaction fee on sales.

## Editorial verdict

Podia can reduce setup work for a creator who wants one place to publish a site, sell digital offers and follow up with an email audience. Its Mover plan has a lower subscription than the tiers above it but keeps a 5% platform transaction fee and tighter email/product limits. Compare the fixed subscription plus payment processing and platform fees using realistic revenue assumptions before choosing a tier.

## Decide whether combining the storefront is useful

Podia bundles a website, checkout, email and creator products such as courses, downloads, coaching and events. A new creator may value having fewer systems to connect. A business with an established storefront and email service should compare migration effort and feature gaps before moving everything into one account.

Define the first offer and how a customer discovers, purchases and receives it. Test the checkout, confirmation, access delivery and follow-up email with a low-cost or test product before launch. Confirm that the platform supports the tax, currency and refund process expected for the audience.


## Calculate both subscription and transaction costs

Podia’s Mover tier carries a 5% platform fee per sale, on top of payment processor fees. Shaker and Earthquaker remove Podia’s fee but have higher fixed subscription costs. The plan comparison also limits products, videos, spaces and email subscribers, which can cause a growing creator to upgrade for reasons beyond transaction fees.

Model fixed monthly cost, expected sales value, refunds and processing costs at several realistic volume levels. Avoid using a best-case revenue assumption. Compare the break-even point between tiers, and re-run it when audience size or product count grows.


## Plan subscriber growth without surprise changes

Podia lists included email subscriber limits for each plan. Its help documentation says it does not automatically upgrade an account when a limit is reached, so the owner needs a plan for staying within the current cap or adding capacity. A fixed subscriber allotment can make the initial price less comparable to an unlimited-contact email service.

Keep consent and suppression records when importing a list. Test double opt-in, unsubscribe handling and sender-domain authentication before a campaign. Do not assume a checkout relationship means the buyer agreed to all future marketing messages.


## Test the customer path and content portability

A bundled platform is only useful if the customer path is dependable: landing page, payment, receipt, access, support and refund. Test each state on desktop and mobile, including a declined payment and a canceled subscription. Check whether a customer can download purchased files or access lessons on the devices they use.

Before building a large catalog, review how to export customers, purchases, email activity and course materials. Ask what happens to published links and access when an account is canceled. This review summarizes vendor documentation and does not claim a revenue outcome or hands-on checkout test.


## Features and decision criteria

- Website, landing pages, email and built-in checkout
- Digital downloads, courses, coaching, memberships and events
- Plan limits on products, videos, spaces and included email subscribers
- Mover charges 5% platform fee; processor fees still apply

## Pricing considerations

Podia offers a 14-day trial without requiring a card according to the pricing page. Processor fees are separate from Podia’s platform fee. Verify monthly versus annual billing, legacy plans, subscriber limits and available payment processors before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Podia charge a transaction fee on every plan?

No. Podia states that Mover charges 5% while Shaker and Earthquaker have no Podia platform transaction fee; processor fees still apply.

### Does the email subscriber limit automatically trigger a higher plan?

Podia says it does not auto-upgrade an account when the subscriber limit is reached; users must change plans or add capacity.

### Is the listed price the complete cost per sale?

No. Payment processing fees are separate, and Mover also has a 5% Podia fee.
