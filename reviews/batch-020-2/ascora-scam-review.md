## The right plan depends on how jobs are structured

The captured pricing distinguishes Essentials from Elite, with a separate custom Enterprise arrangement. Essentials lists core scheduling, estimates, invoices, mobile work and accounting connections. Elite adds project stages, progress billing, custom forms and other advanced workflows. A contractor should identify whether the ordinary work is a single visit, a recurring service or a project spanning phases and changes. Those distinctions can matter more than the number of customer names in the system.

Choose a representative job with the stages and documents the team actually uses, then map each requirement to the selected plan. These are proposed procurement checks, not a trial exercised here. The homepage's broad all-in-one description should not be interpreted as proof that every feature is included in the least expensive tier. Where a summary and a detailed comparison appear to place a capability differently, obtain explicit confirmation for the intended account rather than silently choosing the most favorable reading.


## Per-user prices need an actual account model

The saved price page displays Essentials at $45 per user per month and Elite at $59, in USD excluding applicable sales tax. These are captured observations, not a current quotation. Build the estimate from the people who need to perform the workflow, including staff who approve, schedule or reconcile work. These are proposed commercial checks, not an invoice inspected here. Separate the provider's charge from the time the business needs to prepare and maintain the system.

## Estimate conversion needs a clear acceptance boundary

Ascora describes itemized estimates, options and conversion from an accepted estimate into a job and later an invoice. That can reduce re-entry, but an option shown to a customer is different from an option they accepted. Define which scope, price and conditions become part of the job. A later change should not silently alter the evidence of what the customer originally agreed to receive.

Use a representative estimate with more than one option and inspect the resulting scheduled work and invoice draft. These are proposed workflow checks, not an estimate created here. The saved source does not establish every approval, signature or revision rule. A contractor should verify those details for the actual process rather than assume that a one-click conversion removes the need to review scope and commercial meaning.


## Multi-crew scheduling needs exception handling

The homepage describes drag-and-drop scheduling, live updates and rescheduling when a person becomes unavailable. Those can help coordinate several crews, but the board still needs to reflect the operational constraints that matter: qualifications, travel, required equipment and the customer's availability. A visually open slot should not automatically be treated as a feasible assignment. Define the rules and confirm which are enforced versus left to staff judgment.

A pilot should include a revised appointment and a crew change, then inspect what the office and field staff see. These are proposed checks, not dispatch performed here. The review has not verified notification timing or conflict prevention. A useful system should make the final assignment and any unresolved issue understandable, rather than leave different people acting on different assumptions because a change appeared to succeed only on one screen.


## Offline field work needs synchronization evidence

Ascora's homepage and pricing describe mobile work on iOS and Android, including offline use and synchronization after connectivity returns. That is useful for sites where a connection is unreliable, but the team should identify which data is available before going offline and which actions remain pending. A completed form on a device and a record received by the office are different states.

Evaluate a harmless sample job with photos, materials and a controlled interruption. These are proposed acceptance checks, not a device session run here. Verify the actual behavior when the office changes the same job during an offline period and consult the relevant current documentation for reconciliation rules. The saved marketing description does not establish every conflict or retry outcome, so this review cannot certify uninterrupted or lossless operation for an unspecified field environment.

## Progress billing and change orders need traceable scope

The Elite description includes multi-phase projects, progress billing and change orders. These functions can fit work whose scope or delivery extends across several visits. The implementation should preserve the relationship between the original estimate, approved variations, work recorded and the amount being invoiced. A change visible in the job record should not be assumed to have been commercially approved simply because someone entered it.

Use a small staged project with one clearly authorized revision and inspect the resulting documentation. These are proposed acceptance checks, not billing advice or a project configured here. No invoice was issued. The source establishes a feature category, but the business needs the exact approval and reconciliation behavior that fits its agreements. Keep a drafted charge, an issued invoice and a received payment distinct in the interface and reporting.


## Accounting synchronization and AI bill import are not the same feature

The saved pages explicitly distinguish two-way QuickBooks Online and Xero synchronization from AI supplier-invoice importing. The latter reads information from supplier documents into a job, while accounting sync exchanges records between systems. This is an important boundary: a bill being extracted accurately does not prove that it was mapped, posted and reconciled correctly in accounting. Conversely, a connection working does not establish the accuracy of a scanned document's extracted fields.

Evaluate the exact flow with suitable test records and compare totals, identifiers and the destination job. These are proposed checks, not accounting integrations exercised here. The review has not inspected a financial account or provided accounting guidance. Obtain current documentation for the relevant source and destination, including error handling, before treating the absence of manual re-entry as proof that every record matches.


## Inventory and equipment records need stable identifiers
