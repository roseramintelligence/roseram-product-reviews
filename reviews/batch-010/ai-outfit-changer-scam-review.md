## AI Outfit Changer: keep identity separate from styling

An outfit edit should not quietly change the person’s face, body or pose in ways that alter the meaning of the image. Before evaluating the tool, identify the aspects that must remain stable and the clothing details that should be represented faithfully. This makes review more precise than deciding whether the result is flattering.

The publisher describes a workflow involving a person image and outfit reference. Confirm the current input requirements and how correction works for the specific mode you intend to use. A generated resemblance does not establish an exact match to the real garment, and the image cannot determine comfort, sizing or material behavior.


## A controlled preview with authorized source images

Use photographs and clothing references you have permission to process. Compare the output with both inputs, including hands, garment edges and any existing text or accessories. Inspect at the intended publication size and reject changes that misrepresent the person or the outfit. Keep the original references with the accepted result.

Ask how the current service handles stored uploads and deletion before using personal or customer images. Do not infer a privacy guarantee from the ability to generate a preview. This article does not verify the service’s data practices or the rights associated with a particular input; those need review against the applicable terms and intended use.

Use an outfit reference with an important distinguishing detail and a person image with a pose that makes the edit less straightforward. Review whether the result preserves both the person and that detail. A flattering approximation may be useful for a creative mood board, but it should not be presented as an exact product preview when the garment has materially changed during generation.
