## AI Resume Builder: verify the product behind the generic name

A generic directory label can conceal the identity of the actual publisher. Confirm that the website and purchase route correspond to the ForgeMyCV offer you intend to use. Then distinguish the ability to create a preview from the conditions for downloading the final document. An attractive editing screen is not the same as a usable application file.

The publisher describes AI-assisted resume preparation. That can support clearer writing, but it does not verify a qualification or determine whether an employer will invite an interview. Start with an accurate account of your experience and reject changes that turn involvement into ownership, introduce unsupported numbers or imply skills you cannot explain.


## A truthful application and export exercise

Choose a vacancy you are genuinely considering and prepare a copy of your resume without unnecessary personal information. Identify the experience that supports the role before generating a draft. Review every changed responsibility and achievement against that factual record. The strongest wording is useful only when it remains defensible in an interview.

Inspect the exported file independently of the editor. Check text selection, page breaks and whether important headings remain understandable. A claim about application-system friendliness is not a guarantee that every employer will process the file identically. This review does not report an interview-rate test or validate compatibility with a particular hiring platform.

A useful additional check asks a reviewer unfamiliar with your original resume to summarize the candidate from the final file. Compare that summary with the facts you intended to communicate. If it implies a different level of responsibility or experience, correct the document before submission. This detects misleading emphasis that a sentence-by-sentence spelling check can miss.

Review the document for information that is unnecessary for the intended employer but sensitive enough to avoid sharing casually. A convenient drafting process does not require uploading every historical detail. Start with a restrained test copy, then decide what belongs in the final application. This keeps the evaluation focused on clarity while preserving appropriate control over personal and former-employer information.
