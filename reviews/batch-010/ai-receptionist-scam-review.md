## AI Receptionist: write the boundaries of the conversation

Before evaluating a telephone assistant, define the actions it may take and the promises it must not make. A caller asking about opening hours is different from one requesting an exception, a refund or urgent help. The business should decide how each type of request reaches an accountable person. A system cannot safely infer those rules from a friendly welcome script.

The publisher describes answering and assistant configuration. Ask which information is used in the current setup and who maintains it when the business changes. A stale availability rule can create an incorrect commitment even when the conversation sounds fluent. Evaluate the underlying information and handoff, not only the apparent realism of the voice.


## Test a correction and an unresolved request

Use fictional calls to exercise a normal inquiry, a changed appointment preference and a question outside the approved information. Check whether the resulting record preserves what the caller actually wanted and distinguishes a confirmed action from a request awaiting review. Ask staff to explain the next step using only that record.

Include an interrupted conversation and a caller who corrects a name or date. Confirm the documented escalation route for sensitive or urgent situations before any live use. This review does not verify emergency suitability, language performance or integration reliability. Those are specific requirements to investigate with the publisher for the intended deployment.

A revealing fictional call contains a request that sounds ordinary but falls outside the business’s approved service area or availability. Decide the correct escalation before testing. Then inspect whether the caller and staff receive the same understanding of what was requested and what remains unconfirmed. This connects conversational quality with the business commitment that actually matters after the call ends.


## Compare useful service outcomes

Confirm the current call allowances, connected-system requirements and responsibilities for reviewing exceptions. Count accurate completed handoffs and the work needed to correct mistakes. Answering more calls is not automatically a business improvement if callers receive the wrong expectations or staff cannot recover the context.
