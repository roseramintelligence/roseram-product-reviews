## AI Clothes Changer: appearance is different from fit

A virtual clothing image can make a color or style easier to discuss while still changing details that matter to a buyer. A garment may acquire a different shape, texture or fastening in generation. Before evaluating the tool, decide which details must remain true and which are merely exploratory. Do not treat a convincing image as evidence of measurements or comfort.

The publisher describes uploaded-photo clothing changes across several uses. Confirm the exact input and editing process for the tool you intend to use. A sample does not establish the behavior of your own photograph, especially when pose, lighting or existing clothing makes the task harder. The useful comparison focuses on fidelity and correction effort.


## A permission-based visual evaluation

Use a photograph you are entitled to process and a garment reference whose use is authorized. Review the generated result for unintended changes to the person, inaccurate garment details and obvious image artifacts. Compare it at the resolution and placement where it would actually appear, rather than only as a small preview.

Ask how uploads and outputs are stored and what deletion or reuse terms apply. Do not infer those arrangements from a general privacy slogan. This review does not audit data handling or verify the commercial rights attached to a particular output. Obtain the current terms and use a low-risk test image while evaluating the workflow.

For a more precise review, choose a garment with a recognizable collar, fastening or pattern and compare those details in the output. A generic resemblance is not the same as an accurate product representation. If the purpose is exploration, label the result accordingly; if the purpose is selling a specific garment, apply a stricter acceptance standard and keep real product photographs available alongside the preview.
