## Distinguish hiding a file from encrypting a separate vault

ArkWarden positions its vault as additional file protection rather than merely moving media out of ordinary view. The homepage contrasts its design with the iPhone Hidden album, while also acknowledging that iPhone Data Protection encrypts files. That is a more specific comparison than claiming ordinary device storage has no encryption. The relevant question is which additional access boundary the app is intended to create and whether that boundary addresses the user's actual concern.

Define the scenario before evaluating the feature: another person casually browsing an unlocked phone, loss of a device or access to an exported backup are different situations. These are proposed assessment questions, not attacks tested here. The review does not independently validate the publisher's comparison with every other vault or iOS behavior. Keep the source-described additional protection distinct from a universal assertion that one application solves every privacy problem.
