## Ads Lighthouse: preserve the context of a brand-search observation

Two people can see different advertisements for a similar search. Location, device, timing and search wording can all affect the observation. A useful monitoring record therefore needs more context than a cropped headline. Decide which queries and markets matter to the brand, then ask how the proposed workflow preserves the conditions under which an ad was seen.

The publisher describes saved advertisements and affiliate-related investigation. A visible identifier can be a lead for review, but it is not a complete attribution or contractual judgment on its own. Compare the record with the relevant program terms and other evidence before accusing an affiliate of violating an agreement. This review makes no allegation about any observed advertiser.


## Design an investigation process before collecting alerts

Create a fictional monitoring brief with one brand query, a relevant location and a question the team wants to answer. Ask the publisher to show how a matching observation becomes a record another person can inspect. Include the steps for distinguishing a one-off appearance from a repeated pattern.

Assign responsibility for reviewing alerts and deciding what evidence is needed before a response. Without that process, more observations may simply create more unread messages. We have not tested monitoring coverage or established that an absent result proves no advertisement ran. Ask how limitations in sampling and availability should be interpreted.

Consider a record where a similar company name appears in an advertisement but the destination belongs to an unrelated organization. The team needs to inspect the actual query, visible copy and destination before deciding what it means. Build that identity check into the review process. Monitoring can supply evidence for investigation, while interpretation still depends on context and the applicable commercial or contractual relationship.


## Compare coverage and explainability

Confirm the search sources, locations, devices and observation allowances included in the current offer. Judge the tool by whether it helps answer your monitoring question with understandable records. A large raw count of checks is less useful if the team cannot reconstruct what was observed or connect it to an agreed next step.
