## Core Target Audience and Operational Suitability

Axiom Blue is designed specifically for owner-operators and small trade companies that have outgrown physical desk diaries, whiteboards, paper carbon copies, and fragmented text message threads. In a traditional small trade business, the owner often handles estimates late in the evening, technicians communicate job status through disjointed mobile chats, and field records are collected on crumpled paper receipts. This fragmented approach inevitably leads to forgotten billable hours, unbilled materials, and delayed customer invoices that slowly undermine business cash flow across the financial year.

The application provides a structured operational bridge that connects quoting, technician scheduling, job time recording, and draft invoicing without overwhelming users with complex enterprise menus. It is particularly well suited for service businesses managing residential service calls, scheduled recurring maintenance, and multi-day domestic or commercial installations. For these businesses, having technician schedules, customer notes, and material lists accessible from both the office computer and mobile field devices eliminates unnecessary phone calls and administrative bottlenecks.

Conversely, trade organizations with complex operational requirements should carefully assess the platform's boundaries before migrating. Companies that require deep multi-warehouse barcode scanning, advanced manufacturing shop-floor tracking, complex civil construction equipment telematics, or heavy certified subcontractor tender portals will find Axiom Blue focused strictly on trade service fundamentals. It provides essential quoting and dispatch capabilities rather than broad enterprise resource planning modules designed for massive industrial corporations.


## Quote to Paid Work Lifecycle Architecture

The underlying workflow of Axiom Blue is organized around a unified job file that carries client context through four distinct operational stages: agreed quote, scheduled job, recorded time and materials, and invoice review. The process begins with an estimate that details the proposed scope, separating estimated labour hours from individual material lines. Once the customer accepts the quote, that document establishes the baseline financial and operational framework for the subsequent work, preventing confusion regarding what was originally promised.

When work is scheduled, the accepted scope automatically populates the job record on the dispatch calendar, specifying the assigned technician, the site address, and the target timeframe. When the technician completes the work on site, they log their actual working hours and any physical parts used during the visit. Rather than automatically sending a generated invoice to the customer, the platform preserves both the original quoted figures and the field entries side by side, creating a clear comparison for administrative review.

The final stage is the invoice review process, where a business owner or office administrator inspects discrepancies before sending the bill. If a technician took longer than estimated or installed additional hardware, the system flags these items on the invoice draft. The administrator retains full discretion to bill the extra amount, absorb the overrun, or modify the figures based on client agreements. This intentional review checkpoint ensures that invoices remain accurate and that customers are never billed for unexpected charges without administrative oversight.


## Handling Extra Scope and Variation Controls

A persistent source of financial loss for trade contractors is the casual on-site request commonly known as 'while you're here' work. For example, an electrical contractor hired to replace a domestic switchboard may be asked by the homeowner to install an extra double power point in the kitchen. In manual trade workflows, field technicians often complete these minor additions without recording the extra parts or time, resulting in unbilled labour and unrecovered material costs that erode profit margins over time.

Axiom Blue addresses this operational challenge through two distinct workflow methods depending on the user's subscription tier. On entry-level accounts, technicians record the extra hours and additional parts directly against the job record as standard time and materials notes. When the job returns to the office for billing, the draft invoice displays these added entries alongside the quoted lines, allowing the administrator to manually decide whether to bill the customer for the extra work.

On the Professional tier, the software introduces formal variation tracking with dedicated client approvals and progress billing capabilities. This allows the technician or office manager to log the requested additional scope as a distinct variation linked directly to the parent job number. By establishing formal approval records for scope changes before billing, the business ensures that extra tasks are documented, accepted by the client, and billed accurately without accidentally doubling up charges on final invoices.


## Free Plan Practical Ceilings and Test Usage

Axiom Blue offers a permanent free account tier that requires no credit card at signup and operates without any calendar time limits. This tier is designed to give sole traders and curious operators a risk-free environment to evaluate the platform using their own business data rather than pre-populated sample records. It allows a single user to create quotes, schedule jobs, log working hours, manage receivables, maintain a price book, and access mobile interfaces on supported devices.

However, prospective buyers must understand the explicit usage ceilings enforced on the free plan. The account is strictly limited to one user seat and caps operational volume at a maximum of 10 jobs per week, 10 quotes per month, and 10 invoices per month. Additionally, total database records are restricted to 25 customers and 25 leads. These limits mean that any active trade contractor handling regular weekly call-outs will quickly reach the plan's ceiling and must upgrade to maintain operational continuity.

Equally important are the operational capabilities excluded from the free plan. Most notably, the free tier does not include job photo attachments or document uploads, which are standard requirements for contractors needing to document site conditions, electrical safety certificates, or customer signatures. It also excludes accounting software synchronization and variation controls. As a result, the free plan functions best as an extended operational pilot rather than a long-term software home for an active contracting business.


## Subscription Tiers and Graduated Seat Economics

For expanding businesses requiring higher transaction volumes and team access, Axiom Blue provides two primary paid tiers: Basic and Professional. When paid on a month-to-month basis, the Basic plan starts at $29 AUD for the first seat, excluding Goods and Services Tax (GST), while the Professional plan starts at $59 AUD excluding GST. For companies willing to prepay annually, the publisher offers discounted first-seat rates of $240 AUD per year on Basic and $490 AUD per year on Professional, both excluding GST.

Rather than charging flat fees for every additional team member, Axiom Blue employs a graduated seat discounting model for multi-user teams. Under this structure, the second purchased seat receives a 10 percent discount from the first-seat rate, the third seat receives 20 percent off, the fourth receives 30 percent off, the fifth receives 40 percent off, and the sixth and subsequent seats receive a 50 percent discount. This structure helps reduce the incremental cost of onboarding field technicians as a contracting business grows its vehicle fleet.

Buyers must pay close attention to user license administration when managing team seats inside the billing console. The publisher notes that unassigning a staff member from a seat does not automatically reduce the paid subscription quantity; administrators must explicitly adjust their purchased seat count in the billing interface to modify recurring charges. Furthermore, month-to-month plans involve no setup fees or lock-in contracts, giving trade operators flexibility to scale user counts up or down based on seasonal workloads.


## Professional Plan Toolset and Accounting Integration

The Professional tier represents Axiom Blue's full operational platform, introducing depth designed for growing trade businesses that employ multiple crews. In addition to eliminating the weekly job and monthly invoice caps found on the free tier, Professional unlocks project planning tools with task phases and kanban boards, recurring job series for scheduled preventative maintenance, dispatch route optimization, and a visual sales pipeline for tracking incoming customer inquiries from capture forms.

A critical operational distinction of the Professional plan is its connectivity to external accounting and payment systems. It provides two-way synchronization with Xero accounting software, allowing invoices, bills, and customer records to flow seamlessly between field operations and general ledgers without manual double-entry. It also supports digital credit card payments powered by Stripe, enabling contractors to share payment links through a dedicated customer portal and collect funds directly from sent invoices.

However, business owners evaluating the Professional tier must factor in associated external costs and operational requirements. While Axiom Blue includes the software integration hooks, Stripe card processing incurs third-party transaction fees that are billed separately from software subscriptions. Furthermore, contractors must verify that their chart of accounts, tax rates, and inventory codes match Xero configurations to prevent reconciliation errors when invoices and payments synchronize across platforms.


## Mobile Field Operations and Attachment Storage

Field management software must perform reliably in practical environments, including inside service vans, basements, and remote work sites. Axiom Blue provides dedicated native applications for both iOS and Android devices, allowing field technicians to view their daily schedules, inspect site scopes, read customer notes, and record their on-site labour hours directly from their smartphones. Providing mobile access eliminates the reliance on end-of-day memory and reduces administrative friction between the field and the office.

Document and file storage is a critical operational consideration that varies significantly across the subscription tiers. Job photo and document uploads begin on the Basic plan, which allocates 500 MB of pooled cloud storage, while the Professional plan expands total storage to 10 GB. For trade businesses where technicians take dozens of high-resolution site photographs, capture signed compliance certificates, or upload equipment manuals on every visit, storage capacity can be consumed rapidly over months of operation.

Prospective subscribers must evaluate their historical document volume before selecting a plan. The publisher specifies that businesses requiring additional storage beyond the standard 10 GB limit must contact the company directly to discuss custom requirements and applicable charges. A trade business that routinely archives extensive pre-installation and post-installation photographic evidence should monitor cumulative data usage to avoid unexpected storage restrictions during active projects.

### Does testing Axiom Blue require entering credit card details or signing a contract?

### Which specific tools and capabilities require upgrading to the Professional plan?

The Professional plan is required for scope variation tracking, progress billing, recurring job series, multi-phase project management, route optimization, and the visual CRM sales pipeline. It is also required for system integrations, including two-way Xero accounting synchronization and digital credit card processing through Stripe, as well as asset registers and workflow automations.

### Can field workers attach site photos or documents while using the Free plan?

No. While the Free tier permits mobile app access on iOS and Android for checking schedules and entering hours, file uploads are excluded. Job photo and document uploads begin on the Basic plan with 500 MB of pooled storage, while the Professional plan increases document storage to 10 GB.

### How does graduated seat discounting work on paid Axiom Blue subscriptions?

Axiom Blue applies graduated percentage discounts to each additional seat added to a paid plan. The second seat receives a 10 percent discount off the first-seat rate, the third seat receives 20 percent off, the fourth receives 30 percent off, the fifth receives 40 percent off, and the sixth and subsequent seats receive 50 percent off. Basic and Professional first seats start at $29 AUD and $59 AUD per month ex GST respectively.
