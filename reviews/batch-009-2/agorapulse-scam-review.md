# AgoraPulse review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Social media management and reporting  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Agorapulse brings social publishing, a shared inbox, monitoring and reporting into one workspace for individuals and teams. Its US pricing page lists Standard, Professional and Advanced tiers with per-user monthly prices that differ between annual and monthly billing, plus a 30-day trial without a credit card. Network capabilities, profile allowances and API limitations vary, so test the actual post and inbox actions your team depends on.

## Editorial verdict

Agorapulse is worth comparing for teams that want a combined publishing calendar, message handling and reporting process. Its tier structure adds collaboration, moderation, analytics and support capabilities as teams grow. Budget by user and social profile, and verify whether network add-ons apply. A small pilot should include actual posts, comments, approvals and exports before the team makes it the source of truth for social operations.

## Bring the publishing calendar and conversations together

Agorapulse describes a social management workspace that spans publishing, inbox activity, monitoring and reporting. Its calendar can display scheduled, queued, published and assigned content across profiles; CSV bulk upload and shared calendars are described in its publishing material. The inbox is intended to centralize selected incoming messages and comments. This can help a social team see planned work and audience response without switching among as many separate tabs.

A unified calendar does not make every social network behave alike. Supported formats, permissions, tagging, stories, reels and first comments depend on each network’s API. The official help center maintains a network-by-network publishing guide and an API limitations article. Treat those as part of procurement: list your required networks and post formats, then confirm each exact action in the current plan before connecting the main brand accounts.


## Compare plan pricing and team requirements

Agorapulse’s official US pricing page showed Standard at $79 per user monthly on annual billing or $99 monthly; Professional at $119 annually or $149 monthly; and Advanced at $149 annually or $199 monthly. The page advertises a 30-day trial without a credit card. Higher tiers add more teamwork, moderation, report and analytics capabilities. Custom plans are quoted for larger organizations. These are user prices, so estimate seats as well as profiles.

Count the people who draft, approve, publish, answer inbox items and administer access. Add the social profiles and any network-specific extras, including X publishing or inbox capabilities that the help center says require add-ons. Check whether a seasonal contractor needs a paid seat, how many profiles an included seat can handle, and whether profile additions are billed monthly or annually. Ask for the renewal and cancellation terms in writing, especially if your trial includes advanced tools that your eventual plan will not.


## Pilot real posts, inbox work and failure recovery

Use a limited pilot to schedule one example of each important format on every priority channel. Check image cropping, video limits, links, tags, first comments, time zones and the final published rendering. Agorapulse’s help content notes network-specific constraints; for example, some story effects require notification-based publishing and some profiles or actions are unavailable through the API. Record what published automatically and what required a final phone step so campaign owners know where manual work remains.

Test the inbox with a comment, mention and message on each supported network. Confirm what is synchronized, what can be replied to, how assignments work and whether moderation rules can hide, label or route an item. Then simulate token expiry, a failed post and a teammate leaving the company. Verify access removal, recovery alerts, audit trail and duplicate prevention. Keep the native social app available for unsupported actions or urgent moderation.


## Make reports answer a decision, not just fill a dashboard

Agorapulse lists reporting for audience and top content, with advanced reports, advertising analysis, ROI and competitor benchmarking on qualifying tiers. Before buying those capabilities, decide what management needs to learn: which topics earn saves, what creates qualified visits, how response time changes and which campaigns generate outcomes. Define metrics and date ranges consistently and label data sources. Platform metrics are not always directly comparable, and engagement alone is not proof of revenue.

Export a sample report and ask someone who did not configure the platform to interpret it. Check data retention, report branding, attribution windows, profile connection requirements and whether analytics include paid and organic activity. This buyer guide reflects public feature and pricing pages, not hands-on testing or an independent endorsement. Confirm the current plan, exact account permissions and required add-ons with Agorapulse before migrating a live content program.


## Features and decision criteria

- Calendar and post scheduling with network-specific options, shared calendars and approval workflows by plan
- Social inbox for supported comments and messages, with moderation, assignments and automation features on higher tiers
- Audience, content, ads and ROI reporting, export options and competitor benchmarking depending on plan
- Profile, network and API restrictions apply; X publishing and inbox access require specific add-ons according to current help pages

## Pricing considerations

The official US pricing page shows annual and monthly billing controls and a 30-day free trial without a credit card. Prices are per user monthly; profile add-ons, selected networks and taxes can affect total cost. Verify the live checkout, add-on requirements, trial end behavior and feature matrix for each connected network before committing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Agorapulse cost?

The official US pricing page lists Standard at $79 per user monthly with annual billing or $99 monthly, Professional at $119 annually or $149 monthly, and Advanced at $149 annually or $199 monthly. Profile add-ons and custom plans may change the total.

### Can Agorapulse publish to every social network in the same way?

No. Agorapulse documents different publishing formats and API restrictions by network. Some actions may require a mobile notification or an add-on, so verify the exact post types and inbox actions you use.

### Does Agorapulse include a free trial?

Its pricing page advertises a 30-day trial without a credit card. Confirm the trial’s enabled plan features and whether any profile or usage limits apply when you register.
