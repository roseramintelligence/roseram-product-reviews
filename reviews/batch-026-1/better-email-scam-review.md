# Better Email review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Collaborative email creation and design systems  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Better Email helps marketing teams build reusable email design systems, assemble campaigns, review rendering and export finished HTML to an email service provider. Its editor includes brand guardrails, collaboration, approval and preflight checks; the vendor’s public pricing page says plans are custom-quoted based on organization size, email volume and integration needs. Better Email is the creation workflow, while the connected ESP sends the campaign.

## Editorial verdict

Better Email is aimed at organizations with regular email volume, several marketers or brands, and a need for design control across campaigns. A modular design system may reduce repeated design handoffs, while preview and approval tools support review before export. It is likely more than a low-volume or solo sender needs. Evaluate how it connects to your ESP, who owns the modules and approvals, and what your campaign content and AI prompts send to third-party providers.

## Create campaigns from a governed design system

Better Email positions itself as a collaborative email-creation platform for teams that want brand rules embedded in reusable modules. Designers can define what marketers may edit and which components are fixed; campaign authors then assemble content in a visual editor. The product pages describe live collaboration, review comments, approval stages, version history and export integrations. This approach may help organizations where every campaign currently waits on a designer or where inconsistent templates create repeated QA work.

Document how the system will be owned: who creates and approves a module, who may edit content, who controls brand tokens, and which changes require a second reviewer. Keep the design system narrow enough for editors to use reliably but flexible enough for legitimate campaign needs. Pilot one campaign type and record exceptions before migrating every brand or business unit.


## Understand rendering checks and the ESP handoff

Better Email’s public materials describe previews across email clients and preflight checks before export. These can help find layout differences, broken links, missing text and other issues, but the final sending environment still matters. Confirm that exported HTML preserves merge tags, conditional content, tracking parameters, unsubscribe links and personalization syntax in your ESP. Test in the actual sending platform with sample data and seed inboxes, including mobile and dark-mode views.

The roles of the systems should be explicit: Better Email is the design, collaboration and review layer; the ESP manages recipient lists, consent, suppression, delivery and campaign sending. Check the exact connector for your ESP, what data flows in each direction and how failures are reported. If a connector is unavailable, test HTML export, naming, source control and the manual handoff before committing to a production migration.


## Review AI, privacy and third-party processing

Better Email’s security and terms pages describe optional AI assistance and email-client previews that can involve third-party providers in the United States. The vendor states that customer content is not used to train those models and describes retention limits and transfer safeguards. Settings and provider arrangements can change, so confirm the current subprocessor list, data locations, retention, access controls and workspace-level controls with your privacy team. Test disabling AI and previews if a campaign contains sensitive information.

Do not paste personal data, confidential pricing or unreleased strategy into an AI prompt unless the organization has approved the processing arrangement. Decide which users may access Betty or similar features and require a human to review generated content for accuracy, claims and brand fit. Keep a clear audit trail of who approved a campaign and ensure the ESP still enforces lawful marketing preferences.


## Scope a quote and measure operational value

The official pricing page offers custom pricing based on organization size, email volume and integration needs. Request a quote that separates seats, workspaces, brands, campaign throughput, implementation, training, premium support, AI usage, client previews and contract term. Ask about annual renewal adjustments, data export, deletion, uptime commitments and whether specific ESP integrations carry fees. Compare the quote with current design, production and QA costs rather than counting only subscription expense.

Measure campaign production time, designer workload, revision loops, accessibility defects, rendering issues and approval delays before and after a bounded pilot. Attribute published customer results to the vendor and do not assume the same savings will occur for your team. This buyer guide summarizes Better Email’s public product, pricing, terms and security pages; it is not a hands-on test or a guarantee of campaign results.


## Features and decision criteria

- Brand-controlled design systems with reusable modules, editing zones and campaign assembly
- Real-time collaboration, feedback, permissions, approvals, version history and AI-assisted editing according to current product pages
- Email client previews and preflight checks for rendering, links, accessibility and placeholder content
- Exports campaign content to a separate ESP; optional AI and preview processing may involve third-party providers and regional data transfers

## Pricing considerations

Better Email requires a custom proposal on the official pricing page. Request an order form stating user and workspace limits, campaign volume, integrations, implementation, support, AI and preview entitlements, contract term and renewal cap. Review the terms and security page for data handling, retention, service availability and third-party processing details.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Better Email send marketing campaigns?

No. Better Email says its team creates and reviews campaigns in the platform, then exports finished content to the connected ESP, which handles sending.

### How much does Better Email cost?

The official pricing page says plans are custom-quoted based on organization size, email volume and integrations. Request an itemized proposal for the use case and team size.

### Where does email content go when AI or previews are used?

Better Email’s security page says optional AI assistance and client previews can send selected content to providers in the United States under contractual safeguards; AI can be disabled per workspace or configured with a customer provider and region. Verify current subprocessors and settings before using confidential content.
