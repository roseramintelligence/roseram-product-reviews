# BeProfit review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-18  
**Category:** Ecommerce profit analytics and attribution software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

BeProfit is an ecommerce profit analytics app that combines order, product, shipping, ad and payment information into dashboards and reports. Its current Shopify App Store listing shows a free tier with 150 monthly orders and six months of history, followed by paid tiers that vary in price, order allowance, history and reporting. It requests access to store, customer and Shopify Payments data, so buyers should review the requested permissions and privacy terms. Paid plans auto-renew under BeProfit’s terms, and the company says trial access converts to paid unless canceled before the trial ends.

## Editorial verdict

The reviewed public materials do not substantiate a scam allegation. BeProfit publishes plan allowances, app permissions, subscription renewal and cancellation terms. A profit dashboard is only as reliable as cost, shipping, advertising and refund data connected to it; validate calculations against source records before making budget decisions. Test the order cap and billing flow in a controlled store, and review the app’s broad customer, order and Shopify Payments data access before installation.

## BeProfit SCAM review: a directory label, not a fraud finding

“SCAM Review” is the directory’s label and not a finding that BeProfit is fraudulent. This source-based buyer review examines the app’s published store analytics features, plan limits, subscription terms and Shopify data permissions. No BeProfit account was connected to a live store and no profit report was independently recalculated. That distinction matters: a dashboard can be useful, but its accuracy must be checked against the store’s underlying records.

BeProfit positions itself as a profit and performance analytics app for ecommerce merchants. The product combines order and product information with shipping, advertising and payment data, then presents reports intended to help a store understand margin and channel performance. Its usefulness depends on reliable integrations and complete cost inputs; the software does not itself guarantee that the modeled profit matches a merchant’s books.


## BeProfit product scope: beyond top-line revenue

The Shopify listing describes dashboards for contribution profit, product and order metrics, shipping, refunds, advertising, payment costs and marketing attribution. It lists connections including Shopify, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Klaviyo, ShipStation and UPS, and features such as lifetime value, cohorts, ROAS and POAS. This broad view can help merchants inspect how a sale’s costs accumulate across systems.

The analytics should be treated as a management view, not automatically as a reconciled accounting ledger. Check whether costs are imported at the right currency, date, order and campaign level. Confirm how discounts, returns, shipping adjustments, taxes, payment fees and ad spend are attributed. Compare a sample of displayed orders to the original store, carrier and processor records before using a report to raise or cut budgets.


## BeProfit pricing and plan-specific order limits

The Shopify App Store listing observed for this review shows a Free tier with one shop, 150 orders per month and six months of data history. It displays Basic at $29 monthly for 450 orders, Pro at $99 for 900 orders and Scale at $149 for 1,700 orders; feature access and historical data vary by tier. The page lists a $0.30 charge for extra orders on paid plans, capped at $500 per month in the displayed plan details.

Annual billing may have different allowances and prices: the listing also shows annual options and some paid annual plans with higher or unlimited order limits. Because the plan material can differ by locale or account, do not extrapolate monthly details to an annual quote. Count the store’s expected order volume, including seasonal peaks and test orders, and confirm the actual plan screen before installation.


## Free tier, paid trial and renewal timing

The App Store listing says a free plan is available and that paid tiers offer a 14-day trial. BeProfit’s Terms say subscriptions renew automatically and that a paid subscription can convert after the trial unless it is canceled in time. The terms describe cancellation as taking effect at the end of the current paid period and state that paid subscription fees are non-refundable. Where a subscription is managed through Shopify, the merchant should also inspect the billing and cancellation flow shown by Shopify.

Make the decision before a trial end date, and have a named store administrator responsible for confirming whether the app remains installed and billable. Read the order summary and recurring charge amount, including the order allowance and any usage-based extra-order fees. If the service is no longer needed, use the platform’s subscription controls and verify the cancellation confirmation rather than assuming uninstalling an app alone stops every billing obligation.


## Order caps and extra-order billing

BeProfit’s plan page distinguishes order counts by tier and says the Basic, Pro and Scale monthly options charge $0.30 for each extra order, subject to a stated monthly maximum. The listing also describes different order allowances on some annual plans. A merchant’s bill can therefore depend on its plan cadence and on the number of counted orders, not just the month’s baseline subscription price.

Ask which store events are counted as an order, how cancellations and refunds affect the meter, when the cap resets, what happens at the cap, and whether spending is automatically allowed. The public listing suggests a maximum charge for the displayed monthly extra-order feature but the account’s actual settings control how billing is applied. Set an alert and test a small number of orders before making the report critical to daily operations.


## Shopify permissions include sensitive store information

The Shopify App Store’s data-access disclosure says BeProfit requests access to customer data, including names, email and physical addresses, as well as device and activity data. It also lists access to the store owner’s details, products, inventory, collections, all order history and shipping information, and Shopify Payments account and payout data. This breadth is consistent with a profit analytics tool that combines sales and cost information, but the merchant should review it deliberately.

Install only from the intended Shopify store and check the permission prompt before approving. Consider whether staff and finance owners should be notified that the app can read payout information and customer details. Review the app’s privacy policy and the store’s own data-retention requirements. If a permission appears broader than the chosen use case, ask the vendor to explain it before connecting live customer records.


## Privacy, data retention and offboarding

BeProfit’s Privacy Policy describes data it collects, use of analytics providers and a process for requesting personal-data deletion. It says data may be retained while necessary to provide services, meet legal or business requirements, resolve disputes and enforce terms. The policy also notes that anonymized statistics may remain. A merchant should read the current policy alongside the Shopify app permission screen and its contract.

When uninstalling or closing an account, identify what data is held by BeProfit, what remains in the Shopify store, and what reports the business needs to retain. Export necessary reports and reconcile key numbers before disconnecting. If a deletion request is required, use the vendor’s stated contact route and preserve the response. App removal should not be assumed to instantly delete all copies, backups or legally retained records.


## Data synchronization and integration checks

The product relies on connecting multiple systems, so timing and field mapping matter. BeProfit advertises data synchronization and real-time refresh on selected plans, but a connected source may still have its own delay, permission changes, token expiration or rate limit. A missing ad account or carrier cost can produce an incomplete profit picture even if the dashboard loads successfully.

Connect one store and a small number of sources first. Check how a known order appears, whether the correct currency and shipping cost are shown, and how refunds flow into the calculation. Compare ad spend against the relevant Google or Meta account for the same period and timezone. Document the source of every cost field and investigate discrepancies before presenting the report as company-wide profit.


## Profit, ROAS and attribution should not be confused

BeProfit presents gross and net profit measures alongside ROAS, POAS, customer lifetime value and attribution. Revenue-based ROAS and profit-based metrics answer different questions. A campaign that produces high revenue may still have low contribution after product, shipping, discount and payment costs. Attribution also depends on how orders and marketing interactions are matched across platforms.

Set a written definition for each metric before comparing campaigns. Confirm whether the dashboard uses gross sales, net sales or collected revenue, and what time window applies to ad clicks and conversions. Do not treat an attribution result as causal proof that a particular channel created every order. Use the report as one input alongside platform analytics, accounting records and controlled experiments.


## User feedback and marketplace signals

The Shopify App Store listing shows merchant reviews and a rating at the time of lookup, and includes both favorable comments and a visible complaint about difficulty resolving a subscription cancellation. The listed developer response directs the reviewer to support. These are individual marketplace reports, not independently verified outcomes or evidence that the same problem affects every user.

A buyer can use marketplace feedback to formulate questions: how to cancel a trial, how a billing issue is escalated, and what response time to expect. Read recent comments directly and check whether the review refers to the current plan version. Avoid treating an aggregate rating or one complaint as conclusive proof of quality, reliability or fraud.


## Alternatives and a fair tool comparison

Compare BeProfit with a store platform’s built-in reports, ecommerce accounting tools, spreadsheet-based contribution analysis and other profit analytics apps. Use the same orders, refund window, product costs, shipping invoices, ad accounts and payment-fee sources. Include the work needed to maintain integrations and correct product costs. A free tier may look economical but can be a poor fit if its order cap or reporting history is too limited.

BeProfit may fit a merchant that wants multiple channels summarized in one dashboard and can supply accurate cost data. A business that needs formal financial statements, tax accounting or independently reconciled figures should retain its accounting system as the source of truth. The best comparison is a reconciliation exercise on a sample period, not a marketing claim that analytics are “real time.”


## Who should consider BeProfit

A Shopify merchant with multiple sales and advertising channels may value an app that brings orders, product costs and marketing information into one view. A free tier offers a way to inspect the baseline workflow, while paid tiers add reporting and order capacity. The app may be especially useful when a team has clear owners for product costs, shipping and ad account connections.

It may be a poor fit if the merchant cannot authorize access to customer, order and Shopify Payments information, or if the store’s cost data is not maintained. A low-volume shop may not need a paid analytics layer; a high-volume operation should verify order limits, overages and annual terms. A financial dashboard does not replace accounting review or guarantee better business decisions.


## BeProfit buyer checklist before installing

Confirm the free or paid tier, order allowance, order definition, plan cadence, data-history window, additional-order price and cap, trial period, renewal date and cancellation route. Read the Shopify permission prompt and decide whether access to customer, order, shipping and payments data is justified. Review the privacy policy and identify who can export or delete connected data.

During a trial, reconcile a sample period against store, payment, shipping and ad-platform records. Check currencies, refunds, cost of goods, discounting and attribution windows. Ask support how overages and failed data syncs are handled. Store the vendor’s current pricing and permission screenshots with the purchase record so the business can later verify what was authorized.


## BeProfit SCAM review verdict: useful profit reporting requires careful data and billing checks

The official app listing and vendor terms describe ecommerce analytics, plan-specific order limits, extra-order fees, broad Shopify permissions, automatic renewal and cancellation terms. The reviewed sources do not substantiate a scam allegation. They do show why a buyer should test both the math and the meter: a dashboard depends on connected costs, while the subscription can depend on monthly orders and trial timing.

BeProfit is best evaluated with a controlled reconciliation of real store records and a review of the data access it requests. ## Features and decision criteria

- Profit and expense dashboards for orders, products and marketing costs
- Connections to ecommerce stores, advertising, shipping and payment services
- Plan-specific monthly order caps, history windows and report features
- Shopify app permissions that include customer, order and payments data

## Pricing considerations

The Shopify App Store listing displayed these US-dollar plan prices and limits when checked November 18, 2026. It also shows annual payment options; some annual plans list higher or unlimited order allowances. The same listing and the vendor terms may not use identical plan labels across every locale or account. Confirm the exact plan, order cap, overage, annual terms, tax and renewal total in the store’s installation flow.
