# Superlist review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Personal and team task lists  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Superlist combines tasks, notes and shared plans across desktop, mobile and web. Its pricing page specifies limits for lists, collaborators, uploads and storage on Free, with paid tiers adding capacity, integrations and AI features. Select a plan based on how many shared lists and files a group actually needs.

## Editorial verdict

Superlist is a straightforward option to test for personal lists and small shared projects that benefit from notes alongside tasks. The free plan limits shared lists, people and storage; check those caps before inviting a larger team or uploading project files.

## Keep task context beside the next step

Create a small project with a note, a few subtasks and an owner for each action. Check whether teammates can understand why a task exists without looking through a separate chat. A lightweight list works best when the next step is obvious.

Add a recurring personal item and one task shared with another person. Confirm reminders and ownership on the devices the group uses. This review did not create a Superlist account or test notifications.


## Count lists, people and uploads together

The free plan limits private and shared lists to five and shared-list participation to five people, while showing 500 MB storage. Estimate the whole household or team, not only the owner’s personal lists. Files and shared projects can use capacity sooner than task titles.

Basic and Super use per-person annual pricing and expand different capabilities. Calculate the seat total and identify whether AI meeting notes or integrations justify the higher tier.


## Decide how much AI belongs in the workflow

The publisher lists voice input and AI meeting features across paid tiers. Trial a low-risk meeting or personal note first and review the generated action items before sharing them. A summary can omit nuance or assign responsibility incorrectly.

For meeting content, confirm participant expectations and the applicable recording rules. This review does not audit data processing or test transcript quality. Use only connections and features approved for the information involved.


## Keep a path to your existing work

Before moving a project, check what export and integration options are available in the current plan. Test a small list with due dates, notes and attachments, then verify the destination retains enough context. Migration is complete only when the receiving tool can still support the workflow.

Superlist may suit users who want a calm shared list rather than a complex project suite. This review uses the official product and pricing pages; it does not benchmark reliability or prove every integration for a user’s configuration.


## Features and decision criteria

- Tasks with notes and subtasks
- Shared lists and assigned next steps
- Mac, iOS, Android and web apps
- Paid integrations and AI meeting features

## Pricing considerations

The page lists yearly billing and a 20% saving against monthly pricing. Verify the current monthly price and trial or cancellation terms at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are lists and tasks unlimited on Free?

Tasks and notes are listed as unlimited, but the plan caps private and shared lists at five and shared-list participants at five.

### Does Basic include AI meeting notes?

The pricing page assigns unlimited AI meeting notes to the Super tier.

### Is Superlist available on Android and web?

The publisher lists Android and web alongside Mac and iOS.
