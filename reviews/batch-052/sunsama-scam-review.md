# Sunsama review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Daily planning and task management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Sunsama combines daily planning, tasks and a calendar, with connections to email and work tools. The publisher offers a 14-day trial without a credit card and lists annual and monthly subscription prices. Its daily-planning approach asks the user to shape a realistic day rather than just collect tasks.

## Editorial verdict

Sunsama is worth trying for professionals who want a deliberate daily planning ritual with calendar context. It has no free-forever plan, so evaluate whether the routine earns the subscription during the trial and note the renewal amount before signing up.

## Try the daily ritual, not only the interface

Use a few ordinary workdays to list priorities, check the calendar and choose a realistic workload. At the end of each day, review what moved and what did not. The repeated planning habit is part of the product experience and should feel sustainable.

Sunsama describes a guided daily workflow and connected tools. Compare that ritual with the way the person currently starts and ends work. This review did not connect a calendar or complete the trial.


## Protect the calendar as a constraint

A task list can grow without limit; the calendar shows the time available. Try a day with meetings and one unexpected change. A useful plan should make overload visible and leave space for real interruptions.

Check how tasks flow from connected tools and whether edits sync back to the original service. Keep the source of truth clear so an adjusted time in one view does not create a second conflicting commitment.


## Decide if the subscription earns its cost

The current price is US$22 per person each month or US$17 per person per month with annual billing. The two-week trial needs no credit card according to the publisher. For a team, multiply by the number of people and confirm the prorated charge when inviting colleagues.

Before subscribing, write down what would make the daily ritual worth continuing: a clearer plan, fewer forgotten tasks or an easier review. Check whether that outcome appears during ordinary workdays, not only in a carefully prepared demo.


## Review connected information before enabling AI

The product page lists integrations and AI-related options. Identify which calendar, email or task data becomes available to each connection and whether company policy permits it. Start with the minimum access needed for a useful trial.

This assessment relies on publisher descriptions and proposes a personal evaluation. It does not test integrations, security, AI output or claimed wellbeing outcomes. Confirm the current privacy and trial terms for the account and region.


## Features and decision criteria

- Daily and weekly planning
- Task and calendar views
- Connected work tools
- AI and MCP integrations listed by publisher

## Pricing considerations

The publisher states that no credit card is required for the two-week trial. Review current trial conversion and cancellation details in the account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Sunsama have a free-forever plan?

The publisher says it does not; the current offer is a 14-day trial.

### Can a team invite change the bill?

Yes. The pricing FAQ says each added colleague is billed per person, with prorating for a mid-month addition.

### Does Sunsama replace every task or calendar system?

Not necessarily. Confirm integrations and the system that remains the source of truth for each type of work.
