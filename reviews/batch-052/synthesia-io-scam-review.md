# Synthesia.io review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** AI video creation and localization  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Synthesia is an AI video creation platform centered on avatars, voice, dubbing and studio video. Its current plan pages use credits across several creation features, with plan differences for downloads, watermark removal, seats and collaboration. A free Basic tier is described for trying the editor.

## Editorial verdict

Synthesia is worth evaluating when a team needs repeatable presenter-led training or explainer videos without recording each version in a studio. The best fit depends on how much editing, avatar realism, localization and brand control the workflow requires. Credit usage and export conditions should be tested against a representative script before adopting it for a recurring production pipeline.

## Test a real script before selecting a plan

Prepare a two-minute training script with a name, product term and a sentence that needs emphasis. Generate a short sample and inspect pronunciation, pacing, gestures, captions, transitions and any mismatch between the spoken line and on-screen text. A convincing sample is more informative than a gallery of unrelated demos.

Have a colleague review the video on a phone with sound muted and with sound on. Check whether the message is clear, captions are legible and the presenter feels appropriate for the audience. This review proposes an evaluation method; Roseram did not create a Synthesia account or run a video-generation test.


## Budget using credits and renewal rates

Synthesia now describes credits as a shared pool for AI features, rather than one simple allowance for minutes of video. A creation with avatar, stock footage, motion graphics or dubbing can consume credits differently. Estimate a monthly workload using the current calculator or a sample project, then leave room for revisions and localization.

The pricing page may display introductory offers alongside the regular recurring rate. Record the amount that renews, whether annual billing is charged upfront, what happens to unused credits and the included number of editors or guests. A first-month discount is not the ongoing production budget.


## Check download, watermark and sharing rights

The Basic tier is presented for exploring the editor and carries a Synthesia watermark. Paid plans add downloads and removal of that mark, while other rights and controls vary. Decide whether a share link is sufficient or whether the team must download final files for its learning system, customer portal or internal archive.

Before publishing a generated spokesperson, review the platform’s current policies, consent terms and any applicable rules for synthetic media disclosure. Avoid uploading confidential scripts, personal data or a real person’s likeness until the organization has confirmed the rights and account settings that apply.


## Separate a useful production aid from a complete studio

A presenter generator can accelerate localized explainers and internal training, but it does not automatically solve instructional design, fact checking, accessibility, brand approval or storyboarding. Assign an owner to review the script, caption timing, visual assets and final claims before release. Keep original project files and source material under the team’s control.

Synthesia’s published materials are the basis of this assessment. It is not a hands-on quality benchmark, security audit or guarantee of audience engagement. Confirm the current model, voice, usage and licensing conditions in your own account before moving a production workflow.


## Features and decision criteria

- AI-presenter and avatar-led studio video
- Text-to-speech and video dubbing workflows
- Reusable templates, roleplays and surveys
- Credit-based usage with plan-dependent export and collaboration features

## Pricing considerations

The live pricing page can show first-month offers and plan changes. Confirm regular renewal price, credit rollover or expiration, export rights, seats and watermark rules at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you export an unwatermarked video on the free plan?

The pricing page states that Basic plan videos carry a Synthesia watermark; review current plan terms for export rights.

### Do credits only apply to video rendering?

No. Synthesia describes credits as a shared allowance across usage-based features; dubbing, surveys and other tools may use them.

### Will a paid plan include unlimited generation?

Not for every self-serve plan. Compare the credit allowance and Enterprise terms with your planned monthly volume.
