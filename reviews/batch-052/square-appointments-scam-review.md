# Square Appointments review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Appointment scheduling and payments for service businesses  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Square Appointments combines online booking, staff calendars, reminders and payments for service businesses. Square’s US page lists a free plan at $0 per location, Square Plus at $49 per location per month and Premium at $149 per location per month. Card processing rates vary by plan and channel. Higher tiers add tools such as multi-staff booking, waitlists, marketing, resource management and expanded reporting.

## Editorial verdict

Square Appointments may suit a solo professional or service business that wants booking and payments connected to Square. The free tier is positioned for solo operators, while Plus and Premium add features for growing teams and larger salons. Compare the total per-location subscription and payment fees with your appointment volume, staff count and cancellation policy. Test the booking flow, reminders, deposits, rescheduling and payout reconciliation before moving clients onto it.

## See how Square Appointments fits your service business

Square positions Appointments as online scheduling connected to payments and other Square business tools. Its US page describes booking pages, integrations, appointment reminders, calendars and invoicing, with advanced features for teams such as multi-staff bookings, waitlists and marketing. This combination may be useful for salons, wellness providers, consultants and other service businesses that want clients to book and pay through one system.

Map the client journey from finding a booking page through choosing a service, selecting a staff member, paying a deposit, receiving a reminder, changing an appointment and completing payment. Test what clients see on a phone and what staff see in the calendar. If the business has multiple rooms, equipment, locations or service durations, verify resource availability and buffer times before announcing online booking.


## Compare per-location plans and payment costs

Square’s current US Appointments page lists Free at $0 per month per location, Plus at $49 per location and Premium at $149 per location. The page shows different rates for card-present and online payments. Free lists 2.6% + 15¢ in person and 3.3% + 30¢ online; Plus lists 2.5% + 15¢ and 2.9% + 30¢; Premium lists 2.4% + 15¢ and 2.9% + 30¢. Confirm the current rates and how each transaction type is classified.

Calculate monthly cost from both subscriptions and payment volume. Use your actual average ticket, online booking deposit, no-show rate and number of locations. A higher plan may reduce per-transaction fees or add tools, but it only pays off if those changes match your real business volume. Square also offers custom pricing to eligible higher-volume businesses; ask for a written proposal if your annual processing exceeds the page’s stated threshold.


## Check staff calendars, cancellation and no-show rules

Square says its free plan supports unlimited calendars and lists multi-staff booking, waitlists, cancellation policy and no-show fees among Plus features. Determine whether the free calendar setup can represent your service durations and staff availability or whether team scheduling requires an upgrade. Test a client rescheduling a booking, a staff member becoming unavailable and a waitlisted client receiving an opening.

Set cancellation and deposit policies that clients can understand before booking. Confirm when a card is charged, how refunds are handled, what notices are sent and how the policy is displayed in the booking flow. Ensure staff know when they can override a fee and that the final setup complies with applicable consumer and payment rules.


## Pilot reminders, payments and reconciliation

Square describes automated confirmations, reminders and rescheduling tools. Test the message timing, channel, sender identity, timezone, opt-out behavior and delivery for different appointment types. Avoid sending marketing content through transactional reminders unless clients have consented and the message meets applicable rules. Keep a manual process available if a client does not receive a notice or a booking fails to sync.

Run sample transactions for in-person and online payments, deposits, refunds and no-show fees. Reconcile the appointment record, receipt, processing fee, refund and bank payout with Square reports and your accounting workflow. Verify hardware needs and supported countries if clients pay at the desk. A smooth booking page is not enough if staff cannot reliably close the day or explain a charge.


## Who should consider Square Appointments?

A solo service professional who wants online booking and payment management may find the free tier a low-cost way to test the workflow. A multi-staff salon or appointment business may value Plus or Premium features if the per-location subscription and processing economics work. Businesses using another POS or scheduling platform should compare data migration, customer history, gift cards, deposits and refund handling before switching.

This buyer guide summarizes Square’s public US Appointments and payments pages. It is not a hands-on test of a Square account or independent verification of payment processing. Confirm current plan details, payment fees, hardware availability and booking policies directly with Square before migrating client appointments.


## Features and decision criteria

- Online booking page and integrations, appointment reminders, calendars and invoicing described for the free plan
- Plus adds lower listed processing rates, cancellation and no-show fees, multi-staff booking, waitlists and email/text marketing
- Premium adds lower listed in-person processing rates, 24/7 support, future bookings reporting, service-cost tracking and resource management
- Square connects appointment scheduling with its payment and customer-management products

## Pricing considerations

US pricing and processing fees listed on Square’s current Appointments page when checked. Confirm eligibility, location count, card-present versus online transaction classification, hardware, taxes and negotiated terms before use; rates and plans may vary by market.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Square Appointments have a free plan?

Square’s US page lists Square Free at $0 per month per location for solo professionals, with online booking, reminders, unlimited calendars and invoicing among its featured capabilities.

### What does Square Plus add?

The official page lists Plus at $49 per month per location. It adds features including multi-staff appointment booking, waitlists, cancellation and no-show fees and email/text marketing, alongside lower listed processing rates.

### How much are Square Appointments payment fees?

The US page lists plan-specific rates by channel: Free shows 2.6% + 15¢ in person and 3.3% + 30¢ online; Plus and Premium show different card-present and online rates. Verify how a transaction is classified and the terms for your account.
