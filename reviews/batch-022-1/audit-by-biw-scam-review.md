# Audit by BIW review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** SEO, GEO and technical website auditing tools  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Audit by BIW is a French-language web audit service offering a one-page SEO and AI-search-readiness audit, multi-page site crawls, competitor comparisons and smaller technical checks. Its pages describe checks across content, metadata, indexing, structured data, performance, mobile, accessibility, security headers and HTML/CSS validity. The service uses credits without an ongoing subscription: a one-page audit costs 15 credits, Crawl Site and Competitor VS cost 10 per analyzed URL, and paid packs start at €10 including tax. The provider says audit costs appear before launch.

## Editorial verdict

Audit by BIW may help a site owner triage on-page, technical and crawl issues when they want an explainable report rather than a single opaque score. It is especially relevant for teams comfortable working with a French-language interface and credit-based billing. Treat its GEO indicator as a readiness checklist, not proof that an AI assistant will cite a page; the provider itself says it does not measure actual citations. Pair reports with Search Console, analytics and manual review.

## A set of audits for page, site and competitor questions

Audit by BIW organizes its service around three analysis types: Audit 1 page for one URL, Crawl Site for multiple pages and Concurrent VS for comparing competitor pages. The product page says an individual audit combines technical and editorial checks, while the crawl looks for repeated patterns such as duplicate titles, internal-link structure and pages that are difficult to reach. Smaller tools address narrower checks like sitemap files, metadata, redirects and indexability.

This structure can help a site owner begin with a focused page and expand only when the question requires a wider crawl. Before launching, decide whether you need a page-level diagnosis, repeated issue detection across a site, or competitive content analysis. Each option consumes credits differently, so define the page set first and compare the displayed cost with the scope of the question.


## Interpret SEO and GEO indicators as diagnostic signals

The service describes checks for titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, structured data, performance, mobile presentation, security headers, accessibility and code validity. Its GEO analysis looks for signals that may make content easier for AI systems to understand, such as clear answers, subject coverage, sources and structure. These are diagnostic indicators; they are not proof of ranking, traffic growth or inclusion in an AI response.

Audit by BIW states that its GEO index does not measure whether AI assistants actually cite a page. The vendor also notes that a valid schema block does not guarantee a rich search result and that automated accessibility checks need human evaluation. That framing is useful: treat scores as a checklist for inspection, then compare the results with Google Search Console, analytics and real user behavior.


## Understand the credit model and per-page cost

The official pricing page says Audit 1 page costs 15 credits per URL, Crawl Site and Competitor VS cost 10 credits per analyzed URL, Google crawl costs 5 credits per sitemap URL and most mini-tools cost 2 credits. It describes 20 free credits each month and paid one-time packs beginning at €10 including tax for 300 credits. The page states paid packs do not renew automatically, and costs are displayed before a tool starts.

A multi-page crawl can use credits quickly because the price scales with the number of analyzed URLs. Standard accounts are described as limited to five pages per crawl, subject to available balance. Estimate your test set and check whether crawling a sitemap or a smaller handpicked set answers your question at lower cost. Verify the current plan and balance rules before purchase, especially if using shared or professional accounts.


## Check report privacy and public sharing

Audit by BIW says reports are private by default and appear in the public gallery only after an explicit sharing action. Its public-reports page explains that authors voluntarily publish reports, that the gallery is limited to recent examples and that personal account actions remain private. This is a useful control for agencies or site owners handling client URLs, but confirm the privacy policy and account terms for the exact report data collected.

If you share a report publicly, inspect it for client names, private URLs, query parameters, internal page paths or analytics details first. Use a test domain or remove identifying data when evaluating the tool. If you need to withdraw or correct a public report, the service describes a contact process for correction, anonymization or removal.


## Who may find Audit by BIW useful?

The tool may suit French-speaking site owners, freelancers or agencies who need structured on-page and technical checks with costs shown before use. The per-credit model can be manageable for occasional audits, but ongoing large crawls may make the page-based total important to estimate. Teams that require an English interface, automated rank tracking, backlink analysis or verified AI citation monitoring should confirm availability or use a separate specialized service.

This guide summarizes the provider’s public pages; it is not an independent test of report accuracy, ranking improvements or AI citation outcomes. Audit scores help organize work, but a higher score alone does not guarantee more traffic, leads or search visibility.

## Frequently asked questions

### What audits can Audit by BIW run?

The site describes a single-URL audit, multi-page site crawl, competitor comparison and smaller tools for technical checks such as redirects, metadata and sitemaps.

### Does the GEO score guarantee AI search citations?

No. The provider says the GEO indicator evaluates page readiness signals and does not measure actual citations in AI assistant responses.

### Does Audit by BIW require a monthly subscription?

The pricing page describes free monthly credits and paid credit packs as one-time purchases without automatic renewal.
