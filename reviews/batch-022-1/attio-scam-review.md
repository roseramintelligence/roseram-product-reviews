# Attio review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** AI-assisted CRM  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Attio is a flexible CRM that builds records from email and calendar context and adds workflows, data enrichment, AI agents, reporting and a Model Context Protocol server. Plans range from a free workspace to paid per-user tiers and an annual enterprise-oriented plan with advanced governance.

## Editorial verdict

Attio is a compelling option for teams that want a configurable CRM with AI and workflow features, but the credit system, record caps and seat pricing deserve close attention. Its free tier is generous enough to test a small workflow, while paid tiers become more appropriate for structured team permissions, sequences and reporting. Model the total monthly cost with additional seats and AI credits before migrating the company’s relationship data.

## Build the CRM model around the team’s real records

Attio presents a flexible data model with people, companies, objects and records, and can use email or calendar activity to help create context. That flexibility is useful when a startup needs to shape its own sales process instead of adopting a fixed pipeline. It also creates a design responsibility: teams must agree on field names, ownership and lifecycle stages.

Start with a small model for accounts, contacts and opportunities. Test how email and calendar connections create or update records, then set rules for duplicates and shared relationships. A clean schema makes reports and automation easier to trust than a large collection of loosely defined fields.


## Price seats and AI credits as separate inputs

Attio’s page shows a Free tier, Plus and Pro workspace prices with one included user, and a larger Ultra workspace with five included users. Each plan has distinct included credits and record limits. Additional users and credit packs add costs, so the visible base plan is not necessarily the total price for the team.

List expected active seats and the AI features each group will use. Estimate how often agents, enrichment, summaries and workflows will consume credits, then compare the plan allowance with actual usage during a trial. The vendor publishes additional-credit packs, but unused quota rules and exact consumption should be confirmed in current documentation.


## Test data access before connecting AI workflows

The plan page lists Attio’s MCP server on every plan and SQL access through MCP on Ultra. Connecting a CRM to an AI client can make relationship data easier to query, but it also expands the set of systems that can access sensitive records. Decide which users and agents can access each object before enabling integrations.

Use a sandbox dataset and least-privilege credentials. Test what the connector can read or change, keep an audit trail and prohibit sending sensitive fields to an unapproved model. Review the vendor’s data, retention and AI processing terms before connecting real customer records.


## Plan migration, governance and exit criteria

Attio lists import paths from common CRMs and spreadsheet files and offers migration assistance on paid tiers. A migration can still require mapping, deduplication and validation. Import a sample first, confirm historical activity and associations, and compare counts before moving the complete database.

Set a short evaluation with measurable checks: user adoption, record accuracy, useful automation, report correctness and total credit cost. For larger organizations, review SSO, provisioning, audit logs and administrative controls on the appropriate tier. This review is based on the vendor’s current public materials, not an independent integration test.


## Features and decision criteria

- Records built from email and calendar activity
- Agents, workflows, email sequences and reporting vary by plan
- AI usage is measured with seat and workspace credits
- MCP integration is listed for all plans, with SQL access on Ultra

## Pricing considerations

The price page displays workspace and per-user components, annual discounts, included credits, record limits and extra-credit packs. Confirm the selected billing toggle, included seats, taxes, quota behavior and AI feature limits before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a three-person team use Attio for free?

The pricing page lists the Free plan for up to three users, with record, object and workspace-credit limits.

### Are AI features unlimited in the free or paid plans?

No. Attio lists plan-specific seat and workspace credits and sells additional workspace packs.

### Does every plan include the MCP server?

The current plan page says every plan includes Attio’s MCP server; Ultra adds SQL access through MCP.
