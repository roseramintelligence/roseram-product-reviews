# Carrd review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-23  
**Category:** Website builders  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Carrd provides a hosted website builder. Its plan comparison separates basic sites from custom-domain, form and embed capabilities; those requirements matter more than the lowest advertised price.

## Editorial verdict

The publisher supplies identifiable pricing and domain-setup documentation. These sources support a desk review of the offer, not a finding of fraud or a claim that we tested a live Carrd site. Carrd may fit a compact landing page; a custom application needs a broader evaluation.

## Decide whether the job is a landing page or an application

Our starting question is how much state the visitor needs to change. A page that explains an offer and sends a visitor to a contact form has a smaller operating boundary than a service with accounts, private records and different customer roles. Sketch the complete customer journey before choosing a builder. A polished first screen does not show whether the later steps will work.

For a simple launch, limiting the scope can be an advantage. Evaluate whether you can explain the offer, present trustworthy evidence and give visitors a clear next action without adding unnecessary software. If your brief requires a dashboard or custom business rules, list those separately and ask how each will be implemented. This is a fit assessment, not a claim that Carrd fails an application test we have not run.


## Buy the required capability, not the cheapest Pro label

The practical pricing issue is tier selection. A business owner can hear that a product is inexpensive, buy the entry option and only then discover that the desired setup needs another tier. Write down the intended domain, number of sites, form destination and embedded services first. Match that list to the live comparison before paying. The annual amount should be compared with other annual commitments, not with a monthly number from another vendor.

Our inference is that a small subscription can still sit inside a larger operating budget. Domain renewal, email delivery, payments and any connected service may have separate terms. Create a budget with a named owner for each bill. For client work, decide whether the client or agency owns the account and how that ownership changes at handover. A low platform price does not resolve those responsibilities.


## Treat domain setup and form delivery as acceptance checks

Carrd publishes a custom-domain procedure, including the requirement to register the domain separately. Follow the current instructions shown for your own site rather than copying an example address from a review. Before changing records, identify any existing email and website dependencies. Keep a record of the previous settings and agree on a rollback owner. Our review did not change DNS or measure provisioning time.

A useful proposed check continues after the page opens. Verify the preferred address, secure loading, mobile navigation and a harmless test form submission. Confirm that the person who should receive the message actually receives it and understands what information was collected. Review the page title, social preview and disclosure text. Those checks evaluate the real business outcome; a screenshot alone cannot demonstrate a working enquiry funnel.

## Features and decision criteria

- Hosted site publishing
- Custom domains on qualifying plans
- Forms and third-party embeds on qualifying plans
- Site-sharing and transfer options in the plan comparison

## Pricing considerations

Observed 23 October 2026. These are yearly prices, not monthly equivalents. Domain registration is separate. Higher site-count variants and other tiers exist; confirm the exact features, renewal choice, taxes and checkout total.
