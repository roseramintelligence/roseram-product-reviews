# 30characters review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-18  
**Category:** Google Ads campaign creation and management software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

30characters is a subscription tool for building and overseeing Google Ads campaigns. Its current product pages describe a workflow that scans a landing page, applies account-specific briefs and playbooks, drafts Search, Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns, and lets the user edit before pushing new campaigns to a connected Google Ads account. The publisher says campaigns are created paused, the initial connection is read-only, and users must approve actions. Its dashboard and monitoring features include campaign metrics, negative-keyword suggestions and budget-pacing alerts. The pricing page currently displays Core at $23 per month billed yearly for five accounts and one user, Pro at $47 per month billed yearly for 20 accounts and up to three users, and custom Agency pricing; the page also advertises a 14-day trial. The price symbol’s currency code is not stated in the page text. Terms permit automatic renewal and say cancellation is requested through support; refunds are generally unavailable except at the company’s discretion. The privacy notice was last updated in May 2024 and is generic, while the terms contain unrelated goods and Rytr language. These are diligence questions, not evidence of fraud. Roseram did not connect an account, launch a campaign, test the generated ads, audit security or measure performance.

## Editorial verdict

The public evidence describes an identifiable Google Ads workflow product with a useful boundary: it can speed up campaign preparation and ongoing account review, but the advertiser still owns the Google Ads account, budget and final decision to activate campaigns. The sources reviewed do not substantiate calling 30characters a scam. Nor do they prove that generated keywords, copy, budget alerts or dashboard figures will improve an individual account’s results. Before connecting a live account, inspect the exact Google authorization screen, ask which data is read or written and how campaign information is handled by AI and other providers, then test a low-risk campaign that arrives paused. Confirm trial eligibility, the currency and yearly total at checkout, and the support-based cancellation process. The dated privacy notice and unrelated boilerplate in the terms deserve clarification before a company handles multiple client accounts. This is a public-source desk review, not a product test, ad-performance study, privacy audit or legal opinion.

## 30characters review: a focused tool for Google Ads work

30characters is not a general advertising network and it does not replace a Google Ads account. It is a software layer for teams that create, inspect and maintain Google Ads campaigns. The publisher’s central pitch is practical: reduce repeated setup and account-review work by turning a site and a saved brief into campaign drafts, then bring the user back into the approval decision. That distinction matters. A generated campaign is an input to advertising, not proof that the offer, targeting, landing page or economics are sound.

This review looks at what the public pages say about the campaign builder, Google connection, pricing, privacy and subscription terms. It treats screenshots, testimonials and company-reported user counts as publisher material rather than independent evidence. Roseram did not open an account, connect a Google Ads property, send a campaign or measure outcomes. The title’s use of ‘SCAM Review’ signals a due-diligence article; the evidence reviewed does not support accusing the company of fraud.


## What 30characters says its campaign builder does

The campaign-builder page lays out a sequence: pick a preset, scan a site, review and edit the proposed build, then push it to Google Ads paused. Presets are intended to expand a structure from a list, such as cities or competitor names, into campaigns or ad groups. The page also describes an account brief and Playbook that can carry a brand’s voice, core selling points, market, bidding approach, match-type rules and phrases the team wants to avoid.

That approach can be useful when an agency has a repeatable structure for many similar locations or product lines. It can also scale a poor assumption. If a landing page is ambiguous, a claim is unsupported, or the selected preset is a bad match for the sales process, a larger batch simply multiplies the error. The review-and-edit stage is therefore part of the product’s control model, not a cosmetic extra. Treat the generated result as a draft that needs a human’s fact, policy and account review.


## Campaign types, keywords and ad copy

The publisher lists Search responsive search ads, Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns, with support for multiple major languages. It shows keyword ideas with displayed monthly-search ranges, negative keywords, responsive-search-ad headlines, descriptions and campaign-level settings. The page says headlines fit a 30-character limit and descriptions a 90-character limit, and describes editing keywords and ads before a push. These are the advertised capabilities; no account was connected to verify whether each option is available in every country, vertical or Google Ads account.

Keyword-volume ranges are not a forecast of qualified traffic. A term can have demand but still be irrelevant, too broad, prohibited for a business, or expensive relative to its margin. Generated copy can also be fluent while implying a guarantee or feature that the company cannot substantiate. Before publishing, compare every claim with the destination page, remove ambiguous or prohibited terms, inspect match types and geographic settings, and verify the actual campaign objective and bid strategy in Google Ads.


## Google Ads access and the paused-campaign safeguard

The homepage describes the connection as read-only to start and says the user approves every change. The campaign-builder page says a new campaign is created paused, nothing changes until confirmation, and a user can instead export a CSV for Google Ads Editor. The October 2026 update likewise says pushed campaigns arrive paused for review. This is a sensible staging pattern: a draft does not begin spending merely because it was generated or transferred.

The limit is that these are statements published by the vendor, not a permission audit performed for this review. Read the exact Google OAuth consent screen before authorizing. Check which account is selected, what data can be read, what operations become available when pushing, and whether the application’s role or token scope changes over time. After connection, verify that a test campaign is paused and that no live campaign was altered. Do not assume the word ‘read-only’ covers every later action required to push a new campaign.


## Dashboard, pacing alerts and negative keywords

The product update dated October 28, 2026 describes a dashboard that puts accounts and campaigns together. It lists spend, pacing, impressions, clicks, click-through rate, cost per click, conversions and cost per acquisition, compares the current month with the same days of the preceding month, and says each account is measured in its own time zone. Users can set change thresholds for flags and navigate from pacing into campaign details. The update also describes automatic sync for connected accounts and a separate page for each account.

A dashboard can reduce the time spent finding where to look; it cannot settle whether the attribution setup is correct or whether a change caused a result. Before relying on the numbers, compare a sample with the same Google Ads date range, time zone, conversion definitions and attribution model. A negative-keyword suggestion also needs context: excluding an apparently irrelevant query can cut waste, but a mistaken exclusion can suppress a valuable path. Treat alerts as prioritization prompts and keep a record of why a change was made.


## Who may find the workflow useful

The strongest fit appears to be a Google Ads freelancer or small agency that manages a modest set of accounts and repeatedly builds campaigns from similar sites. Core’s five-account and one-user limit could suit an individual operator; Pro’s listed 20 accounts and three users better match a small team. The per-account brief and Playbook are potentially valuable when several people need to start from shared rules rather than reconstructing the client’s brand voice and campaign preferences each time.

The fit is less clear for a business that runs only one simple campaign, has a highly customized campaign architecture, operates in a sensitive or heavily regulated category, or needs broad omnichannel buying. The reviewed product is specifically framed around Google Ads; it is not an interface for bypassing Google or buying ads across Facebook, TikTok and other networks. A specialist who already has automation scripts and a carefully governed Google Ads Editor workflow may prefer that direct control. Compare the saved time against the subscription and review burden, not against a generic promise of better performance.


## 30characters pricing: annual equivalents, seats and accounts

On November 18, 2026, the official pricing page displayed Core at $23 per month billed yearly and Pro at $47 per month billed yearly. Core lists five Google Ads accounts and one user. Pro lists 20 accounts and up to three users, plus priority support and roadmap input. Agency is custom and is described as including unlimited accounts and users. The page has a monthly-versus-yearly switch, but the captured text did not identify the currency code or expose the month-to-month amounts. The figures here should therefore be read as the displayed yearly-billing equivalents, not a confirmed monthly commitment.

The number of accounts is only one part of total cost. A buyer should ask whether an account is counted at manager-account or client-account level, what happens when connected accounts exceed the plan, whether there are usage limits on campaign generation or sync, and what the custom Agency contract includes. Confirm the currency, tax, annual total and renewal date on the checkout screen. If a trial requires a payment method, set a reminder before its end and verify the cancellation route before starting.


## The advertised 14-day trial and what the terms say

The product and pricing pages advertise a 14-day free trial. The terms say the company may offer trials at its discretion, that a payment method may be required, and that the selected subscription is charged automatically on the last day unless the customer cancels. The same terms state subscriptions renew monthly or yearly, according to the selected plan. This does not prove the advertised trial is unavailable; it means a buyer should verify the actual offer displayed for their account rather than infer eligibility from a general headline.

The cancellation instructions say to contact support at support@30chars.com and state that access continues through the paid period. They say current-period fees are not refunded, while another clause allows the company to consider refund requests case by case. Before checking out, save the displayed plan, currency, billing period, trial end date and cancellation terms. Ask whether cancellation can be completed from the account dashboard or only through support, and keep the confirmation.


## Privacy and connected-account diligence

The privacy notice identifies MeteorSpike Inc. as the operator and says the service may process account details, payment information through Stripe, usage and device data, cookies and other tracking technologies. It also describes analytics and advertising technologies, including Google Analytics. The notice says service providers may process personal information under written contracts. However, the policy is dated May 28, 2024 and uses broad, general language rather than explaining in detail which Google Ads account objects are ingested, what is retained, how long campaign data persists after disconnect, or whether ad copy and account context are sent to generative-AI providers.

That gap is a request for current documentation, not proof of improper data use. An agency should ask for a current data-flow diagram, Google API permission list, AI-provider terms, retention and deletion schedule, support access rules, incident process and any available data-processing agreement. Determine whether client credentials are isolated by account and how a departing employee’s access is removed. If the company cannot answer questions that are material to your client contracts, postpone connecting live client data.


## Terms language that merits a cleanup or clarification

The Terms of Service identify MeteorSpike Inc. and provide a Toronto mailing address, but the document also contains sections about ordering goods, physical delivery and a returns policy linked to rytr.me. Those passages do not read like terms tailored to Google Ads campaign-management software. The subscription provisions are more directly relevant: monthly or yearly automatic renewal, cancellation through support, no routine refund for an already-paid period, and discretionary trial terms.

A boilerplate mismatch is not evidence that the operating company is fictitious or that customers have been defrauded. It does make the document harder to rely on when a customer needs a precise answer about software data, campaign permissions, service interruptions, account disconnection or responsibility for advertising errors. A prospective buyer should ask the company to confirm the current controlling agreement and refund policy in writing. Teams should have counsel review the live contract against their procurement and client-data requirements.


## Testimonials and product screenshots are not outcome guarantees

The homepage says the product is trusted by more than 2,000 Google Ads professionals and publishes several favorable testimonials. The campaign-builder page also shows example accounts and sample campaign data. These provide context for how the vendor wants the product to be understood, but they are company-published claims. The reviewed pages do not provide a controlled before-and-after study, sampling method, cohort size, independently verified savings, or the account conditions needed to attribute performance changes to 30characters.

A buyer should request a reference that resembles their own workload and ask what the customer measured: number of accounts, hours saved, ad-policy disapprovals, conversion quality, spend and time period. Keep process-efficiency claims separate from media results. Faster generation may be useful even if cost per acquisition does not improve; conversely, a polished campaign draft can spend money without producing sales if targeting, offer or landing page is wrong.


## Risks when AI-generated campaigns are scaled

Campaign generation compresses choices that still affect real budgets: locations, search intent, keyword match types, budget, bidding, campaign type, brand claims and landing-page relevance. A mistake in one manually built campaign is contained; a preset that expands across cities can reproduce the same mistake many times. The product’s review-before-push step helps only when a knowledgeable person checks the complete build, including settings that may sit below the ad text.

Use a staged process. Begin with one account and one campaign, confirm the URL and claims, inspect every keyword and negative, compare the generated structure with your account strategy, and check policy-sensitive language. Push only paused items, verify that state in Google Ads, then have an authorized operator decide whether and when to enable them. Monitor actual search terms and conversions after launch. No copy generator can guarantee an ad approval, ranking, click-through rate, conversion or return on spend.


## Alternatives and comparison criteria

The closest no-new-vendor path is Google Ads itself, including its built-in campaign creation, reporting and account controls. Google Ads Editor can support offline bulk edits and review before posting. A team may also use a spreadsheet plus documented templates for repeated structures, or a separate reporting and optimization product if its primary need is cross-account monitoring rather than campaign generation. Those approaches vary in automation and convenience; none should be assumed equivalent without a workload comparison.

When comparing tools, list the number of accounts and operators, campaign types, import/export path, required Google permissions, approval safeguards, API behavior, data retention, client isolation, support commitments and cancellation process. Run a time-boxed pilot against a small set of campaigns and record rework, not just generation speed. 30characters’ differentiator in the reviewed materials is the combination of website-based campaign drafts, playbooks, direct Google Ads push while paused and account-level dashboard features. The public evidence does not establish that it will outperform an existing specialist workflow.


## A buyer checklist before subscribing or connecting accounts

Before trial: capture the exact amount, currency, annual or monthly commitment, payment requirement, trial end date and cancellation route from checkout. Confirm the number of accounts and users included and how manager accounts are counted. Ask support to clarify the Terms of Service passages referring to goods and Rytr, and request the current version of the software agreement and privacy documentation.

Before connection: read Google’s consent screen, record which account is being authorized, identify read and write scopes, and confirm how disconnecting revokes access. Use a test or low-risk account first. Check a generated campaign’s location, language, budget, bidding, keywords, negatives, landing URL and ad claims. Verify it remains paused after the push. For agency use, confirm the handling of each client’s data, AI-provider involvement, retention and deletion, staff access, support access and incident reporting.


## Final verdict: a useful Google Ads workflow with paperwork to clarify

30characters presents a coherent proposition for Google Ads practitioners who repeatedly build campaigns and need a consolidated view across several accounts. The public product material describes presets, reusable briefs, editable ad assets, a paused push, account metrics, pacing and negative-keyword monitoring. That can save setup and review time when the user’s own strategy and quality control are already strong. Pricing capacity is easy to compare at a glance, but the currency symbol, monthly option and checkout total should be verified before purchase.

The evidence reviewed does not substantiate calling 30characters a scam. It also does not validate performance claims or resolve the most relevant diligence questions: the privacy notice is dated and generic for a product connected to advertising accounts, the terms contain unrelated goods and Rytr references, and the trial and cancellation details require careful checkout confirmation. Start only after reviewing Google permissions and receiving answers that meet your data-governance needs.
