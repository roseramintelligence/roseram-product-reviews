# 365Scores review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-17  
**Category:** Live sports scores, news and match data  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

365Scores is a live-score and sports-information service with customizable team feeds, match alerts, statistics, news, lineups and highlights. The company advertises coverage of more than 2,000 competitions across ten sports; Apple and Google store listings describe ads and in-app purchases. Current store privacy declarations list identifiers that may be used for tracking and several categories of collected data, while the publisher-linked privacy policy reviewed here still carries a December 14, 2020 update date and describes cookies, pixels, analytics, marketing and third-party data sharing. Store declarations are developer-provided and not independently verified by the platforms. The company’s About page also uses different audience figures in two parts of the same page, so audience-size headlines should be treated as marketing claims rather than audited reach. This review did not measure score latency, ad load or data practices. The materials establish a functioning product but do not substantiate a scam allegation. Users should check the current store purchase sheet, notification settings, privacy choices and the exact competition coverage that matters to them.

## 365Scores review: a personalized sports scoreboard

365Scores organizes live results, match information and sports news around teams and competitions a fan chooses. Its official pages describe a personal “My Scores” feed, alerts, fixtures, standings, lineups, match statistics, transfer information and highlight content. The company advertises coverage of ten sports and more than 2,000 competitions. Those figures describe the publisher’s stated scope; the useful practical check is whether the leagues, local competitions and data fields a particular fan follows appear in the app and update in a usable way.

The product is an information and notification service, not a guarantee that a user can watch every match. The official site includes sports television schedules and third-party links, while store descriptions emphasize scores, news and data. Confirm whether a match has a licensed stream, a highlight clip, a live tracker or only a score card before planning to follow it through the app. A live score feed and a broadcast subscription solve different needs.


## Coverage claims need a sport-by-sport check

The About page lists soccer, basketball, American football, tennis, ice hockey, baseball, rugby, cricket, volleyball and handball. It names major leagues and tournaments but also suggests coverage of local and lower-division teams. The number of competitions alone does not tell a buyer which data fields are available for a given event. One league may have lineups and detailed statistics while a smaller competition offers only a result and basic schedule.

Build a short list of the exact teams and events that matter to you. Check the current app or website for one upcoming match in each category and verify time zone, fixture, lineup, live event detail and final score. Repeat the check on a lower-tier competition if that coverage is why you are considering the product. Published competition totals are not a service-level commitment that every fixture is complete or that every statistic is available at the same depth.


## Live alerts are useful only when their timing suits your viewing

The company promotes alerts for selected events and warns that notifications may arrive before the user’s broadcast stream. That can be useful when someone cannot watch a match, but it can also reveal a goal before a delayed television or online feed. Users should choose notification types and quiet hours with that tradeoff in mind. A fast alert is not a measurement of universal delivery speed: network conditions, device restrictions, battery settings and broadcast delay all affect what a person experiences.

Test notifications during a low-stakes match before relying on them for a final, a workplace watch party or a betting-related decision. Compare the phone’s alert with a known live feed and note whether the app sends duplicate or late alerts. Do not assume every event is pushed by default; select teams and alert categories deliberately. If a notification is late, the app’s history or match page may still help, but the user should not treat a phone alert as an official scoring record.


## Sports statistics and predictions are context, not certainty

365Scores describes pre-game statistics and predictions, and its website can display odds-related content or links. Match history, recent form, lineups and expected-goals statistics can help a fan understand a fixture, but they do not guarantee an outcome. A model or editorial prediction remains uncertain, particularly when lineups change, players are injured or data coverage differs between competitions. The app’s presence does not make a particular prediction an independent recommendation.

If you are comparing prediction features, ask what the displayed metric means, which time period it uses and whether the app distinguishes a forecast from a result. Treat an external betting link as a separate service with its own terms, age restrictions and risks. Never stake money you cannot afford to lose based on an app headline or a historical trend. This review does not assess gambling products or endorse a wager.


## Ads, in-app purchases and regional price differences

The current Google Play listing marks 365Scores as containing ads and in-app purchases. The U.S. Apple listing also marks the app free with in-app purchases and includes examples such as a one-time ad-removal purchase, a “VIP Subscription” and tip-related items at displayed U.S. prices. Store offers can vary by country, operating system, date and account. The names shown in an extracted listing do not always make the billing period, renewal behavior or exact feature benefit obvious.

Before purchasing, open the offer inside your own store account and record the amount, currency, billing period, renewal date and what the offer removes or enables. Confirm whether an ad-free purchase is permanent, tied to one platform or transferable to another device. If an item is a subscription, identify the store that bills it and use that store’s cancellation screen. A page showing a low starting price is not enough to establish the total cost over a season.


## Advertising and the match-page experience

Advertising is part of the product’s published store positioning. A Google Play review included on the listing described the ad load as disruptive; the developer’s response said ads help keep the app free and pointed to ad removal or an invite-based route. That is one user’s report and a company reply, not a representative survey. It does, however, make ad density a sensible issue to test before adopting the service as a daily scoreboard.

Open the app during a quiet period and during a live match. See whether an ad interrupts navigation, obscures a statistic, delays opening a team page or affects notification handling. Check whether the paid ad-removal option addresses only advertising or also changes other features. If you use a small phone screen or assistive technology, test readability and dismissal controls. Do not assume an ad-free payment removes third-party tracking or changes the data practices described in the privacy policy.


## Privacy policy age and the store’s separate data declaration

The publisher-linked privacy policy reviewed for this article says it was last updated December 14, 2020. It describes account details, phone and country information, IP address and general location, technical data, cookies and other tracking technologies. It discusses analytics, advertising, marketing, business partners and opt-outs. The document is detailed, but its age raises a practical question: whether the current app’s providers, data categories and choices are still accurately represented. Ask for the current policy version if you need the service for a school, club, company or child’s device.

The Google Play Data safety section and Apple privacy label are separate developer declarations and may reflect different platforms, regions or app versions. Apple says the developer supplied its privacy answers and that Apple has not verified them. The current U.S. Apple listing says identifiers may be used to track a person across other companies’ apps and websites and lists purchases, location, contact information, identifiers and usage data among categories that may be linked to identity. These labels are useful screening information, not a privacy audit; check the live store listing and device consent settings before installing.


## Personalization requires attention to notification and account settings

A personalized sports feed works best when the user chooses the right teams, leagues and competitions. That personalization can also create a stream of alerts that becomes noise if every match, score change and news item is enabled. Start with a small number of teams and one notification type. Add categories only after learning which alerts help. If a user account is connected to social features or multiple devices, review the profile settings and sign-in method as well.

The privacy policy says a registered account may provide name, email, telephone number and country, and it describes social-network connections as a possible source of profile information. The product may not require every user to create an account for basic scores, but confirm the current sign-up flow before providing optional personal details. Use a unique password and check the account-recovery path. Remove connections you no longer use and review the device’s notification and location permissions.


## Audience and performance headlines should be read as vendor claims

The 365Scores About page gives different audience figures in two places: the main description refers to more than 40 million fans, while a later page passage claims service to more than 100 million fans. Store listings currently show large download and review counts, but those measures are not interchangeable with active users, unique people, current retention or the size of a specific market. The conflicting figures may reflect different dates or definitions, but the page does not explain the distinction.

The same caution applies to phrases such as “fastest” or “most accurate.” The public pages reviewed do not supply an independent latency benchmark or a methodology for that superlative. If a broadcaster, publisher or analyst needs a documented audience estimate or uptime commitment, request current definitions and measurement sources directly. For an individual fan, the relevant performance test is simpler: follow the competitions you care about for a week and note missing fixtures, incorrect schedule details and alert timing.


## Accessibility, language and device compatibility

The publisher advertises multi-language support and availability across devices. The U.S. Apple listing provides concrete current compatibility details for iPhone, iPad, Mac with Apple silicon, Apple Watch and Apple Vision, plus a list of supported languages. Those requirements can change with store releases. A user should check the app listing for their region and operating-system version rather than rely on an old device screenshot or a broad “any device” slogan.

Test text size, contrast, screen-reader labels and the ability to reach key controls before relying on a live match view. Sports pages can be information-dense, and color may carry meaning in a scoreboard. Confirm that alerts remain distinguishable when sound is off and that the user can mute a competition quickly. A language choice may affect interface text without changing the language of every news story or commentary item, so inspect the actual content sources used in your region.


## How to test 365Scores against another scoreboard

Choose three fixtures: a major league match, a smaller competition that you follow and an event in another sport. Write down what you need: start time, score, lineup, key event alerts, standings and news. Compare 365Scores with one alternative using the same fixtures and device. Record whether each entry is present, how quickly a change appears relative to a known feed, and whether the app tells you what is a live update versus a replay or highlight.

During the test, leave most notifications off and enable only the events you expect to use. Track any interruption from ads and inspect an in-app purchase sheet without buying. This is a suggested evaluation, not a claim that Roseram measured 365Scores latency or performed a comparative test. A week of ordinary use will reveal more about your leagues and device than a single showcase screenshot.


## Alternatives and what to compare

Compare 365Scores with a league’s official app, a broad sports broadcaster, a general score aggregator and the built-in sports feed on your phone. Official league apps may offer a deeper view of one competition; broad aggregators may cover more sports but vary in local league detail; broadcaster apps can combine schedules with licensed video in selected territories. No source should be presumed complete worldwide. Compare the matches you actually follow, the depth of statistics, ad experience, alert controls and the cost of any premium tier.

If data freshness is essential to your work, use an authenticated data provider with a documented feed and rights rather than scraping or relying on consumer alerts. If you only want a simple final score, a smaller or official app may be less distracting. Keep sports data separate from betting advice and broadcast rights. A strong choice is the product that reliably covers your set of events at a cost and privacy level you are willing to accept.
