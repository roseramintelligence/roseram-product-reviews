# Invoice Ninja review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Invoicing and small-business management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Invoice Ninja combines invoices and quotes with payment gateways, recurring billing, projects, time tracking, expenses and client portals. Its plan page separates a free tier limited to five clients from paid options and self-hosting. Payment processing and hosting choices should be budgeted separately from the app subscription.

## Editorial verdict

Invoice Ninja is worth evaluating for freelancers and small businesses that need recurring invoices and billable-project tracking. Free supports up to five clients, while Pro removes the client limit at a published monthly price. Teams should check permissions, banking features and deployment responsibilities before adoption.

## Trace the complete invoice lifecycle

Create a test client, draft an estimate, issue an invoice and record a test payment. Check whether recurring billing, reminders and the client portal behave as expected for the actual business. Verify a correction and cancellation path before using automatic billing with customers.

Confirm that the invoice currency, tax fields and payment link match the business requirements. This review did not connect a payment processor or issue a real invoice.


## Choose hosted or self-managed operations

The pricing page lists hosted tiers and a separate self-hosting route. A self-managed installation shifts responsibility for server updates, availability, backups and access security to the operator. Lower software cost does not eliminate that work.

If choosing hosted service, compare account limits and the retention and export terms. If self-hosting, test a backup restore and update process before storing actual client records.


## Calculate gateway and team costs

A payment button depends on an external gateway whose transaction fees, country support and settlement schedule should be checked. Add those charges to the software tier. Enterprise pricing also changes with account users, while Pro is per subscription.

Run a small invoice test with a non-sensitive item and inspect the resulting ledger record. Payment receipts and accounting reports must reconcile to the gateway statement, including refunds and processor fees.


## Check caps and access roles against the business

Five clients may fit a new freelancer but can become restrictive as a contact list grows. The paid Pro tier removes that cap; Enterprise adds users and permissions. Compare the required access model before inviting a bookkeeper or employee.

This review uses official product and pricing pages. It does not audit financial controls, gateway integrations, security certification or tax compliance. Confirm the current contract and jurisdiction-specific requirements before processing business records.


## Features and decision criteria

- Invoices, quotes and recurring billing
- Multiple payment gateway connections
- Projects, time tracking and expenses
- Hosted plans and self-hosting options

## Pricing considerations

Annual billing is paid upfront; Enterprise user count changes the amount. Payment gateway processing fees and self-hosting infrastructure are separate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is unlimited invoicing the same as unlimited clients on Free?

No. The current Free plan allows unlimited invoices but caps clients at five.

### Does Invoice Ninja itself process every payment?

No. The publisher lists connections to payment gateways; each gateway has its own availability and fees.

### Can I add users and permissions to every tier?

No. The plan comparison lists additional users and user permissions on Enterprise and higher plans.
