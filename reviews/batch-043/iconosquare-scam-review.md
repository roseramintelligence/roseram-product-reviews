## Plan, publish and measure social content in one workflow

Iconosquare describes a platform for planning and publishing content, reviewing social performance and managing selected audience interactions. Its network list includes Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Threads and YouTube. A unified calendar can reduce the work of switching between native tools, while analytics and reports give a team a repeatable way to review posts and campaigns. The practical value depends on which networks and post formats your workflow uses most.

Treat “supported network” as a starting point, not a promise that every action is available everywhere. Before migrating a calendar, check whether each channel supports the post type, scheduling method, first comment, tagging, collaboration, video format, inbox action and analytics dimension you need. Social platforms change their APIs and permissions. Run a small publishing test on each priority network and keep a native-app fallback for features the connected workflow cannot provide.


## Understand the free tier and paid plan boundaries

The official pricing page showed a free plan with up to two social profiles, one user, ten scheduled posts per month per profile and one month of analytics history. It also displayed paid starting prices in euros for annual billing, with profile choices and plan configuration affecting the total. Treat those amounts as regional page values rather than a universal quote; the checkout may show another currency, billing cadence or profile bundle.

List the accounts you manage, the people who need access, posts per profile each month, report recipients and historical period needed for comparisons. Then identify whether you require team approvals, competitor benchmarking, listening, campaign tracking, white-label reports, API access or longer data retention. Select a tier based on those requirements and calculate the annual total at your actual profile count. Confirm trial duration and what happens to features and scheduled content when a trial ends.


## Build a reliable publishing and approval process

A content calendar works best when it captures audience, objective, format, owner, reviewer, asset rights and destination for each planned post. Create a test week with a mix of image, short video and link posts, then compare the scheduled version with the published result on each platform. Check line breaks, aspect ratios, captions, alt text, tags, disclosure language, links and time zones. Clarify whether an item auto-publishes or requires a mobile notification and a final manual step.

For teams, define who drafts, who approves, who can connect accounts and who can publish immediately. Try a late edit, a rejected post, a failed media upload and a disconnected profile. Make sure the calendar communicates failures clearly and that a teammate can recover without accidentally publishing twice. Keep sensitive account credentials in the platform’s supported authorization flow; do not paste passwords into shared notes or campaign documents.


## Use analytics with context and report only what they show

Iconosquare lists analytics, dashboards and reporting, with available metrics and data retention depending on the plan and network. Decide in advance which measures correspond to your goal: reach and impressions for distribution, saves or shares for deeper engagement, clicks for traffic, and qualified inquiries for business outcomes. Platform definitions differ, so document the source and reporting period before comparing channels. A high reach number alone does not prove sales impact, and a short period may be distorted by one unusually successful post.

Set a monthly review that pairs quantitative metrics with a few examples of audience response and the next content experiment. Check data availability during the trial, export a report and confirm that teammates can understand its labels without an oral explanation. This guide summarizes public product and plan information; it is not a hands-on performance test or independent endorsement. Verify the current feature matrix, privacy terms and pricing directly with Iconosquare.
