## Atoms and requests per second are different limits

The pricing describes monthly atom allowances alongside request-rate limits. Those address different constraints: an account can have unused atoms while still exceeding the permitted rate, or remain below the rate while consuming its monthly allowance. The homepage says service references list the cost of a call, which should be used to estimate the actual task. Do not equate one atom with one request without the endpoint's stated metering.

Model ordinary traffic, bursts and application retries for the selected operations. These are proposed capacity checks, not usage measured here. The captured Free, Pro and Pro+ descriptions list different atom, key and rate allowances, with paid top-ups described separately.
