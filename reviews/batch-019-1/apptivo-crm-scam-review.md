# Apptivo CRM review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** CRM and business applications  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Apptivo combines customer relationship management with a broader suite of sales, marketing, project, finance, service and business applications. The CRM plans are priced per user and differ in included apps, custom fields, workflows, dashboards and enterprise integration options.

## Editorial verdict

Apptivo is best evaluated as a configurable business-app suite rather than a narrow contact database. The bundled breadth may reduce the number of separate tools for a business, but onboarding, configuration and plan boundaries deserve close testing. Compare the specific apps and automation limits your team needs, then verify total costs for seats, integrations and implementation before replacing established systems.

## Decide whether you need a CRM or a broader app suite

Apptivo’s CRM page places contact and sales tracking within a larger collection of business apps. A team that wants one vendor for sales, marketing, service and back-office functions may value that breadth. A team that only needs a lightweight contact tracker may find a suite introduces more choices and setup than necessary.

Write down the jobs the system must perform in the first month: contact capture, opportunity stages, follow-up reminders, quoting, support cases or invoice handoff. Then map each job to the app and plan that provides it. Do not treat an app count as a measure of value unless the team will actually use those apps.


## Calculate the plan from seats and configured limits

The official price table lists per-user monthly rates and distinct app, field, workflow and dashboard limits. A growing sales operation may hit a workflow or reporting cap before it reaches the contact limit. Annual billing lowers the displayed per-user rate but involves a longer payment commitment.

Calculate cost using the expected number of named users, not only the first administrator. Include annual payment timing, tax, integration subscriptions and potential custom services. During the trial, create the fields and automation the team actually needs and confirm they fit the chosen tier before importing live records.


## Treat configuration as part of the purchase decision

A configurable system can represent a company’s sales process more closely, but each extra field and workflow creates decisions about ownership, naming, exceptions and maintenance. Start with a small set of consistent lifecycle stages. Document which roles update records and what a completed task means.

Test automations with sample data, especially notifications, assignment rules and any customer-facing message. Ensure a workflow has a clear owner and failure path. A large suite can consolidate tools only if the business has enough time to configure it and keep the processes current.


## Check integrations, access and support expectations

Apptivo describes third-party integrations and support across its tiers, with enterprise options for custom integrations and service commitments. Verify the exact application, connector and plan before assuming an integration exists. Test data direction, sync timing, duplicate behavior and error reporting using a sandbox or small test set.

Review two-factor authentication, user roles, export options and account recovery. For a company that depends on the platform for sales or billing, ask what support channel and response commitment is included and whether an SLA is contractual. This assessment is based on Apptivo’s public pages, not an implementation trial or financial advice.


## Features and decision criteria

- Contact, lead, deal, task and sales activity management
- Broader app suite includes marketing and business operations modules
- Custom fields, workflows and dashboards have tier-specific limits
- Third-party integrations and enterprise services vary by plan

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page lists a 14-day trial and no credit card requirement. Confirm included CRM functions, app entitlements, per-user billing, integration charges and current trial terms against the live comparison before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Apptivo a CRM only?

No. Apptivo presents a broader suite of business applications alongside CRM, including areas such as marketing and operations.

### Do all plans include the same number of apps and workflows?

No. The official comparison lists different app, custom-field, workflow and dashboard limits by plan.

### Can the published starting price represent the full deployment cost?

Not necessarily. Seat count, plan limits, integrations, configuration and onboarding can affect the total cost.
