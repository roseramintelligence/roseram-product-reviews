# Whop review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Digital product marketplace and payments platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Whop offers checkout and payment infrastructure for digital products and online businesses, with marketplace discovery and optional services. Its official network pricing page lists standard domestic-card pricing at 2.7% plus US$0.30 per successful transaction, with other payment methods, international cards, conversion, payouts and optional services priced separately.

## Editorial verdict

Whop may fit a seller who wants hosted checkout, digital-product delivery and marketplace or payment tooling in one service. The advertised lack of a monthly software charge does not mean transactions and payouts are free. Read the current fee schedule, seller obligations, refund and dispute responsibilities, and payout methods before routing an existing business through it. This is a source-based guide, not a seller-account test or a claim about earnings.

## Separate product discovery from payment processing

Whop presents both a network where buyers can discover digital products and tools for sellers to create checkout experiences. Those functions overlap, but they are separate reasons to choose a platform. A business that already has an audience may care more about payment controls and delivery, while a new seller may also evaluate whether marketplace discovery reaches relevant buyers.

Do not treat marketplace presence as a promise of traffic. Write down where your customers currently come from and test whether a Whop product page makes the offer clearer or reduces checkout friction. Track visits, completed purchases, refunds and support requests separately so you can judge the actual effect.


## Calculate the full payment and payout cost

Whop’s official pricing page lists 2.7% plus US$0.30 for each successful domestic card transaction. It lists extra rates for international cards and currency conversion, with distinct prices for local payment methods and payouts. The page also describes optional services that can add a percentage charge when enabled.

Estimate the net amount on a typical order using the payment method your customers will use, then include any payout cost, tax collection setting, affiliate payment and potential dispute. A no-monthly-fee headline can still accompany meaningful variable costs. Confirm which features are enabled by default and inspect the fee shown for a real test transaction before moving a large volume of sales.


## Review your seller responsibilities

Whop’s Seller Terms say a seller is responsible for delivering the product and handling refunds, returns and chargebacks. The terms also describe tax responsibilities that can depend on which tax service mode is enabled and where the seller operates. Read those obligations before accepting payments; a checkout provider does not remove every responsibility of the business.

Prepare a clear product description, support contact, refund policy and delivery process. Test a failed payment, a cancellation and a refund in a controlled way. Keep records that connect an order to what was delivered and the communication with the buyer.


## Check eligibility, verification and payout timing

Payment products commonly require identity and business verification before funds can be withdrawn. Whop describes multiple payout methods with different timing and fees. Confirm your country is supported, what documents are required, the minimum payout threshold if any, and whether a chosen payout method incurs an extra charge.

Do not use a projected payout schedule as available cash. Allow for payment holds, refunds, disputes, and verification review. If Whop is one part of a business, retain a separate reconciliation of gross orders, fees, tax amounts and net payouts.


## Run a small checkout trial first

Before sending customers to a new checkout, place a low-value test order if the seller tools allow it, and confirm the receipt, access grant, customer email and cancellation path. Review the checkout on a phone and verify that the displayed price and renewal interval match your offer.

Compare the result against your current payment flow using a modest, measurable test. A provider’s claims about conversion improvements are not a forecast for your product. This review summarizes public Whop pages and terms, and does not report a Roseram transaction, seller payout or independent fraud audit.


## Features and decision criteria

- Hosted checkout and payment infrastructure
- Digital product pages and marketplace discovery
- Multiple payment methods and payout choices
- Seller remains responsible for product fulfillment, refunds and disputes under the cited terms

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page separately lists additional charges such as international-card and currency-conversion fees, optional orchestration and billing, tax service, affiliate processing and payout methods. Seller Terms say net proceeds are reduced by applicable processing fees, taxes and Whop service fees. Check the live fee table and Seller Dashboard for your account and transaction type.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Whop charge a monthly fee?

Its official network page says standard signup has no monthly or SaaS fee, while successful transactions and optional services can carry fees.

### What is Whop’s standard domestic card rate?

The current official pricing page lists 2.7% plus US$0.30 per successful domestic-card transaction.

### Who handles refunds and disputes?

Whop’s Seller Terms say sellers remain responsible for refunds and chargebacks related to their products, with Whop processing reversals when instructed.
