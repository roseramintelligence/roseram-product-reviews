# Wave Accounting review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Small-business accounting software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Wave Accounting offers a bookkeeping product with a free starting plan and a paid Pro tier for automatic transaction imports, categorization and other workflow features. Its Pro page lists monthly and annual US prices and payment processing rates. Verify bank availability and transaction charges for the account’s country.

## Editorial verdict

Wave is a relevant option for small businesses that want accounting records and invoicing with a free entry point. Automatic bank connections and receipt workflows are tied to Pro, and online payments carry processing fees. Trial the bank import and reconciliation steps before relying on them.

## Compare the free bookkeeping path with Pro

The accounting page offers a free starting point, while Pro adds automatic bank connections and transaction categorization. Begin by entering a small set of sample transactions and exporting a report. Then decide whether the bank connection saves enough time to justify a subscription.

Use the exact bank account and region planned for production during a limited test. Connection availability and supported institutions should be checked directly; this review did not link an account.


## Reconcile imported data instead of trusting it blindly

Imported transactions need categorization and reconciliation against the statement. Test duplicates, pending transactions, refunds and transfers between accounts. Review a profit-and-loss report and confirm it reflects the intended date range and categories.

Keep original statements and review exceptions before closing a period. Automated imports reduce manual entry but do not determine the correct accounting treatment for every business event.


## Include fees for payments and upgrades

The Pro page lists US$19 monthly or US$190 annually and published processing rates for card payments. Compare the chosen billing cycle and expected payment volume. Account for any difference between payment methods and regional processor terms.

Confirm settlement timing, chargeback handling and refund records with the payment provider. A payment transaction and its bookkeeping record are separate parts of a complete reconciliation.


## Use reports as operational records, not advice

Wave describes profit-and-loss, balance-sheet and cash-flow reporting. Validate these outputs against a known sample and consult a qualified accountant about how accounts should be categorized for the business. Software reports do not independently confirm tax treatment.

This assessment draws on the publisher’s product and price pages. It does not connect a bank, process a payment, inspect security controls or certify bookkeeping accuracy. Confirm current country availability and terms before moving financial data.


## Features and decision criteria

- Accounting records and financial reports
- Invoices and cash-flow views
- Pro bank transaction imports and categorization
- Receipt capture and automated reminders

## Pricing considerations

The publisher lists online payment processing at 2.9% plus US$0 per standard card transaction and 3.4% plus US$0 for American Express on the cited page. Rates, availability and country terms can change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is automatic bank import included in every Wave plan?

The publisher lists bank transaction imports and connections as Pro features.

### Is accepting card payments free?

No. The Pro page lists card processing rates; confirm the applicable method and country fees.

### Should I assume every bank is supported?

No. Check the exact institution and connection behavior in the account before relying on imports.
