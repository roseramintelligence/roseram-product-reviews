# Typeform review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Online forms and surveys  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Typeform builds forms with conditional questions and endings. It earns consideration when a visitor’s answer changes the next question or the person who should handle it. Response volume and routing should drive the plan decision.

## Editorial verdict

Typeform publishes its billing and response rules. The strongest case for buying it is a form whose branches save measurable work after a submission. A simple email capture form calls for a closer price comparison.

## Typeform branching logic for a busy intake desk

A quote form can ask a homeowner and a building manager the same opening question, then send them down very different paths. Decide what each visitor can reasonably answer without calling your office. A homeowner can give a postal code. A manager may already have a site plan. Sketch both paths before building either, and remove each question whose answer nobody uses.

Put the **Typeform branching logic** in front of someone unfamiliar with the offer. Ask them to change an early answer and finish the form. Then inspect the exported record and the team’s notification. The useful result is a correct assignment with the context needed for the first call. A smooth form that sends work to the wrong person creates a more attractive backlog.


## Typeform response limits during a busy campaign

The Free plan lists ten responses per month across forms. Assume an account uses one form for new inquiries and another for booking feedback. Those activities share the allowance. Before running ads, estimate both from a normal month and a campaign week. Separate the per-form numbers so an unrelated signup drive cannot quietly consume the capacity you set aside for sales.

The help center says a form may stop accepting answers when the account reaches its cap, subject to the account’s settings and current terms. Decide who watches the allowance and who can authorize a higher limit. If every missed request sends a potential buyer elsewhere, check the *cost of an interrupted form* alongside the bill for additional responses.


## Typeform vs Tally for lead capture and follow-up

Tally advertises unlimited submissions on its free offering within its fair-use policy. Compare the same intake flow in both products. Include a difficult path, a duplicate submission, a mobile embed and a handoff to your CRM. Watch the work the sales team does the following morning. If both give the team equally complete records, the less expensive plan has a strong case.

Choose Typeform if its question flow and team workflow make a demonstrable improvement where you lose inquiries today. Start with the smallest plan that covers the campaign and the necessary integration. Put the test outputs and an allowance estimate beside the checkout price so the purchase has a reason you can explain.


## Features and decision criteria

- Conditional branching, scores and calculations
- Embeddable forms and custom endings
- Response exports and integrations

## Pricing considerations

Typeform applies monthly response limits to both monthly and annual plans. Check the displayed paid plan and response allowance for your account, along with any auto-upgrade terms.
