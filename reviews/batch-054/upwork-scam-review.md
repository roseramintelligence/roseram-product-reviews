# Upwork review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Freelance talent marketplace and contracting platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Upwork connects businesses with independent professionals through hourly and fixed-price contracts, project listings and direct contracts. Freelancer service fees vary from 0% to 15% by contract, while client marketplace fees depend on plan, payment method and location.

## Editorial verdict

Upwork is useful when a project needs proposals, contract records, time tracking or funded milestones and the buyer wants to compare several candidates. Fee uncertainty matters: freelancer rates are fixed per contract but can vary between contracts, and client fees vary by account and payment method. Compare the full buyer budget with the freelancer’s expected net and confirm the fee displayed before accepting an offer.

## Choose the contract structure that fits the work

Upwork supports hourly work, fixed-price milestones, Project Catalog projects and Direct Contracts. A defined output with review checkpoints may fit fixed-price milestones; ongoing work with changing weekly priorities may suit hourly tracking. Choose a structure that makes the scope and approval evidence easy for both parties to understand.

Before hiring, divide the brief into required outcomes, constraints, access needs and decision dates. Ask candidates how they would approach the first milestone and what assumptions they need resolved. A polished proposal is not proof of delivery quality, so begin with a bounded first phase when project risk is high.


## Budget fees on both sides

Upwork’s current help page says freelancer service fees range from 0% to 15% per contract. The exact amount is displayed before the proposal or offer is sent, and becomes fixed for that contract. A freelancer should calculate net income from the shown rate, while a client should treat the marketplace fee as an additional line on top of the agreed work budget.

Client marketplace fees vary too. Upwork says Basic clients typically pay 5%, a 7.99% fee may apply depending on location, and eligible US clients paying by ACH can pay 3%. The displayed fee should be checked before payment. For larger budgets, compare the contract amount, taxes, payment method and plan fee in the same currency.


## Keep evidence inside the contract workflow

For hourly work, agree on what is being tracked and keep task notes meaningful. For fixed-price work, define each milestone and the review requirements before funding it. Store key decisions, file delivery and approval inside the platform so the payment record has context if a question arises.

For a client, check that the proposed work matches the profile and portfolio. For a freelancer, confirm that the client’s requested test or sample is paid when it represents valuable custom work. Do not send passwords in plain chat; use scoped credentials and revoke them when the contract ends.


## Understand how dispute support is bounded

Marketplace payment tools can create a clearer transaction trail, but users still need to follow the relevant payment protection and dispute rules. Read the current policy for the chosen contract type before work begins. Keep time records, milestone acceptance notes and delivery files together rather than relying on a verbal understanding.

Freelancers should review the payout schedule, tax forms and withdrawal options available to their location. Clients should confirm the payment method and the exact fee in the checkout flow. Upwork does not guarantee that a project will create revenue or that a contract will end without disagreement. This review analyzes official fee and workflow documents; it is not a firsthand transaction report.


## Features and decision criteria

- Hourly, fixed-price, Project Catalog and Direct Contract workflows
- Freelancer fees vary per contract from 0% to 15% and lock when contract begins
- Client Basic marketplace fee often 5%, with account and payment-method exceptions
- Milestones and payment protection rules require parties to follow the platform process

## Pricing considerations

Upwork fees vary by contract and client location, and its public pricing surfaces can change. The fee shown in the account, proposal or contract controls; include marketplace fees, taxes, Connects or add-ons where applicable in the budget.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Upwork’s freelancer fee always 10%?

No. Upwork says the freelancer fee ranges from 0% to 15% per contract and is shown before the freelancer submits a proposal or accepts an offer.

### How much does a client pay in marketplace fees?

Upwork says Basic clients typically pay 5%, but 7.99% may apply by location and eligible US ACH payments can be 3%; Business Plus has different fees.

### Does a funded milestone guarantee the project will be dispute-free?

No. Payment protection depends on meeting Upwork’s documentation and contract requirements; users should read the applicable policy and document work inside the contract.
