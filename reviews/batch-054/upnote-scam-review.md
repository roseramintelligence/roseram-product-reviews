# UpNote review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Cross-platform notes apps  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

UpNote offers notes on desktop and mobile, with hashtags, nested notebooks, Markdown, web clipping, offline access and synchronization. Its publisher limits the free version to 50 notes and lists Premium at a monthly or lifetime price. Confirm the store and regional price before purchase.

## Editorial verdict

UpNote is worth testing for someone who wants a structured cross-platform notebook with offline writing and web clipping. The 50-note free cap makes a representative trial important. Check export, backup and lock behavior before treating it as the only copy of important notes.

## Try offline writing and later synchronization

Create a nonsensitive note while disconnected, edit it, then reconnect and inspect the resulting copy on another device. Confirm which version wins if the same note changes in two places. A sync indicator is useful only when the destination has the expected content.

The publisher lists offline access and automatic sync. This review does not test conflict handling, sync speed or backup restoration, so retain an independent export for anything that cannot be recreated.


## Use notebooks and tags for different jobs

Nested notebooks give broad structure while hashtags can mark cross-cutting topics. Assign each one a purpose to avoid building two overlapping systems. Try retrieving notes by title, keyword and tag after adding a few examples.

A clipper, attachments and templates can create a useful collection quickly. Set a routine for reviewing and exporting the archive before it becomes difficult to assess what is still relevant.


## Understand the free cap before migrating

The published free edition allows up to 50 notes. That can support a focused evaluation but may not cover a large imported archive. Select a bounded sample and confirm how attachments, formatting and links behave before moving more material.

Premium is offered monthly or as a lifetime purchase, subject to platform and regional terms. Check what the lifetime option covers and whether a given store supports the desired purchase before treating the listed amount as universal.


## Treat locks, exports and backups separately

An app lock can prevent casual access when a device is unattended, while an export supports portability and a backup supports recovery. These are different safeguards. Verify each one with a test note and a destination outside the app.

The publisher describes automatic backup and version history. This review has not restored a backup or audited encryption. Use the official privacy and help resources to answer security questions that matter to the data being stored.


## Features and decision criteria

- Nested notebooks and hashtags
- Markdown and rich-text editing
- Offline notes and device sync
- Web clipping and multiple export formats

## Pricing considerations

The publisher says prices can vary by region and taxes, and purchase options depend on the app store. Check availability and exact terms for the account and platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the free version hold unlimited notes?

No. The publisher currently lists a 50-note limit for the free version.

### Can notes be edited without an internet connection?

The publisher says notes are accessible and writable offline; test sync recovery on the actual devices.

### Does locking the app encrypt every note?

Do not infer encryption from an app lock. Check the security documentation for the specific protection offered.
