# Apple Podcasts review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Podcast distribution and subscription platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Apple Podcasts is a podcast listening and discovery service. Creators can submit a show through Apple Podcasts Connect using a hosted RSS feed, or create certain subscription shows through the Apple Podcasters Program; Apple validates and reviews shows before availability.

## Editorial verdict

Apple Podcasts is an important distribution destination when a show wants to reach listeners in Apple’s podcast apps and catalog. A creator using an external host can submit an RSS feed without treating Apple as the place that stores the original master files. Paid subscriptions are a separate business decision: in the United States the Apple Podcasters Program has a US$19.99 annual fee, and Apple states that subscriptions require review and published subscriber audio.

## Separate hosting from directory distribution

A podcast usually needs an enclosure feed that points to episode files and metadata. Apple’s creator documentation allows a show to be submitted with an RSS feed hosted by a third party. In that arrangement, the podcast host is still responsible for the feed and source media; Apple is a place listeners can discover and play the show.

Before submission, check that the feed, artwork, episode titles and contact details are correct. Apple’s instructions ask creators to confirm content rights, territory availability and whether the public feed may be included in the Apple Podcasts Catalog API. Treat those choices as part of your distribution plan, not a checkbox to rush through.


## Expect validation and review

Apple says every show must pass technical validations and a review process. That means a prepared podcast listing is not proof it has been accepted or published. Resolve feed warnings, verify that the intended trailer or first episode appears, and check the public show page once Apple makes it available.

The RSS feed can override show metadata entered directly in Apple Podcasts Connect. Keep the source feed as the system of record when your host provides it, and confirm that title, author, description and episode details match. If the feed does not validate, work from the warning rather than creating duplicate listings.


## Budget for paid subscriptions only if they fit

Apple lists a US$19.99 annual fee for the Apple Podcasters Program. Its availability table distinguishes this membership from free audio podcast distribution. A creator should decide whether subscription benefits such as ad-free listening or additional content are worth the fee, the production work and the additional customer support obligations.

A paid podcast still needs consistent content, clear benefit descriptions, a reasonable release schedule and a cancellation path. Apple’s documentation says subscriptions must be reviewed and at least one subscriber-audio episode published before the subscription is available. Do not budget subscription revenue as guaranteed income, and confirm the current commission, tax and payout terms before setting a price.


## Keep ownership and publishing records clear

For a show hosted elsewhere, retain access to the original RSS account and keep a backup of the audio and artwork. The person who can edit the feed may be able to change or remove episodes from distribution. Set up creator roles intentionally and avoid sharing an Apple Account password with a whole production team.

Document the show’s content rights, guest releases and any third-party music permissions before publishing. If a show is later moved to a new host, test the feed redirect and confirm that Apple is reading the expected source. This review is a guide to Apple’s published workflow, not an assertion that a particular show was submitted or approved.


## Features and decision criteria

- Submit a hosted RSS feed in Apple Podcasts Connect or use a participating host
- Technical validation and Apple review precede public availability
- Creators choose show metadata, territories, release timing and public feed distribution
- US Apple Podcasters Program membership fee is US$19.99 per year for paid subscriptions

## Pricing considerations

The annual fee applies to the Apple Podcasters Program, not ordinary free RSS distribution. Subscription availability, taxes, revenue share and payout terms should be confirmed in the current program agreement and Apple Podcasts Connect for the creator’s region.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to pay Apple to list a free podcast?

Apple’s submission workflow accepts a show’s third-party hosted RSS feed. The cited US annual fee is for membership in the Apple Podcasters Program, used for paid subscriptions.

### Does submitting a show make it instantly available?

No. Apple requires technical validation and a review process before the show is available; RSS feed errors must be resolved.

### Does Apple host my original RSS podcast files?

The submission guide describes RSS distribution through a third-party hosting provider. Keep the host account and original media under your control.
