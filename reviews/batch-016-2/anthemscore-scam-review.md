## Desktop editions differ in output and editing scope

A musician who needs an editable interchange file should not assume the lowest edition provides every output mentioned elsewhere on the page.

## Rhythmic structure is different from detected sound events

The homepage describes adjusting downbeats, measures, time signatures, tempo and beat counts. Those are important because a collection of detected pitches is not yet a readable score. A performed passage can admit more than one notation choice, and a mechanical transcription may not communicate the phrasing or structure the musician wants. Define the intended notation and inspect the measure organization as carefully as the pitch content.

A proposed pilot would include a passage with a known rhythmic pattern and a deliberate change in structure. These are checks to perform, not audio analyzed here.
