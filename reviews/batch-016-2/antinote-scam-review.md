# Antinote review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-29  
**Category:** Mac scratchpad and temporary notes  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Antinote advertises a temporary Mac scratchpad with calculations, capture and export. This review examines one-time licensing, platform limits, ephemeral-note retention, OCR, clipboard capture, optional sync, extensions and ambiguous mobile-release references.

## Antinote is designed as a temporary scratchpad

Antinote's saved pages describe a Mac application for short notes, scratch calculations and fragments that do not necessarily belong in a permanent knowledge system. A tool optimized for temporary text may deliberately omit folders or other structures that a long-term research archive needs. The saved descriptions do not establish every exclusion or stopping behavior. This review has not processed an image or monitored clipboard changes, so it does not claim that the result is error-free or appropriate for every sensitive task.

For a pilot, use non-sensitive notes and inspect the behavior across the actual supported devices and versions.
