# Clicky review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Website analytics platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Clicky is a web analytics service with real-time visitor activity, content and referral reports, segmentation, dashboards and API access. The official pricing page lists a free tier for one website and 3,000 daily page views; paid plans start at US$9.99 monthly, with heatmaps and uptime monitoring reserved for Pro Plus and higher.

## Editorial verdict

Clicky may suit a site owner who wants visitor-level, real-time analytics and reports that are easier to inspect during active campaigns. Its free allowance is limited by daily page views, and paid tiers increase website and traffic capacity. Buyers should review data retention, privacy requirements and the difference between traffic analytics and uptime monitoring; heatmaps and uptime alerts are not included in every paid tier.

## Real-time analytics for active website monitoring

Clicky offers web analytics centered on live visitor activity and site reporting. The pricing page lists individual visitor actions, real-time data, content, searches, referrers, basic segmentation, dashboards, API access and widgets. Its live visitor stream can help a marketer observe how a campaign or launch is arriving while it is happening, while reports give a longer view of traffic sources and engagement.

The product also offers visitor segmentation, path analysis, outbound-link and download tracking, email reports and data exports. These features can answer practical questions such as which pages visitors view before leaving or which campaign referrals lead to downloads. They do not replace a carefully defined measurement plan: establish consent, event definitions and campaign parameters before relying on the resulting numbers.


## Compare traffic limits before choosing a tier

The free plan lists one website and 3,000 daily page views. Pro lists 10 sites and 30,000 combined daily page views at US$9.99 monthly or US$79.99 annually. Pro Plus lists the same site and page-view limits at US$14.99 monthly or US$119.99 annually, and adds heatmaps and uptime monitoring. Pro Platinum lists 30 sites and 100,000 daily page views for US$19.99 monthly or US$159.99 annually.

A custom tier is listed for as many as 1,000 sites and 20 million daily page views, with price varying. Clicky also advertises a 21-day premium trial for new accounts with up to three sites and one million total daily page views. Estimate combined traffic across properties and check what happens if the site exceeds its allowance.


## Understand the difference between analytics and monitoring

Clicky’s basic analytics describe visitors, actions, pages, referrers and engagement. Its premium feature table separately lists uptime monitoring and heatmaps, both requiring Pro Plus or higher. A team evaluating Pro should not assume those visual and availability features are included just because it is a paid tier.

Heatmaps can show where users click on pages and can be segmented according to the vendor’s pricing description. Uptime monitoring answers a different question: whether a site is responding. If availability alerts are important, confirm check intervals, notification channels and what counts as downtime before treating the plan as an operational monitor.


## Review history, exports and privacy settings

Clicky says free accounts are limited to 30 days of history and paid plans have indefinite history for most data, while individual visitor actions are guaranteed for six months. Its page describes CSV and XML data exports. Confirm retention and export behavior for your selected account and keep a local copy of reports needed for longer-term business records.

Visitor analytics can involve personal or sensitive information. The pricing page lists custom data fields such as usernames or email addresses, so administrators should evaluate data minimization, consent, access controls and applicable privacy rules before sending identifiers. Disable or avoid collecting data you do not need.


## Who should evaluate Clicky?

Clicky may suit small publishers, marketers and site operators who want a live view of traffic and an approachable set of reports. It can be particularly useful for campaign monitoring when the free or paid traffic cap matches actual volume. Organizations with advanced data warehouse needs, complex identity resolution or strict compliance requirements should validate those capabilities independently.

This review uses Clicky’s public plan and feature documentation. Roseram did not test tracking accuracy or compare the analytics output against another measurement system. Validate event counts, consent behavior and exports on a test property before depending on the dashboard for business decisions.

## Pricing considerations

Clicky advertises a free 21-day premium trial for new accounts, with up to three sites and one million combined daily page views during the trial. The pricing page says paid annual plans save 33%; verify current plan limits and currency before subscribing.
