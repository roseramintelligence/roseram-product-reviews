# Fiverr review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Freelance services marketplace  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Fiverr is a marketplace where buyers order packaged freelance services and sellers deliver projects through platform workflows. Clients pay a service fee at checkout, while freelancers receive 80% of the purchase amount before applicable taxes and payout considerations.

## Editorial verdict

Fiverr can work when a buyer wants to compare clearly scoped service packages and a seller can describe a repeatable deliverable. The buyer fee and freelancer revenue share both affect the total economics: buyers pay a 5.5% fee plus an extra fixed amount on smaller orders, while sellers receive 80% of the purchase amount. Confirm scope, revisions, ownership and delivery dates inside the order before payment.

## Write a scope before choosing a service package

Fiverr’s packaged service format can make it easier to compare deliverables, timelines and seller portfolios. A buyer should still write a short brief: the output required, source materials, file format, deadline, review rounds and what counts as acceptance. A low starting price may describe only a narrow slice of a larger project.

For a seller, a clear package reduces the chance of repeated unpaid revisions or different assumptions about what “complete” means. State what the buyer must supply, how many changes are included and what an additional request costs. Keep important scope decisions inside the Fiverr order so both sides can refer back to one record.


## Calculate both sides of the marketplace fee

The current Fiverr payment guide says buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus an additional US$3.50 on orders below US$200. Fiverr’s earnings guide says sellers receive 80% of the purchase amount. These are separate effects: the buyer’s total rises above the seller’s listed price, and the seller’s net amount is below it.

Before accepting a job, estimate seller net after the platform share, taxes and payout or currency costs. Before ordering, compare the total at checkout rather than only the advertised package. A small order can be disproportionately affected by a fixed fee. Confirm the current policy in the signed-in transaction because help pages and prior order examples may differ.


## Use order milestones to reduce misunderstandings

For custom work, the buyer and seller should agree on inputs, milestones and an approval path before the delivery deadline. Ask a question when the listing leaves a key detail unclear. If the project needs source files, editable layers or commercial usage rights, include that requirement in the package discussion instead of assuming it is included.

Review the delivered files promptly and compare them with the written acceptance points. If something is missing, explain the specific gap and the requested correction. Keep sensitive credentials out of chat and use limited-access test accounts when a service requires access to a website or workspace.


## Treat marketplace protection as a process, not a result

A platform’s payment and order tools help keep a record of the transaction, but they cannot guarantee the quality or business effect of each deliverable. Check seller identity, portfolio samples and recent communication before hiring. For a large project, consider dividing it into smaller milestones that prove the work is on track.

Fiverr says completed order earnings and available balances appear in the seller Earnings page, with withdrawals and financial documents handled there. Optional early payout is described for eligible sellers at a 1% fee. Confirm payout timing, identity checks and payment-provider availability before depending on funds for a bill. This article is an independent buyer guide, not a customer review or fraud conclusion.


## Features and decision criteria

- Service packages and custom offers for buyer-defined work
- Checkout service fee is shown before client confirms payment
- Freelancers receive 80% of the purchase amount for completed orders
- Earnings, withdrawal methods and optional faster payout are managed from seller account

## Pricing considerations

Fiverr has multiple help pages with different older checkout examples; the current payment guide cited here states a US$3.50 additional fee for orders under US$200. The checkout page shows the applicable fee before confirmation. Confirm taxes, currency conversion, refunds and payout methods for the specific account.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a buyer pay in Fiverr service fees?

Fiverr’s current payment guide states a 5.5% service fee and an additional US$3.50 for orders under US$200; the buyer sees the fee before confirming.

### How much does a freelancer keep from an order?

Fiverr’s earnings guide says freelancers earn 80% of each completed order purchase amount, including extras and tips, before taxes.

### Can Fiverr guarantee a successful freelance business?

No. A marketplace can provide listings and transaction tools, but it does not guarantee clients, profitable pricing or ongoing work.
