# Figma review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-24  
**Category:** AI prototyping and product design  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

This assessment focuses on Figma Make within the broader Figma product family. The official pages describe prompt-driven creation and plan-dependent access. A design collaboration purchase and an application operating decision should be evaluated separately.

## Editorial verdict

Figma publishes product and pricing information that supports a scoped desk review. We did not create a Make project, connect data or test a code handoff. The material inspected does not establish fraud or guarantee that a prototype satisfies an organization’s production requirements.

## Compare Figma Make with the task, not the entire Figma brand

A team may use Figma for several different jobs. Specify whether you are buying collaborative design, a functional prototype or a path toward maintained application code. The same company can offer products with different boundaries. Our review concerns the Make comparison, not a claim that an AI workspace replaces every design and handoff capability in the broader suite.

Start with a small interaction and define what makes it useful. A clickable flow, a page using synthetic records and a deployed service handling private accounts have different acceptance requirements. Ask the vendor to demonstrate the scope you actually need. We have not established equivalent production behavior merely from the fact that a demo responds to user input.


## Seat type and AI allowance belong in the purchase brief

Figma’s plan comparison distinguishes seat types and AI allowances. Inventory who needs to design, inspect, comment and build before estimating the team bill. Confirm the exact entitlement for Make in the current plan rather than assuming every paid seat has identical access. We do not reconstruct a single current total from a mixture of seat options.

For a proposed trial, ask two teammates to perform their real roles using harmless material. Record where access is required and how changes are reviewed. An organization that purchases more capability than it needs can overspend; one that purchases too little can interrupt a handoff. The review did not inspect an account or verify an entitlement boundary.


## A handoff should preserve intent as well as appearance

Ask the person maintaining the final codebase to inspect a representative output. Can they identify state changes, dependencies and the source of repeated interface elements? Can a visual revision be traced to a reviewable code change? These are proposed evaluation questions, not a claim that Figma Make generates a particular architecture or fails a repository test.

Also separate synthetic data from a live connection. Decide who owns credentials, what the prototype may read and which changes are authorized. A realistic-looking table does not establish correct record access. Keep the design acceptance and the operational acceptance explicit so that a successful visual review does not silently become approval to launch.

For an established team, include an existing design constraint in the brief instead of starting from an empty canvas. Choose a component with an error state and a long text label, then inspect both the visual result and the representation the maintainer will receive. Ask whether the proposed correction can be reviewed without unintentionally changing other uses of that component. This reveals a more useful buying question than whether an initial mockup looks polished. We did not test a Figma component or repository; the exercise is our suggested way to examine compatibility with the team’s own review and maintenance responsibilities.
