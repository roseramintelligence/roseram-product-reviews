# FastStone Capture review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Windows screenshot and recording tools  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

FastStone Capture combines Windows screenshots, scrolling capture, annotation and screen recording. The official page also describes editable capture files and common image and video outputs. The distinction between a reusable working file and a final shared export is important for recurring documentation.

## Editorial verdict

Evaluate FastStone Capture for annotated instructions and short demonstrations that need an integrated capture workflow. Use the trial to inspect exported results and recording audio. A capture tool cannot decide which visible information is appropriate to share.

## Capture an instruction that someone can follow

A screenshot is useful when it helps the reader identify the next action. Capture one representative task and add only the annotations that explain it. Too many arrows and highlights can obscure the interface the reader is trying to understand.

Ask someone unfamiliar with the task to interpret the exported image. Their uncertainty is a better guide to annotation quality than the number of editing effects available. This review did not create or assess a sample capture.


## Scrolling output needs a completeness check

A long page may contain a fixed header, repeated elements or content that loads while scrolling. Inspect the beginning, middle and end of a saved capture instead of assuming a long image includes everything correctly.

Retain another record when the material is important. A screenshot captures a particular view and moment; it does not preserve the underlying page behavior or establish the accuracy of its contents.


## Separate a working document from delivery

Keep an editable source when a guide will need revisions. A flattened image can be convenient for sharing while making later corrections harder. Name the working file and the final export clearly enough that a teammate knows which to edit.

Open a delivery file outside the capture application before distributing it. Check legibility at the size the recipient will actually see. Source and export management are part of the documentation process, not an automatic result of taking a screenshot.


## Trial the full recording and sharing route

Record a short nonsensitive demonstration with the intended audio inputs. Replay the saved file from beginning to end and confirm the destination before enabling repeated capture or sharing actions. Avoid testing with private account information on screen.

This source-based assessment does not benchmark recording quality, validate redaction or audit the installer. Compare the current paid terms with the actual frequency and complexity of the capture work before purchasing.


## Features and decision criteria

- Window and region capture
- Scrolling screenshots
- Annotation and editable capture files
- Screen recording and common exports

## Pricing considerations

The official product page lists a 30-day trial and a one-time purchase of US$29.95 at the review date. Verify the current order terms and intended license quantity before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the trial a permanent free license?

No. The page distinguishes a 30-day trial from the paid purchase.

### Will an exported image preserve editable annotations?

Do not assume that. The publisher distinguishes its editable FSC format from ordinary delivery formats.

### Should recording audio be checked separately?

Yes. Test the intended microphone and speaker inputs and inspect the saved output.
