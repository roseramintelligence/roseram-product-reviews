# Semrush review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-18  
**Category:** Search engine optimization and digital visibility software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Semrush provides SEO, keyword research, rank tracking, site audit and competitive analysis tools, with newer plans combining traditional search visibility and AI-search reporting. Plans cap monitored sites, tracked keywords and daily reporting capacity.

## Editorial verdict

Semrush offers a deep research suite, but its plan structure now spans several toolkits and add-ons. Buyers should match the exact SEO or AI-visibility package to the workflow, model keyword and website limits and check the full monthly bill before moving recurring reports into the platform.

## Semrush review: search and competitive research in one toolkit

Semrush offers tools for keyword research, position tracking, backlink analysis, site audits and competitive visibility. Its current plan experience also presents AI-search visibility alongside traditional SEO packages. For a marketing team, the platform can provide a shared place to investigate search demand, track technical issues and prepare recurring reports.

A collection of research metrics does not make rankings or traffic predictable. Use Semrush to identify hypotheses, then verify important results in first-party analytics and search-console data. Record the date, geography and device behind each comparison. Competitor estimates are modeled data, not audited traffic figures, and should be described accordingly in client presentations.


## Semrush toolkit pricing and tracked limits

Semrush's current pricing page lists an SEO package at $117.33 per month billed annually or $139 monthly, with five monitored websites and 500 daily tracked keywords. Its combined SEO plus AI-search offering lists Starter at $165.17 monthly on annual billing or $199 month to month, and Pro+ at $248.17 annual equivalent or $299 monthly. Advanced is custom. The displayed plans include different website, keyword and AI-prompt capacities.

The company now separates multiple toolkits, so compare the specific package rather than assuming an older Pro/Guru/Business label covers the same feature set. Additional user seats and report add-ons appear separately. Make a list of sites, keyword targets, daily updates, AI prompts and users, then calculate the actual tier. Confirm whether the renewal uses annual billing, what happens at the limit and which add-ons are included.


## Semrush reports, estimates and campaign measurement

Semrush combines data such as search positions, keyword metrics, backlinks and estimated competitor visibility. Its reporting tools can help a team notice movement and identify technical or content opportunities. Such data is most useful when the analyst keeps the scope visible: country, device, search engine, date range and whether the value is observed or estimated.

For SEO decisions, pair third-party estimates with the site's own Search Console and analytics records. Validate a keyword's intent before treating search volume as demand for the company's product. After fixing a crawl or metadata issue, document the change and wait for the search engine to recrawl rather than promising an immediate ranking increase. A dashboard can organize evidence, but it cannot guarantee a position or revenue result.


## Semrush account access, API and cost governance

Semrush's pricing page lists API access at the higher Advanced tier and offers separately priced user and report add-ons. The developer portal documents API methods and data limits. Agencies should account for both the service subscription and the number of client projects and users who need access to reports.

Assign a clear owner for client workspaces, credentials and report exports. Do not share a single account login across staff. Verify the API endpoint's allowed use and unit consumption before building an automated report that refreshes frequently. Check what data is retained in exported files and remove access when a client engagement ends.

## Semrush SEO and AI-search packages are not interchangeable

Semrush's current pricing page separates an SEO package from combined SEO plus AI Search plans. The SEO tier lists five monitored websites and 500 daily tracked keywords. Starter combines SEO allowances with AI visibility features, including daily tracked prompts and AI reports; Pro+ increases the website, keyword and prompt limits; Advanced is custom and adds higher-scale reporting and API capabilities. A former plan name may no longer describe the same bundle.

When comparing a quote with an older article, check the toolkit name and the exact included limits instead of matching only on words such as Pro, Guru or Business. Save the pricing table shown on the checkout date and record the currency and annual or monthly term. The features and rates summarized here are a dated snapshot of the official page, not a promise that every account or region will see the same offer.


## Semrush report add-ons and team-seat costs

The current pricing page lists additional users from $45 per month, a Lead Generation add-on at $90 per month, Base Report at $10 per month and Pro Report at $20 per month. Those additions can materially change the cost of what first looked like a single-seat analytics subscription. Report scheduling, integrations, white-labeling and shared access are also described as tier-dependent capabilities.

Before sending a proposal to a client, count who needs a login, which reports require external integrations and whether a branded PDF or shared link is necessary. Add these items to the base plan's renewal total. Do not promise a deliverable until you have confirmed that its required report format and sharing permissions are included in the actual subscription.


## Semrush keyword research should guide a testable content plan

Keyword tools help marketers discover queries, estimate relative demand and compare competing pages. They do not reveal with certainty what every searcher wants or how a particular page will rank. A phrase with substantial estimated volume can attract visitors who are not potential customers; an important niche phrase may be valuable even when a third-party estimate is small.

For each target, write down the searcher's likely task, the page that should answer it and what useful evidence the page will add. Compare the tool's geography and database with the market you serve. After publishing, use first-party Search Console impressions, clicks and query data to decide whether the page is matching intent. Avoid repeating the product name in every heading or promising a number-one ranking: neither keyword density nor a paid analytics tier guarantees placement.


## Semrush site audits and technical SEO workflow

Site Audit can identify crawlable issues, but a finding is a prompt for investigation rather than an automatic diagnosis. A missing title, redirect, canonical tag or blocked resource may be intentional or may interact with the site's framework in ways that require engineering review. The package and project limits also determine how many websites and reports the team can maintain.

Set a recurring crawl frequency that matches the release cycle and site size. Review a sample of warnings manually, separate errors from informational notices and document the fix before closing the item. For important changes, validate with the site's own logs, Search Console and a browser check. Keep a change log so that an audit trend is not mistaken for a cause-and-effect result.


## Semrush AI visibility tracking has a measurement boundary

Semrush's new bundles include AI-search visibility reporting and prompt tracking. The pricing page describes specific daily prompt allowances and reporting capacities by plan. Such a report measures the queries, services and settings used in that tracker; it is not a complete record of every answer a person may receive from an AI system. Results can depend on the selected prompt, market, time and reporting method.

Keep a stable prompt set and store collection dates if you are comparing changes over time. Distinguish a recorded brand mention from referral traffic, a customer lead or a purchase. Where the business uses an AI visibility report in a client presentation, explain its scope and limits rather than presenting it as total market share. Pair it with observable site visits and conversions when evaluating a campaign.


## Semrush API and recurring report governance

Semrush describes API data integration as part of its higher-level Advanced offering, while the developer site documents available API methods and usage. This can help an organization build recurring reports, but it introduces questions about API units, refresh cadence, account credentials and the downstream use of retrieved data. Confirm the access level and contract terms before automating data collection.

Keep tokens out of client-side bundles and shared spreadsheets. Assign an owner to monitor consumption and errors, and design the workflow to stop or degrade gracefully if the API is unavailable. Label the collection time and original metric definitions in each export. If a report is delivered to a customer, ensure the data is used within Semrush's current terms and that the customer's access ends when the engagement does.


## Semrush trial, cancellation and refunds

Semrush's pricing page currently advertises a seven-day free trial and says plans can generally be canceled, upgraded or downgraded unless custom signed terms apply. Its online purchase refund policy says cancellation requests must be submitted while logged into the account and completed through its form. A trial that is not canceled before its end can convert to a paid subscription under the applicable checkout terms.

The published policy limits its one-time seven-day money-back guarantee to qualifying initial annual subscriptions and incremental add-on purchases made directly from Semrush.com by credit card; month-to-month subscriptions are excluded. The customer must explicitly request a refund within the stated period. Because the rules vary for custom contracts, purchase channels and plan terms, save the checkout and policy version and submit cancellation using the account that paid. This is a summary of published terms, not a determination about any individual's refund eligibility.


## A Semrush pilot that separates data from outcomes

Choose a small set of representative tasks: research a target query, inspect a competitor page, crawl a test website and schedule one report. Note how long each step takes, whether the metric is estimated or observed, which location was selected and what tier or add-on is required. Compare the output with a first-party source such as Search Console or site analytics where the data overlaps.

A useful pilot should also test limits: add the expected number of tracked keywords, a second user and a report that uses a required integration. Check what warning appears near the quota and what happens when it is reached. This reveals whether the planned package fits the team's actual workflow, rather than whether a polished demo can produce an interesting chart.


## Semrush SCAM Review verdict: substantial toolkit, verify bundle and bill

Semrush's official pages describe a broad SEO and digital-visibility service, including keyword research, technical auditing, rank tracking, reporting and AI-search visibility. The pricing page lists concrete project, keyword and prompt limits alongside paid user and report add-ons. The online refund policy explains a limited refund window for eligible annual online purchases. The sources reviewed do not substantiate a scam allegation.

For a buyer, the central risk is purchasing a package that does not match the intended workload or reading estimated metrics as guaranteed results. Check the current plan name, sites, keywords, prompts, API rights, seats and add-ons at checkout. Save the renewal amount and cancellation route.
