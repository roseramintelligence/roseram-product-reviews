# Shopify review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** E-commerce and point-of-sale platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Shopify provides hosted tools for online commerce and in-person sales. The right plan depends on sales channels, staff, payment options, markets and the full cost of operating a store.

## Editorial verdict

Shopify publishes plan prices and explains that payment options and features vary by country. This is source-based buyer guidance; verify the rates, promotions and transaction fees available to your business before committing.

## Shopify review: calculate store economics before choosing a tier

A commerce platform should be evaluated against the store you intend to operate, not a hypothetical business with every channel enabled. List product count, expected order volume, staff roles, shipping regions and payment methods. Include online and physical sales only if you expect to use both. Build a small test catalog and take a sample order through checkout without charging a real customer. Check product options, tax settings, shipping rules, confirmation messages and how a staff member finds the resulting order.

A polished storefront can hide operational costs that appear after launch. Decide how payment fees, apps, themes, shipping labels, returns and staff access affect the margin on an ordinary order. Add those items to a monthly estimate before comparing tiers. Keep the calculation tied to realistic order values and payment methods available in your region. A platform does not guarantee traffic, sales or profit.


## Shopify plans, staff accounts and what the published price omits

Shopify’s official US pricing page showed Basic at $29 per month with annual billing, or $39 month to month; Grow at $79 annually billed or $105 monthly; and Advanced at $299 annually billed or $399 monthly when checked on 2026-11-16. The page also describes staff-account allowances and features that vary across tiers. This is a dated observation from a US pricing page, not a universal quote. Local currency, tax and feature availability may differ. Plus pricing is substantially higher and should be checked directly for a particular business.

Do not compare subscription prices without modeling payment processing. Shopify lists different card rates by plan and notes that a separate third-party transaction fee may apply when a merchant uses a provider other than Shopify Payments. Availability of Shopify Payments varies by country. Estimate fees using your actual average order value, sales mix and payment methods, then confirm the current rate and all conditions on the pricing page. Include paid apps and point-of-sale costs if the store needs them.


## Shopify catalog, checkout and order operations

Create representative products with the options customers really choose. Test inventory changes, a discount, an out-of-stock item and a refund in a safe test setup. Follow the order from checkout through fulfillment and customer notification. Check what happens when a shipping address is incomplete or a payment needs review. These steps reveal whether the staff workflow matches the business’s actual policies before a customer depends on it.

Shopify’s plan comparison describes online storefront functions and selling through channels, while some capabilities depend on location or plan. Validate each required channel with a test listing and confirm inventory is not unintentionally oversold across channels. If you use a custom theme or app, assign someone to maintain it and document the consequence of removing that dependency.


## Shopify migration, domains and store data portability

Before moving an operating store, inventory the domain, product records, customer data, order history, URL paths, policies and integrations that must remain available. Import a small sample and compare it field by field with the current store. Confirm which URLs need redirects and test them before announcing a cutover. Keep the existing storefront accessible until orders and customer support have been reconciled in the new system.

Ask what can be exported and in which format, then open the files outside the platform. Identify information that requires a separate backup or a third-party app. Preserve customer consent and opt-out state during any migration; do not re-enroll people in marketing merely because the data was imported. If you rely on a specialist developer, retain access to the domain, source code and billing account rather than making one contractor the only recovery path.


## Shopify alternatives and a fair cost comparison

Compare Shopify with another hosted commerce service and, if relevant, an open-source or existing site option. Use the same sample catalog, payment method, shipping rule and return case. Compare total monthly costs, transaction fees, theme flexibility, administration and support. A self-hosted system may offer control while shifting security updates and uptime work to the merchant. A hosted system can simplify operations while imposing platform and transaction charges. The correct comparison includes the work your team is prepared to own.

Ask each provider to demonstrate your most important requirement and show what happens when an order fails or an account is cancelled. Verify whether a preferred payment provider is supported in the countries where you sell. Avoid claims that one platform always converts better; measure your own checkout with an appropriate, privacy-respecting test.


## Shopify payments, privacy and buyer safeguards

Review the current merchant terms and payment-provider requirements. Confirm who can change payment settings, issue refunds, export customer records and install apps. Limit admin access and enable available account protections. If customer information is collected, define retention and marketing-consent practices before launching. Shopify’s published statements about platform compliance do not replace the merchant’s obligations or a jurisdiction-specific review.

Test the recovery path for the domain and store administrator. Keep a copy of business-critical product and order information in a location your team controls. Record the steps needed to pause sales and communicate with customers if a store becomes unavailable. This diligence is not evidence that Shopify is fraudulent; it reduces avoidable operational surprises.


## Who should consider Shopify?

Shopify is worth assessing when a business needs a hosted store and wants product, order and sales-channel operations in one service. A solo seller can compare Basic with expected fees; a team should count staff accounts and include the monthly payment cadence in its budget. Before scaling, test the store’s real catalog and fulfillment process, verify the supported payment methods for the seller’s location and calculate app and transaction costs.

The publisher’s pages support a clear description of Shopify’s plans and commerce features. They do not promise a merchant’s sales outcome. Choose the service only after checking the contract, fee schedule, export route and operational responsibilities for your own store. The “SCAM Review” heading is a buyer-diligence format and not a conclusion of fraud.


## Features and decision criteria

- Hosted online storefront and product catalog
- Order, inventory and customer management
- Online and in-person selling options

## Pricing considerations

Official US pricing displayed on Shopify’s pricing page when checked 2026-11-16. Card-processing and third-party transaction fees are separate and depend on plan, payment provider and location. Promotions may apply to new customers. Prices, tax and payment availability vary by country; verify checkout for your store.
