# Signal review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Private messaging  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Signal is a messaging app. Its support guide separates ways to preserve message history, with options dependent on device and setup.

## Editorial verdict

The publisher documents device and message-history options. Check app-store identity and the sender of any link that asks you to install software or supply a recovery key.

## Signal message backup and restore when a phone changes

Decide what the team must retain before replacing a phone. A conversation that contains a delivery commitment deserves different handling from a casual exchange. Read the **Signal backup and restore** instructions for the old and new devices and choose the documented method that fits.

Rehearse with dummy conversations and an image. Keep any required recovery information in a place the owner can reach independently of the old phone. Verify what actually arrived on the new device before clearing the old one.


## Signal group handoff and an agreed record of decisions

Ask the members where an approved commitment belongs. For a group that uses messages to coordinate work, make a written record of decisions in a system the participants have agreed to use. Keep the scope small and agree who may see it.

Check that a new group administrator can perform the agreed *handoff procedure* with a sample group. A tool can make the conversation convenient. A team still has to decide how somebody finds a decision when the person who made it is out of reach.


## Features and decision criteria

- Private messaging workflows
- Documented backup and restore choices
- Device-dependent transfer options
