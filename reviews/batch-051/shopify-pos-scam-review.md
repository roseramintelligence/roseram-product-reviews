# Shopify POS review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Retail point-of-sale and omnichannel commerce  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Shopify POS connects in-person retail sales with Shopify’s online store, back office, inventory, customer records and payment tools. In the United States, Shopify’s current POS pricing page lists base plans from $39 per month and POS Pro as an additional $89 per location per month billed yearly. Payment processing, hardware, plan selection and location add-ons affect the total; confirm current regional terms before purchase.

## Editorial verdict

Shopify POS is worth comparing when a retailer wants online and in-person sales, inventory and customer data managed in one commerce system. A small seller should check whether the features included with its base plan are enough before adding POS Pro. Multi-location businesses should model staff permissions, returns, inventory movement, payment processing, hardware and location fees together, then test the complete checkout and reconciliation workflow in their own region.

## Understand what Shopify POS includes

Shopify presents POS as a point-of-sale system connected to its broader commerce platform. The product page describes in-person checkout alongside online selling, orders, inventory, customer information, staff and analytics. For a retailer already using Shopify online, this unified approach may reduce duplicate catalogs and end-of-day reconciliation. For a store starting fresh, compare the whole commerce subscription rather than treating POS as a standalone register.

Make a list of the tasks your staff performs at the counter: barcode lookup, discounts, exchanges, returns, gift cards, customer profiles, pickup, shipping an item to a customer and checking stock at another location. Then ask which plan and add-on support each workflow. A vendor feature label is not proof that a workflow fits your tax, payment, hardware or store configuration.


## Calculate the full monthly cost by location

Shopify’s US POS pricing page listed Basic at $39 per month, Grow at $105 and Advanced at $399 when checked. It also listed POS Pro for an additional $89 per location per month billed yearly, and Plus at $2,300 per month on a three-year term. The page describes different payment-processing rates by plan. These are current page figures, not a quote for every merchant or country.

Estimate the total across all locations: base plan, POS Pro locations, card-present and online processing, third-party provider fees, hardware, apps, taxes and any promotional terms. Shopify says the total POS cost combines the plan, transaction fees and POS Pro locations. Confirm whether payment rates apply only when using Shopify Payments and how your actual average order value changes the cost.


## Choose carefully between casual POS and POS Pro

Shopify says paid plans include casual in-person selling, such as taking in-person payments, capturing basic customer information and limited returns or exchanges. POS Pro adds staff roles and permissions, richer customer profiles, inventory tools, deeper retail reports and advanced omnichannel workflows. A simple pop-up or one-person shop may not need every advanced option; permanent stores with more staff, inventory or returns may value the added controls.

The POS Pro charge is per location, so model each store separately. Decide where permissions, inventory counts, purchase orders, low-stock alerts, loyalty insights or cross-location exchanges are genuinely needed. Pilot a representative location and collect feedback from cashiers and managers before enabling the feature set across the business.


## Test payments, hardware and reconciliation

A checkout system must work under real store conditions. Test supported phones or tablets, card readers, receipt printers, barcode scanners, cash drawers, connectivity failures and payment settlement. Shopify’s own pricing page notes that a smartphone or tablet and a Shopify card reader are needed to start, with other accessories optional. Verify regional hardware and payment availability before ordering equipment.

Run a realistic end-of-day close: compare POS orders, refunds, taxes, cash, card payments, online orders and inventory changes with your accounting process. Include a return at another store, partial refund, offline scenario and split tender if these apply. Clarify which reports are included in your plan and whether additional accounting or inventory apps introduce separate subscriptions.


## Who should consider Shopify POS?

Retailers already using Shopify online may find a connected register and shared inventory attractive, especially when they operate pop-ups or multiple stores. A business that only needs an inexpensive card reader, uses a different ecommerce platform or has complex specialty retail requirements should compare alternatives and integrations before switching. The right choice depends on regional payment support, staff workflow, hardware and complete cost per location.

This buyer guide summarizes Shopify’s public product and US pricing pages. It is not an independent payment-security audit or a hands-on test of the merchant’s hardware. Confirm current rates, hardware compatibility, plan terms and regional availability directly with Shopify before migrating sales operations.


## Features and decision criteria

- In-person and online orders managed from a shared Shopify back office
- Inventory, order, customer, staff and reporting features vary by plan and POS Pro location
- Hardware and payment processing options integrated with the Shopify ecosystem
- Additional integrations and customization through apps and partner tools

## Pricing considerations

US pricing shown on Shopify’s current POS pricing page when checked. Fees vary by plan, payment provider, location and billing term; hardware, tax and add-ons may add cost. Rates are displayed as starting prices, and offers shown on the page may have eligibility conditions. Recheck the page and contract before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Shopify POS cost?

Shopify’s US pricing page lists base monthly plans beginning at $39, $105 and $399, with Plus shown at $2,300 per month on a three-year term. POS Pro is an additional $89 per location per month billed yearly. Processing fees, hardware, add-ons and region affect the total.

### Do I need POS Pro?

Shopify includes casual in-person selling features in paid plans and positions POS Pro as an upgrade for advanced staff, inventory, customer and omnichannel workflows. Compare your store’s needs per location before adding the fee.

### Can Shopify POS connect store and online sales?

Shopify describes a shared back office for in-person and online sales, including inventory, orders and customer information. Test the exact exchanges, pickup, returns, payment and location workflows you need.
