The materials highlight continuous internal updates designed to insulate customers from evolving Internet standards, citing features such as Google mobile-friendly ratings and customizable templates pre-filled with industry text.

## Visual Site Construction and Drag-and-Drop Tooling

The primary functional mechanism in Cabanova is a visual, browser-based drag-and-drop workspace. Users build web layouts by placing modular content blocks across digital canvases without modifying source HTML or CSS scripts directly. Promotional headlines claim access to over 300 business templates in certain marketing summaries, while package descriptions quote more than 1,000 template combinations pre-populated with starter copy covering 120 subject areas. The software also permits operators to create custom layouts completely from scratch or modify existing color schemes and typographic choices.

The builder workspace includes what the publisher labels as a Features Megapack. This modular palette allows designers to incorporate standard text containers, image galleries, video players, maps, and interactive elements like guestbooks, polls, counters, shapes, and forms. However, several items listed within this megapack, such as embedded Flash games, Picasa connections, Google+ tools, and Yahoo! maps, reflect web technologies that third-party vendors have deprecated or discontinued over the past decade. Buyers evaluating Cabanova should test whether these visual widgets function using modern web standards or whether they remain dormant legacy assets within the site builder toolbar.


## Hosting Allocation, Disk Quotas, and Bandwidth Rules

Cabanova bundles all site creation tools directly with proprietary cloud hosting, eliminating the need for external server configurations. Across its published tier lineup, the company categorizes service tiers into distinct named packages: Free, Express, Economy, Business, and Shop. The entry-level tiers place strict physical limits on site expansion. The Free and Express packages restrict the user to a maximum of 3 pages, with storage caps set at 50 megabytes on Free and 1 gigabyte on Express. The mid-tier Economy option raises capacity to 20 pages and 7 gigabytes of web space, while the higher-tier Business and Shop options provide unlimited pages alongside 10 gigabytes and 20 gigabytes of server storage respectively.

Regarding incoming network activity, Cabanova advertises that all service packages include unlimited traffic. However, the documentation appends a critical qualifying statement, noting that traffic is unrestricted only within reasonable limits. The public materials do not publish specific numeric thresholds defining what volume of visits or raw bandwidth consumption constitutes unreasonable traffic. Organizations anticipating sudden promotional traffic spikes or high-bandwidth video streaming must recognize that unmetered claims in shared environments are subject to vendor discretion and internal usage policies.


## Commercial Billing Cycles and Automatic Renewal Policies

A major evaluation area for subscription-based software is billing transparency and recurring contract terms. Cabanova's published pricing architecture displays recurring service costs listed in European currency (EUR), noting explicitly that base figures exclude regional sales taxes. The pricing matrix offers contract durations spanning one year, two years, and five years, advertising tiered percentage savings of 20 percent on two-year commitments and 30 percent on five-year prepayments. Subscriptions span pricing ranges from low monthly entry points under one euro per month for Express up to double-digit monthly tiers for full commercial shop capabilities.

Crucially, Cabanova documents an explicit Customer Protection Policy directly addressing user concerns regarding unexpected billing. According to this formal notice, all customer subscriptions operate on a strictly pre-paid basis and will not be extended automatically upon term completion. The vendor commits to delivering advance notification reminders to account owners so they can manually review and renew their packages without fearing unauthorized automatic credit card debits. This transparent policy protects consumers against indefinite billing cycles, though it places responsibility on the site owner to maintain active coverage to prevent website expiration.


## Domain Acquisition, Transfer Rules, and Mailboxes

A functional web identity requires custom domain integration. Cabanova's paid hosting tiers, specifically those running multi-year contracts, advertise the inclusion of a free custom domain registration valid across popular generic and country-code extensions such as .com, .net, .info, and .eu. Alongside the registered address, the vendor provisions up to 50 dedicated email accounts. This allows small business operators to run branded organizational mail without configuring independent third-party mail routing systems.

For organizations that already own an established web address, Cabanova provides a dedicated transfer workflow rather than external DNS pointing. The publisher explicitly outlines that moving a domain requires an Authorization-Code, also known as an EPP key, obtained from the current external registrar. Cabanova warns customers that completing this workflow fully re-registers the domain under Cabanova's administration. While Cabanova states it charges no administrative fee for the transfer process itself, the regular monthly plan fees apply, and users must confirm with their existing registrar that their contract permits immediate outward migration before initiating the transfer.


## Security Architecture and SSL Implementation

Security validation is a fundamental requirement for modern business websites. Cabanova states that all hosted sites, across standard paid tiers and baseline configurations, are secured using complimentary digital certificates issued by LetsEncrypt.org. Automated SSL provisioning ensures that web visitors access pages over secure HTTPS protocols, encrypting browser-to-server data transmission and satisfying standard browser privacy alerts that flag unencrypted HTTP destinations.

In addition to public transport security, Cabanova's builder features include account-level security toggles and administrative password protection options for individual web pages. These privacy tools allow site owners to create gated internal sections, members-only landing pages, or draft content hidden from public inspection without needing custom user database configuration. However, because publisher documentation does not detail granular enterprise security protocols, such as multi-factor login authentication or role-based staff permissions, administrators managing sensitive business records must assess whether single-password page locking meets their security standards.


## E-Commerce Architecture and Checkout Processing

For commercial retailers, Cabanova provides an integrated e-commerce suite housed within its specialized Shop package. Unlike the standard baseline packages which limit catalog listings to 3 demo products, the dedicated Shop edition advertises catalog capacity for up to 10,000 distinct items. The inventory management subsystem enables merchant users to organize inventory items into multi-tiered categories and sub-categories, upload item photographs, write detailed descriptive copy, and configure specific item variations such as individual clothing sizes and product colors.

The checkout system handles basic order fulfillment logic by allowing store managers to configure customized shipping and handling fees tied to regional deliveries. In terms of payment processing, the documentation states that the platform integrates several diverse payment pathways. These supported gateways include PayPal transactions, cash on delivery (COD) fulfillment options, and direct manual bank wire transfers. Because the vendor does not publish detailed information regarding recurring subscription billing, tokenized credit card vaults, or native point-of-sale synchronization, retailers requiring complex subscription checkout funnels should verify checkout flexibility prior to deployment.

## Limitations, Contradictions, and Buyer Risks

A rigorous desk evaluation requires analyzing systemic trade-offs and inconsistencies present within publisher documentation. The most evident friction point in Cabanova's current materials is the contradiction between its active pricing tiers and its published news feed. While the public pricing table prominently advertises an entry-level Free build option with 3 pages and 50 megabytes of storage, the accompanying platform blog distinctly lists titles announcing Free pacakge discontinued and The Cabanova XL version is discontinued. This discrepancy creates ambiguity regarding whether free accounts are fully operational or whether new accounts require paid tier subscriptions.

A second critical limitation centers on architectural exportability and lock-in. Cabanova operates entirely as a proprietary hosted environment. The publisher provides no documentation indicating that users can export raw HTML5 code, transfer database files, or migrate layout templates to self-hosted WordPress or independent Apache and Nginx web servers. If an organization outgrows Cabanova's 20-gigabyte storage ceiling or 10,000-product e-commerce boundary, migrating away requires manually recreating pages and rebuilding product databases on an external system. Furthermore, several bundled marketing integrations reference legacy services, signaling that certain internal dashboard widgets may not have received regular updates.


## Market Alternatives and Category Context

To position Cabanova within the broader landscape of web software, buyers should evaluate how traditional hosted builders compare against modern alternatives across three distinct categories: modern drag-and-drop platforms, open-source content management systems, and specialized headless platforms. Modern hosted platforms like Wix, Squarespace, and Weebly represent the closest functional peers to Cabanova, providing cloud-hosted drag-and-drop page assembly, integrated domain purchases, and bundled hosting tiers. These contemporary peers generally offer deeper app marketplaces and continuous updates, though often at higher recurring monthly price points.

Organizations that prioritize content ownership, code exportability, and unlimited technical flexibility frequently bypass hosted site builders entirely in favor of open-source frameworks like WordPress.org paired with standard cPanel hosting. Open-source solutions eliminate proprietary design lock-in and avoid storage caps imposed by hosted builders, though they require active management of server security, plugin updates, and manual SSL renewals. Meanwhile, modern enterprises with custom development resources increasingly adopt headless architectures and static site generators, separating content authoring from hosting delivery entirely. Buyers must decide whether the simplicity of a closed, prepaid environment like Cabanova outweighs the long-term flexibility of open architectures.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Cabanova renew hosting contracts automatically after purchase?

According to the Cabanova customer protection policy stated on its pricing documentation, subscriptions operate on a strictly prepaid basis and do not renew automatically. The platform issues reminders to owners to manually extend their service before expiration.

### What happens if a user wants to transfer an existing domain to Cabanova?

Cabanova requires users to own the domain and verify that their current registrar permits transfers. The domain holder must obtain an Authorization-Code (EPP key) from their existing provider and complete the transfer form in Cabanova, which re-registers the domain under Cabanova hosting.

### Is there a completely free website tier currently supported by Cabanova?

Publisher records contain contradictory documentation. While the main pricing table displays a free tier with three pages and 50 megabytes of storage, recent blog headlines published in the site's navigation explicitly list Free pacakge discontinued and The Cabanova XL version is discontinued. Buyers must verify actual signup availability directly.

### What payment methods are supported for the integrated online store package?

Documentation for Cabanova's shop package states that the platform supports multiple transaction processing methods for storefront owners, specifically listing PayPal, cash on delivery (COD), and direct bank wire transfers.
