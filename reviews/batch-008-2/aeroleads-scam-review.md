## Core Product Architecture and Data Workflow

The primary function of AeroLeads centers on discovering, verifying, and exporting professional contact data. The publisher describes a three-stage prospecting workflow: finding target profiles through search or uploaded lists, revealing verified contact records, and exporting clean prospects directly into customer relationship systems or spreadsheet files. This structure aims to replace manual internet research with automated lookups across professional datasets.

Prospectors can interact with the platform through a primary web search interface, list enrichment tools, or a Chrome extension designed to grab contact information while browsing professional networks. When browsing potential leads, the interface indicates whether an individual record contains an accessible email address or a direct telephone number before the user spends an allotment of account credits to save the details.

Once records are saved, the system provides verification mechanisms to confirm email deliverability. For organizations with structured pipeline automation, AeroLeads provides CRM integrations and an API to sync contact records without requiring repetitive manual CSV handling. This setup helps outbound sales teams move from initial list building to sales outreach without jumping between multiple disconnected software environments.


## Understanding the Pay-Once Credit Model

One of the most distinctive aspects of AeroLeads is its departure from traditional monthly recurring software subscriptions. The vendor sells fixed credit packs as single, one-time payments through payment processors like Razorpay and PayPal. Standard fixed tiers include entry packs like Take Off at forty-nine dollars for two thousand credits, Climb at one hundred forty-nine dollars for eight thousand credits, and Cruise at two hundred ninety-nine dollars for twenty thousand credits, alongside enterprise options for larger volumes.

Under this transactional structure, no recurring payment mandate is registered against the customer payment card. Once a purchased pack is exhausted, service access pauses until the user chooses to buy another batch. This eliminates the risk of accidental rebilling, unannounced contract renewals, or disputed cancellation deadlines that commonly plague SaaS procurement. A starter option offering ten free credits with no credit card requirement allows prospective buyers to inspect database mechanics before spending money.

Unused credits remain valid for twelve months from the date of the latest transaction. Furthermore, purchasing any subsequent credit bundle resets the twelve-month expiration timeline across the entire remaining account balance. For buyers who find the data mismatched with their needs immediately after purchase, the publisher maintains a policy allowing refunds on unused credits if requested within seven days of the transaction.


## Credit Consumption Rules and Phone surcharges

While the headline credit numbers look generous, prospective buyers must carefully calculate how credits are spent during list-building campaigns. AeroLeads charges one credit for saving a standard contact record containing an email address. However, if a contact record includes a telephone number, the system deducts five additional credits, consuming six credits in total for that single profile.

This asymmetric credit consumption dramatically changes volume yields depending on outbound outreach strategies. For example, a mid-tier Climb pack containing eight thousand credits yields exactly eight thousand verified email leads if phone numbers are excluded. However, if an outbound sales team demands direct telephone numbers for multichannel calling campaigns, that same eight-thousand-credit pack yields a maximum of one thousand three hundred thirty-three complete records.

To help buyers plan outbound campaign budgets, the publisher provides a credit calculator where teams specify their required contact volume alongside expected phone requirements. Because both email-only and phone-enabled contacts can be mixed within a single package, operations leaders must establish strict filter rules for team members inside the platform to avoid exhausting credits unexpectedly on unwanted telephone data.

The publisher highlights a Chrome Web Store rating of 4.2 out of 5 stars based on customer feedback. This browser tool allows sales development representatives to review profiles on public business networks and capture contact records directly into their AeroLeads prospect lists without manually retyping names, company titles, or locations.

In practical outbound prospecting, the browser extension simplifies the transition from identifying an ideal customer profile to executing outreach. However, browser extensions that rely on parsing external websites face inherent stability challenges when host platforms update their web code or interface layouts. The B2B contact intelligence sector is crowded with competing providers, each targeting distinct operational profiles and price points. These tools excel at discovering corporate email patterns but often lack broader telephone enrichment or expansive executive directory browsing. AeroLeads positions itself midway between these categories, providing both email and phone discovery through a lightweight interface backed by non-recurring credit bundles.

When comparing alternatives, buyers should weigh their appetite for contractual commitments against their feature needs. If your sales team requires complex automated sequencing and intent signals, an enterprise platform may justify its steep recurring subscription. If your objective is simply to acquire clean prospect lists on demand without signing a binding contract, AeroLeads offers an accessible, pay-as-you-go alternative.


## A Pragmatic Buyer Evaluation Plan

Rather than committing significant financial resources upfront, prospective buyers should execute a structured pilot evaluation. Begin by creating an account to claim the ten free credits, which require no credit card. Use these initial credits to search for five specific, known contacts within your target market and five completely cold executive targets. Examine how accurately the system identifies their current employment status, domain format, and phone connectivity.

Next, test email verification claims directly. Export your sample contacts and process them through an independent email deliverability checker to verify mail exchanger server responses. If you decide to test a paid bundle, start with the smallest tier, such as the forty-nine-dollar Take Off pack. Dedicate half of the balance to email-only records and the other half to phone-enabled records to evaluate the true utility of the six-credit telephone deductions.

Finally, monitor your sales team outreach results across the test batch. Track hard bounce rates, soft bounces, and telephone connection rates across your first one hundred prospects. Remember that unused credits are refundable within seven days of purchase, providing a clear window to assess database quality before finalizing your operational workflow. This measured approach ensures your lead acquisition strategy is grounded in verified performance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AeroLeads a subscription service with recurring charges?

No. AeroLeads charges for credits on a one-time pack basis without automatic renewals, recurring contracts, or ongoing billing mandates.

### How do contact credits work when phone numbers are included?

Saving a standard contact record uses one credit. If you choose to reveal a contact that includes a phone number, it uses five additional credits, totaling six credits.

### How long do purchased AeroLeads credits remain valid?

Credits remain valid for twelve months from your most recent purchase date. Purchasing a new credit pack resets the twelve-month expiration window for your entire remaining balance.

### What is the refund policy for unused AeroLeads credits?

The publisher offers a refund on unused credits from a purchased pack if requested within seven days of the initial transaction.
