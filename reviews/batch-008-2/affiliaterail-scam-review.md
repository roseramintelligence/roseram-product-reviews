## AffiliateRail: attribution and payable commission are different records

A referral event can identify a possible source of a customer without establishing a final commission. Eligibility may depend on the program agreement, the purchase state and later corrections. Write those rules before testing software. Decide what happens to a refunded purchase, a duplicate referral or a customer associated with more than one source.

The publisher describes referral tracking and payout workflows for SaaS operators. Confirm the exact payment and billing arrangement you use rather than assuming that a named provider covers every transaction type. A tracking interface should help explain the record, but it is not a substitute for a written affiliate agreement or the operator’s approval process.
