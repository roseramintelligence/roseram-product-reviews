# Bluedot review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-21  
**Category:** Meeting recording and AI notes  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Bluedot offers meeting recording and AI notes. Its pricing distinguishes annual and monthly per-user amounts, a five-meeting lifetime Free allowance, and higher tiers with video and administrative controls.

## Editorial verdict

Publisher pricing, security and terms pages provide a basis for comparing the offer. They are not proof of transcript accuracy or participant consent in your meetings. No hands-on accuracy result or fraud finding is made here.

## A low per-user price is not a complete team estimate

The useful budget question is who records and where those recordings belong. Our analysis recommends an inventory of teams, workspaces and regular meeting hosts before comparing tiers. An organization can otherwise estimate the price from total headcount while overlooking a different set of paid recording seats.

Keep the billing term beside every amount in a comparison sheet. Our proposed trial should include a normal meeting and a longer session, with the same host configuration the team expects to use. Check the final invoice preview before adding seats. No recording session or billed seat change was performed for this review.


## Useful notes preserve decisions and uncertainty

An attractive summary is only useful when readers can distinguish a decision from a suggestion. Prepare a harmless sample discussion with an agreed action, an unresolved question and a deliberate correction. Have a participant write the expected interpretation before examining any generated notes.

Check the named owner, deadline and supporting passage for each action. If a summary invents certainty, decide who corrects it before it reaches a customer system. The relevant measure is the effort needed to produce an acceptable record, not a claimed universal accuracy percentage. Those checks are a proposed buyer exercise and do not imply that we benchmarked Bluedot against another recorder.


## Retention and recording permissions belong in the rollout plan

Bluedot describes security controls separately from its subscription terms. The terms assign recording-consent responsibilities to the customer and describe exporting retained content before cancellation. A buyer should read those obligations alongside any administrative features promised by a paid tier.

Our recommended acceptance review asks who can replay a sensitive meeting, share a link and remove a departing colleague. Decide how the team will explain recording to participants and handle an objection before turning on routine capture. Also rehearse an export with sample material. A meeting archive can become operationally important; a clear owner and retention schedule reduce surprises if the subscription changes. This is a configuration review proposal, not legal advice or a compliance certification.


## Features and decision criteria

- Meeting recording and notes
- Audio and video tier distinctions
- Recording-user billing
- Publisher-described access and retention controls

## Pricing considerations

Observed 21 October 2026. Annual figures are monthly equivalents. The publisher says charging applies to recording users and individual workspaces require upgrades; confirm the checkout term and export recordings before cancellation.
