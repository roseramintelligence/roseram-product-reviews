# Castmagic review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** AI content repurposing and transcription platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Castmagic turns long-form audio and video into transcripts and reusable materials such as show notes, clips, summaries, blog drafts and social copy. Its plans are usage-based through transcribed-hour and storage allowances, with team seats, API access and workspace capacity changing by tier.

## Editorial verdict

Castmagic can save repetitive drafting work for a creator who already records podcasts, interviews or long-form video and wants multiple content formats from the source. The main cost driver is the number of transcribed hours and the space or seats needed, not only the headline AI feature list. Start with one representative recording, verify transcription and output editing, and review every generated claim before publishing.

## Start with one source recording

Castmagic is designed for creators who already have long-form audio or video and want to create more useful assets from it. A single interview might become show notes, a transcript, a summary and several social drafts. The value depends on how much of that work the creator can publish after editing, not on the raw number of outputs generated.

Use a recording with names, product terms and a few difficult audio passages. Check the transcript, timestamps and generated drafts against the original before sharing anything. A fluent paragraph can still contain a mistaken name or an invented detail. The workflow should preserve the original file and make corrections easy to carry into every derivative.


## Estimate transcription consumption

The pricing page lists 30, 100 and 400 transcribed hours in the Hobby, Starter and Team plans, respectively. Compare those allowances with actual monthly recording time. If a show publishes one hour a week but also processes interviews, webinars or archived episodes, the total volume can be several times larger than the episode runtime.

Ask whether transcription hours represent a monthly cap, a lifetime library balance or another measurement. The current page calls them a library total, and says more hours can be bought. Confirm the refill and overage behavior in the account and checkout before sending a large back catalog through the service.


## Look at storage, seats and integrations together

A large transcription allowance is not the only capacity question. Castmagic also lists storage amounts, spaces and team seats, while access to API integrations differs by plan. A solo creator may need only one workspace, whereas an agency handling multiple clients may need separate spaces and clear permission controls.

Check whether the integrations you use—such as a drive, RSS feed, video import or automation service—are available on the tier you are considering. Record a test file and follow it to the final asset. If your collaborators need to approve drafts, verify that they can comment or edit without sharing the account owner’s login.


## Review AI drafts before publication

AI-generated show notes and promotional copy can accelerate the first draft, but should not be treated as verified reporting. Compare every factual statement with the recording, especially figures, names, product features and quotations. Review whether a clip removes context or makes a guest’s statement appear stronger than it was.

Keep separate versions of the raw transcript and the edited copy so corrections are auditable. Set an approval step before publishing externally, and disclose synthetic media where it could confuse the audience. Castmagic’s own page describes content creation features; it does not establish a guaranteed return on investment or accuracy for each recording.


## Features and decision criteria

- Transcription and timestamps for audio and video
- AI drafts for show notes, summaries, blogs, newsletters and social posts
- Transcribed-hour libraries and storage limits vary by plan
- Team seats, spaces, API and integrations differ across tiers

## Pricing considerations

The price page offers annual and monthly selectors; the values shown here are the captured annual-billing view. The plan hour library, stored assets, extra seat rates and overage options affect actual cost. Confirm whether unused transcription hours accumulate, and record the checkout renewal terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the main Castmagic plan limit to compare?

The pricing page lists a different total transcribed-hour library for each plan, alongside storage, space and seat limits.

### Does Castmagic publish only a transcript?

No. It also lists AI-generated content such as show notes, summaries, clips, blog drafts and social copy.

### Can a team share one workspace?

The published plan table lists seats, spaces and collaboration features by plan; verify the exact included seat count for your team.
