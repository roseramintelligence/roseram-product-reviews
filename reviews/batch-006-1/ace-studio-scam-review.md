# ACE Studio review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-18  
**Category:** AI music production and singing voice synthesis software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

ACE Studio is a desktop music-production environment built around controllable AI singing and instrument performances. Its central workflow turns MIDI notes and lyrics into vocal parts that producers can edit, alongside voice cloning, AI instruments, stem separation, generative kits and video-oriented tools. ACE Studio 2.0 broadens the studio, but personal plans use yearly billing, a two-year rent-to-own condition and monthly AI-credit allowances; different tools consume credits at different rates. The publisher says users retain rights in generated output subject to feature-specific terms, while not every premade singer is license-free and custom voices require lawful source recordings and consent. Trustpilot showed 2.0/5 from 79 reviews on November 18, 2026, while warning the reviews may not be representative. Public evidence does not substantiate calling ACE Studio a scam. Roseram has not installed or tested it.

## Editorial verdict

ACE Studio is a real music tool whose strongest proposition is detailed control over synthetic singing inside a producer's workflow. A composer who works with MIDI and lyrics, needs guide vocals or arrangements, and understands licensing per voice may find the combination compelling. The friction is material: personal plans are shown with annual billing, the rent-to-own promise requires a two-year horizon, generative work is credit-metered, cancellation affects custom voice slots, and Trustpilot feedback is sharply negative about billing clarity and credit expectations. These concerns deserve diligence, but the public records reviewed do not show fraud. Check the full checkout charge, read the seven-day guarantee rules rather than assuming a free trial, and test a small project before placing a deadline or client vocal on the service. ## ACE Studio review: a controllable singing-production system

ACE Studio sits between recording a live vocalist and asking a general AI service for a finished song. Its core workflow starts with notes and lyrics: the producer lays out a melody, assigns a voice, hears the generated performance and revises musical details. That makes it a tool for shaping a part inside a production, not merely a prompt box that returns a mastered track. A songwriter with an existing MIDI line may use it differently from a person who has only a general idea for a chorus.

ACE Studio 2.0 expands the studio with AI instruments, voice customization, stem separation, generative kits and video tools. The breadth is attractive, but pricing, credits and licensing vary by feature. The useful question is whether this combination of control and tools fits work you already do. It is not safe to read a long feature list as a promise that every output is finished, unlimited or ready for commercial release.


## How ACE Studio turns MIDI and lyrics into a vocal part

Users can create MIDI clips in the canvas or import MIDI or MusicXML, add lyrics and select a singing voice. Notes and syllables remain editable, so a producer can revise text or melody instead of treating the generated vocal as a finished recording. ACE documentation also describes Vocal to MIDI tools for extracting notes and lyrics from existing vocals. It cautions that reverberant or instrumental inputs may reduce accuracy and that language support varies by model. Input cleanup and correction remain part of the workflow.

The approach is useful when a demo needs a vocal line before a session singer is booked, when a songwriter wants to hear a lyric in another key, or when a producer is comparing synthetic timbres. It can expose a phrase that is awkward to sing or a note that needs changing. A virtual singer is an arrangement and production instrument; it does not automatically provide the interpretation and judgment of a human performance. Buyers should expect iteration, not a guarantee that each render is release-ready.


## ACE Studio 2.0 features: vocals, instruments, stems and video

Personal plans list more than 160 premade AI voices and 40 AI instruments, alongside voice slots and credit-based features. The wider feature set includes a voice changer, custom voice training, stem splitting, sound and music generation and video tools. These functions are not interchangeable: vocal synthesis follows notes and lyrics, while sound generation, separation and video use different inputs and credit rules.

Sound On Sound's March 2026 ACE Studio 2.0 review described its move from vocal synthesis toward a more DAW-like AI music environment. MusicTech's review raised workflow and loading concerns in its tested setup. These reviews assess specific builds, not every current computer. If performance matters, try the current version on the machine and project size you intend to use. A feature list cannot tell you how fast a real project opens or whether a particular editing path feels natural.


## DAW integration and ACE Bridge 2

ACE Bridge 2 is the route into an existing DAW. The documentation calls it a VST3, AU or AAX instrument plugin for audio and MIDI synchronization. Playback mode can send ACE Studio audio into a DAW; record mode can transfer MIDI from a DAW track back to ACE. The docs also describe limitations, including one-way sync in some modes and host-specific setup steps.

Before purchase, check that your current DAW and computer support the release. Test which application is the playback master, whether MIDI is polyphonic in the chosen mode, and how exported stems line up with the session. The bridge can reduce back-and-forth once configured, but compatibility and routing still matter. If your normal process is a text prompt followed by one audio download, it may be unnecessary complexity. If you need note-level edits and repeated revisions, it is more relevant.


## ACE Studio pricing: yearly billing behind monthly equivalents

The personal pricing page checked November 18, 2026 displayed Artist at $16.58 per month and Artist Pro at $22 per month, both marked billed yearly and rent-to-own. The page did not identify the currency code in extracted text. Do not read a monthly equivalent as month-to-month payment: confirm the full charge, currency, tax, renewal date and perpetual-license terms in checkout. Preserve a copy of the offer presented to your account.

Artist lists 2,500 monthly AI credits, one Vocal Synth voice slot and one Voice Changer slot; Artist Pro lists 5,000 credits and five and ten slots respectively. The support site separately lists Artist Team at $29 per seat monthly or $199 yearly and Artist Pro Team at $38.50 monthly or $264 yearly, with a three-seat minimum. Teams are not rent-to-own. Calculate the actual seat count and annual commitment instead of assuming personal plan terms apply.


## ACE Studio rent-to-own: clarify the two-year term

The pricing page says “Rent for 2 years, access forever.” Its FAQ says a perpetual desktop license is available after subscribing for two years or purchasing one outright. That does not mean every connected or generative benefit remains unlimited forever. The same FAQ says monthly credits stop refreshing when the active membership ends, even if a user has a perpetual desktop license.

Ask support what happens if you cancel before completing two years, whether upgrades change the qualifying period, which application version the perpetual license covers and which features still require membership or connection. The public page gives the broad promise; the buyer needs to understand the path to it. Save the checkout and terms, especially if the lifetime-license condition is the main reason the plan appears attractive.


## ACE Studio credits: budget the whole project

ACE publishes per-action examples: 15 credits for Inspire Me, 20 for Add a Layer, 3 to upload and 12 to generate with Music Enhancer, 60 for advanced all-stem separation, 100 for Video Composer music and 10 for its sound effects. Sound Generator costs five credits per second; speech uses separate per-character rates. Standard stem separation is listed at zero credits while advanced modes consume credits. Artist receives 2,500 monthly credits and Artist Pro 5,000.

A single brief may pass through several metered tools, and Music Video has a separate model-specific schedule. One Trustpilot reviewer reported a high late-stage credit estimate for a video project, but that is an individual account, not a typical tariff. Inspect the estimate before an expensive render. Base credits refresh on the first of the month Pacific time and unused base balance clears; eligible add-on credits can roll over. Purchased credits are non-refundable and require an active subscription.


## Is ACE Studio free, or is the refund guarantee the trial?

Other registered users receive 100 monthly AI credits, according to ACE's help page. That is not a promise of unlimited access to a paid desktop plan. ACE also advertises a seven-day money-back guarantee. Trustpilot reviewers have described expecting to explore the application before paying for a year. These reports are account-specific, but they show why a buyer should verify exactly what “free” unlocks before installing or entering payment details.

The guarantee applies to personal Artist or Artist Pro purchases, excludes Team plans and credit packs, and is available once per account per plan tier. The support FAQ says to email the company within the period; an approved refund returns to the original payment method and revokes the linked membership. This differs from a free trial without a charge. Check eligibility, start date, request route and access rather than relying on “risk-free” wording alone.


## Commercial use and ACE Studio voice licenses

The terms say users retain rights in generated audio, vocal and musical output, subject to applicable restrictions and feature-specific rules. ACE's license guide says most premade voices are royalty-free but not all. It directs users to inspect each voice and generate a license certificate where available. Access to premade singers may depend on membership tier. For a song going to streaming, advertising, a game or a client, the voice's license is as important as the plan price.

The same guide describes commercial use of some generative outputs, while third-party providers have their own terms. Rights to generated results do not clear lyrics, samples, stems or reference recordings supplied by a user. ACE places responsibility for input rights on the user and does not guarantee unique output when prompts are similar. Record the tool and voice used, save certificates and check any downstream client requirements. This review is not legal clearance.


## Custom voice cloning: consent and recordings

ACE says users can clone their own voice or another person's only with explicit legal permission. Terms require rights to uploaded recordings and written consent or releases for voice talent whose name or likeness is included. The custom-model guide recommends clean recordings and warns that training quality varies, including possible pronunciation or pitch defects. It also says an active membership is needed to use the software even if a user owns the custom singer model.

Keep the recording release, permitted-use scope and access list with the project. Do not treat a voice's public availability as consent to train it. A convincing output is not proof of authorization. ACE's privacy notice says residents or users located in Illinois may not use voice modeling, so they should not try to bypass that restriction. Confirm current eligibility with the publisher.


## ACE Studio privacy: uploads, model training and outside providers

ACE Studio's policy, last updated October 30, 2026, says it does not use user content, voice, uploads, generated materials or personal data to train AI models outside specified authorized uses. Optional use of interaction-trajectory data for model development requires a separate notice and affirmative opt-in; accepting the general policy or requesting a custom voice is not that consent. For a requested voice model, recordings are processed to create and verify it, and ACE says voice data is encrypted. These are publisher statements, not a Roseram audit.

Voiceover and sound-effect tools can send prompts and reference audio to ElevenLabs or SeedAudio by BytePlus, whose own terms apply. ACE says user data outside mainland China is stored in the United States. Cancellation alone does not delete account content; account deletion is separate, with data deletion described as within one day and backup copies removed or expired within 180 days, subject to legal exceptions. A studio handling unreleased music or a performer's voice should review those routes before upload.


## What happens after cancelling ACE Studio?

The pricing FAQ says subscription-provided custom voice slots lock after membership expiry, making those voices unavailable until membership resumes. A perpetual desktop license can continue after cancellation, but generative credits stop refreshing when the active period ends. Different features therefore have different continuation rules.

Cancellation stops the next billing cycle rather than refunding unused time in the current year. The seven-day guarantee is a separate policy. Account deletion is separate again. Before a deadline-critical project, export needed audio and MIDI, keep source files and certificates, and check which elements remain local or depend on an active service. These are continuity checks, not evidence ACE has withheld a customer's work.

## ACE Studio limitations for everyday production

The product's breadth brings a learning curve. Users may need to understand MIDI editing, lyrics per note, voice parameters, the canvas, credits and DAW routing before reaching the result they imagined. Someone without a melody draft may find the singing-synth workflow less immediate than a text-to-song prompt. Generative additions do not remove the need to edit, arrange and judge the output. ACE suits people who want production controls more than people who only want a one-click finished track.

Credit budgeting is harder when a task includes several tools and iterations. Basic functions may be included or listed at zero while advanced separation, sound, speech and video actions consume credits. Yearly personal billing can make a brief experiment costly if a buyer does not complete the two-year rent-to-own condition. Check the estimate and the access consequences of cancellation.


## ACE Studio alternatives: compare the output you need

Compare by task. Dreamtonics presents Synthesizer V as singing synthesis based on MIDI and lyrics, a closer comparison for note-controlled vocal parts. A human vocalist is a different alternative when a performance needs human interpretation and individually negotiated rights. Prompt-based song generators solve a different problem: they draft broader music rather than focus first on a controllable singer inside an existing production.

Give each candidate the same melody, lyrics, language and deadline. Check voice selection, expression control, export format, DAW connection, license, annual commitment, credit policy and access after cancellation. Include time needed to fix a render. A larger suite may be wasteful when you need one singer; a lower sticker price may not help if output needs extensive rework. Compare the task, not a generic feature count.


## How to test ACE Studio before buying

First confirm what the current account can open, the full yearly charge and currency, renewal terms, two-year perpetual-license condition and guarantee start date. If “free” is displayed, verify whether it means full Studio access or only registration and a small credit allowance. ACE identifies email as its support route and says it does not provide telephone support; get material terms in writing.

Then run a short project similar to normal work: import or create a melody, enter a few lyric lines, try a voice, make a correction and bridge the result into your DAW. Separately run one credit-based action and note the balance before and after. Inspect audio and exports. Check the selected voice's certificate. Do not upload another person's recordings unless written permission covers model training and intended uses.


## Who should consider ACE Studio?

ACE Studio is most relevant to producers and songwriters who work with notes and lyrics, need synthetic vocal options and have a repeatable use for the wider tools. It can be evaluated for demo vocals, topline exploration or an arrangement that needs a guide singer. A group may value seats, but must compare team contracts separately from personal rent-to-own plans. Check the voice license before publishing.

It may not fit buyers who need a no-payment trial, expect every generation to be unlimited, cannot accept yearly billing or depend on access to a subscription voice slot after cancellation. It is not a general text-to-speech tool, and a voice-cloning project without recorded consent creates a rights problem. Creators comfortable with production controls are better placed to use its depth.
