## Core Architecture: Browser Utilities Versus Unified Ledger

Accountdesq operates through a distinct dual-layer operational architecture. The public entry point consists of over forty-three standalone web tools designed for rapid financial calculations and document drafting. These include generators for sales invoices, customer quotes, payment receipts, credit notes, and purchase orders. In addition, the utility catalog contains bookkeeping tools such as trial balances, general ledger viewers, journal entry templates, and financial ratio calculators. These tools operate independently without saving historical data between sessions, serving users who require single calculations without setting up a continuous software environment.

The second layer is the connected Accountdesq back-office workspace. While the standalone utilities execute calculations locally on the user's device and clear state upon browser exit, the commercial workspace stores data across a permanent double-entry general ledger. In this unified platform, the entire order-to-cash workflow—from customer quotes and sales orders to invoices, incoming payments, and bank reconciliations—posts transactions directly to one central ledger without requiring manual data exports between fragmented systems. This design targets growing teams that need automatic receivable tracking and consolidated audit logs.

Understanding this architectural division helps prospective buyers choose the correct approach. Teams that only need to calculate markup percentages, check depreciation schedules, or verify value-added tax amounts can utilize the free browser tools without entering payment information or setting up a database. Conversely, organizations seeking audit readiness, historical balance sheets, and multi-user access must configure the full cloud workspace. The separation ensures that occasional users get immediate utility while growing enterprises gain a structured accounting foundation.


## Local Client-Side Processing and Data Privacy Realities

Data privacy is a major consideration when managing corporate numbers online. A defining technical aspect of Accountdesq's free standalone utilities is client-side execution. When an operator enters line items, tax rates, or customer addresses into the free invoice or quote generator, the mathematical calculations execute locally within the web browser. The publisher states that these specific standalone calculations are not transmitted to or stored on remote application servers, providing immediate data privacy for quick administrative tasks.

While local browser processing offers peace of mind for one-off documents, it introduces operational trade-offs that buyers must account for. Because the free standalone utilities do not retain state between sessions, closing a browser tab or clearing browser cache permanently clears all unsaved work. Users cannot reload a previously generated standalone invoice to revise an item code or check historical customer billing. To retain records created via the browser utilities, operators must deliberately export their output as a PDF file or save the calculation to a spreadsheet before navigating away from the page.

When an organization migrates from standalone browser tools to the persistent Accountdesq workspace, the data handling model changes to standard cloud hosting. Within the registered workspace, transaction data, customer records, and ledger balances reside securely on the provider's cloud infrastructure to support multi-user collaboration and automated reporting. Prospective buyers should confirm their internal data retention policies and evaluate whether local browser execution or centralized cloud recordkeeping aligns with their regulatory requirements.


## Evaluating the Multi-Tier Pricing Structure and Add-Ons

Accountdesq structures its workspace subscriptions into clearly defined tiers: Free, Starter, Growth, Business, Scale, and custom Enterprise agreements. The Free workspace tier provides authentic double-entry bookkeeping, supporting one internal business user and one external accountant with one gigabyte of attachment storage and one inventory branch. However, the Free tier restricts operations by capping issued sales invoices at fifty per month. Unlike restricted software trials that expire after several weeks, this free tier includes no calendar time limit, allowing small operations to run indefinitely within these parameters.

Upgrading to paid plans removes invoice caps entirely, offering unlimited transactions across sales, billing, and general bookkeeping. The Starter plan provides three internal user seats, while Growth expands access to five users, two warehouse locations, automated bank matching rules, and receivable analytics. Companies with complex distribution requirements require the Business or Scale plans, which introduce point-of-sale registers, custom user permissions, branch-level access controls, multi-step approval rules, and inventory tracking utilizing batch numbers, serial numbers, and standard costing methods.

Accountdesq avoids forcing businesses into higher tiers solely to acquire incremental user capacity or file storage. The publisher provides modular add-ons that can be purchased individually on a per-unit basis, including additional accounting seats, expense-only submission seats, supplementary file storage packs, extra warehouse locations, and standalone point-of-sale registers. This flexible capacity model prevents small enterprises from absorbing steep subscription price jumps when they simply need to authorize one extra staff member or configure a secondary stock room.


## Invoice Allowances and Downgrade Integrity

A common point of friction with cloud software is how vendors handle account downgrades or plan limits. On Accountdesq's Free tier, the fifty-invoice monthly ceiling applies strictly to finalized, issued documents. Customer invoice drafts, credit notes, and voided transactions do not count toward this allocation. If a business reaches fifty issued invoices in a given month, the system allows the user to continue drafting invoices, saving all line items without locking the interface. The operator can issue these pending drafts once the monthly limit resets on the first of the following month, or immediately upon upgrading.

Equally important is the vendor's policy regarding downgrades and payment lapses. If a subscriber downgrades from a higher plan to a lower tier or returns to the Free plan, Accountdesq does not delete, hide, or restrict access to previously created transactions, ledger entries, or customer profiles. Existing records remain accessible and editable within the platform. The system simply restricts the creation of new entities that exceed the lower tier's allowances, such as registering an additional warehouse or onboarding a new employee seat.

In the event of a failed subscription renewal payment, the system enters a ten-day grace period during which retries occur alongside email notifications. If payment remains unresolved after ten days, premium features pause and the account transitions smoothly to the Free tier without data loss or administrative lockouts. This predictable operational model protects businesses from catastrophic disruptions to historical records during temporary banking hiccups, demonstrating reliable administrative design.

The vendor enforces an automated thirty-day money-back guarantee for every workspace's first paid subscription. Within the initial thirty days of payment, an administrator can cancel directly through the billing settings by selecting immediate cancellation with a full refund, returning funds to the original payment method without requiring human support calls or ticketing approvals. Following this initial window, users can cancel their subscription at any time, maintaining platform access through the end of the paid billing period before transitioning cleanly back to the Free plan.

Pricing stability is another important consideration. Accountdesq maintains a policy where list price increases apply solely to new subscriptions; existing accounts retain their original sign-up rates while they remain subscribed to their plan. Furthermore, annual billing plans receive automated email notifications fifteen to thirty days prior to renewal, stating the exact upcoming charge and renewal date. ### Does Accountdesq require an immediate paid subscription for basic calculations?

No. The public utility tools run entirely in your web browser without an account, credit card, or payment agreement. Calculations execute locally on the device, enabling instant generation of PDF or CSV files for simple receipts, quotes, and tax checks.

### How are external bookkeeping seats handled across paid plans?

Accountdesq provides dedicated external accountant seats free of charge on all tiers, ranging from one seat on Free and Starter plans up to five seats on the Scale plan.
