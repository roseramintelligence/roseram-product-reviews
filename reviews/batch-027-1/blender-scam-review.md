# Blender review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** 3D modeling and animation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Blender is an open-source 3D creation suite. For a client project, the operating costs come from preparing assets, rendering and making the work reproducible.

## Editorial verdict

The official source repository identifies the software. Assess a commercial course, asset bundle or render service on its own terms.

## Blender 3D modeling for a client who will ask for changes

The first success in a commission is understanding the buyer’s next request. Ask which part of the object may change after approval. Make a small **Blender project** with two material options, then change one while the client watches. Save the camera and lighting so both renders can be judged from the same angle.

Give a colleague the scene and its permitted assets. Ask them to open it from a different folder and reproduce the approved view. Missing image textures revealed in that exercise are cheaper to fix than the same problem discovered after the job changes hands.


## Blender rendering costs and deadlines

Measure a short sequence that includes the slowest shot. Keep the test resolution and hardware in your estimate so the result can be reproduced. Budget for a revision render as well as the first pass.

Write down what earns acceptance: an *editable scene*, still images or an animation file. If the contract requires all three, give each its own delivery check and leave enough time to correct a missing asset or a failed frame.
