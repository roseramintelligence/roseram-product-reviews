# PartnerStack review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** B2B partner program marketplace and management platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

PartnerStack supports companies operating partner programs and individuals or businesses applying to promote partner offers. Partner accounts are free to create, but joining the network and individual programs requires review and approval; company platform and commission-invoice fees are negotiated rather than fully listed as a universal public price.

## Editorial verdict

PartnerStack is useful to a creator or agency that wants to discover B2B partner programs from one dashboard, or to a SaaS company managing multiple partner relationships. A free partner account does not mean automatic access or guaranteed commissions: network approval, program approval, terms acceptance and eligible conversions all matter. For companies, contact sales to model the platform and payout-invoice fees because the fee percentage is not disclosed as one universal rate.

## Understand the partner application sequence

PartnerStack’s official onboarding guide describes a free account, a Network Application, approval and then applications to individual programs. A referral link is issued only after the relevant approvals. That sequence means profile completeness and fit matter: creators should explain their audience, promotion channels and the kinds of products they can credibly recommend.

Do not plan around a commission until the company accepts the application and you have reviewed its terms. A partner may be asked to provide tax details and a payout method before money can be withdrawn. The dashboard’s pending state is not proof that an application has been approved or that a lead has earned a commission.


## Compare each offer as its own contract

Marketplace listings may differ in commission percentage, recurring duration, qualifying actions, attribution windows, exclusions and payout timing. Read those details for each program before sharing a link. A high advertised commission does not mean that every click qualifies or that a customer’s payment is final.

Maintain a simple record of the offer, accepted terms, referral URL, first click and any required disclosure. When promoting a partner, describe the product accurately and make affiliate relationships clear. Avoid sending unsolicited mass messages or posting referral links where the platform rules prohibit them.


## Know how company fees affect commission economics

PartnerStack’s company FAQ says the platform charges a fee on the partner commission invoice total. It does not list one universal percentage for every customer; companies should ask their Customer Success Manager for the contractual rate. A merchant’s total cost therefore includes the partner commission, PartnerStack fee and payment or tax administration.

Partners should distinguish their listed commission from the net amount that eventually arrives. Currency exchange, payout provider, tax documentation and the company’s approval decision can affect withdrawal. Use a conservative calculation and confirm the schedule in the specific program’s terms rather than treating dashboard estimates as guaranteed cash.


## Protect the account and keep a record of payout state

PartnerStack’s support documentation describes payout providers such as PayPal, Stripe and direct deposit, along with tax information required before withdrawal. Keep the account email, recovery method and tax identity current. Use the platform’s support and in-app messages for account changes; do not share credentials or payment details in public channels.

Track approved, pending and available-to-withdraw balances separately. PartnerStack says the company running the program decides whether commissions are approved; the platform does not determine that approval. This review is based on PartnerStack’s public help documentation, not a claim that the user has a PartnerStack account or that earnings are guaranteed.


## Features and decision criteria

- Partner discovery and program applications from a central Marketplace
- Free partner registration; network and program approval required
- Referral links and commissions become available after program approval and agreement
- Payout providers, tax details, timing and approvals affect when funds can be withdrawn

## Pricing considerations

PartnerStack does not publish a universal company SaaS price or one platform fee rate in the cited FAQ. Treat each program’s commission and payout terms as separate. Confirm the negotiated company contract, invoice fee, currency conversion, withdrawal threshold and tax rules before relying on a commission estimate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can anyone join PartnerStack Marketplace immediately?

No. PartnerStack says creators first submit a Network application; approval is required before applying to Marketplace programs.

### Is a PartnerStack partner account free?

PartnerStack describes creating a free partner account, but that does not guarantee network acceptance, program approval or commissions.

### How much do companies pay PartnerStack?

PartnerStack says it charges a percentage fee on partner commission invoices, but the exact company-specific rate is available from the customer success contact rather than a universal public price.
