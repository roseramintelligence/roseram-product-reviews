# Wrike review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Project and work management software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Wrike is a work-management platform for tasks, projects, workflows, dashboards and collaboration. Its 2026 plans include a Free tier and paid tiers with user-bundle, storage, AI-action and feature differences.

## Editorial verdict

Wrike’s official plan material sets out meaningful limits on users, storage, spaces, billing bundles and AI actions. Buyers should size the account for actual licensed seats and test the workflow with real roles before purchasing add-ons or migrating a portfolio.

## Wrike review: projects, tasks and shared delivery plans

Wrike brings tasks, projects, spaces, dashboards and workflow tools into a cloud work-management product. Its plan comparison describes features ranging from basic task tracking to interactive Gantt charts, custom workflows, resource planning and advanced reporting. A team can shape the workspace around its work, but that flexibility needs a simple operating model and named owners for each shared process.

Select one live project and map the path from request to delivery. Decide which team owns each task, what an approval means and where blockers are recorded. Test the experience for a full user, a limited user and a free viewer before inviting external collaborators. A polished portfolio dashboard is useful only when its underlying statuses are current and consistently defined.


## Wrike pricing, seat bundles and annual commitments

The current pricing page lists Free at $0, Team at $10 per user per month and Business at $25 per user per month, with those paid amounts billed annually. Team is set for two to fifteen users; Business covers five to two hundred. Wrike says paid subscriptions are sold in user groups, with seat increments varying at different organization sizes. Business and higher are annual-only, while Pinnacle and Apex are sales-quoted.

A seat bundle can make the effective cost higher than a simple headcount multiplied by the displayed rate. Count full users, limited contributors and viewers separately, then ask how a change in team size affects the next bundle. Also budget for paid add-ons such as whiteboards, integrations, two-way sync, data hub and expanded AI actions if the workflow depends on them.


## Wrike Free plan, active tasks and storage

Wrike’s Free plan documentation lists core project tools and limits including one shared space, 2 GB of storage per account and a maximum of 200 active tasks and subtasks at a time. The plan comparison gives higher tiers larger storage amounts and broader workspace options. A team can therefore run into a capacity boundary even when its number of users remains small.

Estimate how many active work items the team retains, not just how many new tasks it creates per week. Review attachment sizes, archived project policy and whether older work can be closed safely. Test what happens when the task or storage ceiling is approached, and assign an owner to keep the workspace from becoming an unsearchable archive.


## Wrike AI, reporting and workflow governance

Wrike’s current page describes AI Essentials on Team and AI Elite actions beginning on Business, with more action capacity and additional integration products at higher levels. The comparison also lists dashboards, Gantt charts, templates and advanced planning features. These capabilities can reduce repetitive setup but do not remove the need to inspect generated tasks, summaries and workflow changes.

Start with reversible automations and keep a record of their trigger, affected projects and failure behavior. Check whether an AI action uses an account allowance and who can approve a new workflow. Before reporting to executives, confirm that project dates and statuses represent commitments the delivery team recognizes rather than stale data.


## Wrike alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Wrike with Jira, Asana and a simpler task board using the same number of paid users, storage, approval paths, reporting needs and integrations. Include seat-bundle math, annual commitment, add-ons, migration and administrator time. A small team may benefit from a light plan, while a project office may need portfolio, resource and security controls.

Wrike’s official plan and limit pages describe a current work-management service; they do not establish that Wrike is a scam. This is a source-based desk review, not a test of project delivery or AI output quality. Confirm account-specific pricing, seat blocks, data region and downgrade behavior before moving a critical portfolio.


## Features and decision criteria

- Projects, tasks, boards and dashboards
- Plan-specific spaces, storage, reporting and resource planning
- Wrike AI features, automation integrations and add-on options

## Pricing considerations

The 2026 pricing page says prices are monthly equivalents billed annually and that seats are sold in user bundles; Business and above require annual subscriptions. Pinnacle and Apex use sales quotes. Pricing applies to new purchases from January 21, 2026 and may not match legacy accounts.
