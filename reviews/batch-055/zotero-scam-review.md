# Zotero review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Reference management and research collaboration  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Zotero organizes research references. Its documentation distinguishes syncing bibliographic data from syncing attached files.

## Editorial verdict

Zotero documents the distinction between records and attachments. Review the terms of a storage plan and confirm who controls shared libraries.

## Zotero data sync vs file sync for a research group

Set up a pilot library with one article and an attachment the group may share. Invite a colleague and ask them to locate the record and open the attached document. Check each step. Finding a citation without the underlying file may still leave the colleague unable to review the evidence.

Size **Zotero attachment storage** against representative PDFs and the way the group expects to work. Decide what happens when someone leaves and who is responsible for the group’s storage and access.


## Zotero group library handoff and citation checks

Build a short document with a citation and bibliography from the pilot. Make a change to an author field and have a second researcher confirm what updates. Record which document format, citation style and software the group intends to keep using.

Archive an *independent copy* of the core references and any permitted files required for a later audit. The value of the library is measured when another researcher can follow the trail, well after the original search is forgotten.


## Features and decision criteria

- Bibliographic records and collections
- Separate data and attachment sync
- Shared research library workflows
