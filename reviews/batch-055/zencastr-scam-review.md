# Zencastr review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Remote podcast recording and publishing platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Zencastr’s current public plan is Vibecastr, listed as free, alongside custom Enterprise pricing. The pricing page describes local multitrack recording, cloud backup, editing, transcription, publishing and analytics features; the help center states sessions support up to six participants.

## Editorial verdict

Zencastr is a strong candidate to test when the main need is remote podcast capture with separate participant tracks and a simple publishing workflow. Its published Vibecastr plan is unusually broad for a free tier, but a production team should still validate backup completion, track downloads, browser compatibility and publishing controls using a real rehearsal. Enterprise pricing is custom, so confirm contract, retention and support terms directly.

## Protect the recording before the interview starts

Separate tracks give an editor more control over interruptions, room noise and individual volume than a single mixed file. Zencastr describes local capture with cloud backup, but a production should still verify that each participant’s track finished uploading and can be downloaded. Keep a local backup where the recording is important.

Run a short rehearsal with the actual microphones, browser and network conditions planned for the guest. Confirm the host can see recording status and that each participant knows what to do if a connection drops. Zencastr’s support page recommends headphones to reduce echo and audio bleed. This review does not claim an actual recording was performed.


## Check participant and device fit

The official FAQ lists support for Chrome, Edge and Brave on desktop, the iOS app, and Android recording through Chrome. It also says sessions support up to six participants including the host. If your show routinely needs more guests, test the custom Enterprise route rather than assuming the public plan accommodates them.

A guest may have an older device, a restrictive workplace network or no headphones. Send clear joining instructions and offer a low-pressure sound check. For each recording, identify who can admit guests, stop the session and retrieve files. These small steps prevent a host from discovering a permission or browser issue after the conversation has ended.


## Separate recording from post-production needs

The pricing comparison lists editing, transcription, AI-assisted postproduction and publishing capabilities within the public plan. Those features can reduce handoffs, but a show with an established editor may prefer to export individual files and keep its own processing chain. Check the exact formats, download behavior and project portability you need before moving a catalog.

Try one representative episode from capture through publishing. Check track alignment, spoken names, transcript quality, chapter edits and the final media feed. Automated transcripts can mishear names and specialist terms, so budget human review for anything public. Treat the feature list as a starting point and verify critical capabilities in your account.


## Read the plan and data terms as a team

A free plan lowers the barrier to a test, but a small team should still decide who owns the workspace and files. Document how backups are retained, how access can be removed, and whether collaborators need named seats. For a commercial show, ask what additional controls and support are included in an Enterprise quote.

The public pricing page does not provide a fixed Enterprise fee. Ask for the billing cadence, storage and export terms, support response expectations, and what happens to hosted episodes if the agreement ends. This is a source-based buyer guide, not user feedback, a security audit or an assertion that every advertised capability has been independently tested.


## Features and decision criteria

- Vibecastr public plan shown at no cost; Enterprise is custom priced
- Up to six participants and separate audio/video tracks described in official help
- Local recording with automatic cloud backup described by the vendor
- Publishing, hosting, editing and analytics listed on the current plan page

## Pricing considerations

The public pricing page presents Vibecastr as free and Enterprise as custom, without a fixed Enterprise price. Feature quantities on the public comparison should be checked against the current product account and written terms before a production rollout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Zencastr have a free plan?

Yes. Its current pricing page lists the Vibecastr plan as free; the help center says it includes recording, editing and publishing.

### How many people can join a recording?

Zencastr’s official recording FAQ says up to six participants, including the host, with separate tracks.

### Are recordings only stored in the cloud?

The FAQ says each track records locally and backs up to the cloud as recording proceeds. Test both the local file and backup before relying on the workflow.
