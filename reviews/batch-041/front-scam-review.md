# Front review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Customer operations and shared inbox software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Front combines shared inboxes, ticketing, team workflows and customer communication channels. Plans are priced per seat and differ in channel scope, automation limits, workspaces, security and AI add-ons.

## Editorial verdict

Front’s public plan comparison explains the cost tradeoffs between single-channel and omnichannel support and lists separate AI and API add-ons. Teams should price seats plus any per-conversation, per-agent or rate-limit fees and test channel permissions before migration.

## Front review: shared inboxes for customer-facing teams

Front combines shared inboxes with ticketing, messages, automation, analytics and a knowledge base. Starter focuses on a single communication channel type, while Professional and Enterprise support omnichannel workflows. The plans also differ in workspaces, roles, SSO, automation rules and AI options. For a team that handles customer questions in several places, a shared view can reduce handoffs, but only if channel ownership is well defined.

Before migrating, list every shared address, chat widget, social account and SMS number. Confirm who can see each channel, whether replies are sent from the expected identity and how internal discussions are separated from customer messages. Test assignment, auto-reply, forwarding and escalation rules with a small group so a new workflow does not inadvertently reply to the same customer twice.


## Front seat prices, channel tiers and add-on costs

Front’s annual pricing page lists Starter at $25 per seat per month for up to 10 seats, Professional at $65 for up to 50 seats and Enterprise at $105. Starter is limited to one channel type; Professional adds omnichannel support, up to 20 rules and up to five workspaces; Enterprise includes unlimited rules and macros, custom roles and built-in Copilot, QA and CSAT capabilities.

The listed seat price is only one part of the potential cost. Front shows Autopilot starting at $0.05 per conversation, Copilot and Smart QA at $20 per seat monthly when purchased as add-ons, and Smart CSAT at $10 per seat. An API-rate-limit increase is also a paid add-on. Build the quote from current seats, channel count, AI use and expected API volume rather than comparing only the entry plan.


## Front trial, automation and API capacity

Front offers a 14-day trial with Professional features and no card required, subject to limits such as teammate, workspace, channel, recipient and message counts. Automation limits vary by tier: Starter includes up to 10 rules, Professional up to 20 and Enterprise unlimited rules and macros. The public price page states that API rate limits can be increased for an extra fee.

Use the trial to test the actual integration, not just the inbox appearance. Confirm what happens when a rule exceeds a limit, API requests are throttled or an automation is disabled. Keep API credentials in a secret store, log only required customer data and use retries that prevent duplicate messages. If a business needs higher throughput, confirm the rate-limit add-on with Front before designing around it.


## Front data, AI and operational controls

Front describes AI Copilot, Smart QA, Smart CSAT and Autopilot as add-ons or tier features. Its pricing page also identifies the AI providers it uses and describes data processing commitments. Any AI-assisted reply still needs a human owner for the underlying policy, particularly for refunds, cancellations, account access and sensitive customer situations.

Decide which teams can use AI features and which conversations must remain human-led. Review access to inboxes, customer profiles and external integrations at least when staff change roles. Preserve an export path for conversations and rule definitions, and give agents a fallback channel for urgent work if the service or an integration is unavailable.


## Front alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Front with Help Scout, Zendesk and Freshdesk using the same seat count, channel mix, automation rules, API rate and AI usage. Include annual commitment, onboarding, usage-based AI, paid rate-limit increases and the effort to transfer historical conversations. A simple email-first team may choose a smaller product, while an omnichannel customer-operations group may value Front’s broader routing.

Front’s official materials describe a support and shared-inbox platform; they do not establish that Front is a scam. This is a desk review, not a test of response quality or system reliability. Verify contractual AI terms, channel support, API limits and renewal pricing in the live quote before routing customer communications through it.


## Features and decision criteria

- Shared inbox, ticketing and knowledge base
- Tier-dependent email, SMS, social and WhatsApp channels
- Automation rules, analytics, external integrations and AI add-ons

## Pricing considerations

Front lists annual per-seat prices and separate add-ons. Autopilot starts at $0.05 per conversation; Copilot and Smart QA are listed at $20 per seat monthly when not included, and Smart CSAT at $10. Increased API rate limits are a paid add-on. Verify current limits, billing cadence and contract requirements.
