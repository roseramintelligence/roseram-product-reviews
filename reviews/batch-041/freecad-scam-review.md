# FreeCAD review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Parametric 3D CAD  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

FreeCAD is a free, open-source parametric modeler. The practical question is whether the next change and downstream handoff stay manageable.

## Editorial verdict

The project maintains an official source repository. Verify the seller and scope of any outside CAD support or manufacturing quote.

## FreeCAD parametric modeling for a revised bracket

Good design begins with the dimension the workshop may change. Start with a bracket whose mounting holes must fit two fixtures. In a **FreeCAD parametric model**, adjust their spacing and examine the surrounding edges for consequences. Record the dimensions that carry an assembly requirement and the ones the designer may change freely.

Use a revision you can check with a ruler or drawing. Give the model to a colleague and ask them to make the same change without instructions from you. The handoff exposes which design relationships need clearer names or notes.


## FreeCAD STEP handoff to a supplier

Ask the supplier which exchange format, units and tolerances they accept. If they request STEP, send one sample part and ask for a measurable confirmation that the mounting holes and thickness arrived as expected.

Keep the *editable design history* separately from the production exchange file. Agree on a revision identifier and put it on the drawing as well as the file. A short confirmation at this stage can spare both sides a second quotation or an incorrect run.


## Features and decision criteria

- Parametric 3D modeling
- Editable model history
- Workflows for CAD file exchange
