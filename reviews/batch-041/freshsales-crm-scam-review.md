# Freshsales CRM review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Sales CRM and customer management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Freshsales CRM combines contacts, accounts, deals, activity timelines and sales communication features. Its public comparison shows a Free tier for up to three users and paid per-user plans with higher workflow, storage and email limits.

## Editorial verdict

Freshworks publishes a detailed comparison of its Free, Growth, Pro and Enterprise plans. Teams should check user counts, email limits, phone-credit costs, workflow caps and data migration before connecting the CRM to active sales channels.

## Freshsales CRM review: contacts, accounts and deal pipelines

Freshsales is a CRM for sales teams that need to manage contacts, accounts and deals alongside activity timelines and pipeline status. The published plan comparison lists record management, sales activities, deal pipelines, email sync, built-in phone, website tracking and import or export functions. Availability for each feature depends on the tier.

Define a small set of sales stages and required fields before importing the entire contact database. Test a lead that becomes a customer, a duplicate contact and a deal assigned to a different salesperson. Verify that account history remains connected and that a representative can see only the records they are authorized to use.


## Freshsales Free and paid plan pricing

Freshworks’ current US comparison shows Free at $0 for up to three users, Growth at $9 per user per month billed annually, Pro at $39 and Enterprise at $59. The feature table includes workflow limits, file storage, sales sequences and differing pipeline capabilities. It also lists phone credits as an add-on starting at $5.

The Free plan can be useful for a very small team, but it has fewer automation and administration controls. Paid plans are priced per user, so include managers, sales operations and temporary staff in the seat calculation. Confirm which email, telephone, storage and workflow quotas apply to the exact tier before selecting the CRM; additional communication credits can make the subscription total larger than the base price.


## Freshsales email, workflow and telephony limits

The comparison shows that email capabilities and daily sends vary by plan. Growth includes limited basic workflows and 250 personalized bulk emails per user per day in the displayed comparison; Pro lists larger workflow and email-sequence capacity. Built-in phone and local or toll-free numbers are also plan features, with phone credits billed separately.

Send a controlled test to internal addresses and confirm how campaign or one-to-one email is logged. Verify opt-out handling and avoid treating a CRM import as marketing consent. Check how calls are recorded, what phone credits cost and which agent roles can access call notes. If your sales process depends on automation, rehearse a rule that updates one test record before applying it to the full pipeline.


## Freshsales data import, governance and exit readiness

Freshsales’ feature list describes data import, export and migration from other CRMs. Those features reduce some setup work but do not guarantee a clean migration. Compare a sample export from the old system with the imported record, including owner, stage, notes, dates, email history and consent indicators.

Designate a CRM administrator and record which system controls customer identity, consent and pipeline value. Restrict exports and bulk deletes, review connected marketplace apps, and disable access promptly when staff leave. Keep an independent backup and make sure the team can retrieve customer records if an integration or billing status changes.


## Freshsales alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Freshsales with Pipedrive, HubSpot and a spreadsheet-backed process using the same number of users, records, pipeline stages, email volume and telephone usage. Include migration effort, phone credits, automation rules and the time required to maintain data quality. A low entry price is only useful if the product fits the team’s sales habits and the next tier’s cost is understood.

Freshworks’ official plan comparison describes CRM software and its published tier limits; it does not establish that Freshsales is a scam. This is a source-based desk review, not a test of sales performance or phone quality. Check the live regional quote, data export and account access rules before placing the CRM at the center of a sales operation.


## Features and decision criteria

- Contact, account, deal and pipeline records
- Plan-dependent email sync, workflows, phone and sales sequences
- Import, export and CRM migration tools

## Pricing considerations

US Freshsales comparison prices checked 2026-11-16. Phone credits are an add-on starting at $5 in the comparison. Feature counts vary by tier, and monthly rates, taxes and localization may change the final quote. Confirm exact user and data limits at checkout.
