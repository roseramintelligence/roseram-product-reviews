# FreshBooks review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Small business invoicing and accounting  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

FreshBooks brings invoices, estimates, projects, time tracking, expenses and financial reports into a small-business service. Its current US pricing table varies by billable clients and paid add-ons. Temporary promotions can change the first months’ price, so compare the renewal amount and processing fees as well.

## Editorial verdict

FreshBooks is a practical option to evaluate for freelancers who need client invoicing, time tracking and expense records in one workflow. Lite is limited to five billable clients on the current table; teams and higher client counts should compare the appropriate plan and add-ons before importing records.

## Follow one client job through the records

Create a sample estimate, convert it to an invoice and record a payment using test data. Check whether the client, work, time and expense details remain connected when a report is produced. A workflow that looks simple at invoice creation can still require manual cleanup later.

Before moving live client history, export a small sample and compare totals, dates and attachments. This review did not create an account, send an invoice or process a payment.


## Plan around the client limit

FreshBooks currently lists five billable clients on Lite, fifty on Plus and unlimited clients on Premium. Count active billable clients rather than only the names in a contact list. Determine which tier applies when a client becomes inactive or returns.

The plan also lists team seats and payroll as add-ons. Include them in the total cost and check whether the offer price applies only for an introductory period.


## Verify fees beyond the subscription

The product supports multiple payment methods and payment-related features. Review card and bank-transfer rates, payout timing and any advanced payment add-on in the checkout terms. The subscription alone does not describe the full cost of collecting money.

Reconcile a test payment against the invoice and financial report. For tax or bookkeeping decisions, verify the accounting treatment with a qualified professional familiar with the business and jurisdiction.


## Keep exports and account access in view

If the service becomes part of invoicing and bookkeeping, identify who can access client details and how a complete export is obtained. Ask whether included reports and stored receipts can be downloaded before selecting a plan.

FreshBooks lists multiple features and paid options that can suit a service business. This review is based on publisher pages, not independent accounting validation, payment testing or hands-on assessment. Confirm current plan and data-handling terms before relying on it.


## Features and decision criteria

- Invoices, estimates and proposals
- Client and project tracking
- Time, expenses and receipt capture
- Reports and payment options

## Pricing considerations

The page currently displays a temporary promotional discount and paid add-ons. Confirm the renewal price, offer conditions, team seats, payment-processing rates and taxes at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the Lite plan support unlimited clients?

No. The current plan comparison lists five billable clients for Lite.

### Is an advertised discount the ongoing subscription rate?

Not necessarily. Check the regular renewal amount and the promotion’s conditions before purchasing.

### Are payroll and additional team seats included?

The pricing page lists these as additional costs on several tiers; check the quote for the actual account.
