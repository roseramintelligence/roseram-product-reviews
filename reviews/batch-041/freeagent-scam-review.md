# FreeAgent review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Small business accounting and invoicing  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

FreeAgent is cloud accounting software aimed at small businesses, freelancers, landlords and accountants. Its product pages describe invoicing, estimates, expense capture, project and time tracking, bank feeds, cash-flow views and tax-related workflows. Eligibility, prices and features vary by market and business type.

## Editorial verdict

FreeAgent is a candidate for UK-centered businesses that want invoicing and bookkeeping workflows alongside HMRC-related features; its US site presents different sales-tax and multi-currency details. The bank-funded free offer is conditional on eligible accounts, and direct plan promotions are temporary. Confirm jurisdiction, entity type, accounting basis and recurring price before moving records.

## Follow an invoice through the bookkeeping workflow

A practical evaluation starts with a small fictional job: create an estimate, turn it into an invoice, record an expense and see how the resulting activity appears in reports. Check that client names, dates and payment status remain easy to reconcile. This makes the demo more useful than judging the dashboard alone.

Before importing live records, export a sample from the current system and check the fields FreeAgent can accept. Keep a copy of the original data and verify opening balances with a bookkeeper. This review did not create an account, connect a bank or file a tax return.


## Check eligibility and local tax fit first

FreeAgent’s US and UK pages describe distinct pricing and tax contexts. The UK site highlights HMRC-related Making Tax Digital workflows, while the US page describes sales-tax reporting and multi-currency invoicing. Treat those as regional product descriptions, not as a promise that a feature applies to every account or location.

Write down the legal entity, reporting jurisdiction, tax basis and required filing tasks before choosing a plan. Ask a qualified accountant whether the product’s workflow is suitable for those requirements. Software can organize records but cannot decide how a transaction should be treated in a specific situation.


## Calculate the ongoing cost, not only the promotion

The US pricing page displayed a temporary discount alongside standard monthly and annual amounts when checked. Compare the recurring price after the offer ends, the billing interval and any add-on required for your receipt volume. A bank-funded account has separate eligibility conditions, so do not assume that a promotional headline is a permanent price.

Include payment processing and any connected services in a cost estimate. Confirm how the plan can be cancelled, what happens to stored records after cancellation and how to download a complete copy before committing.


## Decide whether the workflow matches the business

FreeAgent may make sense when a small business wants invoicing and expense records close to time tracking and project information. A company with inventory complexity, multiple entities or specialized approval rules should map those workflows before switching. Count the manual steps that remain, not only the features in a product list.

This is an editorial assessment based on the publisher’s linked pages, not a hands-on trial, security audit or accounting opinion. Confirm current pricing and feature eligibility directly with FreeAgent, then test using sample data before relying on it for business records.


## Features and decision criteria

- Invoices, estimates and payment options
- Expense capture, project tracking and time entry
- Bank feeds and cash-flow views
- Tax workflows that vary by country and business type

## Pricing considerations

Promotional amounts and eligibility may change. The cited US page says prices exclude applicable taxes and lists an optional Smart Capture add-on. Check the actual renewal amount, add-ons and regional terms at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is FreeAgent free for every business?

No. The cited pricing page makes the free account conditional on eligible banking arrangements; otherwise a direct plan applies.

### Are FreeAgent tax features identical everywhere?

No. Product features and tax workflows depend on region, entity type and selected accounting basis.

### Does the advertised first-term discount last forever?

No. The pricing page distinguishes a promotion from the regular renewal price.
