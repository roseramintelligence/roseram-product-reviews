# Format review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Portfolio website builder for creators  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Format is a hosted portfolio website platform aimed at photographers and other visual creators. Its plans include site templates, galleries, image and video hosting, client workflows and store capacity; page, image, gallery and storage limits vary by tier.

## Editorial verdict

Format is a plausible choice for a visual creator who wants a portfolio, client galleries and a basic online store in one service. The Basic tier has tighter page and image limits, while Pro and Pro Plus raise storage and workflow capacity. A portfolio should be tested on mobile, at full image size and through a real client-proofing process before the creator commits to an annual plan.

## Build around the work you need to show

A portfolio website is often the first place a client checks whether a creator’s work fits a project. Format offers templates, image galleries and client workflow features aimed at photographers and visual creators. Start by grouping a small, coherent set of work and writing captions that explain the problem, your role and the result.

Large image files and custom layouts can look polished but make a site slow or difficult to navigate. Preview the portfolio on a phone, confirm that the most important work loads promptly and make contact information easy to find. The vendor’s published feature list is a starting point; test the exact theme and media sizes you expect to publish.


## Plan for limits on pages, images and galleries

Format’s current plan comparison lists different limits for pages, high-resolution images, storage, client galleries, file transfers and store products. A simple portfolio may fit the entry plan, while a working studio with many client proofing spaces can need a larger allowance. Count the assets and active client projects you expect to maintain during a busy season.

Confirm whether a plan limit applies to published content, stored drafts or both. If a limit requires upgrading, calculate the new annual cost and the effort of organizing or removing assets. A creator who has outgrown a plan should be able to find that transition in the account before a client receives a broken gallery link.


## Budget for domain, email and annual renewal

The Format help article lists domain and email services as separate add-ons supplied through third-party providers and managed through Format. The official pricing page also shows introductory offers that may change the cost for the first term. Budget the standard renewal price for the portfolio plan and each add-on separately.

If you move an existing domain to Format, document its current DNS records and mail configuration before making changes. Test the domain and sender address after setup. A website subscription, domain registration and hosted email are related but separate services; a change to one should not unintentionally interrupt the others.


## Test the client delivery and portability path

Creators often need more than a public portfolio: a password-protected gallery, downloads, contracts, proofs or file transfers. Run through those tasks with a sample client account, including a mobile review and a large-file download. Check whether a client can select favorites and whether permissions remain clear after the project is complete.

Before loading a full archive, verify how to export original images, page text, gallery data and customer details. Keep original media stored outside the website builder. This review is based on Format’s published plan information and does not claim to have built or tested a live portfolio site.


## Features and decision criteria

- Hosted portfolio builder with templates and mobile-optimized pages
- Plan limits for pages, images, galleries, video minutes, file transfers and store products
- Optional domain and email add-ons renew separately
- 14-day trial listed without a credit card on the public pricing page

## Pricing considerations

Format’s page may display seasonal discounts, and its help article may show different standard prices. Add-on domain and Google Workspace email renew separately. Confirm the regular annual price, page/image allowance, store count and renewal amount at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Format only for photographers?

Format positions its portfolio platform for visual creatives; the portfolio templates and galleries may also suit other creators who need a visual project showcase.

### Does the cheapest plan include unlimited pages?

No. The current comparison lists page caps on Basic, with unlimited pages on higher tiers.

### Does the free trial require a credit card?

The current Format pricing page describes a 14-day trial with no credit card required; confirm the signup flow in your region.
