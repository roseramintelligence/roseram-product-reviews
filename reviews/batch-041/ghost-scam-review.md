# Ghost review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Newsletter publishing and memberships  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Ghost combines a publication website, newsletters, and memberships. Ghost(Pro) handles hosting and managed email delivery. A publisher can also run the open-source software on its own infrastructure.

## Editorial verdict

Ghost documents both its managed service and the underlying open-source project. The cited sources identify the offer and establish no fraud finding. Check the seller and terms of any third-party hosting package separately.

## Ghost(Pro) pricing for a first paid newsletter

Price the edition you will actually launch. The **Ghost(Pro) Starter plan** covers a solo publication, but the comparison places paid subscriptions and custom themes on higher tiers. Sketch the first offer, including which articles will be public, which will have a paid tier, and how readers will get to checkout. Quote the lowest tier that supports those specific steps.

Model the bill at the current member count and at the next likely threshold. Treat *registered members* and paying customers as separate totals. Add processor fees and the cost of any existing email service that still has to handle receipts or account notices. Buttondown lists free sending for the first 100 subscribers and itemizes paid subscriptions as a $9 monthly add-on. Compare that price at the same list size against the Ghost plan that fits your website and paid membership requirements. Test the signup and cancellation experience with each.


## Ghost self-hosting vs Ghost(Pro) when something breaks

The self-hosting guide describes running Ghost on your own server. Put a named owner against updates, sender configuration and incident recovery before deciding that a VPS is cheaper. For a small publication, restoring a Friday issue during a holiday weekend is a better comparison than the monthly server sticker price.

Run a proposed recovery drill on a secondary domain. Restore content and images, check a member login, then send one issue to addresses you control. Measure the time between a clean server and a working publication so you have an operating cost to compare with managed hosting.


## Ghost newsletter migration for an existing publication

Use a sample of historical posts with image captions, embeds and old URLs. Follow those addresses after import and note which ones require redirects. Review how signed-out readers encounter a paywall and whether they can return to the same article after signing in.

Import a small test CSV before moving the main list. Check tags, email status and subscription records against the source export. Ask the person who sends the newsletter to reconcile those fields and explain how they would reverse the switch if a scheduled issue goes out from the wrong system.


## Features and decision criteria

- Website and newsletter publishing
- Member CSV import and export
- Managed hosting and self-hosting options

## Pricing considerations

The Ghost(Pro) comparison shows different feature limits by tier. Check the audience size, staff seats, billing interval and fees from your payment processor.
