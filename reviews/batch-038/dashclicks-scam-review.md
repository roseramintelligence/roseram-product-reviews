# DashClicks review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** White-label marketing agency platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

DashClicks offers an agency software suite with CRM, funnels, websites, projects, reporting, reviews, inbox and client-facing features, alongside a separate marketing-service fulfillment marketplace. Its pricing page lists software access at US$199 monthly or US$1,788 annually; fulfillment services are separately paid.

## Editorial verdict

DashClicks may suit an agency that wants a white-label client platform and a separate catalog of marketing fulfillment services. The US$199 software subscription does not include the cost of purchased services, and DashClicks’ service policy says fees are prepaid and non-refundable. Review each service’s scope, deliverables, cancellation and client obligations before reselling it under your brand.

## Separate agency software from service fulfillment

DashClicks sells an agency-focused software platform and also lists white-label fulfillment services. The software page describes CRM, funnels, websites, projects, reporting, reputation and client-management apps. The fulfillment catalog includes services such as advertising, SEO, content, listings and social posts. These are separate cost decisions.

A software subscription may centralize client communication, but it does not automatically include campaign labor, ad spend, content production or backlink work. For each service, identify the deliverable, who performs it, what the client sees, and how your agency remains responsible for accuracy and quality.


## Budget the subscription and fulfillment separately

DashClicks’ pricing page lists Pro software at US$199 per month or US$1,788 annually. It describes unlimited users and sub-accounts and access to its apps. The same page calls the fulfillment center free to access while saying buyers pay for services they order.

Build a full agency cost estimate with seats or accounts, fulfillment orders, advertising budgets, taxes and client support time. Annual billing lowers the subscription amount but ties up cash for the term. Do not tell a client that a marketing service is included simply because its catalog can be opened from the platform.


## Read the service policy before ordering

DashClicks’ current service policy says services are prepaid and non-refundable, and describes account setup as beginning immediately after purchase. It also states that cancellation of a subscription is available through the account dashboard or by contacting the company. Those terms make the service-order description especially important.

Before purchasing, request the exact scope, timeline, revision limit, account access required, reporting method and cancellation or refund rules for that service. Confirm whether the work is fulfilled under your brand and what happens if an external platform rejects or removes a campaign. Keep the written order with your client agreement.


## Check white-label and client-access behavior

The platform is marketed for agencies reselling tools and services under a brand. Test what a client can see, how invoices and project updates are labeled and whether you can control access by sub-account. Verify how data is separated between clients before moving live customer records.

Use a test sub-account to check lead intake, communications, reports, payments and permissions. Confirm how a staff member’s access is removed and how client data can be exported. This review is not a DashClicks implementation or a claim about the quality of third-party fulfillment.


## Protect your agency’s own claims and reputation

When reselling a marketing service, your agency remains accountable for what you promise the client. Avoid guaranteeing search rankings, leads or revenue unless the contract and evidence support that promise. Explain the difference between software access, service labor and media spend.

Ask for reports that tie the purchased work to the agreed scope. Review creative, listings and outreach before publication, and retain a record of client approvals. This review summarizes DashClicks’ public product and policy pages; it does not report a service order or audited agency outcome.


## Features and decision criteria

- White-label agency software with CRM, websites, funnels and reporting
- Unlimited users and sub-accounts listed with Pro software plan
- Separate fulfillment center for ads, SEO, content, listings and other services
- Paid fulfillment services have distinct service scope and payment terms

## Pricing considerations

DashClicks’ service policy says services are prepaid and non-refundable, and that subscriptions can be canceled through the dashboard or by contacting the company. Read the specific service agreement and payment terms before purchasing. Annual software price does not include advertising budgets or fulfillment work.
