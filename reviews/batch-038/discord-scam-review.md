# Discord review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Community messaging and voice  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Discord runs communities organized into servers and channels. Its support guide describes server roles and channel permission overrides.

## Editorial verdict

Discord documents how permission controls work. Verify instructions from any third-party moderator, bot vendor or server seller independently.

## Discord roles and permissions for a private channel

Start by asking members what they expect to be private. In a spare server, create a welcome area and an organizer channel. Check them through a new member and a moderator. Apply **Discord channel permissions** deliberately and verify who can read and post after their role changes.

Inspect what a new arrival sees before giving out invitations. The people who need help should know where to ask without being shown conversations intended for organizers.


## Discord moderator handoff and a record members can find

Write down how a volunteer reports a problem, whom they contact and which actions they can take. Rehearse with a harmless message in a test channel. Confirm that the volunteer can act without holding unnecessary control over the rest of the server.

Keep *important community decisions* in a place members can find later. Chat can settle a question quickly. A clearly organized reference saves the next member from having to ask it again.


## Features and decision criteria

- Community channels and roles
- Channel-specific permission overrides
- Private channel setup
