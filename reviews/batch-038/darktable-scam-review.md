## Editorial verdict

The project documentation explains the editing workflow. A paid download or training bundle should identify the seller and what the fee buys.

## darktable RAW workflow for a paid portrait job

The customer will remember the photograph and the delivery date. Pick one indoor RAW file and one daylight image, then edit both to the same brief. Export two proof sizes and inspect skin tones and texture. Keep the original exposures so another editor can explain or undo a difficult correction.

Build that practice into the first delivery. Record what was approved and what still needs a revision. A client should be able to ask for a brighter background without having to repeat an earlier conversation about the subject’s skin tone.


## darktable XMP sidecar backup and restore

The manual describes **XMP sidecars** as part of the editing workflow. Move copies of a RAW image and its sidecar to a fresh test library. Compare the recovered edit with the delivered proof and see what organizational data is still available.

Ask who owns the *editable archive* when the photographer leaves a project. Store a verified export with the original files and edit information. A backup routine earns its place when a second person can retrieve the photograph a client requested.
