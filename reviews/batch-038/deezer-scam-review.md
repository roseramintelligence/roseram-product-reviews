# Deezer review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Music streaming subscriptions  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Deezer is a music-streaming service with free and paid listening paths on the checked US page. Its official materials describe playlists, recommendations and plan-dependent offline listening. Availability, trial eligibility and device support should be checked for the actual account and region.

## Editorial verdict

Deezer is worth evaluating through the music and devices a listener actually uses. Check specific recordings, playlist transfer and the post-trial experience rather than choosing from a headline catalog size. A streaming subscription supplies a listening service, not general rights to reuse music in commercial content.

## Try the recordings you care about

A large catalog is less informative than whether the particular album version or recording you want is available. Build a short test list containing familiar favorites and a few harder-to-find items. Inspect the actual results rather than assuming a title refers to the version you intended.

Use the service on the devices that form the ordinary listening routine. This review did not authenticate an account, compare sound quality or verify regional availability of specific tracks. The official overview establishes the service’s scope, not a complete catalog audit.


## A playlist transfer deserves a sample check

A transfer feature can save repetitive selection while still needing review. Start with a small playlist and compare its destination entries, especially recordings with similar names or several releases. A reported completion count does not tell you whether every musical choice was preserved.

Keep the original list until the result is understood. This is a proposed acceptance exercise, not a claim that Roseram tested the provider’s transfer path or found a particular matching defect.


## Offline use needs to be tested before the interruption

If listening away from a connection matters, confirm the actual plan and download process before traveling. Check that the intended content is available through the app under the relevant terms. A playlist visible in the interface is not the same observation as successful offline playback.

Inspect current account and device requirements directly. The provider’s feature list should prompt a practical check instead of a promise that every device or situation behaves identically.


## Separate the trial from the recurring commitment

The checked page distinguishes a trial and a monthly rate, with eligibility conditions. Use the ongoing amount and current cancellation terms in the comparison. Temporary access should not obscure the cost of keeping the same experience.

Compare alternatives using the same music and devices. Roseram did not purchase a subscription, benchmark playback or determine reuse rights for a particular recording. This source-based review distinguishes a listening service from a completed account acceptance test.


## Features and decision criteria

- Free and paid music listening
- Playlists and discovery features
- Plan-dependent offline listening
- Device and library-transfer resources

## Pricing considerations

The checked US page advertises a one-month trial for eligible users. Verify location, eligibility, taxes and the actual checkout; an introductory trial is not the recurring rate.

### Does a one-month trial mean the subscription stays free?

No. The checked offer displays a recurring price after the eligible trial.

### Can I use subscription music as my video soundtrack automatically?

A listening subscription does not establish the separate rights needed for reuse in a project.
