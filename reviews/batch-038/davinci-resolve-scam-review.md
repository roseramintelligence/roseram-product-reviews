# DaVinci Resolve review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Video editing and color finishing  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Blackmagic Design presents DaVinci Resolve as an editing, color, effects and audio application and offers a separate Studio edition.

## Editorial verdict

The publisher describes both editions. Verify the edition and seller if you are offered a software license or bundled hardware.

## DaVinci Resolve free vs Studio for a paid edit

Start with the camera file that caused the last production delay. Open that sample, make a short cut and export to the client’s specification. Use the **DaVinci Resolve edition comparison** to check whether a missing feature or format affects the job before budgeting for Studio.

Compare a simple and a demanding shot on the workstation that will do the work. Ask which delay would be more expensive to a customer: an extra render, an excluded effect or rebuilding a sequence close to delivery.


## DaVinci Resolve color handoff and approval playback

Send the colorist a short timeline with a source image, a graded shot and one clip that needs revision. Agree on the color and output settings. Ask the client to review a reference where they actually watch the work.

Give the next editor an *approved reference export* and the agreed editable materials. Label what can change and what has already been approved so the next editor can make a late correction while preserving the client’s prior approvals.

- Video editing and color grading
- Visual effects and audio tools
