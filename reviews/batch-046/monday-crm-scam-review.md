# monday CRM review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Sales CRM and customer relationship management software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

monday CRM is a sales-focused product on the monday.com platform for managing contacts, deals, communications and sales workflows. Plans differ in contact capacity, workspaces, dashboards, automation and AI features.

## Editorial verdict

monday CRM offers configurable sales boards with plan-specific contact and workflow caps. Buyers should verify the seat minimum, current price for their team size, renewal terms and the record model before moving sales activity into the platform.

## monday CRM review: sales records on configurable boards

monday CRM adapts the monday.com board model to leads, contacts, deals and sales activity. Teams can configure columns, dashboards and workflow rules around their sales process, and eligible plans include communication timelines, mobile access and AI features. The visual model may be approachable for teams that already use monday.com for other work.

Configuration is not a substitute for a consistent sales method. Define how a lead becomes qualified, what counts as a forecastable opportunity and which fields are mandatory at each stage. Pilot with a few representatives and compare the CRM record with the actual email, call and meeting history. If the team maintains an independent spreadsheet for the same funnel, identify why before launching a full migration.


## monday CRM pricing, seat minimum and contact caps

The current monday CRM pricing page displays annual per-seat rates of $12 for Basic, $17 for Standard and $28 for Pro at the shown configuration, with Ultimate priced by quote. It lists 1,000 active contacts and deals for Basic, 10,000 for Standard and 100,000 for Pro; Ultimate is described with unlimited contact and deal capacity. The page says plans start at three users, offers monthly or annual payment and notes that prices depend on billing country and exclude tax.

The three-seat minimum means a solo buyer should compare the total account amount rather than the per-seat headline. Forecast contact growth, duplicates, inactive records and any imported test data against the cap. Include AI credits, automations, dashboards and email features in the tier decision. Confirm that a displayed annual rate is billed upfront for the selected term and record the renewal date before connecting the sales team's mailbox.


## monday CRM communications, automation and AI

The plan comparison describes a centralized communication hub and mobile access in Standard, with mass email and sequences listed at Pro. Automation allowances also rise by tier, and the product page describes AI credits and assistant features. These distinctions can affect adoption: a team may see CRM as an email tool, but the amount of email functionality and automated follow-up available depends on the specific plan.

Run a controlled workflow with internal test records. Verify opt-out handling, who can send bulk messages, whether the CRM records replies and how automated tasks behave when an owner changes. Review AI output before it writes to customer records or contacts a prospect. Keep consent fields and suppression lists intact during imports; putting a person's address in a CRM is not the same as permission to send marketing.


## monday CRM migration, permissions and mobile use

A sales CRM holds customer identifiers, notes and commercial information, so migration and access design matter as much as board layout. monday.com publishes a trust center and plan documentation, but a buyer should still verify the contract, data region, role controls and administrative features that apply to the purchased tier. Teams should also decide how a contact is retained or removed when a relationship ends.

Export a small sample from the existing system and compare owners, deal stages, notes, dates, activity history and consent markers after import. Use separate roles for administrators, representatives and read-only reviewers. Test the mobile app with an account executive who is actually on the road, including offline or weak-network conditions if those are part of the job. Document how a departing employee's access and customer assignments will be handled.


## monday CRM alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare monday CRM with HubSpot, Pipedrive, Freshsales and the sales features in your existing business suite. Keep the number of paid seats, contacts, email volume, dashboards and automation actions constant. Include seat bundles, annual payment, integration administration and time spent cleaning records. A flexible board-based CRM can be a good fit when teams value configuration; a sales organization with complex forecasting or service handoffs may require more specialized controls.

monday.com's official CRM pricing and support pages describe a commercial sales CRM and plan limits; they do not establish that monday CRM is a scam. This desk review did not run a sales campaign or assess conversion performance. Confirm current country-specific pricing, record caps, email permissions, data export and renewal terms before importing a production contact database.


## Features and decision criteria

- Lead, contact and deal tracking on configurable boards
- Email and activity timelines, dashboards and automation by plan
- Mobile apps and AI features with plan-specific credit allowances

## Pricing considerations

The page says CRM plans start at three users and offers monthly or annual billing. Displayed totals depend on selected seats and billing country; prices exclude tax. Confirm the final checkout amount.
