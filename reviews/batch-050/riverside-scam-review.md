# Riverside review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Remote audio and video recording platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Riverside is a remote recording, editing, hosting and live-streaming platform for podcasts and video shows. Plans meter separate-track downloads and studio counts, while listing local recording, cloud backup and high-resolution capture; its pricing page can display changing introductory and annual-billing offers.

## Editorial verdict

Riverside is worth evaluating when a remote interview needs separate audio and video tracks that can be edited after the call. The plan comparison shows up to 4K desktop video, recording for up to 10 people and different monthly allowances for separate-track downloads. Confirm the plan’s billing total, export allowance and mobile quality before recording a season; a strong recording setup still needs a backup and a tested guest workflow.

## Protect the source recording

Riverside is designed for remote recordings where each person’s audio and video can be captured separately. Separate tracks are useful when a guest coughs, a microphone level changes or an editor needs to make a precise cut. Before inviting a guest, test that each track is visible and that the browser or app has microphone and camera permissions.

The plan page lists local recording and automatic cloud backups. A backup feature reduces the risk of losing a session, but the producer should still confirm that every participant’s file completed upload and that the downloaded project opens in the editor. For an important interview, consider a local recorder as a second path. This review describes documented features, not a completed Riverside recording test.


## Plan around separate-track limits

Riverside’s current comparison shows that plans can differ by monthly hours of separate audio and video track downloads. That is a different quantity from the recording-hours allowance. A show that captures multiple weekly episodes may fill the download allowance sooner than expected, especially if the team downloads several takes or edits from multiple versions.

Estimate the number of recording hours, number of participants and source-quality files you intend to retrieve each month. Include both the final master and any backup assets. If a plan’s number is not clear, ask the vendor whether a session with multiple guests counts by elapsed session time or by track duration before committing to a production schedule.


## Compare real checkout and publishing costs

The pricing page currently presents monthly equivalents alongside yearly totals, and promotional displays can change between visits. Store a screenshot or order summary that shows the chosen plan, annual total, tax and next renewal date. Do not multiply a displayed monthly figure by one month if checkout charges the full annual commitment at once.

Riverside lists publishing and hosting features by tier, including support for different numbers of podcast shows. If your podcast host already handles distribution, compare that service’s cost and feed ownership against the bundled Riverside feature. Keeping a portable copy of your RSS metadata and original media makes it easier to move if the product no longer fits.


## Rehearse the guest and editor experience

A remote studio can produce a clean file, but the guest still needs a simple invitation, browser permissions and an audio setup that avoids echo. Send a short checklist, ask guests to use headphones and record a one-minute test. Confirm that the host can see each participant, start the session and find the finished files afterward.

If you plan to use transcription, clips, AI show notes or live features, test those separately from recording quality. Review names, edits and captions before publishing. Keep the original track and a corrected transcript with the episode. The vendor’s plan details help compare capabilities; they are not a guarantee of audience growth, a hands-on quality rating or evidence of user satisfaction.


## Features and decision criteria

- Remote recording with separate audio/video tracks and local capture
- Desktop recording up to 4K; mobile video quality varies by plan
- Track downloads have monthly hour allowances even when recording hours are listed as unlimited
- Podcast hosting, transcripts, AI editing and live tools vary by tier

## Pricing considerations

The official page currently shows dynamic offers and annual-billing prices, and another page view may show a different promotional amount. Recording hours and separate-track download hours are distinct limits. Verify the exact checkout total, renewal, tax, host count and trial terms before purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does unlimited recording mean unlimited downloadable tracks?

No. The plan comparison lists unlimited recording hours but separate monthly allowances for downloading separate tracks.

### How many people can join one recording?

The cited plan comparison lists up to 10 people recorded in a single session.

### Is Riverside’s listed monthly price a month-to-month bill?

Not necessarily. The current comparison displays annual billing totals for the prices shown; confirm the billing selection and amount due at checkout.
