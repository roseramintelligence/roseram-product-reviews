# Restream review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Live streaming and multistreaming service  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Restream helps creators broadcast from a browser or encoder to multiple destinations, with studio scenes, shared chat and cloud recording features. Its free plan supports two simultaneous channels with Restream branding; paid plans add channels, quality, recording and team options.

## Editorial verdict

Restream suits a creator who wants to manage a live show and distribute it across several platforms from one workflow. The free tier is a practical starting point for two destinations, while the paid tiers are most relevant when you need extra channels, Full HD studio output, longer cloud recordings or teammates. Verify destination-specific restrictions and the monthly versus annual price selector before purchase.

## Choose destinations before choosing a tier

Restream’s free tier allows two destinations at once, which can be enough for a small creator testing whether audiences respond on more than one platform. The next decision should be where viewers actually watch, not how many logos a plan can connect. List the destinations you will use for the next month and check whether each supports the connection method your show requires.

A multistreaming setup still needs a reliable source signal and a prepared show. Rehearse from the same camera, encoder and network you expect to use live. Check audio sync, overlays, chat moderation and the destination-specific stream preview. This review draws on Restream’s published documentation and does not report a hands-on broadcast test.


## Compare creator and team features separately

Paid tiers change more than the number of destinations. The pricing page lists differences in Studio resolution, guest channels, cloud recording, a website player, team seats and co-producer controls. A solo host may prioritize channel count, while a small production team may care more about control-room roles and a backup person who can manage the show.

Make a short feature list tied to the production you actually run. If you only need a browser studio, encoder compatibility may not matter. If you use OBS or another tool, confirm that your connection path is supported. Avoid paying for a higher tier based only on a feature that your current workflow never uses.


## Calculate storage and add-ons

Restream lists recording-length and storage-retention differences among plans. A creator who wants to repurpose each stream should check how long files remain accessible and whether separate guest tracks are included. Decide where the finished recordings will be archived before the plan’s retention window expires.

Clips and webinars are shown as paid add-ons in the current pricing display, so they should be considered separate budget lines. The apparent monthly plan price may also change with annual billing selection. Compare the total due today and the renewal interval, then test a download and archive process before making Restream the only copy of important recordings.


## Plan for platform dependencies

Restream centralizes distribution, but the social platforms still control their account permissions, stream keys and policy requirements. Review which account owns the destination, how an administrator can revoke access, and what happens when a team member leaves. Do not share stream credentials in a public production document.

A safe trial is to schedule an unlisted or low-stakes stream, confirm each destination receives the expected format, and inspect the audience experience. Keep an independent copy of the source recording and event notes. Published feature descriptions are useful for buying decisions, but they are not a promise that every destination connection will work under every account’s restrictions.


## Features and decision criteria

- Browser studio and encoder integrations for multistreaming
- Free tier streams to two channels and includes Restream branding
- Paid tiers increase simultaneous destination and studio features
- Recording duration and storage retention differ by tier; add-ons priced separately

## Pricing considerations

The official page offers a monthly/annual selector; its annual view applies a discount. Recording limits and retention differ by tier, and Clips/Webinars may be separate paid add-ons. Verify the current currency, annual total, destination eligibility and add-on price at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I multistream with Restream for free?

Yes. Restream lists two simultaneous channels on its free plan, with Restream branding.

### Does the free plan include all Studio capabilities?

No. Paid plans add channel capacity and other features such as branding removal, higher studio quality, recording or team controls.

### Are recording features unlimited?

Not across all tiers. The plan comparison lists recording length and storage retention limits, which vary by plan.
