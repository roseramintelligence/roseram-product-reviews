# AvidViews review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Digital qualitative research and moderated interview platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

AvidViews is Avidestal’s digital qualitative-research platform for moderated video or audio interviews and focus groups. Its product materials describe participant and moderator roles, in-call polls and media, recording, transcription, and optional facial-coding sentiment and eye-tracking analysis. Avidestal lists Basic at US$49 per month and Standard at US$89 per month, with yearly totals shown, plus trial and custom plans; the pricing table has several usage conditions and add-on charges that buyers should confirm directly. Calls can include sensitive research data, participant recordings and biometric-style analysis, so consent and retention controls need careful review.

## Editorial verdict

AvidViews may fit market-research teams that need live moderated interviews, small focus groups and structured media or poll prompts in one workflow. It can be more involved than a general video meeting tool because the product also includes research controls and optional analysis. Before selecting a plan, confirm recording minutes, participant caps, transcription and analysis add-on prices, data storage and the treatment of face or eye-tracking signals. Run a consented pilot with non-sensitive research before moving a client study onto the platform.

## A moderated research room for interviews and focus groups

AvidViews is positioned as a digital qualitative-research suite rather than a general-purpose meeting room. Avidestal describes one-to-one discussions and focus groups, with roles for moderators, clients or backroom observers, respondents and a translator. The product page says clients can observe and communicate with moderators without respondents seeing them, a workflow intended for moderated research sessions.

The listed maximum is 16 people across defined roles: three moderators, three backroom participants or clients, nine respondents and one translator. Confirm whether those caps apply to your intended plan and whether external participants can join through the browser or mobile app without installing separate software. Test the exact joining experience with a small pilot group before scheduling a field study.


## Use polls and media to structure a live session

AvidViews describes in-call polls with several question formats, including single and multiple selection, image selection and rating. Moderators can also share image or video material and add polls during a call. These tools can help a research team compare reactions to a concept, advertisement or product prototype while the discussion is underway.

Plan the discussion guide before the session and test each prompt, media file and poll. Confirm the display quality on phones and laptops, accessibility of the materials and whether responses are associated with a participant in exported results. If observers use the backroom, set clear internal rules for who may communicate with the moderator and what participants are told about observation and recording.


## Review recording, transcription and analysis separately

The vendor lists automatic recording and transcript exports, alongside optional sentiment analysis and heat-map reporting. These functions involve different data and should be evaluated separately. A transcript may contain names, opinions or confidential customer feedback; video can capture faces and voices; facial coding and eye tracking may infer sensitive information. Determine what is collected, where it is processed, how long it is retained and who can access it.

Get informed participant consent that accurately describes recording and any automated analysis. Avoid enabling optional analysis simply because it is available. Ask whether consent can be tailored per study, how participants can withdraw, whether recordings can be deleted and what happens to backups and derived metrics. Review local legal and research-ethics requirements with qualified advisors for the specific study; the product page is not a compliance determination.


## Compare plan limits and extra charges

Avidestal’s public table displays Basic at US$49 per month and US$588 per year and Standard at US$89 per month and US$1,068 per year. The page also shows different trial and free-account access, meeting-minute limits, recording allowances and storage amounts. It lists transcription at US$6 per hour, emotional analysis at US$12 per analysis and heat mapping at US$5 per capture. The vendor says prices exclude taxes.

The plan table has several usage definitions and an enterprise or custom option, so verify in writing how a “minute,” “analysis” or “capture” is counted, whether a session can continue after a limit is reached, and whether there are overage fees. Avidestal’s FAQ says an ongoing paid call is not disconnected when its minutes run out, but new calls may be blocked until the account has a paid subscription. Confirm that behavior under the plan you intend to buy.


## Who may find AvidViews useful?

AvidViews may fit researchers who need moderated calls, structured prompts and a way to export session artifacts for analysis. It may not fit a team that only needs inexpensive video conferencing or cannot obtain participant consent for recording and automated interpretation. Compare the total study cost, including add-ons and recruitment, with a simpler meeting tool and a separate analysis workflow.

This guide summarizes Avidestal public product, pricing and FAQ pages; it is not an independent usability test, privacy audit or validation of sentiment-analysis accuracy. Pricing, limits and data practices can change. Confirm them with the vendor and your study participants before use.


## Features and decision criteria

- Audio or video one-to-one interviews and focus-group calls
- Moderator, client/backroom, respondent and translator roles with vendor-stated participant limits
- In-call polls, media sharing, recording and transcript exports
- Optional sentiment analysis using facial coding and eye-tracking heat maps, with separate add-on pricing shown

## Pricing considerations

The vendor’s public plan table contains several trial, daily-minute, meeting-minute and add-on rows, and indicates displayed costs exclude taxes. Verify exact plan eligibility, billing cadence, usage definitions and data terms with Avidestal before purchasing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of research does AvidViews support?

Avidestal describes moderated one-to-one interviews and group discussions, with polls, media prompts, recording and transcript exports.

### How many people can join a call?

The product page describes up to 16 roles in a call: up to three moderators, three backroom clients, nine participants and one translator.

### Are facial coding and eye tracking included?

The vendor lists these as optional analysis features and shows separate add-on charges. Confirm current plan access and the consent and data-handling details before enabling them.
