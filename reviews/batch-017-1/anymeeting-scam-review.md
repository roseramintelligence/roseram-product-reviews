# AnyMeeting review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-28  
**Category:** Video Conferencing and UCaaS Meeting Software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

AnyMeeting is the video conferencing and digital meeting feature set integrated directly within Intermedia Unite, a unified communications as a service platform. Rather than serving as an isolated consumer utility, AnyMeeting provides encrypted virtual rooms, interactive screen annotation, cloud recording storage, and automated AI meeting recaps. The solution scales across desktop, mobile, and web clients, hosting up to one hundred video participants on base tiers and two hundred on advanced enterprise licenses. Procurement requires evaluating broader communication needs, as AnyMeeting functions alongside VoIP telephony, messaging, and central administration.

## Platform Packaging Inside Intermedia Unite

To understand AnyMeeting today, an evaluation team must examine how Intermedia packages the software. The product does not operate as an isolated software-as-a-service application managed through an independent billing console. Instead, AnyMeeting serves as the embedded video meetings engine inside Intermedia Unite. When a business signs up for an account, users interact with a unified interface that controls cloud phone extensions, digital faxes, internal chat, SMS text messaging, and shared document storage. Initiating a conference occurs directly within the Meetings tab of the desktop, mobile, or web client rather than through a dedicated AnyMeeting login portal.

This architectural packaging has significant operational implications for procurement teams. Buyers who merely desire a simple screen-sharing screen for occasional customer demos must weigh whether adopting a complete voice-over-IP carrier makes commercial sense. On the other hand, organizations seeking to replace disparate vendors for desk phones, mobile softphones, team chat, and conference calling can consolidate those operational budgets into a single Intermedia console. Because AnyMeeting inherits the identity management and security configurations established at the tenant level, permissions, recordings, and meeting links remain synchronized across the wider organization.


## Pricing Structure and Procurement Requirements

Intermedia publishes transparent starting rates for its core Unite subscriptions, although full enterprise procurement involves careful review of peripheral charges. The entry-level tier, Intermedia Unite Pro, starts at twenty-seven dollars and ninety-nine cents per user per month. This base plan includes unbundled business phone calling across thirty-four countries, team chat, mobile and desktop applications, fifty gigabytes of user cloud storage, and video meetings hosting up to one hundred participants. For companies managing larger teams or broader conferencing demands, the Intermedia Unite Enterprise tier is priced at thirty-two dollars and ninety-nine cents per user per month, boosting meeting capacity to two hundred video attendees and expanding individual cloud storage to two hundred gigabytes.

While these starting numbers provide a concrete baseline, buyers must remember that a partial starting amount does not represent an all-inclusive offer for complex deployments. Unified communications platforms often involve ancillary operational line items depending on how an organization routes telephone traffic. Intermedia documents a variety of optional add-ons, including secondary fax lines at twelve dollars and ninety-nine cents per month, personal resource lines at seven dollars and forty-nine cents, and company messaging services ranging from nine dollars and ninety-nine cents to twenty-four dollars and ninety-nine cents per line. Additionally, enterprise compliance archiving, specialized contact center queues, and toll-free telephone numbers require custom sales quotes. Due diligence demands that financial officers map out their complete user roster, required hardware devices, and compliance requirements before estimating total annual cost.


## Core Meeting Capabilities and Participant Thresholds

In functional day-to-day operations, AnyMeeting delivers the core toolset required for structured remote collaboration. The software supports high-definition video feeds alongside independent audio channels, permitting participants to connect simultaneously using computer microphones or standard telephone dial-in numbers protected by meeting PIN codes. During live sessions, hosts and attendees can engage in real-time chat, maintain running session notes, and leverage interactive screen annotation tools to highlight specific data points on shared presentations or technical schematics. The system also supplies integrated whiteboards to facilitate spontaneous diagramming during sales demos or internal development scrums.

Participant capacities are determined strictly by the underlying Unite subscription tier assigned to the organizing host. On the Unite Pro tier, meetings are constrained to a ceiling of one hundred simultaneous video participants. Organizations that run firm-wide quarterly briefings or extensive customer training sessions may find this limit restrictive, necessitating the Unite Enterprise license to unlock the two hundred participant cap. Dial-in audio conferencing supports up to two hundred participants across both plan levels. Crucially, external guests do not need Intermedia account credentials to attend calls. A host can share a standard conference link or permanent personal room address via email, SMS, or third-party chat, allowing guests to join instantly from any modern web browser without administrative onboarding.


## AI Meeting Recaps and Productivity Workflows

A prominent capability highlighted across Intermedia's product documentation is the integration of automated artificial intelligence tools directly into the conference workflow. Branded as AI Meeting Recap, this system functions automatically on recorded sessions to reduce the manual administrative effort traditionally required after team calls. Once a session concludes, the engine processes the audio transcript to generate high-level executive summaries, chronological conversation insights, and parsed action items assigned to specific meeting participants. These summaries can be sent out to absent stakeholders or filed for accountability without demanding dedicated note-takers during the call.

Automated recaps offer clear productivity dividends, yet teams must verify their actual utility across complex technical discussions. Summarization algorithms often struggle with obscure internal acronyms, fast-paced code reviews, or heavy industry jargon. When evaluating AnyMeeting, an organization should design a pilot test with two separate recorded calls: one general management update and one dense technical planning session. Reviewers should carefully verify whether the post-meeting action items capture precise deadlines and technical nuances or if team leads still need to manually audit and revise the system transcript before distributing task lists to staff.


## Recording Storage and Administrative Retention Controls

Meeting documentation is deeply tied to digital storage policies, an area where AnyMeeting presents distinct advantages paired with strict regulatory caveats. Intermedia advertises unlimited cloud recording storage for meetings across its core tiers. This enables project managers and sales supervisors to record customer interactions, vendor demonstrations, and internal tutorials without calculating individual video file sizes or worrying about unexpected cloud overage fees. Meeting links and stored video files can be retrieved and reviewed directly through the desktop console or shared securely with external partners.

However, administrative officers must not confuse unlimited operational recording storage with legal evidentiary archiving. Intermedia's standard subscription includes a rolling thirty-day retention period for baseline communication data. Organizations operating under strict compliance mandates—such as financial brokerages, municipal agencies, or regulated healthcare providers—cannot rely on thirty-day rolling buffers to satisfy statutory discovery obligations. For extended preservation, Intermedia sells dedicated archiving add-ons supporting one-year, three-year, seven-year, or ten-year retention schedules for an additional charge. IT directors must review their internal data retention guidelines to ensure their Unite tenant is configured with the correct legal holds.


## Security Architecture, Privacy, and Compliance Alignment

Enterprise buyers routinely scrutinize video conferencing platforms for data protection controls, especially given heightened scrutiny surrounding eavesdropping and unauthorized access. AnyMeeting provides baseline conference defense controls, including end-to-end encryption features, meeting room locking, individual password requirements, and granular attendee permission toggles that prevent uninvited guests from seizing screen control or broadcasting unwanted audio. For healthcare organizations handling protected health information, Intermedia asserts that Unite can be configured to comply with HIPAA security rules. The company provides a formal Business Associate Agreement upon request, provided the client implements the administrative settings detailed in their operational security guides.

Security compliance is never an automatic software toggle; it requires disciplined internal execution. Intermedia notes that meeting compliance under HIPAA, GDPR, or CCPA depends heavily on internal administrative practices. For example, if a clinic host shares a non-expiring personal meeting URL over insecure personal text messages or fails to enforce meeting room passwords, patient confidentiality can be compromised regardless of underlying transmission encryption. Technical reviewers must thoroughly audit Intermedia's privacy policy and security whitepapers to ensure tenant settings align with their internal governance mandates.


## Software Delivery and Cross-Platform Accessibility

Modern remote work forces communication platforms to operate cleanly across highly fragmented endpoint hardware. AnyMeeting operates through the consolidated Intermedia Unite application, supported across desktop systems running macOS and Microsoft Windows, as well as mobile operating systems across iOS and Android via Apple App Store and Google Play downloads. In addition to native clients, the meeting suite supports browser-based access, permitting users on Chromebooks or restricted corporate laptops to join interactive video calls without running local installer packages or requiring elevated operating system privileges.

Intermedia claims that users can launch calls with effortless setup and without heavy IT support. In real-world enterprise environments, that claim depends on local network configurations. High-definition multi-party video conferencing demands stable network bandwidth, low packet loss, and unrestricted UDP port access for media streams. A business rolling out the platform across hundreds of branch offices or remote home offices must verify how the desktop client behaves through corporate firewalls, software-defined wide area networks, and enterprise proxy systems. Testing should evaluate whether video resolution downgrades gracefully during bandwidth contention or if media drops cause audio stuttering.


## Third-Party Integrations and Workflow Connectivity

Isolated communication tools create operational friction, prompting most businesses to seek deep integrations with their existing productivity suites. AnyMeeting connects with mainstream corporate environments through Intermedia Unite Extend modules. Built-in hooks link the meeting platform directly to Microsoft Outlook, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and Slack. These connectors allow knowledge workers to schedule video calls, attach personal conference room links, and launch impromptu video sessions directly from their everyday calendar interfaces or active chat channels without manually toggling between independent applications.

For customer-facing teams, the platform integrates with leading customer relationship management platforms. Intermedia documents compatibility with tools like Salesforce, ServiceNow, HubSpot CRM, Zendesk, Zoho CRM, SugarCRM, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, and Pipedrive. These connectors permit screen-pop caller identification, automatic interaction logging, and faster context retrieval during calls. However, buyers must review tier entitlements closely: while basic integrations like Google Workspace or Outlook calendar synchronization function on the Unite Pro plan, advanced CRM platforms such as Salesforce or NetSuite require the higher-priced Enterprise tier or custom configuration. IT managers should map out their essential software stacks prior to negotiating license tiers.

## Operational Limitations and Evaluation Blindspots

Despite its unified breadth, AnyMeeting exhibits several operational boundaries that prospective buyers must account for during vendor evaluations. The most prominent constraint is participant scale. With a maximum ceiling of two hundred participants on the Enterprise plan, AnyMeeting cannot host large corporate town halls, global developer summits, or expansive marketing broadcasts without purchasing specialized webinar add-on packages. Organizations with workforces numbering in the thousands will find standard Unite meeting rooms incapable of hosting all-hands assemblies, requiring secondary conferencing software or supplemental broadcast licensing.

Another operational boundary lies in Intermedia's bundled delivery model. Businesses that are already committed to long-term telephony contracts—such as an existing enterprise voice deployment with Cisco, Avaya, or Microsoft Teams Phone—may find paying twenty-seven dollars or more per seat redundant if they only want video screen sharing. While Intermedia provides an embedded Teams integration to layer voice features onto Microsoft's interface, organizations that strictly seek an inexpensive, ad-hoc conferencing tool will incur unnecessary overhead by acquiring an entire UCaaS stack. Procurement managers must honestly assess whether their organization will truly utilize the hundred-plus phone features, hunt groups, and virtual fax lines bundled alongside AnyMeeting.


## Structured Buyer Due Diligence and Pilot Testing

Organizations considering AnyMeeting within Intermedia Unite should avoid signing long-term annual agreements before executing a structured, empirical evaluation. A rigorous evaluation plan begins with a controlled thirty-day proof-of-concept involving a diverse cross-section of users, including remote knowledge workers, customer support agents, and technical team leads. The pilot should evaluate call quality under varied network environments, testing video smoothness and audio fidelity over home broadband, mobile cellular connections, and managed office local area networks.

During this evaluation window, administrative leads must systematically test security enforcement and workflow handoffs. Administrators should verify the ease of generating Business Associate Agreements if HIPAA compliance is required, confirm that meeting passwords and host controls operate reliably, and evaluate the accuracy of AI Meeting Recaps across different types of business discussions. Furthermore, testing must scrutinize calendar synchronization with Outlook or Google Workspace to ensure meeting links populate cleanly without attendee confusion. By measuring actual user adoption, administrative overhead, and peripheral add-on costs during a trial, buyers can confidently determine whether Intermedia Unite represents an efficient communication investment or an unnecessarily complex bundle.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can buyers purchase AnyMeeting as a standalone video conferencing product?

According to published documentation from Intermedia, AnyMeeting is integrated into the Intermedia Unite platform rather than sold as an isolated standalone utility. Accessing AnyMeeting requires purchasing a Unite license, such as the Pro or Enterprise tier, which bundles cloud voice telephony, business texting, chat, and file management alongside video conferencing.

### What participant limits apply to AnyMeeting video calls?

The platform supports up to 100 video participants on the Intermedia Unite Pro plan. Organizations requiring larger conferences can upgrade to the Intermedia Unite Enterprise plan, which accommodates up to 200 video participants and 200 audio conferencing participants.

### Do meeting attendees need to download software or register accounts to join?

Only the meeting host is required to hold an active Intermedia Unite account. Invited participants can join calls directly by clicking a shared conference link or personal URL via web browser, desktop, or mobile applications without creating personal accounts.

### Does AnyMeeting support compliance standards like HIPAA?

Intermedia states that AnyMeeting, as part of Intermedia Unite, includes encryption and permission controls designed to help enable compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA. For healthcare entities subject to HIPAA rules, Intermedia provides Business Associate Agreements when combined with recommended operational security settings.
