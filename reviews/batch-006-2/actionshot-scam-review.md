## ActionShot: a screenshot is evidence with missing context

A captured screen can contain an instruction, a tentative suggestion and a reply that changes the earlier instruction. A useful task extractor should not make those fragments equally authoritative. Before evaluating the result, decide which actions are explicitly requested and which would require clarification. The difference matters when a generated checklist appears more certain than the source image.

The publisher describes editable tasks and deadlines from screenshots. Editability is important because an image may not identify the responsible person, time zone or final decision. Treat a proposed deadline as something to verify against the actual conversation. This review does not establish that ActionShot resolves every ambiguous date or understands material outside the uploaded image.


## Test extraction with a deliberately ambiguous example

Create a fictional screenshot containing one clear action, a conditional suggestion and an incomplete date. Write your own expected checklist first. Compare the generated result with those expectations and inspect how easily a mistaken action can be corrected. A useful demonstration should show how uncertainty is handled, not only how a clean example becomes a neat list.

Use images you are permitted to process and remove unnecessary account details or personal information. Ask about the current storage and deletion arrangements before uploading work communications. A privacy-related slogan is not a substitute for understanding the published terms and the configuration you are using. We have not audited the product’s data handling.

Include a screenshot where the words next Friday appear without the date of the original conversation. The correct checklist may require a clarification rather than a guessed deadline. Review whether the resulting task makes that uncertainty visible. A system that saves a few seconds of typing but assigns the wrong date can create more coordination work than it removes from the process.
