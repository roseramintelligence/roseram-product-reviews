# Bullhorn ATS review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Staffing applicant tracking and CRM  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Bullhorn ATS & CRM combines applicant tracking and client relationship workflows for recruitment and staffing firms, with AI and automation options under its Amplify products. Bullhorn publishes some per-user prices for its small-agency tiers, while Pro, Max, larger-agency and specialized modules require a quote. Vendor productivity figures are marketing claims and are not independently verified here.

## Editorial verdict

Bullhorn ATS is designed for staffing workflows that connect candidates, open roles and client accounts. It may suit an agency that needs recruiting CRM, candidate tracking, communications, reporting and integrations in one operating system. Buyers should map their current recruiting process, test data migration and email/calendar workflows, and compare the actual seats and modules required with the published entry plans and quote-based tiers. This is a product buyer guide, not a report of hands-on testing.

## Understand the staffing-specific ATS and CRM model

Bullhorn positions its ATS & CRM around the connected relationship among candidates, open roles and client accounts. The product page describes job and candidate management, recruiter communication, client relationships, mobile access, analytics and integrations. That focus is useful to evaluate if your firm runs a staffing desk; a general-purpose applicant tracking system may organize employer hiring differently and may not model agency sales and candidate pipelines in the same way.

Before a demo, draw your actual workflow from a new client request through candidate sourcing, screening, submission, interview, placement and follow-up. Include how recruiters share a candidate across desks, how duplicate records are handled, and how client terms and job details are updated. Ask the vendor to demonstrate those specific transitions using sample data shaped like your own process.


## Compare published plan pricing with the real seat mix

Bullhorn’s pricing page listed Starter at $99 per user per month and Core at $165 per user per month for small agencies when checked. It describes Starter features such as applicant tracking, resume parsing, client management and job posting. Core adds items including marketplace access, custom fields and workflows, LinkedIn integrations and an email sidebar. The page directs buyers to request a quote and describes Pro and Max as talk-to-sales tiers. Treat the displayed prices as a starting point, not a full contract estimate.

Build a seat-by-seat estimate that separates recruiters, account managers, administrators and occasional users. Confirm any minimum seats, annual billing requirements, module charges, data migration, implementation, support, message or usage costs and renewal adjustments. Ask whether the published amount applies to your geography and firm type. Put every promised discount, included integration and service commitment into the final order form.


## Evaluate Amplify AI and automation with recruiter oversight

Bullhorn describes Amplify capabilities such as AI search and match, candidate summaries, job-post drafting, transcription, data enrichment and process automation. Its product page also publishes performance figures, including a statement that recruiters place 36% more and an agency outcome claim associated with using AI, automation and analytics. These are vendor claims and should not be treated as guaranteed results or as independent evidence about your organization.

Choose a small sample of real, appropriately permissioned records and test a few high-frequency tasks. Check whether search results surface qualified candidates, whether summaries preserve important context, whether generated outreach is accurate and respectful, and how much recruiter editing is needed. Measure time saved alongside quality indicators such as candidate response, submission acceptance and correction rates. Keep a person responsible for decisions that affect applicants.


## Check integrations, migration and candidate data handling

The product page describes integrations with email services and access to a marketplace, and Bullhorn publishes developer documentation. Confirm the exact versions and direction of data sync for your email, calendar, job boards, payroll, background-check, messaging and reporting systems. A marketplace listing is not proof that an integration supports your configuration or that it is included in the plan.

Ask for a migration inventory: record counts, attachments, notes, consent and retention fields, duplicate cleanup, historical activities and custom fields. Test exports before signing, and confirm which data can be exported in usable formats when a contract ends. Review access roles, audit logs, backup and recovery, data processing terms and AI use of candidate records with your legal and security teams.


## Who should evaluate Bullhorn ATS?

A staffing firm that wants candidate tracking, recruiting CRM and agency operations in one system may find Bullhorn’s specialized model worth a structured evaluation. A solo recruiter or a company hiring only for its own workforce may prefer a simpler ATS with a different pricing and workflow model. Compare the total seat cost, required add-ons, recruiter adoption and implementation plan rather than choosing from a feature list alone.

This buyer guide summarizes Bullhorn’s public product, pricing and developer pages. It does not claim Roseram tested Bullhorn or verified its outcomes. Confirm the current plan matrix, contractual terms, integrations, data export and AI controls directly with Bullhorn before committing.


## Features and decision criteria

- Applicant tracking and recruitment CRM workflows for candidates, jobs and client accounts
- Resume parsing, job posting, email sidebar and configurable fields or workflows across listed tiers
- Amplify AI features vary by plan and product, including search and matching, drafting, transcription and automation
- Marketplace and integrations intended to connect staffing systems and services

## Pricing considerations

Prices shown are the published per-user monthly amounts visible on Bullhorn’s pricing page when checked. Obtain a written quote confirming billing cadence, minimum seats, taxes, contract length, add-ons, implementation scope and renewal pricing; advanced tiers and other agency sizes may be priced differently.
