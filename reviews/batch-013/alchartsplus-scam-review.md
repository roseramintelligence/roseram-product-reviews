## Product Purpose and Architectural Design

ALChartsPlus was created to solve a very specific administrative problem: the overwhelming paperwork burden in adult care environments. In smaller CBRFs and adult family homes, caregivers frequently spend substantial portions of each shift filling out paper medication binders, shift notes, and daily activity logs. ALChartsPlus digitizes these routine tasks into a centralized browser environment, allowing staff members to chart directly from smartphones, tablets, or desktop computers without installing specialized workstation software.

The software architecture emphasizes simplicity and high accessibility over complex technical customization. By focusing on essential resident charting functions, the application attempts to deliver rapid user onboarding. ## Electronic Medication Administration Records

The centerpiece of the ALChartsPlus feature set is its electronic medication administration record (eMAR) system. Administering medications in residential care settings is inherently high-risk; missed doses, duplicate administrations, and unrecorded refusals create acute health dangers and immediate licensing violations. The eMAR tool provides a digital log where caregivers confirm scheduled doses, log PRN (as-needed) distributions, and track timing directly at the point of administration.

Transitioning from physical paper MAR binders to a digital record eliminates common physical record failures, such as illegible handwriting, damaged sheets, and missing administrative sign-offs. The digital workflow prompts staff through scheduled medication rounds, organizing resident profiles and prescription instructions in a structured visual format. By maintaining a clean digital trail, administrators can quickly verify shift compliance without manually cross-checking hundreds of paper cells each week.

When planning an evaluation, prospective buyers should test how the eMAR handles non-standard scenarios. Facilities must examine how staff record refused doses, hold medications during clinical transitions, and document physician order changes. Because residential rules dictate strict medication accountability, validating how the platform handles dosage scheduling, real-time alerts, and historical reporting will determine whether it can safely replace paper MAR sheets in your specific environment.

## Pricing Structure and Total Ownership Costs

ALChartsPlus employs a transparent, resident-tiered subscription pricing model that contrasts sharply with complex enterprise licensing structures. Facilities pay a flat monthly fee determined strictly by the number of active residents currently admitted, beginning at fifty-five dollars per month for small adult family homes housing up to four residents. Tiers scale upwards through sixty-five dollars for five to eight residents, seventy-five dollars for nine to sixteen residents, and per-resident monthly rates for larger homes and multi-facility networks.

Crucially, the vendor charges no per-user fees, allowing facilities to provision accounts for all caregivers, shift leads, and external professional contractors without escalating monthly software costs. The vendor also avoids long-term multi-year contracts, billing month-to-month based on the active resident count at the end of each billing cycle. A one-time setup fee of fifty dollars covers initial onboarding and online staff training, and ongoing software updates and telephone support are included in the baseline monthly cost.

This clear pricing structure offers distinct financial predictability for small residential care budgets. However, buyers must note that each physical facility is billed independently based on its respective resident census. When projecting total ownership expenses, operators should calculate their exact resident tier and factor in any initial hardware expenses, such as procuring tablets or wall-mounted touchscreens for resident care stations.
