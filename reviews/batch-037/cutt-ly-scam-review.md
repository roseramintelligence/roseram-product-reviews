# Cutt.ly review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** URL shortening and branded link management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Cutt.ly provides short links, branded domains, QR codes, link-in-bio pages, surveys and click analytics. Its free plan lists 30 short links monthly, one branded domain and 30 days of analytics; paid plans start at US$12 monthly for Starter and scale with link volume and team features.

## Editorial verdict

Cutt.ly can be useful when a team needs branded short links and click reporting, especially when campaigns use many destinations. The free plan is a standing tier rather than a trial, but it limits link creation and analytics history. Before using short links in customer or email campaigns, confirm domain ownership, redirect behavior, link editing, analytics retention and what happens if the subscription ends.

## Decide whether short links need a permanent owner

Cutt.ly shortens URLs and adds branded domains, click analytics, QR codes, link-in-bio pages and surveys. A short link can make a campaign easier to share and measure, but it becomes a dependency: a social post, printed flyer or email may continue using that destination long after publication.

Use a domain controlled by your organization for important links, and document who renews it. Keep a redirect inventory with campaign, destination and owner. A shortener can help manage links, but it should not become the only copy of a critical destination or policy page.


## Compare the standing free plan with paid link volume

Cutt.ly’s current pricing page lists Free at 30 links per month, three custom aliases per month, one branded domain and 30 days of analytics. Starter costs US$12 monthly or US$132 yearly and lists 300 links per month. Single, Team and Team Enterprise raise link limits and add longer history or team capabilities.

The free plan is a standing tier according to the company’s explanation, not a trial. Link creation limits reset monthly, while clicks are listed as unlimited. If your campaigns need more URLs or long-range analytics, price the plan based on actual links and retention rather than the word “unlimited.”


## Check branded domains, redirects and API limits

A custom domain helps keep a link associated with your brand, but setup depends on DNS and plan details. Verify the domain before printing or sending links. Test redirects on mobile and in common messaging apps, and make sure a destination update does not send people to an unexpected or unsafe page.

The API exposes programmatic link management, but the pricing page lists different monthly API volumes and rate limits. Match your request volume to the account tier, handle throttling, and never place API tokens in browser code. Check which analytics fields are returned and whether the plan supports exporting them.


## Interpret click analytics carefully

Click counts can include automated scanners, preview bots or repeated visits from the same person. Use Cutt.ly analytics as a directional campaign signal, not as a direct count of unique buyers. Compare clicks with downstream form submissions or purchases in the system where those events occur.

Check the plan’s history window and export capability before using data for quarterly reports. The Free plan lists 30 days of analytics; higher tiers extend the period. Keep a copy of campaign data you need to retain under your own reporting policy.


## Protect trust when sharing shortened links

A short URL hides the destination until it is opened, which can make recipients cautious. Use a recognizable branded domain, explain the destination in surrounding copy and avoid changing the redirect after distribution without a good reason. Never use a shortener to disguise a deceptive or unrelated destination.

Review link access and team roles, especially when contractors can edit redirects. Keep an audit record of material destination changes and remove access when a project ends. This review is based on Cutt.ly’s official feature and pricing pages; it does not report a link-security audit or campaign result.


## Features and decision criteria

- Short links on Cutt.ly or branded custom domains
- Click analytics, QR codes, surveys and link-in-bio pages
- API link-creation and analytics access varies by plan
- Team workspaces and longer analytics history on higher tiers

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page says link-clicks are unlimited. API request limits and branded-domain API allowances vary by plan. Confirm which custom domain uses SSL, analytics history and API volume before purchasing; the pricing table is the current reference for changes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Cutt.ly Free a trial?

Cutt.ly says Free is an ongoing plan with monthly link limits, not a trial that expires.

### How many links can Cutt.ly Free create?

Its current pricing page lists 30 short links per month and three custom back-halves per month.

### Are clicks limited?

The pricing comparison lists unlimited link clicks, while the number of links and analytics history depend on the plan.
