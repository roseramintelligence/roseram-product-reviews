# CoHost review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Podcast analytics and audience insights  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

CoHost Prefix adds podcast analytics and audience insights to an existing podcast host, so a publisher does not need to migrate hosting to use the analytics product. Plans include tracking links, listener demographics and B2B analytics; Pro is listed at US$89 per show monthly when billed annually and Premium at US$170, with feature tiers and custom Enterprise pricing.

## Editorial verdict

CoHost Prefix is aimed at brands, agencies and podcast networks that want campaign measurement or audience insights while keeping their existing hosting provider. Tracking links and download analytics can help compare promotion channels, while demographic and B2B reports are intended to show who may be listening. Buyers should validate the methodology, sample coverage and privacy disclosures behind inferred audience data, and compare annual per-show pricing with the value of the reporting they will actually use.

## Podcast analytics without changing hosts

CoHost Prefix is an analytics product for podcasters who want audience insights while keeping their current hosting platform. CoHost says Prefix can provide tracking links, advanced demographics and B2B analytics without requiring a migration. This is useful for brands and agencies that need reporting across shows but do not want to alter their publishing infrastructure.

The product’s dashboards include downloads, unique listeners, consumption and campaign links. A tracking link can associate marketing-channel clicks with podcast downloads, helping a team compare campaign sources. Attribution has limits: a download linked to a campaign is not automatically a sale or a confirmed individual listener. Use a separate conversion measure for revenue outcomes.


## Understand the plan tiers and per-show price

CoHost’s official pricing page lists Pro at US$89 per month per show when billed annually and Premium at US$170 per month per show. Pro includes B2B Analytics Level 1, audience demographics and tracking links; Premium adds Level 2 B2B analytics, custom exports and Salesforce integration. Enterprise pricing is custom and includes higher-tier B2B reporting and integrations.

The pricing page says annual billing saves 10% and monthly billing is also available. It also states that the price is the same whether a customer selects Podcast Hosting or Prefix, while some features are unavailable on the Prefix product. Confirm the exact per-show count, billing cadence and selected feature list in a quote.


## Evaluate how audience insights are produced

CoHost describes advanced demographics such as location, age, interests and listening behavior, and B2B analytics such as companies and industries associated with listeners. These are analytics outputs, not necessarily verified profiles for every listener. Ask the vendor how the data is collected or inferred, what proportion of the audience is represented, how estimates are refreshed and what confidence limitations apply.

For sensitive advertising claims, avoid presenting inferred company or demographic results as a complete census. Compare reports across a representative period, check how the platform handles low-volume data and understand whether the dashboard aggregates data from external sources. Review privacy terms before using audience insights in public-facing materials.


## Compare Prefix with full podcast hosting

CoHost offers both podcast hosting and the Analytics Prefix. The company says Prefix is designed for customers who do not want to move hosts, while hosting adds publishing operations such as one-click episode publishing, podcast websites and ad insertion. Some features listed on the pricing page, including AI transcription, Salesforce integration or consumption data, may not be available on the Prefix product even when the price is the same.

Before selecting a product, identify which system controls the RSS feed, episode files, analytics prefix and campaign links. Ask how existing links and historical data are handled, and whether setup affects directory distribution. A seven-day trial is listed for the Prefix and hosting products, so use it to verify reporting against a known episode and campaign.


## Who should evaluate CoHost?

CoHost Prefix may suit brands, networks or agencies that need podcast audience reporting but want to keep the current host. Smaller independent podcasters may find the per-show plan cost high unless they use the audience insight and campaign attribution features. A pilot should compare tracking-link results with the show’s existing analytics and determine whether the additional reports change a marketing decision.

This buyer guide is based on CoHost’s public pricing and analytics materials. Roseram did not validate the vendor’s audience-data methodology or independently measure campaign attribution. Confirm current features, trial terms, data handling and the final quote before signing up.


## Features and decision criteria

- Analytics Prefix can work with an existing podcast host
- Tracking links measure clicks and attributed downloads across marketing channels
- Audience demographics and B2B insights vary by plan
- Podcast hosting and Prefix are distinct products with some feature differences

## Pricing considerations

CoHost states hosting and Prefix use the same plan pricing, but some features differ by product. Annual billing saves 10%; the pricing page also says monthly billing is available. Confirm if prices are per show, the selected billing cadence and any service features that only work with hosting before purchase.
