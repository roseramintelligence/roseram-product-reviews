# Coursera review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Online courses and professional certificate platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Coursera hosts online courses, Specializations, projects and professional certificates from universities and companies. Learners can purchase individual programs or Coursera Plus; the current public page lists Plus at US$59 monthly or US$399 yearly, with a monthly trial offer and program-specific prices.

## Editorial verdict

Coursera can fit a learner who wants a structured course from a university or company and needs assignments or a certificate to document completion. The subscription makes sense only if the courses you need are included and you have time to complete them; some programs remain separately priced. Check whether a certificate is recognized by your employer, what happens to access after cancellation and whether existing subscriptions will overlap.

## Match the course to a concrete goal

Coursera offers courses and programs from universities and companies. A learner might use it to explore a topic, build a skill or complete an assignment-based certificate. Start by defining the outcome: a specific concept to learn, a portfolio project to finish or a credential an employer has agreed to recognize.

Read the course syllabus, expected weekly hours, prerequisites and certificate requirements before paying. A well-known institution name is not enough to show that a program fits your role. Check who issues the certificate, whether assignments are graded and how long you can access materials after the course ends.


## Compare subscription with individual purchases

Coursera’s public Plus page currently lists US$59 monthly and US$399 annually. Individual courses and Specializations have their own prices, shown by program. If you plan to complete one short course, a subscription may cost more than a single purchase; if several included courses are part of a realistic schedule, Plus may be a better fit.

The membership catalog is not every course on the platform. Confirm that the specific program shows included with Plus before subscribing. The page lists a 7-day trial on the monthly plan and a 14-day money-back guarantee for annual billing. Check the current terms and whether tax or regional billing changes the amount.


## Avoid overlapping subscriptions

Coursera notes that existing Specialization subscriptions do not automatically cancel when a learner starts Plus. A learner can therefore have two charges running at once if they forget to manage the prior subscription. Review the account’s active purchases before starting a trial and make a list of the dates each plan renews.

If you cancel, check whether course progress remains saved and what access ends. Keep certificates and project files in a place you control. A certificate page can disappear from a subscription view even when the learning record remains in the account, so confirm how to access it after a plan change.


## Evaluate certificates and outcomes carefully

Certificates can document course completion, but their value depends on the employer, profession or licensing body evaluating them. Ask whether the credential is accepted for the intended purpose before enrolling. For regulated work, check the official professional requirement instead of relying on a course sales page.

Use projects and assessments to demonstrate what you can do, not only what you watched. Set a completion date and reserve study time before starting a subscription. This review is based on Coursera’s published pricing and program information; it does not promise employment, a salary increase or an independent course quality rating.


## Features and decision criteria

- University and company courses, certificates, projects and Specializations
- Plus plan lists access to thousands of courses and certificates for included programs
- Some single courses and programs are priced separately
- Monthly trial or annual refund terms vary by billing option

## Pricing considerations

Prices and included catalog may change by region, promotion and course. Coursera’s FAQ says existing Specialization subscriptions do not automatically cancel when starting Plus, so review active subscriptions to avoid overlapping charges. Confirm included programs and renewal price at checkout.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Coursera Plus cost?

Coursera currently lists US$59 per month or US$399 per year on its public page; promotions and regional prices may differ.

### Does Coursera Plus include every course?

No. The Plus catalog covers many courses, but Coursera notes some course or program purchases may be separate.

### Will Plus automatically cancel my existing Specialization subscription?

Coursera says existing Specialization subscriptions do not automatically cancel when Plus begins; cancel them separately if no longer needed.
