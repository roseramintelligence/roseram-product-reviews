# Cold Turkey review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Website and application distraction blocker  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Cold Turkey is a desktop productivity suite with tools for blocking websites and applications, scheduling focus sessions and writing without distractions. Its Blocker Pro is a one-time purchase with lifetime updates and a 30-day refund guarantee; a free version and seven-day Pro trial are available. Supported operating systems are Windows and macOS.

## Editorial verdict

Cold Turkey is designed for people who want deliberate friction against distracting sites or applications rather than another time-tracking dashboard. Blocker supports scheduled and timed blocks, while Micromanager and Writer target different kinds of interruptions. The locked-block behavior can be useful for self-imposed commitments, but it is intentionally restrictive. Test the free version and understand emergency access, supported operating systems and refund terms before enabling long blocks.

## Make distractions harder to reach

Cold Turkey provides desktop tools for blocking distracting websites and applications during focus sessions. Blocker is the website and application blocking product, while Micromanager and Writer serve different use cases. The official comparison lists scheduling, timed blocks, breaks and lock features for Blocker, with distraction-free writing available in Writer.

Unlike a passive analytics product, a blocker changes what the user can access. This can support an intentional work session, but users should begin with a short test schedule and ensure required work sites, communication tools and accessibility resources remain available. The vendor’s guide explains how to configure blocks and supported browser extensions.


## Try the free version before locking a schedule

Blocker Free includes basic website blocking, exceptions, timed blocks and statistics. Cold Turkey says the free app includes a seven-day Pro trial that can be started from its in-app upgrade control. Use the trial to test schedules, application blocking and lock settings before relying on them during a long work period.

The locking features can make a block difficult to change once it starts, which is the point for some users. Before enabling a long or strict lock, verify that essential work, emergency information and necessary accounts are not on the block list. Keep the first schedule short enough to correct mistakes.


## Compare Blocker, Micromanager and Writer

Cold Turkey’s comparison page says Blocker provides website blocking and scheduling, while Micromanager focuses on application whitelisting and timed blocks. Writer is for distraction-free writing and can block the rest of the computer while it is in use. These tools solve different problems; a user seeking only site limits does not necessarily need the writing application.

The product comparison lists email support, free lifetime updates and a 30-day guarantee for the products. It also says a single purchase can be used on all computers personally used by the buyer. Check the current pricing and license terms for household, workplace or shared-device use.


## Check platforms and setup requirements

Cold Turkey’s pricing FAQ says its product key works for Windows and macOS, but not Linux, ChromeOS, Android or iOS. Blocker uses browser extensions and, on Windows, a background service for application blocking. The user guide includes setup and troubleshooting information, so confirm that the app and browser extension work on the devices you use before planning around them.

A blocker can be bypassed if browser access, app permissions or device access are not configured as expected. Test the actual browser profiles and applications you intend to restrict. If the computer is shared or managed by an employer, obtain the appropriate administrator approval before changing system-level settings.


## Who should evaluate Cold Turkey?

Cold Turkey may suit individuals who benefit from strong focus boundaries and prefer a one-time purchase over a recurring subscription. Its free version allows a practical trial before buying. It may be a poor fit for people who need a phone-first solution or frequently switch between blocked tasks and urgent communication.

This guide summarizes Cold Turkey’s public pricing, comparison and support materials; Roseram did not install or test the desktop app. Verify the current purchase amount, operating-system support and refund conditions before buying, and begin with a short block schedule.
