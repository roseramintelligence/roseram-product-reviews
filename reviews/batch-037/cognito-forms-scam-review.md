# Cognito Forms review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Online forms and workflow automation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Cognito Forms is an online form builder for collecting information, payments and signatures and automating workflows. Its public pricing lists a free Individual plan, Pro at US$24 monthly, Team at US$49 and Enterprise at US$174, with lower effective prices shown for annual billing and entry, user and storage limits that vary by plan.

## Editorial verdict

Cognito Forms suits organizations that need more than a basic survey: conditional forms, payments, save-and-resume, approvals and document workflows are part of its tiered feature set. Its free plan includes unlimited forms but limits monthly entries and storage. Estimate submission volume and user access before selecting a tier, and test payment, signature and workflow logic with non-production data before using forms for customer or patient records.

## Build forms that do more than collect answers

Cognito Forms is an online form builder for registrations, intake, orders and other data-collection workflows. The product supports conditional fields and calculations, and its pricing page describes payments across plans. Higher tiers add save-and-resume, electronic signatures, workflow and approvals, data lookups, client portals and API access.

This range can reduce the number of separate tools needed for a form-to-process workflow, but it also makes plan fit important. A simple contact form may not need a paid tier; a multi-step intake process with roles and approval routing may. Sketch the actual form and submission flow before comparing plans.


## Estimate monthly entries and storage

Cognito Forms lists the Individual plan as free with 100 entries per month and 100 MB of storage. Pro lists 2,000 monthly entries and 1 GB, Team lists 10,000 entries and 10 GB, while Enterprise lists unlimited entries and 100 GB. The pricing page shows monthly and discounted annual amounts, so use the billing selection that matches your expected commitment.

Estimate not only ordinary submissions but test entries, duplicate submissions, file uploads and seasonal peaks. Check what happens when the entry quota is reached and whether stored records count toward the plan limit. If the form is business-critical, include response export, retention and backup procedures in the rollout plan.


## Compare user roles and workflow features

The public page lists one user on Individual, two on Pro, five on Team and twenty on Enterprise, alongside a guest allowance on some tiers. Pro adds electronic signatures and save-and-resume. Team adds workflow approvals and data lookups, while Enterprise lists enhanced security features such as HIPAA compliance and single sign-on. Verify the exact user model for staff who build, administer or review submissions.

Build a test form with branching, calculations, payment, signatures and approvals as needed. Validate each route with sample data, including incomplete submissions and changes after an approval. Keep a simple version of the form available while testing so users are not routed into an unfinished workflow.


## Review payment and sensitive-data requirements

The pricing page describes payment fields, while the form builder can collect information that may be sensitive. Confirm supported payment providers, transaction fees and refund responsibilities before accepting customer payments. For health, financial or other regulated information, read the security and contractual materials for the exact plan and verify that required controls are included.

Do not assume that a plan label alone satisfies a compliance obligation. Confirm data residency, access logs, retention, deletion, encryption and any business-associate agreement requirements with the vendor and your organization. Use non-production submissions during evaluation and remove unnecessary personal data from test records.


## Who should evaluate Cognito Forms?

Cognito Forms may suit small businesses, schools and teams that need online forms connected to payments or approvals. Its free plan can test basic collection, while the paid tiers are organized around entry capacity, users and workflow needs. A team with many file uploads or a large monthly volume should test quotas and export behavior before migrating existing forms.

This buyer guide summarizes Cognito Forms’ public pricing and product pages; Roseram did not audit the service or independently test a payment workflow. Verify current rates, limits, processing fees and security terms directly before launch.


## Features and decision criteria

- Unlimited forms across plans with monthly entry and storage quotas
- Payment fields, conditional logic and calculations
- Save-and-resume, e-signatures and workflow approvals vary by plan
- Higher plans add more users, audit history, API allowance and security options

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page can display monthly and annual rates together. New paid-plan trials are listed as 14 days without a credit card. Confirm entry definitions, included payment processing, user counts, storage and HIPAA terms in the live plan comparison.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Cognito Forms have a free plan?

Yes. The Individual plan is listed as free, with one user, unlimited forms and 100 entries per month.

### Which plan includes workflow approvals?

The pricing page lists workflow and approvals in the Team plan and higher.

### How long is the Cognito Forms free trial?

The pricing page says a paid-plan trial lasts 14 days with no charge during that period; verify current terms at signup.
