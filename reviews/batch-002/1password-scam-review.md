# 1Password review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-16  
**Category:** Password management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

1Password offers password management for individuals, families, and organizations. This review focuses on the business pricing page and the buying questions it raises for a team.

## Editorial verdict

The linked business page provides concrete plan information. That supports a pricing comparison, not a claim that Roseram audited the product or proved its security. Compare team size, access needs, and renewal terms before choosing a plan.

## What matters for a small team

Start with the number of people who need accounts. A low per-user rate can still have a minimum team size. Compare the actual annual bill for your team rather than the smallest number in a pricing card. Then list the people who administer access, the information each role needs, and what happens when a person changes jobs.

A password manager is a specialist product. An AI assistant or coding workspace should not be described as a replacement merely because it can help write instructions. Keep credential storage, access management, and project creation as separate decisions. Choose the product that is designed for each job and test how the pieces fit together.


## Features and decision criteria

- Team password management
- Role-based sharing and access
- Business administration options
- Developer workflow support

## Pricing considerations

These are the USD annual-billing prices visible on the checked page, not month-to-month quotes. The page also describes a 14-day trial. Confirm current taxes, seat requirements, offers, and renewal terms before checkout.
