# 20i Web Hosting review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-17  
**Category:** Web hosting and cloud infrastructure  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

20i sells Linux shared hosting with an autoscaling platform, CDN, SSL, malware scanning, migrations and daily backups. Its U.S. hosting page displayed Startup at $1 for the first month then $15/month, with one website, 10 GB storage and 50 GB bandwidth; higher tiers carry larger explicit limits and prices. The marketing pages describe autoscaling and cancel-any-time plans, while the April 2026 terms specify a 10% server-processing cap on non-VPS packages, a 30-day hosting money-back guarantee with exclusions, a support-ticket cancellation process requiring confirmation, and immediate permanent service-data deletion after cancellation. These conditions make package sizing, independent backups and a data-export plan essential.

## Editorial verdict

The official material reviewed does not substantiate calling 20i a scam. It identifies 20i Limited, a current range of hosting plans, support and migration documentation, and written hosting terms. The practical risks are commercial and operational: the introductory price rises to a substantially higher monthly rate; shared plans list capped websites, storage, mailboxes, databases and bandwidth; the terms impose a CPU-use limit for non-VPS packages; a cancellation request must be reconfirmed; and the terms say hosted service data is permanently deleted immediately on cancellation. The 30-day refund language applies to hosting and excludes domains, VPS and add-ons. No account, migration, restore, load test, cancellation or support interaction was performed for this desk review.

## 20i Web Hosting review: understand the plan, not just the platform pitch

20i offers several services under the same name: shared Linux web hosting, WordPress hosting, reseller hosting, virtual private servers and managed cloud servers. This review focuses on the U.S. shared web-hosting product because that is what the public page and listing identify. The product page describes an autoscaling cloud platform, CDN, migration tools and support; the packages themselves still specify the number of websites, storage, bandwidth, mailboxes and database capacity. Do not assume the broad “unlimited” or autoscaling platform copy removes the limits shown for a particular package.

20i’s U.S. terms identify the operator as 20i Limited, registered in England and Wales, and say its contracts are governed by English law. This is useful buyer information, although U.S. customers should verify which terms and statutory rights apply to their order and location. The service is not anonymous from the public contract: the terms provide a legal company name, registration number and registered office.


## Plans, introductory offers and what renews

On November 17, 2026, the U.S. product page showed Startup at $1 for the first month and $15 per month thereafter. That tier includes one website, 10 GB storage, 50 GB bandwidth, ten 10 GB mailboxes and 1 GB MySQL storage. Plan 1 was $1 for the first month and then $30 monthly, with 100 GB bandwidth and 5 GB MySQL storage. Plan 2 was $1 for the first month then $60 monthly, with three sites, 20 GB storage and 200 GB bandwidth. Plans 3, 4 and 5 were shown at $100, $200 and $400 per month after the $1 first month, with higher website, storage and bandwidth allowances.

The introductory price is easy to notice; the renewal price is the figure to use in a budget. The page presents a monthly rate and “no contract – cancel any time,” but it remains important to confirm the exact product and amount in the final order summary. Model expected traffic and file use against the listed allowance, and include domain registration, SSL add-ons, backup products or VPS costs only where the order actually includes them. The page’s general scalability statements should not be read as unlimited bandwidth for every shared package.


## Autoscaling, backups and migration: useful claims with limits to verify

20i describes automatic resource scaling, global load balancing, a CDN, free SSL, a web application firewall, malware scanning and daily backups. Its hosting FAQ says paid web-hosting packages include automated daily backups and that restore points cover the prior seven days. The separate backup product page describes on-demand snapshots and additional backup features; 20i support documentation distinguishes its separate Timeline Backups subscription, whose article describes 30 days of web and database snapshots. Confirm which retention period is included in the specific package and whether an enhanced backup product adds a recurring charge.

The migration center is advertised as free for website files, databases and email, and 20i says staff can assist with transfers. Its migration page notes that domain registration does not move automatically with a content migration. For a live business site, inventory DNS records, email, scheduled tasks, database jobs, SSL, payment callbacks and third-party integrations before switching nameservers. Keep a backup at the old provider until the new host has served real traffic and mail has been tested. These are recommended acceptance checks; no migration was performed for this review.


## Resource rules and workload fit

The U.S. plan tables make the most important shared-hosting allowances visible: website counts, storage, bandwidth, mailbox totals and MySQL storage. The U.S. Terms add a processing limit for non-VPS hosting packages: a customer may use up to 10% of a server’s processing capacity, with 20i reserving discretion to allow more and to discuss requirements where one account affects others. The terms also say mailboxes not accessed for 100 clear days are automatically deleted. Those written rules are worth reading before choosing a low-cost shared package for a CPU-heavy application or long-lived mailbox archive.

Autoscaling is a provider-described capability, not a substitute for asking how the selected package behaves when it reaches its bandwidth, storage or CPU thresholds. A personal WordPress site, a set of client sites, a high-traffic store and a custom application can have very different failure modes. Request the exact resource and overage policy for your plan, and decide what happens if a traffic spike exceeds the stated quota. If the workload needs dedicated resources or custom server control, compare 20i’s separate managed cloud or VPS offerings rather than assuming shared hosting is interchangeable.


## Cancellation, refunds and data deletion deserve special attention

The Terms describe a 30-day money-back guarantee for a hosting service, limited to one per customer. They list exclusions including domain registrations and renewals, private SSL certificates, add-ons, VPS products and associated services. The cancellation instructions require a support ticket before the 30-day deadline, then a reply to reconfirm the request; otherwise, the provider says the service continues and cancellation is ineffective. This is more procedural than the product page’s short “cancel any time” phrase suggests, so save the ticket and confirmation.

The same Terms state that once a service is cancelled, data held or hosted for that service is immediately and permanently deleted and tell customers to make appropriate copies beforehand. This makes an export and restore test part of the cancellation plan, not an optional cleanup step. Keep local or independent backups of files, databases, mail and DNS settings before submitting the cancellation. The public daily backup feature should not be assumed to preserve material after termination unless the provider confirms that in writing.


## Support, account administration and contract details

The product page advertises 24/7 support through expert staff and online chat or tickets. The Terms state that support will help resolve service problems but does not include programming support, and they say technical support is not provided by telephone. A developer relying on application-code debugging should distinguish hosting troubleshooting from implementation work. Ask whether an issue involving a custom plugin, a slow query or a third-party integration is covered before building the operating model around the headline support claim.

The Terms also say a customer’s material remains theirs while granting 20i a licence to store and publish it as needed to provide hosting. They place responsibility for the legality of hosted material on the customer and set a contractual liability limit subject to the exclusions and applicable law. This is ordinary contract language to evaluate for the business risk at hand, not a finding that the service is unsafe. Review the current data-processing agreement and any add-on terms, retain the order confirmation, and ensure that the billing owner and technical administrator can both access the account.


## Alternatives and a practical hosting comparison

Compare 20i with other shared hosts, managed WordPress providers and a virtual private server using the same traffic and support scenario. Include the renewal price after an introductory offer, included bandwidth, site and database limits, mailboxes, backup retention, migration help, CDN and security features, support hours, developer assistance, cancellation steps and what happens to data after closure. A lower first-month cost does not reveal the total annual cost or the effort needed to operate the site.

A useful pilot is to migrate a non-critical site, capture a baseline response time, run the site through normal administrative tasks, test one restore, verify mail delivery and confirm the site’s quota metrics. Ask support one ordinary configuration question and one failure-recovery question to see how the published support boundary works. Keep the current host active and maintain an independent backup until the pilot passes. These tests are suggested evaluation steps, not outcomes we claim to have measured.


## Is 20i Web Hosting a scam? Evidence-based conclusion

The reviewed official pages identify 20i Limited, publish detailed U.S. hosting plans, describe migrations and backups, and provide written contract terms. The evidence does not substantiate calling 20i Web Hosting a scam. The real buyer checks are whether the introductory offer is followed by a sustainable recurring price, whether a shared plan’s concrete resource limits fit the workload, how the 30-day refund and cancellation confirmation work, and whether all important data has been copied before cancellation. The service’s platform performance, uptime, support responsiveness and backup restores were not independently tested.

This is a source-based desk review, not a hosting account, benchmark, security audit, live migration or legal opinion. Prices were captured on November 17, 2026; the terms page was last updated April 3, 2026. Before ordering, compare your checkout summary with the published plan, ask about thresholds for CPU and bandwidth, confirm standard versus add-on backup retention, and keep an independent copy of all hosted data. Before cancelling, export and verify each site, database and mailbox because the terms state service data is permanently deleted immediately after cancellation.

- Linux shared hosting packages with explicit site, storage, bandwidth, mailbox and database quotas

- Free migration center for website files, databases and email, with manual handoff available through support
- My20i hosting control panel and Twentie AI assistant listed in current U.S. plan features
- Separate VPS, managed-cloud, reseller and WordPress products have different resource models and terms

Prices and allowances were captured from 20i’s U.S. web-hosting page on November 17, 2026. The $1 introductory month is followed by the stated ongoing monthly price. Confirm the exact plan, renewal amount, term, taxes and service-specific conditions at checkout.
