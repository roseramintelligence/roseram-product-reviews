## 1stDibs scam review: a marketplace, not the item seller

1stDibs is an online marketplace for furniture, art, jewelry, fashion and other design goods offered by independent sellers and dealers. The product pages, buyer policies and user agreement describe a marketplace model: sellers list items, set prices and return terms, and buyers can use platform messaging and secure checkout. A buyer’s question about a “1stDibs scam” should distinguish the platform’s role from the specific seller, item description, shipping arrangement and payment path involved in a transaction.

The official help center says prospective sellers must apply and be selected, and that seller commission varies by item type, price, membership plan and seller business type. A curated marketplace and seller application process may reduce some risks, but neither makes every listing identical or guarantees that a buyer will like an item. The company publishes separate Authenticity Guarantee, Money-Back Guarantee and Return Policy pages, each with its own limits.

The primary sources reviewed here do not establish that 1stDibs is a scam. They do show that buyer protections require attention to details that are easy to miss on a high-value purchase: listed condition and dimensions, seller-specific returns, secure checkout, shipping arrangements, report deadlines and the narrow scope of authenticity coverage. ## 1stDibs prices, seller listings and total purchase cost

There is no single product price for a marketplace. Each listing has its own asking price, seller, shipping method and return terms. The buyer’s total may also include shipping, insurance, sales tax, VAT, import duties or other charges depending on the item and destination. The User Agreement says buyers are responsible for applicable taxes and duties. Request a written total before ordering an expensive piece, especially if it crosses a national border.

Seller-side costs are also not represented by one fixed public commission. 1stDibs says its rate varies with item type, sale price, membership plan and business type, and that sellers must apply and be approved. A dealer deciding whether to list should compare the full subscription and commission terms with expected sales, shipping support, payment processing and any costs of preparing professional photography or descriptions. Treat marketing claims about audience reach as vendor statements, not a guarantee of sales.

For a buyer, compare the listed item to alternatives of similar age, provenance, dimensions and condition rather than comparing only the headline price. Ask whether restoration or replacement parts are included, who pays for return shipping and how an international return is handled. Keep the listing page and seller correspondence with the order record. The item price alone does not capture risk if the piece is fragile, custom-made, difficult to insure or expensive to return.


## What the 1stDibs Authenticity Guarantee actually covers

The Authenticity Guarantee page says claims must be made within one year and that eligible reimbursement can reach $100,000 for the purchase price and related shipping. It specifies covered attributes such as the listed creator or brand, described materials, stated time period and certain construction techniques. That is more useful than a generic badge because it identifies the representations the policy addresses.

The policy also excludes some descriptors and attributes. It says the guarantee does not apply to items described as “attributed to,” “circle of,” “in the style of” or “after” a specific creator in the item details. It excludes listed attributes such as place of origin, condition, movement, style, size and dimensions from this authenticity program, though other issues may be reportable under the Money-Back Guarantee. Read the exact listing language before treating a work as authenticated or ascribing it to a named maker.

To seek authenticity reimbursement, the policy says the order must use secure checkout, the buyer must contact 1stDibs within one year and provide written opinions from two independent recognized experts within one year of delivery, subject to limited discretion to waive the second opinion. The buyer must supply additional requested material and return the item in the same condition. 1stDibs reviews the claim with the seller and has the final decision under the policy. A buyer should factor appraisal cost, expert availability and the condition requirement into any high-value purchase.


## Money-Back Guarantee: report deadlines and eligible problems

The Money-Back Guarantee covers certain cases where an item is significantly different from its description, damaged in transit or does not arrive, up to the policy’s stated limit and subject to its terms. The policy requires an online secure-checkout order and says the buyer should report the issue within seven calendar days of delivery, or within seven days of the promised delivery date for an item that has not arrived. It also asks the buyer to provide requested evidence within seven days.

The policy excludes dissatisfaction with an accurately described, undamaged item from this guarantee. It also excludes items where the buyer arranged shipping or picked the item up in person, and purchases completed outside secure checkout. For damage claims, the buyer should retain packaging, photograph the issue from multiple angles and report it promptly. The policy describes an evidence review that may take up to ten business days and says the platform may propose repair, restoration or replacement as an alternative resolution.

The policy states that determinations are made by 1stDibs and are final under its terms, and that an eligible refund may be withheld until return conditions are met. This does not mean every dispute will have the same outcome. Before buying, save the listing and policy; after delivery, inspect the item immediately and preserve shipment documentation. If a seller asks to take payment off platform, the support center says only orders made through secure checkout are eligible for the Money-Back Guarantee.


## Return rules vary by seller and item

The Return Policy says sellers set their own return policies and that buyers can find them in the listing’s Shipping & Returns section. The post-purchase order page preserves the seller’s terms. The policy also says EU and UK buyers have a 14-day return period where eligibility requirements are met. A buyer should not assume that every item is freely returnable because the marketplace has a general returns page.

For international returns and selected domestic returns of furniture and fine art, 1stDibs says it must oversee the shipment. It may arrange a quote for fully insured return shipping, and costs can be deducted from the refund. The policy notes that additional duties or taxes may apply. Trade 1st members may have extended timeframes, but eligibility and item categories are specified separately. A large piece’s crating and reverse freight can be a substantial part of the economic decision.

Before ordering, ask the seller questions inside the platform about condition, restoration, measurements, provenance, packaging, estimated delivery date and return cost. Request close-up photographs of areas that matter to you and confirm whether a listed dimension includes fittings or handles. Keep all written replies. An accurate, detailed exchange helps the buyer evaluate the item and gives a clearer record if the delivered object materially differs from the listing.


## Shipping, cancellation and the first inspection after delivery

The shipping guide says large, fragile and one-of-a-kind pieces may require extra coordination for pickup, packing and delivery, and that sellers use different services depending on item size and type. It describes scheduled deliveries and signed receipt for some methods. International shipments may require personal data for customs. Ask who will crate the item, whether it is insured for its full value and who must be present to inspect and sign.

1stDibs support documentation says buyers should report damage or another item issue within seven days, retain packaging and provide images. For a delivery that needs a signature, it advises inspecting before signing and noting visible damage in the carrier paperwork. This makes it important to arrange enough time at delivery rather than having a third party accept the item without instructions. Photograph the unopened crate, packaging and item before moving or repairing anything.

A separate cancellation policy applies to an order before shipping, and the user agreement contains different provisions for consumer buyers in the EU. Because the exact item, seller and shipping status matter, check the live order page and current policy rather than assuming that a general cancellation window applies. For custom or made-to-order items, ask the seller in writing when fabrication begins and what cancellation costs apply before confirming the purchase.


## Safe communication and marketplace seller checks

1stDibs encourages buyers to use its Ask the Seller messaging tool and keep all transactions on the platform. Its help center says off-platform orders are not covered by the Money-Back Guarantee and cautions buyers against exchanging contact details to complete a separate payment. That advice is especially relevant for expensive or one-of-a-kind goods. A seller’s request to pay by an unrelated wire transfer or payment app should be treated as a reason to pause and contact support.

Before purchase, review the seller’s profile, location, return policy and other listings. Ask for documentation that is relevant to the item: condition reports, restoration details, provenance, certificates or receipts where applicable. A seller response is part of the due diligence record but is not an independent appraisal. For a collectible or branded luxury item, consult a qualified specialist before paying if authenticity or resale value is material to the decision.

The platform says it screens or approves prospective sellers, but the buyer should still assess each listing. Look for mismatched descriptions, vague age claims, inconsistent dimensions, missing condition photographs or pressure to bypass secure checkout. Do not treat seller ratings, polished photography or a high price as proof of authenticity. Keep the platform’s message trail and order confirmation in case you need support.


## Privacy and public content on 1stDibs

The 1stDibs privacy policy covers information associated with account use, purchases, communications and site activity. The User Agreement states that content a user posts can become public, and that 1stDibs may use, reproduce, modify and display submitted user content under a broad license. A buyer or seller should think carefully before posting private addresses, confidential correspondence, personally identifying details or images that they do not have rights to share.

Payment processing can involve separate service providers. The privacy policy identifies PayPal and Hyperwallet as independent controllers for their own processing in applicable payment contexts and directs users to those providers’ terms. A seller should review identity-verification and payout requirements; a buyer should use the platform’s checkout and avoid sending payment or identity documents to an unverified party. The current privacy notice is the best place to check data rights and contact options before creating an account.

Marketplace transactions involve seller, carrier, payment and platform data flows. For a high-value item, share only information needed to arrange delivery, use a secure address and confirm who will receive the shipment. If the purchase uses white-glove delivery, verify whether the carrier needs a phone number, access details or a customs identifier. Do not include sensitive data in public reviews or open profile fields.


## Alternatives and an informed high-value purchase

Alternatives include purchasing directly from an established gallery, auction house, specialist dealer, local antiques shop or a different marketplace. These channels vary in expertise, inspection access, buyer premiums, shipping, return rights and dispute procedures. A direct gallery may provide a conversation with a specialist; a marketplace may make it easier to compare many sellers. Neither arrangement removes the need to verify provenance, dimensions, condition and full cost.

For a major purchase, compare total delivered cost and return exposure. Ask for a current condition report, provenance documentation and an insured shipping quote. If authenticity is central, confirm which listing claims fall within the platform’s written guarantee and whether independent experts in the relevant field could evaluate the piece if a dispute arose. Check the seller’s return window and the seven-day issue-reporting deadline before committing.

Use the secure checkout, archive the listing and messages, and inspect the shipment promptly. Keep the crate and packing materials until the item is accepted. A dealer or independent appraiser can help judge an item’s value; the marketplace’s guarantee is a contractual process, not an appraisal or investment promise. Compare the item to alternatives and proceed only when the evidence, condition and costs match what you intend to buy.


## 1stDibs scam review: conclusion and buyer checklist

The reviewed primary sources identify 1stDibs as a marketplace with seller applications, item listings, secure checkout, seller-defined return policies and written authenticity and money-back programs. The evidence checked here does not establish that 1stDibs is a scam. The key risk for a buyer is misunderstanding the scope: ordinary returns depend on each seller, the money-back guarantee has strict eligibility and a seven-day reporting window, and the authenticity guarantee excludes several types of qualified descriptions and requires expert evidence.

This is a source-based desk review, not an order, appraisal or legal analysis. Marketplace prices and seller fees depend on individual listings and seller terms; the seller commission support page says fees vary by plan and business type rather than providing a universal rate. Policies can change, so review the live listing, order terms and guarantee text when purchasing.

1stDibs may suit buyers seeking distinctive furniture, art, jewelry or collectibles from specialist sellers who are prepared to verify details and manage delivery. It may be a poor fit for someone who expects every listing to have identical returns, who plans to pay off-platform or who cannot inspect a fragile item promptly. Ask questions before purchase, keep the transaction on 1stDibs, save the written policies and report problems within the required timeframe.
