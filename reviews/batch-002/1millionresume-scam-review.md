# 1MillionResume review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-17  
**Category:** AI resume builder and career documents  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

1MillionResume combines a resume builder, templates, AI-assisted optimization, job-description matching and separately priced human writing services. The free plan and fixed-duration paid plans are documented, but applicants should verify the market-specific checkout price, refund eligibility and handling of uploaded resumes and job descriptions.

## Editorial verdict

The current terms describe one-time plan purchases with no automatic renewal, automated scores as estimates rather than employer ATS results, and no promise of interviews or employment. Its current privacy and refund pages give useful specifics while leaving applicants responsible for reviewing generated claims and protecting sensitive career data.

## 1MillionResume review: builder, AI tools and human writing are different offers

1MillionResume presents several related services: a self-service resume builder, templates and downloadable files, AI features such as optimization and job matching, free checking tools, and separately priced professional writing or review work. Those should be evaluated as distinct purchases. A resume-builder plan gives access to software for a stated period; a human writing package has its own scope, delivery timing, revision rules and refund stages. A buyer should not assume that purchasing the software includes a professional writer or a job-search outcome.

The site's terms say the free resume checker and free plan have limits that may change and that scores and reports are automated estimates. The terms also state directly that the company does not guarantee interviews, job offers or any employment outcome. Those are appropriate limitations for a career tool. A persuasive **1MillionResume review** should therefore ask whether the document is accurate, readable and useful for the target role, rather than treat an ATS score as a predictor of an employer's decision.


## 1MillionResume pricing and plan limits

The public plan comparison describes a free tier with a limited number of monthly PDF downloads and a small template set, a Basic tier with more templates and customization but no AI, and a Pro tier with AI optimization, job matching, resume checks and a cover-letter builder. The pricing presentation is not identical across the site's pages: one currently shows Basic at $4 for one month and Pro at $30 for three months, while another describes $4 per month for Basic and $11 per month for Pro with term options. The FAQ says plans vary by market and currency.

That presentation should prompt a normal checkout check, not a fraud conclusion. Before paying, screenshot the specific market and selected duration, note the currency and taxes, count the exports and resume checks included, and write down the expiration date. The terms describe builder plans as fixed-period, one-time purchases with no automatic renewal; after expiry, the account returns to the free tier. If a payment page or receipt says something different, contact support before proceeding and preserve the response.


## AI resume optimization and applicant tracking system claims

The paid features include AI-based optimization and job-description matching. The site describes resume checker results as estimates and its terms say the output may be inaccurate or unsuitable. That caution matters because ATS products differ by employer, configured fields, document parser and recruiter workflow. A score from one checker cannot establish that a document will parse correctly in every system, reach a human recruiter or rank above other applicants.

Use matching as an editing aid. Verify every employer, title, date, qualification and result against the original facts. Do not add keywords for skills the applicant does not have or invent metrics to satisfy a score. Compare the final PDF's text layer with the visual page, then copy it into a plain-text editor to catch reading-order problems. A human reviewer can confirm that the resume remains truthful and legible. The product can help organize a job-specific draft, but applicant judgment remains essential.


## Resume uploads, job descriptions and privacy

The current privacy policy explicitly includes uploaded resumes, job descriptions and files created in free tools without an account. It says AI-enabled features process content through automated systems and third-party AI providers acting on the company's behalf, with only the information needed for the feature shared. The policy also names payment, hosting, email, analytics, security and support providers as categories of processors. Job histories can expose employers, education, contact information, dates, skills and other personal details, so this is sensitive career data rather than an ordinary blank form.

The same policy says account data is kept while the account is active and as needed for service; after account deletion, personal data is deleted within 90 days except for specified legal, tax, fraud or dispute records. Files submitted to free tools without an account are kept for one year. Those timelines deserve attention if a user uploads a resume for a one-time check and then expects it to disappear immediately. Remove references and personal contact data that the tool does not need, review the privacy policy and ask how each AI provider handles temporary inference data before uploading confidential or third-party information.


## Refund conditions and one-time purchase terms

The refund policy, updated October 2, 2026, says builder-plan requests are assessed individually. It normally approves a request made within 72 hours when the applicant has not downloaded more than the free-plan allowance and has not heavily used paid AI features. The company first tries to resolve the product problem; the policy states that the 72-hour eligibility period is measured from purchase to request, so time spent troubleshooting does not consume that window. Approved amounts are generally returned less payment-gateway and related transaction charges, except for specified failed, duplicate or incorrect payments and cases where the company cancels or is late.

The stated process includes acknowledgement within two business days, a resolution attempt that can take up to five business days and a decision sent within three business days of request or the end of the resolution period. Refunds go to the original payment method, with the policy estimating five to ten business days for funds to appear after initiation. A buyer who needs a refund should write promptly from the registered email, describe the issue and retain the transaction ID. For human writing services, eligibility changes with the production stage, so read that separate table before placing an order.


## Human writing packages and delivery expectations

The expert-services page lists packages for resume writing and combinations with LinkedIn optimization or a cover letter. It describes an intake process, a delivery interval after the provider receives inputs, revisions and editing access. The terms say scope, price, revisions and delivery are agreed through email, chat or call, and that the clock starts after payment and receipt of the required information. This is a more involved service than an automated builder and should be assessed against the written quote rather than the software plan grid.

Before commissioning work, agree on the target role, seniority, geography, source material, number of documents, number of revisions, format and completion date. Give the writer verified accomplishments and explain which statements they may not embellish. The refund policy ties the amount available to how far drafting has progressed; after a first draft is shared, a refund is normally not available because revisions are included until satisfaction. Save the agreed scope and make sure the draft's claims can be substantiated in an interview.


## Account eligibility, commercial use and AI accuracy

The terms set an 18-and-over eligibility rule, say the services are for individuals preparing their own job applications and restrict commercial preparation of documents for other people unless the company authorizes it. They also prohibit account sharing and creating accounts to bypass limits. Resume agencies, career coaches and educational programs should not infer that an individual builder plan grants them commercial rights; the terms direct commercial users to contact the company.

The terms further say users own their resume content and grant permission to store and process it to provide the service. They warn that AI can make mistakes and require the user to review output, while the disclaimer rejects promises of uninterrupted operation or accurate results. These are operationally important boundaries. Keep a source copy of the resume, compare each suggestion with facts, and do not send generated statements to an employer until the applicant has checked them. For an organization, obtain written terms covering the handling of multiple applicants' files.


## Cancellation, deletion and customer recourse

Because the builder tiers are described as non-renewing, the primary billing risk is less about a hidden recurring debit and more about selecting the wrong term, missing a short refund window or assuming features persist after expiry. The privacy policy offers account-deletion requests through the registered email and sets a general 90-day deletion period after account deletion, subject to retained records. Free-tool uploads without an account have a separate one-year retention statement. Those are two distinct data paths and should not be conflated.

If a document is particularly sensitive, ask support to confirm which deletion pathway applies before upload. After a paid plan ends, export the final files while access remains and preserve the original editable copy. For a disputed charge or refund, the terms ask customers to contact the company first and identify Indian law and courts for disputes. A customer in another jurisdiction should review applicable statutory rights independently; the contract itself says it does not remove rights that cannot legally be waived.


## 1MillionResume verdict: practical editing aid, not an employment guarantee

The free tier, fixed-duration plans, clear feature lists and separate professional-writing packages give job seekers multiple ways to try the service. The current terms make two important points explicit: its automated scores are estimates and it does not guarantee employment outcomes. The privacy page is unusually specific about resume and job-description uploads, third-party AI processing, one-year retention of unauthenticated free-tool files and account deletion timing. Those details allow a buyer to make a more informed decision than the landing page alone.

The unresolved purchasing work is straightforward: pricing varies by page and market, refund eligibility depends on time and usage, and an uploaded resume contains sensitive information. This source-based desk review is not a paid-account test or an evaluation by a recruiter. The public evidence does not establish that 1MillionResume is a scam. Use the free plan first, verify the exact checkout terms, minimize unnecessary personal data, confirm the AI provider handling and review every generated claim before submitting it to an employer.
