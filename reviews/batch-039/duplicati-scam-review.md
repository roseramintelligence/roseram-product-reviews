# Duplicati review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Backup software and management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Duplicati’s current site presents encrypted backup workflows built around an open-source engine and storage chosen by the user. It also offers a management console and separate pricing paths. The backup engine, management service and destination storage should be evaluated as distinct parts of the setup.

## Editorial verdict

Duplicati is a relevant candidate when a user or team wants control over backup destinations and can maintain the recovery process. Judge it through a restore of expendable data, not only a successful job status. A backup is useful when the intended files can actually be recovered under the conditions that matter.

## Start the trial at the recovery end

Choose the files a person would need after losing the original computer. Back up an expendable sample and restore it into a separate location. Open the recovered files and check that the result represents the version you intended to preserve.

This exercise turns a broad promise of protection into an observable outcome. A dashboard showing a completed job remains useful information, but it is not the same evidence as a successful recovery. Roseram did not execute such a restore for this review.


## Bring-your-own storage brings operating decisions

A flexible destination model lets a team compare storage options, but someone still owns the account, access and budget. Identify who maintains those arrangements and what happens if a credential changes or a destination becomes unavailable.

Estimate storage growth and the cost of an unusually large recovery under the selected provider’s terms. Keep that calculation separate from the backup-management plan. The existence of several supported destinations does not make their operational requirements identical.


## Encryption and retention need a written recovery plan

An encrypted backup is not useful if its required recovery material is lost with the original device. Understand what the actual setup needs and preserve that material through an appropriate independent process. Test with disposable data rather than improvising during an incident.

Decide which past versions the workflow needs to keep and how a person identifies the right one. An accidental deletion discovered later is a different event from a machine failing today. Retention should reflect those scenarios instead of being selected without a purpose.


## Management convenience is a separate product question

Duplicati’s current website includes management offerings alongside the underlying engine. List what the operator needs to observe and control, then compare the relevant plan with the simpler arrangement. A feature-rich console is useful when it makes a real responsibility easier to carry out.

Roseram did not audit encryption, verify paid entitlements or benchmark backup performance. This review attributes capabilities to public materials and evaluates the practical distinction between a configured job and a recoverable backup.


## Features and decision criteria

- Encrypted backup workflows described by the provider
- Open-source backup engine
- User-selected storage destinations
- Management console and separate commercial offerings

## Pricing considerations

The current site separates backup software, management offerings and storage chosen by the user. Review the actual pricing and destination fees; no single all-inclusive backup price is established here.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Duplicati automatically include paid cloud capacity?

Its current site emphasizes bring-your-own storage. Verify the destination account and its charges separately.

### Does a completed backup job prove recovery will work?

No. Restore representative expendable files and verify them independently.

### Should encryption credentials be stored only inside the backed-up computer?

A recovery plan needs access to its required credentials when that computer is unavailable. Follow the current documented recovery requirements.
