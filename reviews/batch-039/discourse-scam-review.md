# Discourse review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Online forums and community hosting  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Discourse provides discussion topics and real-time chat. It offers managed hosting tiers and open-source software for self-hosting.

## Editorial verdict

Discourse documents a forum product with both managed and self-hosted options. The cited sources establish no fraud finding. The publisher’s plans and a third-party hosting offer have separate terms.

## Discourse pricing for a community with volunteer staff

Count the moderators who need staff access, then list features the forum depends on. The pricing table lists a different staff allowance by plan and places some capabilities, such as standard migration services, at higher tiers. Compare the actual moderation roster with the plan limits before comparing headline prices.

Add the hours spent handling reports, onboarding moderators and maintaining email delivery. If you self-host, identify who can perform a restore and patch the service when the primary operator is away. A bill for hosting and the cost of covering an incident belong in the same budget.


## Discourse private categories and permission checks

Create one public topic and one **private Discourse category** in a test community. Open both as a visitor, a member and a moderator. Check search results, notifications and direct links. Test what happens when a member leaves the group that granted access.

Post a question in chat and move the durable part of the answer into a topic. Have a second person locate it a day later. Measure whether your proposed *support knowledge base* saves people from asking the same question again.


## Discourse forum migration and backup recovery

Sample a thread with replies, a closed topic and a post with an attachment. After a migration rehearsal, check authorship, permissions and old links. Ask a moderator to apply the usual actions on the migrated copy before directing the community to it.

Rehearse a restore to a separate test instance and compare the restored topic and attachment counts. Plan who owns the redirect map and member communication. Those tasks still matter when a hosting plan includes some migration help.


## Features and decision criteria

- Persistent forum topics and real-time chat
- Private categories and group controls on documented plans
- Managed hosting and self-hosted deployment

## Pricing considerations

The publisher also lists a free hosted plan. Check current staff seats, feature access, migration services and renewal terms.
