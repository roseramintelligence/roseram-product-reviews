# Duolingo review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Language learning app with freemium subscription  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Duolingo offers language lessons with a free tier and paid Super subscription features. The company says course content remains accessible for free; Super adds convenience tools such as unlimited hearts, mistake review and an ad-free learning experience, while prices vary by region and subscription option.

## Editorial verdict

Duolingo is well suited to someone who wants short, frequent language practice and a game-like reminder to return each day. Its free content is useful for building a habit, while Super removes ads and some practice limits. It should not be treated as a complete substitute for conversation, listening to native speakers or feedback on real-world writing. Check trial renewal and family-plan details before subscribing.

## Use the app to build a repeatable habit

Duolingo breaks language study into short lessons and provides progress cues that can help a learner return regularly. That can be helpful for someone who struggles to schedule a long class, especially when practice fits into a commute or a short break. Pick a target that is small enough to repeat instead of racing through a course for a streak.

A streak measures activity, not mastery. After a lesson, try using a new phrase aloud, writing a sentence without prompts or listening to a short clip in the target language. Recalling material without the answer choices is a stronger check that the lesson is becoming usable.


## Compare free lessons with Super features

Duolingo’s company says learners can access course content for free. Super adds features such as unlimited hearts, mistake review, additional practice options and an ad-free experience. These features can reduce interruptions and help a learner revisit errors, but they do not guarantee faster learning or fluency.

Decide whether the paid features solve a real obstacle in your study routine. If you rarely run out of hearts or do not use the extra review, the free version may be enough. If you choose a trial, note the renewal date and exact amount shown at signup. The displayed offer can depend on device, country and subscription channel.


## Treat the trial as a recurring subscription

Duolingo’s Super page currently describes a one-week trial and shows a charge on day seven. A user should confirm the precise date, price, tax and cancellation method in the purchase flow. App-store billing may require cancellation in Apple or Google settings rather than inside the Duolingo app.

Set a reminder before the trial ends if you are evaluating features. Canceling a trial can change access immediately or at the end of the period depending on the store terms, so read the confirmation message. Keep the receipt and verify the subscription status afterward.


## Pair app practice with real communication

A language app can help with vocabulary and basic patterns, but everyday conversation includes speed, accents, context and unexpected replies. Add listening and speaking practice that matches the language you want to use. A class, tutor, exchange partner or local conversation group can provide feedback an app cannot fully replace.

Some languages offered in a catalog may have less content or different speaking practice. Try a few lessons and listen to the available audio before committing to a long routine. This review is a guide to the public product and subscription terms, not a formal assessment of Duolingo’s learning science or a claim that the app alone teaches mastery.


## Features and decision criteria

- Free language course content with optional paid subscriptions
- Super adds unlimited hearts, mistake review, skills practice and no ads
- Super Family is described for up to six Super accounts
- Trial converts to paid billing unless canceled before the stated deadline

## Pricing considerations

Duolingo’s public pricing page does not display a single stable USD amount for all regions or billing channels. Confirm the exact renewal price, trial end date, tax and cancellation method on the screen where the subscription is purchased.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I learn with Duolingo without paying?

Yes. Duolingo says its course content is available for free; paid Super adds convenience features.

### What happens after the Super trial?

The public subscription page shows a one-week trial and says the account is charged at the end unless canceled in time.

### Will Duolingo alone make me fluent?

No app can guarantee fluency. Language progress also depends on practice, listening, speaking, feedback and the learner’s starting point.
