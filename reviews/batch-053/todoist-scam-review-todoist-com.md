# Todoist review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Task management and personal productivity  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Todoist organizes personal and shared tasks across plans called Beginner, Pro and Business. Buyers should verify current task, project and collaboration limits for their use.

## Editorial verdict

Todoist publishes current plan details and pricing updates. This review avoids stating an unverified price and recommends confirming the region-specific checkout amount.

## Todoist review: capture work without losing the next action

A task list is valuable when it makes the next action easy to capture and retrieve. Put a few real tasks into a test project, assign dates where they matter and try a recurring item. Ask a second person to find what is due today and what is waiting on someone else. Keep task titles concrete and include enough context that the future you can act without reconstructing the original conversation.

Todoist’s current plan names are Beginner, Pro and Business. The publisher has issued pricing updates, and prices can vary by region and billing channel. Verify the current plan table in your own account before buying. This review does not repeat a legacy price as though it were still universal.


## Todoist shared projects, ownership and reminders

If you intend to share a project, test permissions with a second account. Determine who can add, complete or reschedule tasks and what happens when a collaborator leaves. A reminder helps only if its date and time are meaningful; test the timezone and notification channel on the device the user will actually carry.

Use a small sample to test recurring tasks after completion and rescheduling. Confirm that the next occurrence is created as expected and does not create duplicates. Write down which person owns the task and who should be contacted if it is overdue.


## Todoist limits, upgrades and total value

Check the official pricing page for current task, project, history, reminder and collaboration limits. These details can change, and some features may be limited by plan. Estimate the number of active projects and shared users rather than upgrading because a feature is listed. If a free plan already covers the task load, additional features may not justify the recurring cost.

Compare the price offered in your region and billing channel with the value of the functions you will use. Review the renewal and cancellation terms before activating a trial. Keep a copy of the plan description shown at purchase so a future change can be compared accurately.


## Todoist migration, account access and data portability

Before moving a personal or team task list, export a small sample and open it outside the account. Confirm that titles, dates, notes, project groupings and useful links are present. Decide who controls the shared workspace and what happens to tasks if the owner leaves. Avoid putting passwords, payment details or confidential customer data into task notes unless your organization has approved that use.

Test account recovery and enable available security measures. If multiple people depend on a shared project, identify another administrator or a documented way to continue. Keep critical project decisions in a durable location if losing a task account would interrupt operations.


## Todoist alternatives: compare daily task capture

Compare Todoist with your operating system’s reminders, a shared task manager and the team’s project tool. Use the same tasks and recurring schedule in each. Evaluate speed of capture, clarity of ownership, reminders, offline behavior and sharing. A lightweight tool may be a better fit for personal work; a project platform may be better for dependencies and reporting across teams.

Choose the tool whose routine people will actually maintain. Check export and plan limits before importing all historical tasks. Avoid reading a feature list as proof of productivity gains; measure whether the real team misses fewer handoffs during a short, well-defined trial.


## Who should consider Todoist?

Todoist may suit individuals or small groups that want to capture tasks, organize projects and receive reminders in a focused interface. Verify collaboration and project limits, the current plan names and region-specific price before moving shared work into it. Test recurring tasks and recovery first.

The official pages support the plan and feature descriptions, not a guarantee of improved productivity. This review is buyer diligence, not an allegation of fraud. Choose based on the workflow you can demonstrate and the data you can recover.


## Features and decision criteria

- Tasks, dates and recurring schedules
- Projects and task views
- Plan-based collaboration, reminders and productivity features
