# thinkific review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Online course and learning business platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Thinkific hosts online courses, cohorts, coaching, communities and memberships with payments, quizzes, certificates and student management. Its current plans differ in price, community capacity, branding, API features and sales tools, while third-party payment gateway fees can vary by plan and country.

## Editorial verdict

Thinkific is worth evaluating for creators and training businesses that need structured courses and learner tools rather than a simple file checkout. The Start and Grow plans provide a wider selling stack, but annual versus monthly pricing, changes to published plans and payment-gateway fees all matter. Test an end-to-end student flow and review the platform’s latest plan update before budgeting against older articles.

## Decide how much of the learning stack you need

Thinkific combines a course site with lessons, cohorts, coaching, quizzes, certificates, memberships and community features. A creator who only needs a simple downloadable guide may not need that full learning stack. A training business that requires student progress, assignments and groups may value those functions in one system.

Describe a student’s path from first visit through enrollment, lessons, assessment, certificate and support. Build that path with a test course before importing learners. Check how students participate on mobile, how instructors view progress and how a team manages late or incomplete work.


## Use current pricing and the account’s actual eligibility

Thinkific’s official pricing page lists Start, Grow and Expand plans, with monthly or annual rates. A separate 2026 update describes price changes for Basic, Start and Grow, while existing discounts and nonprofit accounts may be treated differently. Older reviews and cached screenshots can therefore show amounts that no longer apply to a new account.

Confirm whether the quoted rate is new-customer pricing, an existing-account renewal or an eligible discount. Compare the annual total with the monthly flexibility and list the communities, administrators, student records and bandwidth required. Verify any add-on such as a branded mobile app separately.


## Calculate payment fees by gateway and geography

Thinkific’s help materials distinguish the platform’s own transaction fee for third-party gateways from ordinary payment processor fees. The amount can depend on plan, merchant location and whether the creator uses Thinkific Payments. The official guide states that certain annual sales thresholds or payment routes may change fee treatment, so read the full current rules.

Model payment fees with the actual countries and methods students will use. Test a card and alternative payment flow, then reconcile the resulting transaction record with the expected payout. Keep refund and chargeback assumptions in the estimate; a platform’s headline fee is only one part of checkout cost.


## Check student privacy, exports and vendor lock-in

A learning site stores student identities, progress, assignments and sometimes payment details. Review the admin roles, data export options and student removal process before enrolling a real cohort. If a team plans to use an external CRM or analytics tool, confirm the plan’s API and webhook features and minimize sensitive data transfer.

Download a representative course and student export before launch. Ask what media, quiz responses and historical progress can be moved if the business later changes platforms. This review is based on official product and pricing materials, not a live course delivery test or a legal assessment.


## Features and decision criteria

- Courses, video lessons, coaching, cohorts, memberships and communities
- Course quizzes, assignments, certificates and payment plans
- Plan-specific student, community, administrator, bandwidth and API limits
- Third-party gateway fees differ; Thinkific Payments availability matters

## Pricing considerations

Thinkific’s official 2026 notice states new Basic/Start/Grow prices, and existing discounts or nonprofit accounts may be treated differently. Third-party gateway transaction fees can apply; the official help page lists different rates by plan and says Thinkific Payments may avoid that fee where available. Verify country, gateway, sales cap and account-specific pricing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Thinkific plan prices stay the same for existing customers?

The official 2026 pricing update describes changes for certain plans and says some active discounts and nonprofit accounts may be exempt.

### Can a third-party Stripe payment add a Thinkific fee?

The official cost guide says third-party gateway fees may apply in supported regions and differ by plan; processor fees also apply.

### How many communities are included?

The current plan comparison lists one for Start, three for Grow and ten for Expand.
