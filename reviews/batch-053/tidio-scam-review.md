# Tidio review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Live chat, customer service and AI automation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Tidio offers website chat, ticketing, automation Flows and the Lyro AI Agent. The free service includes monthly customer-service limits, while Lyro’s free allowance is a separate lifetime quota that does not renew.

## Editorial verdict

Tidio’s current help center distinguishes monthly support conversations and Flows visits from the one-time free Lyro quota. Teams should track these meters separately and verify selected add-ons, paid conversation bands and human handoff behavior.

## Tidio review: live chat, tickets and Flows

Tidio offers a website chat widget, ticketing, visitor context, integrations and no-code Flows for customer-service or sales interactions. Its Growth tier describes workflow automation and automatic chat assignment, while higher plans provide more scale and administration. A store can use the widget to answer routine questions, but it must still give visitors a clear route to a human when a question is sensitive or unusual.

Test the chat on mobile and desktop, including a visitor who leaves and returns, an agent handoff and a message outside business hours. Review what personal information appears in the agent view and which integrations can read conversation data. Write escalation rules for billing, delivery problems and account access rather than letting an automation improvise policy.


## Tidio Free plan, conversations and Flows limits

Tidio’s help center states that the Free version allows up to 10 live-chat agents and 50 customer-service conversations per month. Flows have a separate allowance of 100 unique visitors reached per month. Paid Starter is displayed at $24.17 per month on annual billing for 100 billable conversations, while Growth starts at $49.17 annually equivalent and offers a selectable conversation quota from 250 up to 2,000.

The conversation and Flow meters measure different actions and reset on different cycles. Monitor both before a campaign or store promotion drives a traffic spike. The Free plan can stop displaying incoming communication or responding after its monthly conversation limit is reached, according to the help article. A support team should decide what fallback appears to the customer before an allowance runs out.


## Tidio Lyro AI quota and paid automation

Lyro’s allowance is separate from the general support conversation quota. Tidio’s November 7, 2026 article says the first 50 Lyro conversations are a one-time lifetime allowance across the trial and Free plan; the quota does not renew. A Lyro conversation counts when it replies in a thread, and paid packages provide a renewable monthly quota. Immediate human handoff and playground testing are treated differently under the provider’s published rules.

Do not tell a team that free Lyro usage resets each month. Set the answer source, permitted actions, escalation threshold and post-trial plan before publishing the AI agent. Review logs for unsupported claims and test questions about refunds, account changes and exceptions. Price the next quota based on a realistic volume rather than the small lifetime allowance shown during setup.


## Tidio integrations, privacy and operational fit

Tidio’s help pages describe integrations with services such as Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp, plus desktop and mobile agent apps. A chat integration can consolidate channels, but it also creates another place that stores conversation content and customer identifiers. Review user roles, export and retention settings, and each connected application’s permissions.

Check how the widget affects page performance and consent behavior on your site. Identify the owner of each Flow and set a review date so outdated promotions do not remain active. If chat is a main support channel, keep an alternate email or contact path available if the widget or provider is temporarily unavailable.


## Tidio alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Tidio with Help Scout, Intercom and Freshdesk using monthly conversation volume, unique visitors, channel count, automation triggers and AI resolutions. Separate support cost from lead-capture features and calculate the exact price of Lyro, Flows or other add-ons needed. A website with low traffic may fit the Free tier; a busy store should understand exactly what happens when a quota is met.

Tidio’s official pricing and help materials describe metered support and a limited Lyro allowance; they do not establish that Tidio is a scam. This desk review did not test live chat performance or AI accuracy. Verify usage definitions and a human fallback against the current subscription before making the widget the only way customers can get help.


## Features and decision criteria

- Website live chat, shared tickets and visitor context
- No-code Flows and third-party channel integrations
- Lyro AI with separately metered conversation allowance

## Pricing considerations

Prices were checked on Tidio’s US pricing and help pages on 2026-11-16. Starter and Growth amounts reflect annual-equivalent pricing; monthly billing and add-ons differ. The first 50 free Lyro conversations are a lifetime quota, not a monthly reset. Verify the exact conversation package and add-on charges before enabling customer-facing automation.
