# Teachable review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Online course and creator commerce platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Teachable provides course, coaching and digital product publishing with checkout, student management, taxes, affiliate features and creator marketing tools. Plans cap product and administrator counts, and transaction fees differ by tier; processor fees remain separate.

## Editorial verdict

Teachable is a reasonable option for an educator who wants course delivery and commerce in one hosted school, particularly when payment and tax workflows are part of the decision. The Starter tier’s 7.5% platform fee can matter materially, while higher tiers remove that plan fee when using Teachable Payments but still incur payment processing costs. Compare course limits, payout and payment methods, student migration and the exact trial terms before launch.

## Map the product catalog and student journey

Teachable counts published courses, coaching offerings and digital downloads as products under its plan limits. A course with many modules still counts as one product, but a creator with multiple courses and downloads can reach the cap quickly. Select a tier based on planned offers, not only the first course.

Build a test school with a sample product and check enrollment, lesson completion, student communication, coupons, refunds and access removal. Confirm how free enrollments and bundles count under the current rules. The course platform should match the learning experience, not only the marketing page.


## Compare platform fees with processing fees

The pricing page lists a 7.5% Teachable plan fee on Starter and 0% on Builder and Growth when using Teachable Payments. Separate processor charges still apply and can vary by card, country and payment method. A “zero transaction fee” headline therefore does not mean every sale is fee-free.

Estimate fees against realistic order values and refund rates. Include any optional BackOffice charge, chargeback fees, international cards and alternative payment methods. Ask for a written example invoice for the expected sales mix so the choice between a lower subscription and higher per-sale cost is grounded in actual numbers.


## Confirm payment, tax and payout requirements

Teachable lists tax handling and payments as product features, with some differences between plans and gateways. Verify which gateway is available in the creator’s country, how the payout schedule works and which taxes the provider calculates or remits. Payment terms can vary by merchant location and buyer method.

Run a test purchase, refund and payout before promoting a course. Keep receipts and accounting records separate and ask a qualified tax professional about the business’s obligations. Platform automation does not replace registration, tax filing or consumer-protection duties.


## Use the trial while tracking its end date

Teachable’s pricing page lists a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee, with paid plans billed monthly or annually. A trial is useful for checking product limits, checkout and student tools, but it is short enough that the evaluation should focus on a specific course path.

Record the renewal date and confirm how cancellation affects the school, students and content access. Export student and transaction data before changing providers. This review is based on public product materials and is not a claim about course sales or student outcomes.


## Features and decision criteria

- Course, coaching and digital-download products
- Built-in checkout, payments, tax handling and affiliate features vary by plan
- Plan limits include product, administrator, API and support access
- Starter takes 7.5%; Builder and Growth list 0% plan fee with Teachable Payments

## Pricing considerations

The official page also lists a 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Payment processing fees are additional and differ by payment method and location; optional BackOffice may carry separate charges. Check the live plan and terms before signup.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Teachable have a free permanent plan?

The pricing FAQ describes a paid service with a free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee, not a permanent free plan.

### Does zero percent transaction fee mean no fees on sales?

No. It refers to the Teachable plan fee on eligible payment routes; payment processing fees can still apply.

### How many products can I publish?

The current pricing page lists 5 products on Starter, 10 on Builder and 50 on Growth.
