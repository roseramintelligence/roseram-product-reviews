# Adaface Job Description Generator review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-28  
**Category:** Job description drafting  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

The Adaface Job Description Generator presents editable job-description material. Its useful role is to provide a starting structure for a vacancy, while the employer remains responsible for the actual responsibilities, requirements and employment terms. Evaluate the final brief against the role you can explain rather than the number of phrases generated.

## A job description should describe a real job

An appealing template can quietly turn a straightforward role into an unrealistic list of requirements. Before generating text, identify the work that must be done, the decisions the person will own and the support available to them. Separate essential skills from skills that can be learned. This makes the draft easier to evaluate and gives candidates a more accurate basis for deciding whether to apply.

The publisher’s generator is distinct from its broader assessment offering. Do not assume that using a job-description template includes a paid assessment, a hiring workflow or a guarantee that applicants meet the requirements. Review the exact tool and offer you are using. A useful template should make your own requirements clearer, not replace the work of defining them.


## A practical edit pass for an AI-assisted vacancy

Choose a fictional role and write five actual responsibilities before using the generator. Compare the resulting brief with that list. Remove unsupported claims about the company, inflated experience requirements and duties that belong to another role. Ask the eventual manager to explain how each essential requirement connects to the work.

Read the application instructions from a candidate’s perspective. Confirm that the location, working arrangement and decision process match the real opportunity. Any employment-law or policy requirements need appropriate organizational review. This article does not provide legal approval of a template or claim that the generator has checked your local requirements.

A useful final review asks the manager to point to an ordinary week of work that justifies each essential requirement. Remove a requirement when there is no clear connection. This helps prevent a template from becoming an accidental barrier to suitable applicants. Also compare the final role title with the responsibilities: a familiar title can still create misleading expectations when the actual scope is substantially different.


## Use the draft consistently across the hiring journey

Keep the approved brief with the evaluation criteria used during interviews. If the role changes, update both so that candidates are not judged against an unpublished expectation. Confirm whether the generator’s output can be edited and reused in the way you intend under the current terms. Do not infer special rights from the availability of a text field.
