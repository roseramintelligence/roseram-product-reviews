# Joplin review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Notes and synchronization  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Joplin is a note-taking application with documented synchronization targets. The choice of target affects who operates and pays for storage.

## Editorial verdict

The project describes how notebooks synchronize. Verify the terms and identity of any managed service you plan to use with it.

## Joplin sync targets for a field notebook

A field notebook must work when the connection is poor. On two test devices, write one plain note and attach a small photo. Change a title offline and reconnect. Watch which updates arrive and how a conflict is shown so a field worker knows what to do next.

Choose the **Joplin sync target** with an owner who can resolve login and storage problems. The cost of the target matters alongside the time it takes to help someone after a device has been replaced.


## Joplin notebook recovery after a lost device

Use a dummy notebook to rehearse a restore. Confirm you can recover the text, attachments and notebook structure without the first device. Keep a separate *recoverable copy* for a deletion scenario as well as a connection problem.

Move a short week’s notes before moving years of material. Ask the person who must find them to retrieve a decision, a photo and a meeting record. Zapier’s comparative review highlighted Joplin as an open-source choice for people leaving Evernote. Put that fit to the test with your own export of notebooks and attachments. If the job is navigating a folder of Markdown files from another editor, compare Obsidian using the same sample.


## Features and decision criteria

- Notebook organization
- Multiple documented sync targets
- Cross-device note workflows

## Pricing considerations

The published Basic tier has 2 GB of storage and a 10 MB limit per note or attachment. Check current checkout and the cost of any third-party sync service separately.
