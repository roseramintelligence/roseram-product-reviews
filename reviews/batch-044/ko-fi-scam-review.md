# Ko-fi review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Creator tipping, memberships and shop platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Ko-fi offers creators a page for one-time tips, goals, memberships, commissions and digital or physical shop products. The Ko-fi Free mode charges 0% platform fee on one-time tips and 5% on other listed sales; Standard mode charges 5% across those creator payments, with payment processor fees on top.

## Editorial verdict

Ko-fi is attractive for a creator who wants a simple link for direct support or small product sales while receiving payments through their own PayPal or Stripe account. The fee mode changes the net amount: Free mode keeps one-time tips platform-fee-free but charges 5% on memberships, shop, commissions and monthly tips; Standard charges 5% across all categories. Compare processor fees, tax duties and the features you need before picking a mode.

## Choose how supporters can help

Ko-fi combines a creator profile with tips, goals, memberships, commissions and a shop. A person who wants to support a single post may prefer a one-time contribution, while someone selling a repeatable digital product needs a clear listing and delivery process. Choose the shortest path that explains what a supporter is paying for.

Write the page around a real offer, not only a donation button. Explain whether a tip is optional, what a membership includes, how commission requests are handled and when a shop item will arrive. Digital sales should include file details and refund expectations; physical goods need shipping and fulfillment details.


## Understand the fee mode before posting a payment link

Ko-fi’s help page describes Free mode as 0% platform fee on one-time tips and 5% on monthly tips, memberships, commissions and shop sales. Standard mode has no monthly subscription cost but charges 5% across the listed transaction types. That makes the right choice depend on the mix of income, not a simple “free versus paid” label.

PayPal or Stripe processing fees apply separately. Estimate a small and a typical transaction at your actual currency and country, and include the processor fee in the net calculation. A low-value tip can lose a larger proportion to a fixed processor charge than a bigger sale. Check the current transaction breakdown before making a public claim about how much the creator keeps.


## Use direct payment with careful account hygiene

Ko-fi says payments go to a creator’s own PayPal or Stripe account. This can shorten the platform payout path, but it also means the creator must secure those payment accounts, keep recovery methods current and handle the applicable tax and customer records. Do not expose private payment credentials or use someone else’s account without authorization.

Test a low-value transaction, refund and digital delivery before announcing a launch. Confirm that the intended payment account receives funds and that the order details can be reconciled. If a shop item is a pre-order, Ko-fi’s help center says it should be clearly labeled and customers updated about progress.


## Set expectations for memberships and commissions

Memberships create ongoing obligations, so state the content cadence and what happens when an episode or post is delayed. Ko-fi’s membership guide describes service fees on membership payments. A creator should plan the time needed to fulfill benefits and moderate any supporter-only space.

For commissions, make the scope, turnaround time, revision policy and delivery format visible before a customer pays. Keep a queue and communicate if an order is delayed. Direct payments can arrive quickly, but immediate payment does not eliminate the creator’s responsibility to deliver, resolve disputes or follow consumer rules. This source-based review does not claim that Ko-fi guarantees creator revenue or customer satisfaction.


## Features and decision criteria

- Creator page for one-time tips, goals, monthly support and memberships
- Shop for digital or physical items and a commissions workflow
- Payments go to the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account
- Service fee differs between Free and Standard; processor fees apply separately

## Pricing considerations

Ko-fi’s help page describes two fee modes and notes that payment processor fees still apply. Fee settings can change, and older Ko-fi Gold terms differ from new-creator defaults. Confirm the selected mode in account settings and check tax obligations, payout availability and processor pricing for your country.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Ko-fi free to join?

Ko-fi’s fee guide says both Free and Standard have no monthly subscription cost, though service fees apply to some transactions.

### Does Ko-fi Free charge a platform fee on one-time tips?

Ko-fi states that Free mode charges 0% on one-time tips and 5% on other listed categories such as shop sales and memberships.

### Does Ko-fi process and hold my payouts?

Ko-fi says payments go directly to the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account; those processors’ own fees still apply.
