# Kajabi review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Creator business platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Kajabi combines website, courses, coaching, digital products, email, automations, checkout and communities in one creator business platform. Its current plans set limits on products, contacts, websites, communities and administrators, with annual billing discounted against monthly payment.

## Editorial verdict

Kajabi is positioned as an all-in-one suite for a creator who expects to sell multiple products and wants marketing, checkout and delivery under one account. Its higher fixed price can be justified only if those bundled functions replace systems the business would otherwise pay for and maintain. Start by mapping actual products and contacts to plan limits; do not confuse software capacity with a guarantee of audience growth or earnings.

## Add up the tools that the bundle would replace

Kajabi combines a website, course or product delivery, email, checkout and automation. That can simplify a creator’s stack if these are all required and currently spread across services. A business should compare the bundle against its actual existing subscriptions rather than assuming every included feature will be used.

Inventory websites, products, contacts, automations and payment tools. Identify which systems Kajabi can truly replace and which would remain necessary, such as accounting, customer support or external analytics. Include migration and setup time in the comparison.


## Map products and contacts to the right tier

Kajabi lists limits on products, contacts, websites, communities and administrators across its plans. A creator with a handful of offers may fit the Basic tier, while a larger catalog or team may require Growth or Pro. Plan selection should reflect the expected number of active records and sites, not only the marketing feature checklist.

Estimate contact growth and inactive subscribers, and define how products are counted. Test whether drafts, bundles or variations consume plan capacity. If capacity is close to a limit, ask Kajabi about enforcement and upgrade timing before migrating a mailing list or publishing an additional offer.


## Read annual billing and total commitment clearly

Kajabi’s help article explains that the annual price shown as a monthly amount is the full year’s total divided by twelve and paid up front. The documented annual savings are about 20%. That can lower average monthly cost while increasing the initial cash commitment and making it important to understand refund and cancellation terms.

Compare the annual prepayment with month-to-month costs and the time required to launch. Confirm taxes, promotional terms, payment processor fees and any services that are not included. Avoid buying an annual plan before the business has tested the checkout, email deliverability and customer experience.


## Treat marketing features as infrastructure, not outcomes

Email broadcasts, automations, landing pages and checkout can make it easier to coordinate a sales funnel. They do not ensure that an offer has demand or that recipients will open or buy. A sustainable plan should include consent-based list growth, clear pricing and a way to measure refunds and customer satisfaction.

Test enrollment and payment edge cases, unsubscribe behavior, purchase receipts and content access on mobile. Keep copies of customer and sales data in a format the business can use outside the platform. This review covers the vendor’s public materials and makes no income or conversion guarantee.


## Features and decision criteria

- Courses, coaching, digital products and communities
- Websites, email campaigns, automations and checkout
- Plan limits on products, contacts, websites, communities and admin users
- Marketing, payments and learning delivery integrated in a hosted service

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page displays annual monthly equivalents while charging the full annual amount upfront; annual billing saves about 20% according to Kajabi. Confirm whether listed plans, product limits, promotions and payment options apply in your region.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Kajabi have separate product and contact caps?

Yes. The current plans list different product and contact limits by tier.

### Does an annual monthly equivalent mean monthly billing?

No. Kajabi says the annual total is paid upfront once a year; the displayed monthly amount divides that annual cost by twelve.

### Is the platform bundle a guarantee that a creator will make sales?

No. The software provides publishing, marketing and checkout tools; audience, offer fit and sales results remain uncertain.
