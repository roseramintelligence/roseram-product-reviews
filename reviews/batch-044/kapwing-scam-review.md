# Kapwing review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Collaborative online video editing  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Kapwing is an online creation and editing workspace for video, audio, images and GIFs. Its plan comparison describes export duration and resolution, monthly editing allowances, AI subtitle and translation minutes, file size, storage, collaboration and workspace privacy.

## Editorial verdict

Kapwing is useful to compare when a creator or small team wants browser-based editing and review links. The free plan’s watermark, export duration, resolution and project storage limit matter for routine publishing. Pro and Business add longer exports and collaboration features, but they are recurring subscriptions that automatically renew and are not refundable under the publisher’s stated policy.

## Check the free export against your channel

Kapwing’s plan table lists free exports at 720p with a watermark, up to four minutes per video and 30 minutes of exported video per month. That can be enough to explore an editor, but it may not fit a weekly publishing schedule. Compare your actual video lengths and branding requirements with those caps before editing a backlog.

Free workspace projects are described as unlisted, meaning anyone with the link may view them; the page distinguishes those from private projects. Treat a share link as access, and do not place confidential footage in a workspace without verifying the current visibility controls.


## Compare AI editing allowances separately

The pricing comparison lists plan-based monthly amounts for subtitles, translation, text-to-speech, clean audio and lip sync. A team producing multilingual training videos should estimate these minutes separately from final exports. A large project could fit the upload size and still exceed a translation or speech quota.

Build one short sample and track all actions from upload through final export. Note which features consume a limited allowance, how much storage the source files occupy and whether the final video keeps project links or collaboration comments.


## Understand billing and refund terms

Kapwing’s FAQ says Pro and Business subscriptions are recurring, auto-renew until cancelled and are not generally refunded after an upgrade. The page lists a lower monthly equivalent for annual billing, which is charged as a yearly amount. Make sure the person approving the purchase understands the billing interval and how to stop renewal.

If a team has multiple editors, check seats and invited users before checkout. Store a copy of finished assets outside the workspace and identify what happens to project files when access ends. The official page describes project retention differently across free and paid plans.


## Use collaboration without losing editorial control

Comment links can make review easier for a distributed team, but decide who can view, edit or export a project. Assign one owner to approve audio, captions, titles, licensed assets and factual content. A clear approval step helps prevent a draft from being mistaken for the final campaign.

This review is based on Kapwing’s public pricing and product guidance; it does not report hands-on testing, legal advice or a privacy audit. Confirm current export limits, file storage, refund policy and workspace visibility in the account before uploading material that should remain private.


## Features and decision criteria

- Browser-based editing for video, image, audio and GIF projects
- Automatic subtitles, translation and text-to-speech with tier limits
- Team review links, workspace folders and live collaboration
- Different export, storage and privacy rules by plan

## Pricing considerations

The pricing page is dynamic and rates may change. The official FAQ says paid subscriptions auto-renew and refunds are not generally offered after upgrading; confirm current price, seat count, storage and limits before subscribing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I publish a watermark-free four-minute video from the free plan?

No. The comparison lists free exports with a watermark and a maximum of four minutes.

### Is a paid trial included?

Kapwing says it does not currently offer a trial for paid plans, though its free tools can be used before upgrading.

### Are paid subscriptions one-time purchases?

No. The publisher says Pro and Business are recurring subscriptions that auto-renew until cancelled.
