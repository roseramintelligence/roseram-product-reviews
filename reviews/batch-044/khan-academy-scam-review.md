# Khan Academy review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Free online learning platform  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Khan Academy is a nonprofit online learning service with lessons, videos, articles and practice exercises across school subjects and test preparation. Its core learning library is free and ad-free, supported by donors and grants; Khanmigo AI access can have separate eligibility or pricing rules.

## Editorial verdict

Khan Academy is a strong starting point for a learner who wants structured practice without a subscription charge. Its free lessons and questions suit self-study and family learning, but the platform should be matched to the learner’s course, grade level and need for teacher feedback. If you are considering Khanmigo or school features, verify those terms separately; they are not the same as the core free library.

## Start with the subject and learning goal

Khan Academy is a nonprofit learning library that offers videos, articles and practice questions. It can help a student review a topic, work through exercises or explore a subject at their own pace. Begin by choosing one course or skill and setting a small goal, such as completing a unit or improving on a specific kind of problem.

The right course level matters. A learner who already understands the basics may need more challenging practice, while someone filling a gap may benefit from short explanations followed by guided exercises. Use the course sequence as a map rather than a promise that every learner progresses at the same speed.


## Know what the free promise covers

Khan Academy states that its core lessons and practice library is free and that it does not show ads in that experience. The organization says it relies on individual donations and grants. This is a meaningful distinction from education platforms that charge for certificates or subscriptions to access course material.

Some newer tools and institutional offerings may have different terms. Khanmigo, teacher administration and school products should be checked separately in the account or program information. Do not assume that a broad statement about the core library means every AI feature, integration or support option is free for every kind of user.


## Use practice as feedback, not as a final credential

Practice questions can reveal which concept needs more attention. After a missed answer, ask the learner to explain the correction in their own words and solve a similar problem without looking. Track progress across several sessions rather than treating one quiz score as a complete measure of understanding.

If the goal is a formal credential, confirm whether Khan Academy provides one for the course or whether a school, testing body or separate course provider is responsible. A learning platform may help prepare a student without issuing a recognized certificate or college credit. Check the requirement with the institution that will evaluate it.


## Consider guidance and accessibility needs

Self-paced online learning can help when a learner wants to study outside class hours, but some students need feedback from a teacher, tutor or parent. A dashboard can show progress without explaining why a learner is stuck. Combine practice with conversation, written notes or an instructor’s feedback for topics that require deeper support.

Before building a study routine around the site, check device access, screen-reader support, language options and the learner’s preferred format. Keep a regular schedule that is realistic and protect time for breaks. This review reflects Khan Academy’s public descriptions, not a measured learning outcome or an independent classroom assessment.


## Features and decision criteria

- Free lessons, videos, articles and practice questions

- No ads in the core learning library according to Khan Academy

## Pricing considerations

The cited free-use article was updated in 2021 and describes the core library. Newer AI and school offerings may have different eligibility or pricing. Confirm any Khanmigo charge and local account features before use.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Khan Academy free?

Khan Academy says its core library of videos, articles, practice questions and lessons is free for anyone.

### Does the free library show advertising?

The cited Khan Academy Help Center says the core program has no ads.

### Does the free learning policy include all AI tools?

Not automatically. Khanmigo and school-related offerings can have separate eligibility, access and pricing rules.
