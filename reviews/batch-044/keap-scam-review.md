# Keap review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Small business CRM and marketing automation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Keap combines CRM, pipeline management, email marketing, automation, appointments, payments and texting for small businesses. Its pricing scales with contact and user inputs and includes a published starting amount, while text capacity and some implementation services have separate costs or requirements.

## Editorial verdict

Keap may fit a small business that wants CRM and customer follow-up automation in one platform and is ready to invest in onboarding. The main evaluation risk is underestimating the all-in cost: contact count, user seats, texting, implementation and annual billing affect the actual commitment. Ask for a written quote based on real list size and workflows, then confirm that its automation can be managed by the people who will operate it.

## Check whether an all-in-one workflow earns its cost

Keap combines CRM, lead management, email campaigns, automation, appointments, payments and customer communications. This can simplify a small business stack if those workflows currently live in disconnected products. It can also create unused feature expense if the business needs only contact management and a basic pipeline.

Map the customer journey from inquiry through follow-up and purchase. Identify which steps Keap will automate and which remain manual. Build a small test automation with clear consent, a pause rule and a human review point before enabling campaign traffic to a full list.


## Get a quote using real contact and user counts

The official pricing page shows a starting subscription amount and inputs for users and contacts. Because these values affect the quote, the visible starting price should not be treated as a guaranteed price for every business. The page also presents implementation services as required to help customers get started.

Ask sales to itemize subscription, included contacts, users, implementation, additional contacts, texting, phone features and taxes. Request both monthly and annual totals, cancellation terms and promotional expiration. Compare those figures with the current stack and the time needed to maintain automations.


## Budget texting separately and respect regional rules

Keap’s pricing page lists an included text tier with 500 messages and 100 voice minutes, higher monthly tiers and metered overages. It also limits some one-to-one and marketing text features by geography. A customer database being global does not mean every phone feature is available in every market.

Estimate conversational texts, automated messages and calls separately. Confirm consent requirements, quiet hours, opt-out keywords and local sender registration before enabling outreach. Set limits and alerts so a high-volume workflow cannot produce an unexpected bill.


## Verify onboarding, ownership and operating effort

Keap emphasizes implementation and strategy services as part of onboarding. Ask exactly what data is imported, which automations are configured, how many training sessions are included and what happens when requirements change. Confirm whether the business can edit and export its own workflows and customer records.

Assign an internal owner for contact hygiene, campaign approval and automation troubleshooting. Test unsubscribe handling, duplicate detection and reporting with a small opt-in segment. This assessment is based on current public Keap materials and does not guarantee revenue results or independent campaign deliverability.


## Features and decision criteria

- Contact management, segmentation and sales pipelines
- Email campaigns, follow-up automation, appointments and payments
- Pricing calculator varies by contacts and users
- Text messaging has region limits, usage allowances and add-on pricing

## Pricing considerations

Keap’s price calculator and promotions can change the subscription amount. The page states region restrictions for texting and presents implementation services. Confirm contacts, users, billing cadence, setup fees, taxes and add-ons in a written quote.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Keap free to use long term?

The pricing page directs visitors to a trial or sales demo and lists paid subscriptions; it does not describe a permanent free plan.

### Is the starting monthly price guaranteed for any list size?

No. The official pricing page includes user and contact inputs; request a quote with the actual contact count and billing term.

### Can a business text customers from any country?

No. Keap’s pricing notes that some texting features are limited to the United States or the United States and Canada.
