# Kdenlive review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Desktop video editing  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Kdenlive is a video editor with a documented project, timeline and rendering workflow.

## Editorial verdict

The user manual identifies the editing workflow. Confirm the scope and provenance of outside offers for templates, project files or editing.

## Kdenlive timeline editing for a first client cut

Get agreement on the story before polishing a transition. Assemble a short opening with the interview, one cutaway and a title. Show it to the person who approves the work. Ask which information a viewer should take away from the first thirty seconds.

Keep the **Kdenlive project** and its media in a handoff a second editor can understand. Deliberately open the pilot on another workstation. Find missing media and fonts while the film is short enough to fix in an afternoon.


## Kdenlive rendering for approvals and final delivery

Make a small review export and a final-format sample from the same range. Watch both in the player used by the recipient. Check readable text, audio balance and whether the opening and closing frames survived.

A clearly named *approval cut* lets a client refer to the right version when requesting changes. Put the approved timestamp range and required export details in the delivery note. That discipline helps an editor finish the work even when the person who started it is away.


## Features and decision criteria

- Project bin and timeline editing
- Preview and versioned project workflows
- Rendering for delivery
