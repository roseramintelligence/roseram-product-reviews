# Later review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-17  
**Category:** Software & online services  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Later Social combines social profile management, post scheduling, a visual planner, analytics and team collaboration. Current plans differ in social sets, monthly post caps, user seats, AI credits and data history.

## Editorial verdict

Later can be a practical calendar and publishing hub for social teams, particularly when visual planning and approvals matter. Check each profile’s supported publishing path and count post, user, social-set and analytics-history allowances against a real month of work before subscribing.

## Later review: a visual social publishing calendar

Later Social is built around planning and publishing content across multiple social profiles. Its current feature comparison lists Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn and Snapchat, with the number of profiles organized into Social Sets. The calendar, media library, caption storage and multi-profile scheduling support a visual editorial workflow. For a solo creator, the calendar can help turn a batch of photos and clips into a manageable publishing plan; for a team, approval and access features become more important than the calendar alone.

A scheduling dashboard is not the same as native platform behavior. Post formats, account types and publishing permissions vary by network. Later’s feature matrix and help center document platform and plan qualifications, so verify the exact account type before building a campaign around auto-publishing. Test one Reel, Story, short video and image post if those formats are part of the workflow. Confirm whether the post is published automatically or requires a notification and manual completion.


## Later plan prices, profile sets and monthly post limits

Later’s current pricing page lists Starter at $18.75 per month when billed yearly, Growth at $37.50 and Scale at $82.50, with taxes extra. Starter includes one Social Set of eight profiles and one user; Growth lists two sets and two users; Scale lists six sets and four users. The plan comparison specifies 30 scheduled posts per profile per month on Starter, 180 on Growth and unlimited posts on Scale. It also lists 5, 50 and 100 monthly AI credits respectively.

A Social Set can include one profile from each supported platform, so count profiles rather than brands or calendars. Later explains that scheduled posts count when scheduled, whether or not they have been published yet, and that each social profile has its own monthly allowance. Draft volume and rescheduling practices can therefore affect the limit. Build a calendar from the busiest month you expect and include seasonal campaigns, revisions and test posts before choosing a tier.


## Later publishing, formats and analytics history

Later lists auto-publishing for several platforms and post formats, but the matrix includes qualifications that can depend on account type, format and plan. A connected Instagram creator profile, for example, may behave differently from a personal profile, and a Story or feature requiring a platform action may not follow the same path as a standard feed image. Verify one real post per format during the trial and check the resulting post on the destination network.

Analytics retention also scales by plan: Later’s pricing page describes three months of historical analytics on Starter, up to a year on Growth and up to two years plus custom analytics on Scale. That history can matter when a team evaluates seasonal performance or compares a campaign year over year. Export a sample report and note which metrics come from the social network and which are calculated by Later. A dashboard metric should not be treated as a verified sales result without a matching conversion source.


## Later team access, approvals and social inbox

Growth and Scale add collaboration that Starter does not provide, including internal and external feedback workflows, post comments, activity history and team access groups as described in the current plan reference. Those tools can reduce review scattered across email and chat, but they introduce account-permission decisions. Decide which roles may draft, approve and publish, and test whether an external reviewer can see only the intended post preview rather than the whole calendar or media library.

Later’s plan documentation notes that additional users and Social Sets are add-ons only on certain tiers and that some options are limited to desktop signups. The social inbox and analytics history also vary by plan. Before subscribing, verify whether your billing channel permits the add-ons you need, whether roles are scoped by profile, and how access is removed when a contractor leaves. Keep the original media and captions in your own storage so the publishing tool is not your sole archive.


## Later alternatives and the SCAM Review question

Compare Later with Buffer, Hootsuite and the native scheduling tools in the networks you use. Count the same number of profiles, scheduled posts, users and approval steps, then include analytics history, inbox features, AI credits and add-on pricing. A visual-first creator who posts at moderate volume may value Later’s calendar; a publisher with heavy collaboration or customer service needs should compare access control and inbox depth. A team using only one or two social networks may find native tools sufficient.

Later’s official pricing and help pages describe a social media management service with explicit plan limits; they do not establish that Later is a scam. This source-led review did not connect an account or publish a post. Confirm plan eligibility, profile types, monthly post counts, analytics retention, trial conversion date and cancellation terms in your account before moving an active publishing calendar.


## Features and decision criteria

- Visual calendar and post scheduler
- Social profile sets across supported networks
- Media library, saved captions and link-in-bio page
- Team approvals, inbox and analytics on eligible tiers

## Pricing considerations

Later lists a 14-day trial and a 25% annual discount on the pricing page. Taxes are extra, extra social sets and seats are available only on some plans, and channel rules can affect publishing.
