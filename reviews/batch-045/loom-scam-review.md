# Loom review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Screen recording and asynchronous video messaging  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Loom provides screen and camera recordings with shareable pages. Its Starter tier limits video count and recording length; paid tiers add capacity and editing features.

## Editorial verdict

Loom’s official pricing page lists Starter limits and paid capabilities. Verify where the account is purchased because Atlassian and Loom billing paths may differ.

## Loom review: screen recordings need a clear audience and purpose

A **Loom screen recording** is useful when it shows a task that would be cumbersome to explain in a message. Record a sample with the camera bubble and system audio, then open the shared link as a viewer. Check whether the recipient can understand the context, read the interface and find the action they need to take. Trim out sensitive tabs and notifications before sharing.

Decide who owns the video and whether it should be private, workspace-only or available by link. Test access in a separate browser session. A *share URL* is convenient, but the team should understand who can watch, comment, download or redistribute the content.


## Loom Starter limits and paid plan costs

Loom’s pricing page listed Starter at $0, with up to 25 videos per person and a five-minute recording limit. It showed Business at $18 and Business + AI at $24 per user monthly, with more recording capacity and editing features. These values were observed 2026-11-16 and should be checked in the plan selector. Loom also describes differences between purchases on Loom.com and Atlassian.com, so the same brand name may involve different billing and administration paths.

Estimate the number of creators, videos and recordings likely to be retained. Confirm if paid features are billed by creator or workspace member and whether annual billing is selected. If video is a permanent knowledge base, assign someone to archive obsolete instructions and update links.


## Loom privacy, workspace ownership and AI features

Screen recordings can reveal customer data, internal dashboards and personal notifications. Create a short recording policy: what may be shown, who approves external sharing and when a video should be deleted. Test privacy defaults with a sample video and revoke a test user’s workspace access.

Loom’s pricing page lists AI features such as summaries, titles and chapters on paid plans. Verify the data handling and feature terms before using recordings that contain confidential conversations. Keep a human review step for summaries and action items before forwarding them as an official record.


## Loom alternatives and async communication fit

Compare Loom with the screen recorder built into your operating system and your existing meeting platform. Use the same demo, audience, recording length and privacy requirement. Evaluate capture quality, editing, captions, viewer access and where the video will live. A meeting recording may be better for a conversation; a short async clip may be better for a walkthrough.

Check download and export options, retention after cancellation and account transfer. Keep critical instructions in a maintained document rather than relying only on a video that may become outdated.


## Who should consider Loom?

Loom may fit teams that want quick screen and camera recordings for walkthroughs, feedback or asynchronous updates. Verify Starter limits, workspace access and purchase channel. Use a test video to understand privacy and editing before sharing with customers or a broad internal audience.

Official sources support the plan and feature descriptions but do not demonstrate the fit of a particular team’s communication process. This review is buyer diligence, not a finding of fraud. Confirm terms and account ownership before standardizing.


## Features and decision criteria

- Screen and camera recording
- Shareable video pages with viewer interactions
- Plan-based storage, video count and editing features

## Pricing considerations

Observed on Loom’s official pricing page on 2026-11-16. Billing cadence, purchase path, taxes, workspace seats and regional pricing can change the total. Loom notes differences between Loom.com and Atlassian.com subscriptions; confirm features and billing terms for your account.
