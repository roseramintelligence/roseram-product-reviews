# LeadSquared review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Sales CRM and marketing automation  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

LeadSquared combines lead and account management, sales activity tracking, workflow automation and marketing tools for organizations managing high-volume customer journeys. Its US sales CRM pricing page lists Sales Pro at $60 per user per month and Sales Super at $100 per user per month when billed annually. The product is especially positioned for education, healthcare, financial services and other B2C teams; confirm the exact modules, implementation scope and regulated-data terms for your use case.

## Editorial verdict

LeadSquared is a candidate for teams that need structured lead routing, mobile sales activity and configurable workflows across a sizable pipeline. The breadth can help operational teams coordinate capture, follow-up and reporting, but it also makes discovery and implementation important. Compare the per-user plan cost with required add-ons, marketing volume, storage and onboarding. A small team with a simple pipeline should pilot the daily workflow before committing to a larger system.

## A CRM built around lead movement and follow-up

LeadSquared presents a connected set of sales CRM and marketing automation tools for organizations that handle many inquiries and need consistent follow-up. The official US overview emphasizes capturing leads, distributing them to teams, tracking activity and supporting sector-specific workflows. Its Sales CRM pricing page lists lead, account and opportunity management, scoring, duplicate blocking, distribution, activities, tasks, pipeline reporting and mobile CRM. These functions are most useful when the business can define clear stages, ownership rules and next actions.

Before evaluating software, map the process from first inquiry to qualified opportunity and closed outcome. Identify where leads arrive, which fields are essential, how duplicates should be resolved, what makes a lead urgent and who owns each follow-up. Then test whether LeadSquared can represent those decisions without creating a maze of custom fields or notifications. The system should make the next useful action obvious to a representative and make exceptions visible to a manager.


## Compare Sales Pro and Sales Super against real workload

The official US pricing page showed Sales Pro at $60 per user per month and Sales Super at $100 per user per month on annual billing when checked. It lists differences in quantities and capabilities such as storage, email inclusions, workflow allowances, reporting and platform usage. Treat the displayed figure as a starting point for the Sales CRM subscription; LeadSquared sells other modules, and the marketing automation page has separate pricing. Do not assume a CRM quote includes campaigns, chatbot sessions, dynamic landing pages, onboarding or every integration.

Build a cost model for one year using the number of full users, field representatives, administrators and occasional collaborators. Add the modules you need, expected email or conversation volume, migration, configuration and training. Ask the vendor to identify which limits are pooled and which apply per user or account. Request written confirmation of renewal pricing, payment cadence, minimum commitment, data export and the consequences of exceeding a usage allowance.


## Test routing, mobile work and automation before migration

Create a pilot using a small set of representative lead sources and real but approved sample records. Check form capture, duplicate handling, assignment, escalation, task creation, reminders and reporting. Test both ordinary cases and exceptions: an incomplete inquiry, a returning customer, an unassigned territory, a reassigned opportunity and a lead that opts out. Make sure automated actions are explainable and can be paused when source data or routing rules are wrong.

For field teams, examine the mobile experience under the connectivity conditions they actually encounter. The product page lists mobile CRM capabilities, including lead and activity management and offline-oriented tools. Verify which features function without a connection, how updates resolve after reconnecting, whether location tools are optional and how the company governs employee location data. Compare field notes and call outcomes with the desktop record so managers can trust the pipeline without asking employees to double-enter information.


## Review governance, integrations and implementation ownership

LeadSquared’s pricing material lists API access, webhooks, permission templates and audit-related capabilities with plan-specific limits. Translate those into a concrete integration checklist: source and destination systems, field mapping, sync direction, failure alerts, rate limits and who owns support when a connector stops working. Evaluate permissions using job roles and least privilege. If the CRM will hold sensitive or regulated information, review current contracts, security documentation, retention, deletion, data location and access logs with the vendor and your compliance lead.

The official site describes business-process mapping, migration, onboarding and training. Ask for deliverables, named responsibilities, timeline, test criteria and post-launch support in the implementation proposal. Keep an exportable source copy and reconcile record counts before retiring the previous CRM. This guide is based on public product and pricing information; it is not a hands-on test, security audit or customer testimonial. Confirm the current configuration and quote directly with LeadSquared.


## Features and decision criteria

- Lead, account and opportunity management with quality scoring, duplicate blocking and configurable distribution
- Sales activities, tasks, pipeline reports, email integrations, mobile CRM and location-oriented field tools
- Workflow automation, dynamic forms, reporting and API or platform extensions with plan-specific limits
- Marketing automation and communication modules are available as related products; confirm whether quoted CRM pricing includes them

## Pricing considerations

Prices are from LeadSquared’s US Sales CRM pricing page and are shown with annual billing. They describe the Sales CRM plans, not necessarily marketing automation, communication modules, onboarding, taxes or negotiated enterprise terms. Confirm the quote, minimum seats, add-ons, data residency, support level and implementation costs with LeadSquared before purchase.
