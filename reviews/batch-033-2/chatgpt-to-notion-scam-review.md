Inspect code, references and ordering where those details matter. A neat page that hides the correction can preserve the wrong knowledge.

Review destination sharing before using private work material. The audience for a Notion space may differ from the audience for the original chat. This review does not verify access restrictions, export completeness or a particular extension’s security.
