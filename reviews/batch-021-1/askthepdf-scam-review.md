## PDF chat needs a well-defined document question

The publisher describes uploading PDFs and asking direct questions, with source links to particular pages. This is potentially helpful when a reader needs to locate a relevant passage in a long document. It is a different task from determining whether an argument is sound, a clause applies to a particular situation or a study result transfers to another population. A reply can accurately summarize the text it found while still be unsuitable for the reader's broader decision.

Start with a question whose evidence boundary can be inspected: which section states a condition, what assumptions the author lists, or where two definitions differ. Ask for supporting pages and read the surrounding material. If the question requires several documents, verify how the service represents that scope rather than assuming every uploaded file participates in every answer. The saved pages advertise PDF interaction but do not document every retrieval detail. No claim is made here about how the underlying system selects or ranks passages.


## Page links are an audit aid, not a substitute for reading

The captured homepage shows an answer accompanied by multiple page references. That illustrates the advertised source-linking interface; it is not a result reproduced in this review. A page reference helps a reader inspect provenance, but the referenced passage still needs to support the precise sentence. A nearby discussion of a topic is weaker support than a statement establishing the actual claim. Even a correct citation can omit an exception, uncertainty or definition that changes the interpretation.

For a consequential summary, make a short claim-to-passage record. Preserve the document version, distinguish printed page numbers from viewer positions where necessary, and quote only the minimum text needed to verify the point. Check that references survive the workflow used to share the answer with colleagues. This review has not tested clickable references, numbering behavior or persistence after export. Its recommendation is to use source links for verification, not to infer accuracy from their presence alone.


## An absolute hallucination claim deserves a controlled test

The publisher's comparison includes the phrase that there are no hallucinations in document-based queries. That is a strong marketing claim and is not endorsed as a measured fact here. Grounded interfaces can still require checks for unsupported inference, misreading, omission and incorrect attribution. This review does not assert that any particular failure occurred in AskThePDF; it also does not treat an absolute statement on a comparison table as evidence that every possible question will be answered correctly.

A useful evaluation includes questions whose answer is absent, questions containing an incorrect premise and questions that require separating competing positions in the same document. Check whether the response acknowledges missing information and whether every substantive conclusion has support. Use reviewed examples rather than selecting only the most fluent output. Record the conditions and scope before generalizing from results. No such benchmark was run here, and no reliability percentage or guarantee is invented to make the product appear better or worse.


## OCR and visual queries need their own acceptance cases

The captured paid pricing lists OCR support, while the English comparison mentions querying graphs and images. These are distinct capabilities from extracting text already embedded in a PDF. A scanned page, a densely labeled chart and a clear digital paragraph pose different interpretation problems. Listing support for those inputs does not establish accuracy on handwriting, unusual layouts, small annotations or every language. The captured material does not define a comprehensive supported-input specification for those cases.

Choose representative pages from the intended workload and verify the extracted meaning against the original image. Check numbers, units, column headings and notes before using a graph summary. If a page cannot be interpreted confidently, keep the original and escalate the question instead of allowing a rewritten paragraph to hide uncertainty. No OCR engine, chart interpretation or processing limit was exercised for this review. The advertised feature is a reason to test relevant material, not a completed assurance about the reader's documents.


## A large research index is not a relevance or access guarantee

The publisher advertises literature discovery across more than two hundred million scientific papers, including search, filters and automatic summaries. That describes the scale of an asserted research source, not the number of papers relevant to one question or the ability to obtain each full text. It also does not establish which versions are indexed, whether a particular field has adequate coverage or how quickly a new publication appears. The captured pages do not resolve those coverage questions.

Evaluate a search using known relevant papers as well as a genuinely open question. Inspect titles, authors, dates, publication venues and accessible originals before treating a result as established evidence. Distinguish a preprint from a reviewed publication and a summary from a direct reading. A search result that appears authoritative still needs an authenticity and relevance check. This review did not query the index or verify its size, and it does not present a database count as a prediction of research quality.


## Literature synthesis must preserve disagreement and limitations

Finding several papers on a topic is only the beginning of a useful synthesis. Results can refer to different methods, populations, measures or time periods. Combining them into a confident paragraph can obscure those differences even when each individual summary sounds plausible. The publisher advertises faster research and writing, but speed does not remove the need to identify which claim each source actually supports. The product pages do not establish independent scholarly review of generated conclusions.

Create a comparison record with the question, method, relevant finding and stated limitation for each source. Separate a paper's reported observation from an explanation supplied by the writer or the AI. If findings conflict, retain the conflict rather than asking the editor to smooth it into agreement. This is a research practice recommendation, not evidence that the service mishandles disagreement. No article, cited author or study result shown in the publisher's examples has been independently peer reviewed for this review.


## The AI editor changes wording and can change meaning

The captured interface describes improving fluency, paraphrasing, spelling and grammar fixes, expansion, academic rewriting and sentence completion. Those are different editorial operations with different risks. Correcting a typographical error is narrower than asking a tool to make an argument longer. Expansion can introduce new assertions, and a more academic tone can make uncertain material appear stronger without adding evidence. A polished sentence should therefore be checked against the intended meaning rather than approved because it sounds professional.

Keep an original version when revising substantive material, and compare claims after each significant rewrite. Verify that qualifications, negative results and references remain attached to the right statements. Do not use paraphrasing as a way to disguise another author's work or to imply authorship of unreviewed research. The saved pages advertise editorial controls, not an assurance that every output preserves scholarly attribution. No editing operation was performed here, and no claim of originality detection or institutional acceptance is inferred.


## Export formats solve delivery, not editorial approval

The publisher lists Word, ODT, Markdown and GFM exports for paid document work. Supporting several formats is relevant when a draft must move into a report, repository or review process. It does not establish identical handling of headings, references, tables or page-linked evidence across all those destinations. A format name identifies an output route; the reader still needs to inspect the actual document in the application where it will be edited or delivered.

Before relying on a large project, export a small representative draft containing the structures the final document needs. Check heading hierarchy, links, paragraphs and the association between a claim and its source. Determine which information is an interactive feature of the original service and which survives in the exported file. This review has not generated or opened any exports, so it cannot certify formatting fidelity. Acceptance should be based on the required receiving workflow rather than a general promise that export is supported.


## Free and unlimited still have different capacity boundaries

The German pricing capture describes a Free tier with three PDF questions per day, one PDF upload, three AI writing requests per day and a fifty-megabyte file-size limit. It describes the paid tier as unlimited for several question, upload and document-writing counts while listing a hundred-megabyte maximum file size. These are different measurement dimensions. An unlimited question allowance does not mean a single file can have unlimited size, nor does it establish that every technical processing constraint disappears.

Map the intended workload to questions, files, writing requests and maximum document size separately. Ask for clarification if the work involves batch processing, unusually long scans or a shared research team, because the captured offer does not fully specify those cases. Do not assume an organization-wide license from a personal signup page. No quota was reached or reset behavior tested here. These captured limits are useful evaluation inputs, while the actual offer and applicable usage terms still need verification before purchase.

## Privacy statements require the actual processing agreement

The English page makes GDPR-related statements, mentions a European server location and advanced privacy features, and the paid comparison lists Privacy Mode. A server-location statement alone does not explain every processing provider, retention period, support-access path or use of uploaded documents. Before uploading restricted material, obtain the applicable privacy terms and clarify how Privacy Mode affects storage and processing. Determine who can access documents, how deletion is requested and what contractual protections apply to the intended use. Keep those questions specific to the real data instead of treating a compliance label as a universal permission to upload. No control was audited here. ## Choose by research responsibility and the task being completed

Compare it with alternatives using the same permitted documents, known questions and output requirements. Keep answer verification and privacy review in the evaluation budget. A source-linked workflow can be useful without being a substitute for scholarly judgment, domain expertise or an authorized professional review of a consequential decision.
