# NotebookLM review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-18  
**Category:** Source-grounded AI research notebook  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

NotebookLM, now described in current Google support pages as Gemini Notebook, is a research workspace that answers questions from sources a user adds and can turn them into summaries, reports, audio or video overviews, study materials and other artifacts. Its usage limits depend on the selected features, prompt complexity, source count and Google AI or Workspace plan, and Google says quotas can change. Sources can be large, but file and word caps apply. The tool is designed to ground answers in supplied materials, not to guarantee that every summary is complete or that all source details receive an individual citation. Buyers should review source permissions, sharing settings, data terms and current quotas before relying on it for research or confidential work.

## Editorial verdict

This desk review does not make a fraud allegation or claim that Roseram tested a NotebookLM workspace. Google publishes product help, plan and usage-limit documentation, and privacy guidance that support a buyer-focused review. Those pages do not guarantee that every answer is correct, every source will be cited individually, or that a quota will remain fixed. The product is best evaluated as a source-based research aid: verify its output against original material, control notebook sharing and confirm the Google account's data protections and plan limits.

## NotebookLM is now presented as Gemini Notebook in Google's support pages

The product many people know as NotebookLM is described in current Google support materials as Gemini Notebook. It remains a source-based research workspace: users bring documents and other materials into a notebook, ask questions about them, and generate summaries or study and presentation artifacts. The naming shift is worth explaining because older links and search results may still say NotebookLM. Check that the product and account region are supported before building a workflow around a bookmark or tutorial that uses an earlier name.

The central design choice is to organize work around a defined set of sources rather than start with an open-ended question about the entire web. That can make a notebook useful for a course, policy set, project archive or research packet. It also means the source set controls the answer's scope. If a needed fact is not in the documents you supplied, the system may not have it; if a source is incomplete, the generated summary inherits that gap. Source selection is part of the research method, not just an upload step.


## Grounding answers in uploaded material still requires source verification

Google's help page says Gemini Notebook answers questions using information in the notebook's sources and may not answer if the information is absent. It also notes that source length can affect whether an answer has an individual citation. This approach helps readers trace many claims back to supplied material, but it is not a guarantee that every sentence is complete, every interpretation is correct or every citation points to the passage a researcher expected.

For a consequential claim, open the source and inspect the relevant passage. Compare numbers, dates, names and qualifiers with the original document. Ask the notebook to identify which source supports the point, then check the source yourself. Keep contradictory materials in view instead of allowing a single summary to flatten them into agreement. Treat generated reports and overviews as research aids for a human reviewer, not as an authoritative substitute for the source record.


## Large source limits are generous but do not remove document-preparation work

Google's current FAQ lists a limit of 500,000 words per source or 200 MB for a local upload, and says copy-protected PDFs may fail to import. Those caps allow substantial documents, but source quality still matters. Scanned pages, missing text layers, duplicate versions, outdated policies and files with unclear titles can make a notebook harder to query. A large source collection may also affect compute usage and how the model retrieves relevant passages.

Before uploading a project archive, remove duplicates, label files with dates and keep a record of what was included. Test a few representative documents and ask questions whose answers you already know. Check that tables, footnotes and page references survived import. If a document is confidential or belongs to a client, verify permission and account data terms first. Good source hygiene improves research clarity and makes it easier to explain what the notebook did and did not consider.


## Usage limits reflect compute and feature choices, not one fixed prompt count

Google says Gemini Notebook uses compute-based limits that consider prompt complexity, chat length, number of sources and selected features. The publisher states that limits refresh on time windows and can vary by plan; higher access is available through Google AI subscriptions and some Workspace or Google Cloud arrangements. A complicated report or video overview can use a different portion of a quota from a short question. A single daily prompt number is therefore an incomplete description of what a user can do.

The account interface shows remaining usage and when a limit will refresh. Check it during a realistic pilot, especially if the workflow depends on a batch of audio or video overviews. Some generations can be deferred for later, according to Google's help information. Do not plan a deadline around the assumption that an upgrade creates unlimited use. Confirm plan entitlement, quota, region and account type, and keep a manual fallback for work that cannot wait for a refresh.


## NotebookLM has no simple standalone subscription price in the reviewed plan pages

The product help pages describe a standard level and higher limits through Google AI plans, eligible Workspace licenses or Google Cloud, rather than a separate NotebookLM-only subscription price. This makes the bill depend on the broader account or organization plan. A person may already have access through a subscription, while another must upgrade or obtain administrator approval. Compare the complete Google plan and its other included services instead of attributing the entire package price to this research product.

Plan names, limits, availability and terms vary by country and account type. For workplace use, an administrator may control whether Gemini Notebook is enabled and which data protections apply. Get the current price and eligibility from the billing surface for the account you will use. A table of usage multipliers can help compare access, but it is not a cash price or a guarantee that every feature is available in every region.


## Privacy and notebook sharing depend on the account and plan

Google's consumer help states that notebook data is not shared by Gemini Notebook and directs users to the product's privacy information. Plan-specific guidance says data handling differs for consumer, Workspace and Google Cloud use. The help pages describe consumer data as not used to train Gemini Notebook unless the user provides feedback; with feedback, Google may review the context of that interaction. Workspace users receive different stated handling, and Google Cloud enterprise pages describe additional protections and regional controls.

A private notebook can become shared with specific collaborators or publicly accessible if the sharing setting is changed. Check the icon and sharing list before adding sensitive material. For organizational use, verify the exact edition, administrator setting, terms, retention and data location that apply. Do not assume that a consumer account inherits the protections of an Enterprise or Cloud configuration. The correct privacy question is about the particular account and feature you will use, not only the product name.


## A research pilot should test citation quality and failure cases

Choose a set of source documents with known answers, including one answer buried in a long file, one conflicting fact and one question that cannot be answered from the material. Ask the same questions in the notebook, then compare each answer and citation to the original source. Include a prompt that asks for an uncertainty or missing information. Track whether the tool says when a source is silent and whether it invents a connection that the documents do not support.

Also test importing a file with a table or scanned page, sharing the notebook with one collaborator and removing that collaborator again. Note the time needed to prepare sources and verify results, plus any quota used for reports or overviews. This is a proposed buyer test, not a claim that Roseram ran NotebookLM experiments or established a pass rate. The product is most useful when its outputs remain easy to trace back to evidence a person can inspect.


## Who should consider NotebookLM, and what alternatives should they compare?

NotebookLM may fit students, researchers and teams who have a bounded set of material and want to explore, summarize or turn it into study aids. It may not fit a workflow that needs unrestricted web research, guaranteed citations for every sentence, fully deterministic answers, or a plan whose data terms cannot be verified. It can complement a document search system, but should not silently replace the source archive or a subject expert's judgment.

Compare it with a general AI assistant, enterprise search product and conventional document-management workflow using the same source set. Include file preparation, citation review, sharing, quota, data retention and account cost. Check whether your institution already provides access through Google Workspace or Cloud. Choose the tool that helps your readers reach the original evidence with less effort, while keeping uncertainty visible and sensitive documents under the right access controls.


## Features and decision criteria

- Question answering grounded in source materials selected by the user.
- Notebook collections with source and conversation context.
- Audio and video overviews, reports, flashcards, quizzes and research artifacts, with limits that vary by plan.
- Source imports from files and other supported materials with size and word-count caps.
- Usage quotas tied to feature and compute demand, with paid Google AI or Workspace upgrades for higher limits.
- Notebook sharing controls and separate consumer, Workspace and Google Cloud data-handling terms.

## Pricing considerations

NotebookLM is now presented in Google documentation as Gemini Notebook and does not show a standalone per-user price on the product help pages reviewed. Higher limits and features are bundled through Google AI plans, qualifying Workspace editions or Google Cloud. Prices, regional availability and plan names vary; review the current Google subscription or Workspace quote rather than treating a bundled plan as a NotebookLM-only fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is NotebookLM a scam?

This review found no source-based reason to call NotebookLM a scam. Google publishes a research notebook product and current help, plan and privacy documentation. That does not guarantee that every generated answer is accurate or suitable without review.

### Is NotebookLM still called NotebookLM?

Google support pages now describe the service as Gemini Notebook while many users still search for NotebookLM. This review uses both names to make the product identity clear; check the current Google interface and supported URL in your region.

### Does NotebookLM answer only from my sources?

Google describes responses as grounded in the source materials selected for the notebook. Its help page also says the conversation history is used and notes that some answers may not include an individual citation when source content is short. Verify important claims in the original document.

### What are the NotebookLM source limits?

The current help page lists up to 500,000 words per source or 200 MB for local uploads and says copy-protected PDFs may not import. Source counts and other limits vary by plan and may change.

### Does NotebookLM have fixed daily limits?

Google says Gemini Notebook uses compute-based limits that consider prompt complexity, selected features, source count and chat length. Quotas refresh on different schedules and can change; check the usage display in your account.
