# NewsBlur review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-24  
**Category:** Personal news reading and filtering  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

NewsBlur is a personal news reader with feed organization and preference-based intelligence training. Its current official site distinguishes several plans with different source, archive and feature allowances. Search, history and AI-related capabilities should be matched to the actual tier.

## Editorial verdict

NewsBlur is relevant when a reader wants chosen sources with deliberate filtering and retrieval. Test whether the preferred view surfaces useful articles without obscuring the wider feed. Plan limits and archive behavior matter when reading becomes a long-term research routine.

## Train around a reading purpose

Preference controls can make a broad collection easier to use when the reader knows what they want to surface. Start with a modest source list and a clear topic. Check whether the resulting emphasis helps rather than simply making the stream look more agreeable.

Include an occasional review of the wider collection. A preferred view is a selection of the material, not independent evidence that everything relevant has been seen. This review did not train a live account or measure recommendation quality.


## A long-lived archive needs explicit expectations

A reader collecting material for future reference should decide what must remain accessible and in what form. The current plan distinctions make archive requirements a commercial as well as a practical question.

Test retrieving a representative older item and inspect any required export path. A visible saved label does not settle every preservation requirement. Roseram did not verify permanent retention or account recovery behavior.


## Reading layout should help the actual device

A compact list, article view and other layouts can fit different routines. Use a real document with the intended screen and navigation method. The best arrangement is the one that makes the useful information easy to reach without unnecessary movement.

Try returning to an article after an interruption. Reading state should make sense to the user across the chosen applications. This review does not report a synchronization or accessibility test.


## Match the plan to the maintained collection

Compare source count, history and any additional features against the collection you actually intend to keep. The annual and monthly numbers on the current site describe different tiers, so they should not be treated as interchangeable prices for one product.

Check current billing and feature terms directly. This source-based review does not reproduce provider popularity claims as independent proof or promise improved research results. It identifies the documented reader and a bounded evaluation.


## Features and decision criteria

- Personal feed reading
- Preference-based intelligence training
- Saved stories and search paths
- Plan-dependent archive and broader features

## Pricing considerations

These are different plan scopes, not equivalent billing choices. Verify current source allowances, archive, AI and fetching features before subscribing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the lowest paid tier include every archive and AI capability?

The current site distinguishes Premium, Premium Archive and Premium Pro. Review the actual tier’s features.

### Does training remove the need to review sources?

No. Preferences influence selection; source quality and coverage remain separate questions.

### Is a saved story the same as a guaranteed permanent independent copy?

Do not assume that. Verify current archive and export behavior for the selected arrangement.
