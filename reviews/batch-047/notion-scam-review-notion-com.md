# Notion review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Documents, knowledge bases and project workspaces  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Notion combines documents, team knowledge and databases in a configurable workspace. Its flexibility can suit different teams, but buyers should plan structure, permissions and exit requirements.

## Editorial verdict

Notion’s official pricing and help pages describe its plans and project databases. This is a source-based buyer review, not a hands-on test or a finding that the service is fraudulent. Confirm current limits, AI terms and export behavior for your account.

## Notion review: a flexible workspace needs a clear starting structure

Notion can hold documents, reference pages and database-driven work in one workspace. That flexibility is attractive when a team wants its project notes beside its task records; it also means someone must decide how those records are organized. Before building a company wiki, select one small use case: a launch plan, a support handoff or a meeting-to-task process. Write down where a reader should begin, who maintains the information and how a stale page will be recognized. A compact structure that people keep current is more useful than a sprawling collection that merely looks complete.

Create a front page with a few clear destinations and ask a new collaborator to find one current instruction and one related task. Notice whether the labels make sense without a tour. If a user cannot tell which page is authoritative, add a named owner, last-reviewed date or link to the current source. Notion’s product overview positions the service as a place to capture information, find answers and automate work. Those are capabilities to evaluate in your environment, not a promise that an unstructured workspace will answer every question correctly.


## Notion projects and tasks: databases, ownership and useful views

Notion’s official project guide describes related Projects and Tasks databases. Project records can collect properties such as status, people and related tasks; tasks can include an assignee, due date and status. The same database can be shown as a table, board, timeline, calendar, list or gallery. To test whether this fits your work, build a small sample with one project, several tasks, an owner, a due date and a dependency. Check whether contributors can find their next action and whether a manager can see the project’s overall state without maintaining a separate report.

Try editing the same task from two different views. A date changed in a table should remain understandable from the calendar; a blocked task should not disappear simply because the board is grouped by status. Notion’s guide explains that a project can relate to multiple tasks and that the database can calculate progress from completed tasks. Validate those relationships with a deliberately incomplete record. If a dashboard relies on a property people rarely fill in, it will create a false sense of precision rather than useful oversight.


## Notion knowledge management: decide what belongs in a page

A workspace becomes a knowledge base only when readers can tell what to trust. Separate stable instructions from temporary discussion, and give recurring processes an owner. Use a short page for the decision and link to supporting material rather than pasting the same paragraph into five locations. For a policy that changes, include the effective date and a clear path to the current version. These are operating practices, not features that a subscription automatically supplies. The product should make the practice easier to maintain, but the team still owns its accuracy.

Test search with questions a colleague actually asks. Try a common term, an acronym and the name of a project that has changed. Check whether results distinguish a current instruction from an archived draft. If answers depend on connected apps or AI features, verify which sources are included, what permissions apply and how a reader can inspect citations or underlying records. Do not put sensitive content into a workspace until its access model and data handling have been reviewed against your own policy.


## Notion pricing and AI features: verify the included access

Notion’s official pricing page, checked 2026-11-16, displayed Free at $0, Plus at $10 per member per month and Business at $20 per member per month. The page describes a limited trial of AI capabilities on lower tiers; Business lists Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search Beta. Confirm whether these tools meet your requirements and how usage or credit charges work before relying on them. Product names and included limits can change, so treat the publisher’s page and the checkout summary as authoritative for the account you will use.

Compare the monthly workspace bill with the cost of the systems it might replace, including storage, project tracking, documentation and integrations. Then add migration time, maintenance and any usage-based AI charges. A lower seat price does not guarantee a lower total cost if the team has to duplicate data or purchase extra services. Conversely, paying for features that no one uses is not evidence of quality. Start with the smallest plan that can support a representative workflow and set a date to reassess whether the team adopted it.


## Notion alternatives: make a like-for-like comparison

Compare Notion with the tools your team already uses for documents and project tracking. Prepare the same short brief in each candidate: one decision, a handful of tasks, a related file and a reader who needs to find the current version. Compare the effort to create, search, share, revise and export that material. Include access controls and the number of paid members as well as the visible feature set. A specialist task manager may be clearer for assignments; a document-first service may be easier for policies. The right comparison depends on the job, not the broad category label.

Ask every provider what survives export and which content is omitted. Try to move a sample page and database to a separate location, preserving links and context where possible. If the result loses important relationships, record that migration cost before expanding. Check whether alternatives charge separately for automation, AI, storage or external guests. Comparing the same workflow and billing period keeps a marketing page from deciding the outcome on your behalf.


## Notion permissions, workspace ownership and portability

A shared workspace can gather sensitive plans alongside ordinary reference material, so test permissions with separate accounts before importing live data. Check what a guest can see, who can invite members and which pages inherit access from a teamspace. Remove a test user and confirm that the intended access ends. The official guide notes that access to databases placed in a teamspace follows that teamspace’s permissions; confirm current behavior in the product rather than assuming that a page link itself is private.

Choose an administrator and a second person who can recover the workspace. Document how to export important pages and databases, where backups are kept and how a team would continue working during a service interruption. Restore a small sample and inspect the result before calling the backup complete. This review does not establish any particular security certification or promise that every export retains every relation. Ask Notion for the current security and data-retention documentation if those points are part of your procurement criteria.


## Who should consider Notion, and what to verify before paying

Notion is worth evaluating when a team wants documents and structured project information to live together and can assign someone to maintain the workspace. Its database views and linked records may reduce the need to keep parallel notes, provided people use consistent properties and permissions. Start with a contained project, observe whether readers can find current guidance and verify the plan’s member limits, AI terms and export options before expanding. A demonstration using your own realistic example is more informative than a list of features alone.

The available official sources support a measured conclusion: Notion offers configurable pages and database-based project workflows, with plan features and prices published by the company. They do not prove that every team will benefit, nor do they justify claims of fraud. Keep the decision tied to an outcome you can check: faster handoff, fewer duplicate records or a maintainable reference library. If you cannot name the owner, the recurring cost or the exit path, resolve those questions before committing to a broad rollout.


## Features and decision criteria

- Pages and shared team knowledge
- Databases with multiple views and properties
- Project and task relationships with assignees, dates and status
