# OBS Studio review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Recording and livestream production  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

OBS Studio handles recording and streaming. Its recording guide discusses output settings, container choice and audio tracks.

## Editorial verdict

The OBS project publishes a recording guide. Verify the terms of third-party plugins, production services and training packages.

## OBS Studio recording settings for a webinar rehearsal

Rehearse the event your audience will attend. Switch from camera to slides and take one question. Inspect the recording for readable text and check that microphone and desktop audio arrived at the levels the audience expects.

Write down the **OBS Studio recording settings** beside the rehearsal file. Name the person responsible for watching audio and checking disk space. When the presenter is busy, the audience depends on someone else noticing a problem.


## OBS Studio audio tracks and recording recovery

Use a short recording to confirm the *separate audio tracks* are recognized by the editing application. Check which track a normal player opens by default. Ask the editor to turn the presenter down while keeping the material being demonstrated audible.

In a disposable rehearsal, test what happens when recording stops unexpectedly. Use the project guide’s container guidance and confirm the exact file format the next editor can accept. A decision made before the event is worth more than a repair attempted after the audience leaves.


## Features and decision criteria

- Scenes and sources for production
- Recording output configuration
- Separate recording audio tracks
