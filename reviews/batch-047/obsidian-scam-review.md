# Obsidian review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-11-16  
**Category:** Local notes and knowledge management  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Obsidian stores notes in a local vault and sells an optional Sync service. The decision includes how the notes survive a change of device, plug-ins or personnel.

## Editorial verdict

The publisher documents its sync service. Confirm current terms on the official site and consider any third-party extension on its own merits.

## Obsidian local Markdown vault for a new colleague

Give the newcomer a question your team has answered before. See whether they can follow two relevant notes and find the decision they need. In a **local Markdown vault**, good filenames, useful links and short summaries often save a visitor more time than another plug-in.

Open a sample of the same notes in a plain editor. Inspect links, attachments and any special syntax. Write down which parts a future reader can recover from the files and which require the application or a particular extension.


## Obsidian Sync costs and a lost-device exercise

Compare the listed Sync tiers using the number and size of vaults you intend to keep. Rehearse connecting a spare device and retrieving an attachment. Ask who retains the necessary recovery details when the person who configured the service leaves.

Keep an independent *vault backup* and try recovering a deleted note. Zapier’s note-taking review highlighted links between notes as an Obsidian strength. If your library is a collection of PDFs and clipped material, compare Joplin Cloud Basic with the same attachments. The publishers list a 5 MB per-file limit for Obsidian Sync Standard and a 10 MB per-note-or-attachment limit for Joplin Cloud Basic. Compare prices in their listed currencies and decide which files actually need to travel.


## Features and decision criteria

- Local note vaults
- Markdown-based notes and links
- Optional paid sync service

## Pricing considerations

Standard lists one synced vault and a 5 MB limit per file. Plus lists ten vaults and a 200 MB limit per file. The prices cover optional Sync.
