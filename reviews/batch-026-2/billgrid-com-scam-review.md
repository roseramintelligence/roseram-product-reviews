# BillGrid.com review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-27  
**Category:** Invoicing & Billing Software  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

BillGrid.com provides web-based invoicing, time tracking, expense tracking, and retainer management for solo operators and small teams. It connects with Stripe for card collections without taking a secondary platform fee. Subscriptions range from single-operator setups to multi-seat teams with multi-business controls. Features include document attachment, automated payment reminders, late fee assessments, and data exports in CSV or Excel formats. A thirty-day evaluation period requires no payment card, and users retain full control over data export and subscription renewals.

## Software Capabilities and Daily Invoicing Workflow

BillGrid.com consolidates client invoicing, project milestones, task management, and billable hour recording into a single web application. The daily workflow begins at the project level, where users can establish specific milestones, register subtasks, and log incurred expenses alongside project labor. For example, a design agency can establish a deliverable milestone, track individual layout revisions, attach hard pass-through production costs, and convert those accumulated elements into a complete itemized invoice with one action.

The software includes customizable invoice layouts, offering four visual styles alongside corporate logo placement and brand color matching. Invoices display itemized descriptions, tax calculations, payment terms, and recipient addresses formatted for desktop viewing, mobile browsers, or PDF downloads. Once an invoice reaches draft status, users can send it directly to the customer with personalized text messages and attached supporting documentation.

To test this workflow during an evaluation period, a prospective buyer should create a test project containing both billable hourly labor and fixed expense lines. Finalizing those subtasks should automatically calculate an accurate draft invoice balance without manual mathematical reconciliations. Verifying that project milestones mark complete while accurately compiling line items confirms that the software handles fundamental bookkeeping sequences reliably.


## Subscription Tiers and Pricing Context

Understanding subscription structures prevents unexpected budget surprises when transitioning from a trial to active business use. BillGrid organizes its commercial catalog into Solo, Pro, Team, and custom Enterprise tiers. The Solo tier serves independent professionals managing a single trade identity with unlimited clients, invoices, and estimates. The Pro tier adds multi-business management for up to three company identities, Google Calendar connections, and AI assistant capabilities, while allowing the addition of two extra staff logins for an added monthly fee per seat.

For expanding organizations, the Team plan packages four logins covering an administrator and three staff members, along with the ability to invoice across an unlimited number of operating businesses. Additional seats require a standard monthly fee per user. Furthermore, the Team tier introduces granular administrative permissions, permitting company owners to restrict staff visibility regarding overall business income or limit specific personnel to designated client accounts. Priority email support is also reserved for the Team tier.

Buyers must recognize that yearly subscription plans are billed upfront and explicitly carry a non-refundable policy. While standard monthly billing permits cancellation ahead of the subsequent monthly charge, committing to an annual plan locks the capital investment for twelve months. Testing the full thirty-day trial prior to selecting annual billing protects businesses from committing funds to an administrative structure that might not suit their long-term team expansion.


## Retainer Administration and Recurring Billing

Managing ongoing service contracts through general-purpose office spreadsheets frequently produces billing errors, unbilled hours, and awkward client disputes. BillGrid incorporates a dedicated retainer module engineered to monitor monthly recurring fees alongside contractual hourly limits. The platform provides oversight into consumed hours, scheduled recurring billing cycles, and unbilled work across individual client accounts.

A critical feature within the retainer module is the ability to enforce contractual caps on unused hours carried forward into subsequent billing cycles. A service provider offering twenty monthly consulting hours can configure the system to permit a maximum carryover of five unused hours into the following month, preventing unchecked liability accumulation. Conversely, hours worked beyond the contractual retainer allowance can be earmarked for supplemental invoicing based on predefined client terms.

A buyer evaluating this system should test a simulated six-month retainer agreement using historical client records. By inputting fluctuating monthly service hours, the evaluator can verify whether the software accurately calculates monthly retainer base fees, automatically applies carryover restrictions, and correctly flags supplemental hours. Ensuring this balance calculates without manual spreadsheet intervention validates the platform's suitability for predictable client accounting.


## Timekeeping Precision and Expense Allocation

Accurate revenue capture depends on capturing small, billable operational tasks that service providers frequently forget to log. BillGrid includes a quick-entry system where professionals can type conversational work descriptions, such as specifying task titles alongside explicit time windows. The software parses these inputs to record hours, associate the labor with the correct client account, and present unlogged gaps across the working day for administrative review.

In addition to real-time timer functions, the platform supports both daily and weekly timesheets for staff members. Unbilled hours remain visible on administrative dashboards, showing the precise financial value waiting to be transformed into customer invoices. Expenses incurred during project delivery, such as specialized materials or pass-through print proofs, can be logged alongside timesheets and designated for immediate invoice inclusion.

Organizations transitioning from standalone tracking tools can import existing client lists via CSV and migrate historical time records from platforms like Harvest or Toggl 2.0. Buyers should verify during their onboarding review that imported time records maintain correct project associations and billing status flags, ensuring that previous unbilled client balances transfer seamlessly into the new operational ledger.


## Client Experience and Stripe Payment Gateway Execution

The end-client payment interaction determines how quickly invoices get settled and influences external impressions of a company's professionalism. BillGrid routes digital invoice payments through direct Stripe connections, ensuring card data is handled exclusively within Stripe's certified payment infrastructure. Crucially, BillGrid does not impose intermediate transaction surcharges on top of standard Stripe merchant fees, preserving full payment net value aside from payment gateway costs.

Clients receive an online link providing access to a secure customer portal where they can inspect invoice breakdowns, read attached files, and submit card payments directly. The platform supports automated workflow configurations that enforce late fees according to stated payment terms and issue sequential payment reminder emails before or after invoice due dates. Partial payments can be received, recorded, and reflected against remaining balances in real time.

A proper due diligence plan should include testing the live checkout flow using Stripe test transaction credentials. Evaluators should confirm that submitting a test payment immediately updates the invoice status to paid, logs the transaction in the system ledger, displays a zero balance to the customer, and terminates any scheduled automated reminder sequences. Confirming this instant reconciliation prevents embarrassing automated debt collection notices from reaching satisfied clients.


## Document Attachments and Paid Content Delivery

Service businesses frequently need to deliver final deliverables, project assets, receipts, or technical sign-off sheets alongside their formal invoices. BillGrid addresses this operational requirement by providing allocated cloud storage across its plans, starting at 10 GB on the Solo tier, expanding to 50 GB on Pro, and reaching 100 GB on the Team tier. Additional storage blocks can be purchased on any tier for a flat monthly surcharge.

A practical workflow feature is the ability to lock specific attached documents until the corresponding invoice is marked fully paid. For instance, a commercial photographer can upload high-resolution asset packages or final print licenses that remain inaccessible inside the client portal until the customer successfully clears their outstanding balance through the Stripe payment form.

To validate this delivery mechanism, evaluators should upload a sample deliverable file set with access gated behind invoice settlement. By accessing the link from an external browser simulating the client view, the evaluator can verify that access remains restricted until the test transaction completes. Once settled, the customer portal should instantly unlock file download links without manual administrative approval.


## Data Portability, Reporting, and Account Security

Vendor lock-in represents a significant operational risk when adopting proprietary cloud software. Businesses must retain the ability to extract financial records, audit trails, and client details whenever internal practices change or tax authorities demand physical documentation. BillGrid provides built-in reporting screens covering revenue performance, historical client payments, recorded hours, and logged expenses, with raw data exports supported in standard CSV or Microsoft Excel formats.

From an operational security perspective, the publisher mandates HTTPS encryption across every web page to safeguard transmitted accounting data. Because the application delegates credit card processing to Stripe, sensitive card numbers never touch or reside on BillGrid servers. Furthermore, users can terminate service directly through account settings panels without being forced to navigate cumbersome telephonic sales retentions.

Before loading sensitive customer books into the platform, administrators should generate an experimental export covering test client records and invoice ledgers. Opening the resulting CSV or Excel spreadsheets in external software confirms that column headers, payment timestamps, tax amounts, and project identifiers remain legible, properly formatted, and suitable for external bookkeeping or long-term archiving.

## Operational Boundaries and Platform Limitations

While BillGrid handles core invoicing and hourly billing effectively, it does not function as a comprehensive double-entry accounting suite or general ledger system. Companies requiring balance sheets, deep inventory tracking, depreciation calculations, or direct bank feed reconciliations must maintain external accounting software. The platform focuses narrowly on practice management, milestone billing, and accounts receivable collections.

Another operational boundary involves communication volume. The platform limits automated reminder emails and invoice dispatches to one hundred recipients daily under standard tiers, requiring businesses with larger immediate distribution requirements to contact support for quota adjustments. Additionally, Solo tier accounts are limited to a single operating trade identity, meaning entrepreneurs running multiple distinct corporate brands must upgrade to Pro or Team tiers.

Finally, support channels vary according to plan level. While standard email support is available across all paid tiers, priority triage is explicitly limited to Team subscriptions. Buyers who operate complex organizations and demand rapid, contractual support turnaround times should take this tiered help desk model into account before making a deployment choice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does BillGrid take a cut of invoice payments?

BillGrid does not charge an extra platform processing fee on client payments. Card collections pass directly into the user's connected Stripe account, where standard Stripe credit card processing fees apply.

### Can unused retainer hours carry forward to subsequent months?

Yes. The platform provides retainer settings that allow business owners to track included hours, monitor actual usage, and cap unused hours that roll over into the next billing period.

### What happens when the 30-day free trial expires?

If a payment method is not added, the account pauses automatically. BillGrid sends email notifications ten, five, and one day before trial completion, keeping existing data safe until a plan is selected.

### Are annual subscriptions refundable if canceled early?

No. Annual subscriptions are billed upfront and are strictly non-refundable. Canceled accounts retain full operational access through the end of the prepaid billing period.
