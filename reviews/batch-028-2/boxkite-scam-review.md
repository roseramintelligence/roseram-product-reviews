# Boxkite review: features, pricing and buyer considerations

**Last editorial review:** 2026-10-28  
**Category:** Xero data backup  
**Method:** Source-led desk review; no hands-on testing is claimed.

Boxkite describes copying Xero data to storage controlled by the customer or producing a one-off download. The central evaluation is recoverability: whether the records needed for your purpose are present, understandable and accessible when required. A successful transfer message is not enough to establish a complete recovery plan.

## Boxkite: define the recovery question before choosing a backup

A business may need records for continuity, historical reference or moving to another arrangement. Those purposes can require different material. List the accounting records, attachments and relationships that matter before evaluating an export. A folder containing many files can still be incomplete for the specific question you need to answer.

The publisher describes Xero data backups and customer-controlled storage destinations. Confirm the current scope and destination you intend to use. A readable copy of data should not be assumed to be a complete restorable image of an accounting application. Ask what the documented output supports and what additional work would be required to use it.


## A sample retrieval and reconciliation exercise

Use an authorized test or low-risk sample and choose a record whose supporting details you already understand. Locate the corresponding exported information and check important relationships and attachments. Ask another responsible person to repeat the retrieval using the documented process. This evaluates the usefulness of the copy rather than only the transfer itself.

Review destination access and retention. A backup placed in a broadly shared folder can expose information even if the source account is carefully restricted. Confirm which account owns the destination and how access is removed when staff change. This review does not audit Boxkite’s security or establish a verified restore procedure for your business.

A useful recovery question is whether an authorized colleague can find a specific historical record after the person who configured the backup is unavailable. Test the documented procedure using a harmless sample. This evaluates access ownership and practical retrieval together. Do not wait for a real incident to discover that the files exist in a personal storage account that the business cannot reliably access.


## Compare backup operation and recovery work

Confirm the current record scope, frequency and pricing arrangement. Include the effort of monitoring failures and checking that copies remain usable. A backup process should have an owner and an explicit response when something is missing; silent accumulation of files is not a complete operating plan.
